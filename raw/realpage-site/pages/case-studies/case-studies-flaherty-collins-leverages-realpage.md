#### Streamlined purchasing, invoicing and vendor management increases efficiency and enables property managers to budget with precision accuracy.
Flaherty & Collins Properties management team recognized the need for a seamless, easy-to-implement solution that would enable them to approve vendors, estimate expenses, create budgets, split monthly invoices, provide accounting help, improve efficiency and generate cost savings. Implementing multiple solutions from RealPage®, Flaherty & Collins Properties has dramatically streamlined their workflow, creating efficiencies across the organization and gaining full control of purchasing, invoicing and vendor management — with the transparency they need to rein in rogue spending.
“[With Ops Technology™], all I do is create an order and split it among all of our properties. I just did 92 properties worth of invoices in 20 minutes,” says Gavin Greene, Director of Management Systems at Flaherty & Collins Properties.
