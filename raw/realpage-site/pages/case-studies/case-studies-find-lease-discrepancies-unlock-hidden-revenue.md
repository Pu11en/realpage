## How Summit Identified $110K in Just 3 Weeks
Manual lease audits were slowing Summit Property Management down. Teams spent days reviewing leases against ledgers, checking rent, fees, and deposits—only to still risk missing discrepancies that could lead to lost revenue. As the company prepared to expand across multiple states, this time-intensive process became a barrier to efficient growth.
Summit implemented the RealPage AI Operations Agent to automate and streamline lease audits. The solution continuously compares leases to ledgers and flags discrepancies, all within their existing OneSite® workflow. What was once a manual, reactive process became a faster, more accurate, and always-on operation that reduced staff workload while improving compliance and visibility.
The impact was immediate. Within just three weeks, Summit completed 789 audits, **saved 131 hours of staff time, and uncovered up to $110,000 in potential revenue across seven properties. Audit times dropped from days to about 1.5 hours per property** , giving teams the speed and insight needed to scale operations and capture revenue opportunities more consistently.
