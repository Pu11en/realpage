#### Columbia Residential turned to RealPage to create a comprehensive package to manage vendor credentialing.
When Columbia Residential began experiencing rapid growth, they also started to struggle with onboarding their vendors quickly and getting them compliant.
They partnered with RealPage to help manage vendors that service their properties in various locations through a Vendor Credentialing solution that was customized to meet their unique business needs.
It’s a solid partnership that provides on-demand access to all vendor information documents, ensuring a complete audit trail. And it eliminates the time-consuming work of onboarding and following up with the entire portfolio of vendors to update insurance paperwork, licensing and vendor agreements.
As Columbia Residential continues to grow, the RealPage Vendor Credentialing team will be essential in maintaining the expanding vendor enrollment and compliance while helping minimize risk.
For more information, visit [RealPage Vendor Credentialing](https://www.realpage.com/vendor-credentialing/).
