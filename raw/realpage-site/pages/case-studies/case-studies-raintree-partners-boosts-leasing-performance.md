# Raintree Partners Boosts Leasing Performance with Knock CRM

#### Discover how Raintree Partners saved 3,500+ staff hours annually by unifying its front office technology with Knock CRM®.
Raintree Partners transformed its property management operations by migrating from fragmented, siloed systems to RealPage's integrated Knock CRM platform. Before the transition, inconsistent reporting, inefficient tour scheduling, and duplicate data created significant operational challenges across their 45 communities.
By implementing Knock CRM, Raintree centralized its front office operations with a unified dashboard and advanced automation. This strategic shift delivered immediate, measurable results. **Prospects now self-schedule 70% of all tours** , leading to higher show-up rates and a frictionless experience. The platform’s efficiency gains **save leasing staff over 3,500 hours annually** , allowing them to focus on high-value, lead-converting activities instead of administrative tasks. With an intuitive interface and powerful analytics, the platform provides full visibility from first touch to renewal, enabling data-driven decisions.
