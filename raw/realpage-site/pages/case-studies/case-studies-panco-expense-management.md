#### Panco Management rolls out Expense Management suite to transform company-wide operations for continuous improvement and growth.
Panco Management selected the comprehensive RealPage Expense Management suite to drive a company-wide transition toward paperless processes and increased operational efficiency for continued year-over-year growth.
Using the innovative features of RealPage Procurement and Utility Management across 34 properties and 12,000 units, Panco implemented RealPage’s advanced technology, training and data to automate and streamline paper-based processes, making the entire operation more efficient, creating more value, and allowing employees to focus on resident satisfaction.
Thanks to the powerful tools for purchasing, invoicing, ordering, billing, utility invoicing and resident billing and ongoing close collaboration with the RealPage team, Panco has already generated gains in NOI, cost control and productivity and expects even greater future improvements—fulfilling its own mission to outperform for clients.
