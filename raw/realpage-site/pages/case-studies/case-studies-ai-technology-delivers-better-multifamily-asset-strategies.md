#### A client gained a competitive edge with a new AI-driven approach to revenue management
A RealPage® institutional client wanted to execute their asset strategy better and advance their pricing strategies across their national real estate portfolio of 55 properties.
With the help of the RealPage advisory services team, the client engaged in a pilot study that upgraded six of their multifamily properties in the Denver market from YieldStar® to AI Revenue Management.
In this case study, find out:
  * What the client’s key motivators were behind the AI technology upgrade
  * What happened to effective revenue growth, retention average, effective rent roll growth and occupancy average once AI revenue management software was implemented
  * What the client did after the pilot study was complete
