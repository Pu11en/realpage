#### Purpose-built solution for commercial real estate management delivers customized, integrated capabilities for expanding family-run firm’s approach to accuracy
With more than 750K SF of retail, service business and office rental space throughout Delaware, Rojan Investments experienced increasing difficulty to efficiently handle data, documents and administration for their growing statewide portfolio of developments, properties and tenants. Outdated software required excessive time to support accounting and property management, creating challenges in delivering accurate, relevant and timely information internally and externally.
RealPage Commercial Property Management and RealPage Accounting Suite provided Rojan Investments with a comprehensive solution suited for their unique real estate operation, including financial and property reporting, precise calculations of tenant charges, notifications of rent or lease adjustments, secure document storage and access, ease of use and updates, and efficient reporting for owner visibility into relevant assets.
Smooth implementation and training gave the Rojan team the confidence and guidance to immediately improve processes and results. Seamless integration of banking, billing and accounting data with operational information has streamlined their approach to accurately maintain, change and report up-to-date numbers for all the activity across their property centers. RealPage Commercial Property Management and RealPage Accounting Suite were perfectly customized to fit all of Rojan Investments’ needs in one unified platform.
