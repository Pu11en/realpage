At the epicenter of the AI and tech industry boom is California’s Bay Area, which is experiencing a subsequent surge in apartment demand. Southern California, meanwhile, presents a mixed performance picture—ranging from slow-and-steady conditions in Orange County and select Los Angeles suburbs to acute demand challenges in San Diego and hyperlocal supply headwinds in Los Angeles’ urban core.
What other factors will influence California’s 2026 apartment market outlook? And what are economists and industry analysts most focused on as they assess statewide supply and demand dynamics? Join **Carl Whitaker, RealPage Vice President & Chief Economist**, and **Adam Couch, RealPage Market Research Analyst** , for an in-depth webcast examining the forces shaping apartment performance across the nation’s most populous state. Drawing on RealPage’s proprietary data and market expertise, Carl and Adam will break down how shifting supply pipelines, demand drivers, and performance metrics are setting the stage for 2026.
### Carl Whitaker
