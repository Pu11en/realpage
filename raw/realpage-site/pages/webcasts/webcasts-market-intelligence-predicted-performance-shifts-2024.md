# Market Intelligence: Predicted Performance Shifts in 2024
Aired on Thursday, December 14, 2023
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/market-intelligence-predicted-performance-shifts-2024/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/market-intelligence-predicted-performance-shifts-2024/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/market-intelligence-predicted-performance-shifts-2024/#hosts)

RealPage® data analysts’ 2024 multifamily market performance predictions are in. Major rental real estate metros can expect to see upward and downward performance shifts. What does this mean for your multifamily portfolio as you plan for 2024? Find out at our upcoming webcast.
Join RealPage data analysts Carl Whitaker, Senior Director of Research & Analysis, and Casey Christian, Strategic Performance Advisor, for their overview of the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats that may impact 2024 strategies and transactional benchmarking STARS data and triggers that will drive change.
Topics to be discussed in this webcast include:
  * Occupancy, demand and lease trade out driving NOI
  * Transactional performance benchmarking across multiple KPIs
  * Key factors causing the expected change in performance trends

Markets to be covered in this webcast:
  * Miami
  * Chicago
  * Washington, DC

### Carl Whitaker
Vice President & Chief Economist, RealPage, Inc.
Carl Whitaker is RealPage’s Chief Economist, leading the company's team of economists and market analysts. Carl and the Market Intelligence team specialize in analyzing and disseminating economic and market trends for the rental housing industry. He primarily focuses on presenting trends in the rental housing industry to a wide audience, including investors, asset managers, operations professionals and media, among other personas. Carl has been cited in the Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg and GlobeSt and is a frequent keynote speaker for industry events. Carl holds degrees from the University of North Texas, including a Master of Science in Applied Geography.
### Casey Christian
Strategic Performance Advisor, RealPage, Inc.
Casey Christian has been with RealPage® since 2017. He served as a revenue manager working with a diverse portfolio of clients utilizing YieldStar® before joining the Strategic Performance Advisory team in 2021. He has more than 14 years of experience in operations with roles that include leasing, marketing, regional management and director.
