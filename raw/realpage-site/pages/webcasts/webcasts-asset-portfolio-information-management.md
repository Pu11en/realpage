### Colin Kovas
Industry Principal, Investment Management, RealPage
Colin Kovas is VP and Industry Principal in the Investment Management group with responsibility for and oversight of product, delivery, Key Accounts and Sales. Mr. Kovas joined RealPage in July 2019 through the acquisition of Hipercept Solutions, where he served as CEO of the global consultancy. Prior to Hipercept, Mr. Kovas worked at Brookfield Asset Management. With over 20 years of experience in large-scale business transformations, global operations and management, Mr. Kovas brings a wealth of experience to the Investment Management team.
