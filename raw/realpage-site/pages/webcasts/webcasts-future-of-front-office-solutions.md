# The Future of Multifamily Front Office Solutions
Aired on Thursday, January 26, 2023
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/future-of-front-office-solutions/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/future-of-front-office-solutions/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/future-of-front-office-solutions/#hosts)

What does the future of multifamily’s front office solutions look like? An integrated platform that brings together smart digital marketing and CRM technology to provide a better experience for your leasing teams, prospects and residents.
In this informative webcast, multifamily experts share how an integrated front office solution can help you reshape the prospect-to-resident experience and prepare for future challenges in the industry.
  * What problems can front office platforms address?
  * How can responsive marketing data help you make smart budget decisions?
  * What are the best marketing and CRM strategies to address a slowdown in apartment demand?
  * How can you combine AI chat and voice, call scoring, tour self-scheduling and smart digital advertising to optimize your customer experience?
  * How can centralization help boost efficiency?

Hosted by:
  * John Hinckley, SVP/Leasing & Living, RealPage®

Panelists:
  * Demetri Themelis, SVP/Renter Engagement Innovation, RealPage®
  * Katie Nelson, Vice President of Marketing, CAPREIT

### Katie Nelson
Vice President of Marketing, CAPREIT
Katie Nelson is the Vice President of Marketing for CAPREIT, responsible for marketing, advertising, digital marketing, analytics, design, and brand management. Before joining CAPREIT, she was a proprietor of a marketing consulting firm and a Real Estate Agent. Behind Katie's charismatic charm is an innovative, tough, and successful leader in multifamily marketing. She has a strong drive and passion for propelling the industry to find creative ways to culminate new customer experiences. Katie considers herself an extroverted introvert and loves fast cars and delicious red wine.

### John Hinckley
SVP, Living Suite, RealPage, Inc.
John Hinckley, CEO at Modern Message (a RealPage company), is a born entrepreneur who has focused the past 15 years creating technology-based solutions for multifamily real estate. He was the Founder of IRIO, the first text messaging solution for multifamily and student housing. He spent the past 8 years leading Modern Message which is best known for creating the #1 resident engagement and loyalty app for apartment communities in the United States called Community Rewards. With a track record in building successful startups, he is committed to the advancement of new ideas and disruption within the world of apartment technology. Mr. Hinckley is a 2004 Business major graduate from Baylor University and currently lives in West Plano with wife Kate, and 3 children.
