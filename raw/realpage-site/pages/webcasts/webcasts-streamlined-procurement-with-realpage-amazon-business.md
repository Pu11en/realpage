# Streamlining Procurement with Realpage and Amazon Business
__Thursday, September 24, 2026 __01:00PM CDT

Join experts from Realpage®, Amazon Business®, and The Michaels Organization® for an insightful discussion on how integrated procurement solutions can help property management organizations streamline purchasing, improve spend visibility, strengthen approval controls, and drive operational efficiency.
This customer-focused session will explore the Amazon Business PunchOut experience within Realpage and share real-world perspectives from The Michaels Organization on implementation, adoption, and the impact of centralized purchasing across properties and teams.
Procurement in property management can be complex, especially when teams are purchasing across multiple properties, vendors, and approval processes. Without the right systems in place, organizations may face limited visibility into spend, inconsistent purchasing practices, and time-consuming manual workflows.
In this session, we’ll cover:
  * How Realpage and Amazon Business help simplify procurement workflows
  * The benefits of PunchOut integration, including improved spend visibility and approval controls
  * Customer perspectives on implementation, adoption, and operational efficiencies
  * Best practices for centralizing purchasing across properties and teams
  * Practical ways finance, operations, and procurement leaders can improve purchasing efficiency and cost control

This Realpage webcast will help you improve efficiency, increase purchasing visibility, and better manage procurement across properties and teams.
### Kamila Snuffer
Financial System Specialist, The Michaels Organization
**Kamila Snuffer** is a **Financial Systems Specialist at The Michaels Organization** , where she has spent more than **19 years** helping drive operational excellence through technology and process improvement.
Throughout her career, Kamila has trained users with varying levels of system experience, translating complex financial systems and processes into practical, easy-to-understand guidance and training materials. She has developed innovative strategies to streamline financial operations, improve efficiency, and support successful technology adoption across the organization.
A longtime **Realpage OpsTechnology** user, Kamila has extensive experience optimizing procurement and purchasing workflows and recently led the implementation of the **Amazon Business Punchout integration** , helping simplify purchasing while improving compliance and operational efficiency.
