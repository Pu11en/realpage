# Aligning to Your Accounting Solutions with a Streamlined Source-to-Pay Program
Aired on Wednesday, February 25, 2026
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/aligning-your-accounting-solutions/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/aligning-your-accounting-solutions/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/aligning-your-accounting-solutions/#hosts)

A streamlined source-to-pay program is crucial for organizations aiming to increase operational efficiency and achieve seamless integration with their accounting solutions. When these processes are properly aligned, companies can develop a unified financial workflow.
Join Sundar Kamak, VP & GM, Spend Management, RealPage, Brandee Williams, Solutions Representative, RealPage and Drew Cohalla, Operations & Communications Coordinator, Yarco Property Management as they discuss how this alignment supports accurate reporting, helps maintain compliance, and enables improved decision-making throughout the organization.
## Alignment Benefits
  * Enable data consistency between procurement and accounting systems
  * Reduce manual data entry and minimize the risk of errors
  * Facilitate timely payments and smooth reconciliations
  * Support compliance with financial policies and regulations
  * Enable better spend management and provide enhanced visibility

Aligning accounting solutions with a streamlined source-to-pay program reinforces the entire financial process. This approach delivers greater accuracy, accountability, and operational effectiveness across the organization.
## Key Takeaways for Attendees
  * Understand how a streamlined source-to-pay process can reduce operational complexity and enhance vendor management
  * Learn about the integration of source-to-pay programs with accounting solutions for improved financial control
  * Gain best practices and real-world insights from our guest panelist
