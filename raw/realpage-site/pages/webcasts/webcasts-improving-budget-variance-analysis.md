Proactive property managers regularly report variances in their budgets and submit those variances for review each month.
This timely webinar, hosted by RealPage®’s Dan Newbern and Victoria Sicula, will show both prospective and current customers how using Budget Variance Analysis—a tool in RealPage Accounting and the Financial Suite that empowers customers to optimize their business process by reporting operational activity for their properties—will generate reporting periods and easily submit this critical information.
This webcast will cover topics including:
  * Current customers will find value in the demonstration and presentation of the benefits currently available
  * Prospective customers will come to see a deeper, tangible value to RealPage Accounting and the full Financial Suite
