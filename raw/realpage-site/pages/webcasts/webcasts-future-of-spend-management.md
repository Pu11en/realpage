# The Future of Spend Management Is Source-to-Pay
Aired on Wednesday, March 23, 2022
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/future-of-spend-management/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/future-of-spend-management/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/future-of-spend-management/#hosts)

Managing the procurement-to-payment processes for your properties is only part of the equation with it comes to controlling spend and maximizing savings.
This webcast shows you how a "Source-to-Pay" strategy for managing all sourcing, vendor selection, procurement, invoicing and payment processes can help you achieve real operational efficiency.
Join RealPage® Senior Vice President, Sara Jones, and Product Marketing Director, Gayle Boone, as they discuss the differences between "Procure-to-Pay" and "Source-to-Pay" and why this transformation is critical for companies looking to achieve peak performance.
As an added bonus, you'll get a preview of the new bids and contracts module in Vendor Marketplace that simplifies the process to create bid requests for vendors, view bid response details, award bids and create contracts.
### Sara Jones
SVP, GM Spend & Vendor Management, RealPage, Inc.
Sara is responsible for product management and operations for all RealPage Spend and Vendor Management solutions including OpsTechnology, Vendor Credentialing, Vendor Marketplace, eSupply GPO, Vendor Compliance Services and SmartSource Accounting Services. Sara has been with RealPage for over 13 years and has over 18 years of experience in the multifamily industry. She’s focused on leading product and operations teams to achieve company goals and delivering strategic metrics to maintain workflow success for clients.
### Gayle Boone
Product Marketing Director - Spend & Vendor Management, RealPage, Inc.
Gayle joined RealPage in 2018 to develop marketing campaigns for spend management, vendor management, accounting, finance, maintenance and vacation rental solutions. She now leads go-to-market and launch initiatives for RealPage spend and vendor management solutions. She enjoys being a product marketing catalyst who brings leaders from sales, client service, support, product development and marketing together to propose and deliver solutions that delight customers.
Gayle completed her Core Product Marketing Certification with the Product Marketing Alliance in 2020, and currently serves on the leadership committee of RealPage’s Empow-HER employee resource group.
