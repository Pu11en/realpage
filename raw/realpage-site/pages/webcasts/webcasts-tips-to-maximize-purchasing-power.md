# 5 Tips to Maximize Your Purchasing Power and Control Spend
Aired on Thursday, March 5, 2020

In order to maximize your purchasing power and control spend, you need clear visibility into your procurement process. Join Sara Jones, Senior Vice President Spend Management, and Jeff Hanrahan, Senior Director Product Marketing, to learn how to gain this visibility to ensure better control and increase your net operating income.
Get our must-have tips to help your team:
  * Improve processes
  * Control rogue spending
  * Reduce risk
  * Integrate into an expense management ecosystem

This informative webcast offers proven methods to streamline your purchasing, invoicing and vendor management processes to make the most of your spend performance.
### Sara Jones
SVP, GM Spend & Vendor Management, RealPage, Inc.
Sara is responsible for product management and operations for all RealPage Spend and Vendor Management solutions including OpsTechnology, Vendor Credentialing, Vendor Marketplace, eSupply GPO, Vendor Compliance Services and SmartSource Accounting Services. Sara has been with RealPage for over 13 years and has over 18 years of experience in the multifamily industry. She’s focused on leading product and operations teams to achieve company goals and delivering strategic metrics to maintain workflow success for clients.
### Jeff Hanrahan
Senior Director, Product Marketing, RealPage, Inc.
Jeff is Senior Director of Product Marketing for RealPage Expense Management, providing property management companies with solutions for procurement, utility management and sustainability.
