# Close Your Books in Record Time with Financial Close Management
Aired on Thursday, April 28, 2022
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/close-books-record-time/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/close-books-record-time/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/close-books-record-time/#hosts)

Join Dan Newbern, Vice President, RealPage Financial Suite, and RealPage Business Analyst, Victoria Sicula, for an introduction to the new and enhanced reporting and financial close management capabilities in RealPage Accounting. Learn why it’s now simpler than ever to set up financial close management, gain visibility in real-time close progress and steps that have been completed and create month- and year-end financial packages. You'll gain new insights into how Financial Close Management can streamline your business processes, give you better visibility into month- and year-end close and help you prepare work papers for your auditors.
During this webcast, you’ll also learn how to:
  * Configure tasks needed to accomplish each close
  * Track the close steps that have been completed
  * Update completed tasks
  * Configure the system to auto-create month-end work paper packages
  * Generate preliminary and final closings
  * Manage workflow process and internal controls
