# Operational Excellence Starts With the Right Software
Aired on Thursday, April 1, 2021
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/right-software-for-operational-excellence/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/right-software-for-operational-excellence/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/right-software-for-operational-excellence/#hosts)

Get insights into how you can optimize performance and increase property value across your portfolio—even in challenging market environments—with smart, simple property management software.
The RealPage® Property Management Suite has the trusted OneSite® solution at its core with a new, sleek and easy-to-use interface and all the functionality you need now and for growing your business in the future.
Join RealPage property management experts Rhonda Dutton and Amanda Traylor as they showcase how the new OneSite experience enhances the benefits of the Property Management Suite to help multifamily property operations achieve peak performance.
Discussion topics include:
  * How to win back valuable time in your daily operations with simplified and intuitive workflows and bulk functions
  * How to gain visibility and transparency across your portfolio while connecting your operations from end to end
  * How to realize operational excellence with business insights to help your teams improve operations at underperforming properties and celebrate properties with peak performance

### Rhonda Dutton
Director, OneSite Property Management, RealPage, Inc.
Rhonda has a background in multifamily property management, holding various property management positions from Leasing Consultant to Regional Trainer. Rhonda has been part of the RealPage family for 25 years and is currently with the OneSite team, ensuring that customers are getting the most value from their property management system.
### Amanda Traylor
Solutions Engineer, RealPage, Inc.
Amanda joined the RealPage SE team in 2012. She came to us with 12 years of experience in the multifamily property management industry where she gained hands-on experience with many of the RealPage solutions, including OneSite Leasing & Rents, Payments, and YieldStar. Amanda has a background in marketing with a focus on systems implementation and training for new construction sites.
