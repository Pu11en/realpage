After mostly stable performance over the past two to three years, apartment market conditions across much of the Northeast are beginning to soften. While this pullback has not been as severe as in many Sun Belt markets, the region’s largest metros are facing increasing pressure, largely driven by elevated supply levels following a later construction peak than in other parts of the country.
How is this wave of new supply shaping current market performance and near-term expectations? What economic forces are driving demand across the region? And what do performance trends in the second half of 2026 signal for the 2027 outlook?
This webcast will provide a comprehensive assessment of supply, demand, and performance trends across the Northeast's major apartment markets, with the following markets featured:
  * Albany-Schenectady-Troy, NY
  * Boston-Cambridge-Newton, MA-NH
  * Bridgeport-Stamford-Danbury, CT
  * Hartford-West Hartford-East Hartford, CT
  * Nassau County-Suffolk County, NY
  * New York-White Plains/Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie, NY
  * Newark-Jersey City/Hemlock Farms, NJ-PA
  * Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington, PA-NJ-DE-MD
  * Pittsburgh, PA
  * Providence-Warwick, RI-MA
