# 5 Operational Excellence Strategies for Accounting
Aired on Thursday, September 8, 2022
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/5-accounting-operational-excellence-strategies/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/5-accounting-operational-excellence-strategies/#overview)

Accuracy is paramount in accounting, but are there still ways to expedite and automate to streamline processes and optimize workflow? In this webcast we'll explore 5 strategies to help accelerate your journey to operational excellence. Join Dan Newbern, Vice President, RealPage® Financial Suite, and RealPage Business Analyst, Victoria Sicula, on September 8, 2022, for an in-depth discussion of operational excellence strategies for accounting in multifamily. You’ll gain new insights into streamlining accounting processes and gaining efficiencies in financial management.
During this webcast, you’ll learn how to:
  * Expedite financial close through budget variance
  * Schedule payment and check selection to optimize your AP team
  * Automate/outsource vendor payments
  * Build complete operational report packages
  * Expedite your bank reconciliation process
