In the past few years, multifamily markets across the Lone Star State have seen apartment demand struggle to keep pace with supply that crested to levels not seen since the 1980s. Now that supply has begun to taper off, the question becomes if – and when – demand can begin to rebalance.
For metros in the coveted Texas Triangle region, consistently strong migration alongside robust economic growth has supported demand tailwinds even prior to the 2020s cycle. But with the slowing of nationwide job growth and Texas-bound migration, industry professionals eagerly wait to see how the next 12 to 18 months will unfold.
Join RealPage® experts as they recap recent performance fundamentals in the Lone Star State and share their thoughts on the mid-to-long-term viability of Texas-based markets.
