As the new year moves into its second month, RealPage returns with its refreshed Regional Market Webcast Series, featuring newly refined subregions designed to reflect today’s market realities.
The South Central webcast delivers a comprehensive view of supply, demand, and performance across Texas, Arkansas, and Oklahoma—with a metro‑level perspective on what’s changing, what’s stabilizing, and what operators should prepare for as we head toward peak leasing season.
Join RealPage economists and market experts for a data‑driven look at how fundamentals are evolving, where demand is diverging from supply, and how performance metrics are trending across major markets in the region.
## **What You’ll Learn**
**Supply & Demand Fundamentals**
Supply is cooling across much of the South Central region—but demand headwinds persist in several major metros.
  * Will 2026’s supply cooldown be enough to counteract softening demand?
  * Which macroeconomic forces are shaping renter behavior, development pipelines, and absorption?

### **Performance Metrics & Market Signals**
Get clarity on current and emerging trends across the region:
  * Occupancy, rent growth, and renewal performance
  * Which metros are leading, lagging, or beginning to soften
  * Early indicators operators should be watching as leasing season ramps up
