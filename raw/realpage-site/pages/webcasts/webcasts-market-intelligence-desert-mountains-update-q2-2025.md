Markets across the southwest and up through the Rocky Mountains have seen supply and demand trends exhibit significant shifts in the past few years. Migration into the region has normalized from its post-2020 peak, although many markets continue to see some degree of inbound movement.
Meanwhile, supply has shifted from an environment of undersupply to one of oversupply, causing rents in select markets to adjust downward. But continued (and emerging) pressures on local construction pipelines suggest that the region will soon shift back into a more balanced supply/demand landscape.
Join RealPage® experts as they review trends and themes across the Desert/ Mountains region. This discussion will include a recap of recent performance for the region's key markets, as well as an overview of what's to come through 2026 and beyond.
