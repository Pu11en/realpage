Stubborn supply pressure, normalizing migration, and slowing economic growth have worked in conjunction across most Texas markets through 2025, In turn, Texas lags many national performance benchmarks heading into the final few months of the year. Still, a deeper dive into local market trends highlights some areas of potential strength heading into the coming months. Texas apartment demand remains solid despite a nationwide tapering of job growth. Supply has moved well past peak levels as well, indicating that even a modest slowdown in demand could be offset by a substantial pullback in forecasted deliveries through 2026.
Join RealPage market experts as they recap 3rd quarter 2025 performance metrics across key Texas markets and share their perspective on what lies ahead for 2026.
