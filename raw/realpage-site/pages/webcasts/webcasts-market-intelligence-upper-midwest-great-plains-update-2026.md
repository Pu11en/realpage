The 2020s have delivered plenty of ups and downs but across the Upper Midwest/Great Plains, both the peaks and valleys have been far more measured. While much of the nation is navigating a prolonged period of elevated supply pressure, relative supply levels across this region remain modest. A steady stream of demand has helped keep local performance buoyed at or slightly above national benchmarks.
What demand drivers are shaping recent market performance across the Upper Midwest/Great Plains? Has the region’s relative resilience altered investors’ long-term outlook? And how are these markets positioned to perform through the rest of 2026?
Join RealPage experts Carl Whitaker, VP & Chief Economist, and Adam Couch, Market Research Analyst, for an in-depth look at supply, demand, and performance trends across the western half of the Upper Midwest/Great Plains.
This webcast will explore key dynamics across Iowa, Illinois, Kansas, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, South Dakota, and Wisconsin.
## Key Topics Include:
  * Supply & Demand Fundamentals: How will a period of suppressed apartment starts affect short- to mid-term supply/demand balance? And is the region’s recent migration-driven population growth a temporary boost—or the beginning of a longer-term structural shift?
  * Performance Metrics: A deep dive into occupancy, rent growth, and renewal trends across major metros. Which markets are leading, which are lagging, and where are we seeing early signs of softening—or renewed strength?
