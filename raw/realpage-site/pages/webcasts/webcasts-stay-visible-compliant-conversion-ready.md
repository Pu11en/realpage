# Your Website Can Cost You Leases: Stay Visible, Compliant & Conversion-Ready
Aired on Thursday, September 25, 2025
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/stay-visible-compliant-conversion-ready/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/stay-visible-compliant-conversion-ready/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/stay-visible-compliant-conversion-ready/#hosts)

Multifamily marketers are being asked to do more with less — prove ROI, drive high-quality leads and stay compliant — all while digital marketing is evolving at breakneck speed. AI is reshaping renter search. Fee transparency is under legal scrutiny. Compliance and accessibility lawsuits are constant. And renters are expecting fast, mobile-first websites with detailed unit pricing and conversational content.
If your website isn’t built for AI search, accessible to all and engineered to convert, you’re not just behind — you’re invisible. That invisibility can cost you leases, budget and credibility.
Join RealPage® Industry Principal Lisa Haskins and special guest Thomas Lavallee, Corporate Counsel at RealPage, as they sit down with Courtney Bastian from Dayrise Residential to discuss what marketing and legal experts are seeing in the field and what multifamily websites must deliver in 2026.
In this webcast, you’ll learn:
  * How SEO + GEO keeps your properties visible in both traditional and AI search experiences
  * How G5 websites leverage mobile-first UI, speed optimization and conversion-driven features like interactive widgets and micro-animations to turn your website into a high-performing leasing engine
  * How Dayrise achieved an 88% lift in conversions and a 57% increase in signed leases
  * How upgraded fee transparency tools dynamically show costs based on renter selections, keeping you compliant and competitive
  * How built-in WCAG compliance and accessibility helps you reach more renters and reduce legal risk without relying on third-party overlays

Keep your website from being the weakest link. Learn how to stay visible, compliant and conversion-ready — without adding more to your plate.
