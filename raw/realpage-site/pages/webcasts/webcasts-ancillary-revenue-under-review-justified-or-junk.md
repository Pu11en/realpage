Income generated outside base rent has always been a feature of multifamily housing operations. However, with the increased scrutiny around supplemental apartment charges being labeled “junk fees,” it is more important than ever to ensure the services that carry extra cost to a renter are additional services they value.
In this conversation, we will explore renter sentiment and expectations around additional fees beyond rent and hear which strategies operational peers are using to manage the rising costs, complexities and demand for resident services.
Join us in the next Voices for 2023 webcast for an engaging and timely discussion led by RealPage® market expert Andrew Bowen that will cover:
  * Types of services renters indicate they value & are willing to pay for
  * How operators justify an additional fee that drives ancillary revenue
  * Real-world examples of win-win service solutions, including zero-cost-to-renter options
