# Overcoming Facilities and Maintenance Management Challenges Is as Easy as 1-2-3
Aired on Thursday, May 16, 2024
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/overcoming-facilities-maintenance-challenges/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/overcoming-facilities-maintenance-challenges/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/overcoming-facilities-maintenance-challenges/#hosts)

Facilities and maintenance management is a significant multifamily challenge. And it can be a drain on your staff and resources. Now is the time to change that.
In this webcast, you'll learn how new RealPage® functionality, integrations and automation are revolutionizing facilities and maintenance operations. RealPage continues to build integrations and AI into our Facilities solution. If you leverage spend, we’ll show you how it all plays together. You’ll also learn how our solutions can help you connect the dots between inspection and service requests to delight residents.
Discover how RealPage makes it easy as 1-2-3 to meet three current multifamily facilities and maintenance challenges:
#### 1: Complicated inspections that drain your resources.
  * Streamline inspections and service requests
  * Gain mobile flexibility to keep maintenance staff productive in the field
  * Detect and prevent maintenance issues before they grow into expensive problems
  * Stay in compliance with NSPIRE inspection and reporting mandates
  * Due diligence inspections

#### 2: Managing service requests in a timely fashion.
Imagine how quickly you could complete a service request if you could order parts from Facilities. Parts ordering gets a lot easier when it can be accomplished within the same solution as maintenance requests and work orders.
  * Initiate orders for parts
  * Assignment notifications
  * Asset management

#### 3: Measuring staff performance.
How do you track technician time on service requests? And how do you survey residents about their experience?
  * Resident inspection
  * Resident survey
  * Geolocating
  * Technician workload monitoring
  * Generate consolidated reporting
