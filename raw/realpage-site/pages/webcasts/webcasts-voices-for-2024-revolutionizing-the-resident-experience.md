In a digital-centric era, keeping up with renter preferences is a challenge. And with companies like Apple and Amazon setting the customer service bar high, the multifamily industry needs to get creative to improve the customer experience. One way to set your communities apart from the competition is to lean into the intersection of innovation and hospitality.
Join us for a timely and informative discussion on what apartment owners can learn from the hospitality industry. Hear from multifamily experts, RealPage® industry principals and other thought leaders on technology’s role in addressing changing renter preferences, including solutions that enhance convenience and efficiency for both residents and operators. You’ll also learn about successful hospitality-like initiatives that multifamily companies are implementing today.
### Lindsay Duffy
Associate Managing Director, Mark-Taylor, Inc.
Lindsay Duffy is the Associate Managing Director for Mark-Taylor, an Arizona-based developer, owner and investment manager of Class-A multifamily real estate. Lindsay brings 20 years of marketing, operations, sales and training experience in the multifamily arena.
Lindsay's vast experience has equipped her with the knowledge and expertise to assist teams in leasing up their communities, marketing various assets from value add to new builds as well as providing exceptional customer service to clients.
Lindsay's roles have included corporate branding, sales and property marketing, social media strategies, personal brand strategies, marketing analytics, leadership coaching, employee training and mentoring, and realigning marketing and performance strategies based on operating budgets and occupancy needs.
Lindsay is passionate about cultivating a positive work environment with an amazing culture that encourages growth and innovative ideas. Lindsay has a bachelor’s degree in business management and marketing and is a dedicated speaker at a variety of multifamily conferences, including Social Media Summit, AIM and NAA.

Industry Principal, Agentic AI Workforce, Realpage, Inc.
