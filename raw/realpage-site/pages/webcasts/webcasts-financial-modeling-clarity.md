Utilize flexible scenario modeling, comparisons, and forecasting capabilities to support real-time valuations. Learn how your industry peers enable clients to make informed decisions with the help of RealPage's integrated platform. Join RealPage® VP Chris Perry III, Valuations – Investment Management for the webcast Transformational Visibility: Clarity in Financial Modeling.
Discussion topics include:
  * Powerful valuations, modeling and forecasting to value the full investment lifecycle.
  * Real-time integrations into a wide variety of real estate solutions.
  * Secure data sharing for seamless collaboration with stakeholders.

### Christopher Perry
Vice President, FUEL Valuation and Forecasting
Christopher Perry III, Vice President of FUEL Valuation and Forecasting, has spent his career invested in disrupting the CRE industry. A +20-year CRE industry veteran and former software engineer, Christopher has a unique blend of experience in CRE finance and technology. With FUEL Valuation, now part of the RealPage family, Christopher leads a cloud-based valuation and forecasting platform for commercial and multifamily real estate. He has led continuous product improvements and forged data integration partnerships with CRE’s most trusted data sources to create the industry’s leading commercial property valuation and cashflow forecasting platform.
Christopher is a co-founder of FUEL and a previous CEO of the company, which was owned by Hipercept, prior to being acquired by RealPage in 2019.
