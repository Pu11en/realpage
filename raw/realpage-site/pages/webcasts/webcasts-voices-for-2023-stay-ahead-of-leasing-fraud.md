Knowing who you’re leasing to is critical to protecting not only cash flow but also residents. But it’s not always easy. Leasing fraud has skyrocketed in recent years and remains a threat in 2023 — with fraudsters aggressively innovating faster than many property managers can adapt.
As fraud takes many new forms, from fake or synthetic IDs and paystubs to payments fraud, yesterday’s strategies and technologies aren’t always keeping up with today’s threats.
How can multifamily property owners and managers stay ahead of rising fraud tactics to protect their communities and residents? And how can you minimize risk without slowing down the leasing process — a primary concern when we see heightened focus on expediting and simplifying the prospect-to-resident journey.
Join an all-star webcast with operational executives, moderated by RealPage® SVP and Chief Economist Jay Parsons, who will share insights on the increase in fraud threats, as well as tips for developing strategies and best practices to mitigate the risks. Panelists include GWR Management’s Ryan Raser, RPM’s Shanna Berrin and STYL Residential’s Amber Flores, as well as RealPage risk management experts Lisa Gedmin and Ed Tonarelli.
### Amber Flores
Screening Manager, STYL Residential
As Screening Manager for STYL Residential, Amber Flores uses her strong background in process development and improvement to oversee designing, maintaining and improving the company’s screening processes. Prior to joining STYL, Amber managed and developed the applications department at Home Partners of America (single-family lease with a right to purchase program) for more than five years. There, she managed the application screening for 18,000+ single family properties in 25 states.
### Ryan H. Raser
Director of Operations, GWR Management
Ryan has worked in the housing industry for more than 25 years. He started in university student housing and moved into multifamily in 1999, beginning his career in the industry as a leasing specialist and working through all onsite positions to a supervisory role with experience across A-, B- and C-class garden-style and mid-rise communities. As a regional supervisor, he oversaw property operations of large portfolios and focused on building effective teams to deliver desired results.
In his current role as Director of Operations, Ryan oversees the Operational Support Department at GWR Management and all business operating systems. He specializes in finding ways to streamline operations and enable the company with the tools to help deliver an excellent customer experience as well as improve cash flow.
Prior to GWR, Ryan worked with Arcadian Real Estate Services and Equity Residential in several different roles, which provides him with a broad skill set. Ryan enjoys collaborating on projects and serves as an advisor to several vendor boards. Originally from Northeast Pennsylvania, he is a proud graduate of East Stroudsburg University of Pennsylvania, is married and lives in Cypress, Texas.
### Shanna Berrin
Director of Risk Management, RPM
Shanna Berrin has spent the last two decades or more of her 30+ years of multifamily industry experience focused on the risk and insurance management platform. As Director of Risk Management, she is responsible for RPM’s master insurance program, risk and contract management, and compliance.
Previously, Shanna created and ran the risk and insurance department at one of the largest owner/operators based in Austin, Texas, managing a $2.5B portfolio placement. She is credited with implementing their renter’s insurance platform, along with OSHA program and training, and establishing and managing a highly successful captive program. Shanna studied at St. Edwards University and holds RIMS-CRMP and CIC designations. In her free time, Shanna enjoys spending time working and hunting with her German shorthaired pointer pups.
