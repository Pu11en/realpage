# Transformational Visibility: Investor Relationship Management
Aired on Thursday, January 28, 2021
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/investor-relationship-management/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/investor-relationship-management/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/investor-relationship-management/#hosts)

Now, more than ever before, sponsors must learn how to keep investors engaged and abreast of new market opportunities even without interacting face to face. By leveraging technology, firms can access the insights needed to identify new opportunities, creating the transparency that investors expect and prepping them for changes in the market.
Join RealPage® VP of Engineering & Product of Investment Management Andrew Jenkins and VP of Customer Success & Delivery of Investment Management Ron Rossi for the webcast, Transformational Visibility: Investor Relationship Management, as these experts talk with special guest Naseem Wenzel, CPA, Partner at CohnReznick.
Discussion topics include:
  * The evolution of investor expectations
  * The vital need for technology in today’s market
  * How top firms leverage technology to manage risk

### Andrew Jenkins
Vice President of Engineering & Product, Investment Management, RealPage
Andrew Jenkins serves as the VP of Product and Engineering for RealPage Investment Management. Andrew has over 20 years of product development and technology experience with a focus on PropTech and commercial real estate analysis solutions. He has a proven track record of creating and managing SaaS solutions as well as building businesses for growth or acquisition.
Andrew was the EVP of Product for a national commercial real estate research and listing platform as well as the owner/founder of a multifamily and commercial real estate research and listing platform in the Southeast.
### Ron Rossi
Vice President of Customer Success & Delivery, Investment Management, RealPage
Ron Rossi is the VP of customer success for the Investment Management and Commercial teams at RealPage. His team manages the entire customer journey as well as managing and collaborating with the Invest Management client advisory board and the power user board. Ron’s career spans 20 years in CRE and finance with a focus on technology the last 10 years. Ron takes great pride in being the voice of and advocating for all of our customers, he also participates in many FinTech conferences and webinars.
