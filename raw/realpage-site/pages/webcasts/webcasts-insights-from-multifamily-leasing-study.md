#### Insights from RealPage’s Multifamily Leasing Study
Artificial intelligence is rapidly reshaping multifamily operations, but what do leasing agents, the people using these tools every day, actually want from AI?
Join Krista Hurley, Industry Principal at RealPage, Katie Jobe, Product Manager of Leasing AI at RealPage, and Jeannine Zapata, Director of Learning and Resident Programming at LYND LIVING as they break down findings from RealPage’s latest research on AI in leasing — revealing what on-site teams trust AI to do, where they remain cautious and how these insights can guide your next steps. Dive into how Knock Omnichannel AI provides 24/7 support for leasing teams and delivers a better experience for prospective renters.
**In this webcast, you’ll learn:**
  * Which leasing tasks do teams feel confident handing to AI and which they won’t?
  * How comfortable are on-site staff with today’s AI tools?
  * What concerns are slowing down AI adoption and how to address them?
  * How AI can elevate every stage of the leasing journey, from the first touch to resident support.
  * Plus: **A** **live demo of Knock Omnichannel AI** , showing how smart automation is already transforming real workflows.

Whether AI-curious, cautious or fully committed, this is your roadmap to smarter, more human-centered leasing.
