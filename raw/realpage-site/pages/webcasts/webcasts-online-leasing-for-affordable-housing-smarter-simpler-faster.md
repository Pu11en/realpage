# Online Leasing for Affordable Housing: Smarter. Simpler. Faster.
Aired on Thursday, November 7, 2024
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/online-leasing-for-affordable-housing-smarter-simpler-faster/#form7)

Staying ahead of annual recertifications and applications are never-ending tasks. The good news is that nearly 80% of adults with household incomes below $30,000 a year own a smartphone1.This means there’s a real opportunity for an Affordable online leasing solution in which an application can be completed and signed from a mobile device—anytime, anywhere. Additionally, applicants are looking for the ability – when needed – to complete applications over multiple sessions and minimize travel, time off work and time spent waiting in line to apply, qualify and get approved!
Join RealPage’s Barry Weaver, Vice President of Affordable Product, and Ashwin Naik, Vice President of Leasing Product, as they talk with Steve Sterquell, Vice President of Property Management, and Travis Coley, Training and Development, at Cambridge Management, Inc. about preferred Affordable online application solutions that:
  * Decreases data entry and paper processes
  * Provides AI-enabled language translation
  * Shortens time-to-decision and curtail vacancy days
  * Reduces compliance exposure and mitigates applicant risk
  * Stay ahead of annual recertifications

Learn how you can create a better Affordable online leasing application and annual recertification process that meets applicants and future residents where and how they prefer.
