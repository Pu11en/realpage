# Streamline Leasing with Knock CRM’s Centralized Platform
Aired on Thursday, March 6, 2025

Knock CRM offers a modern solution to simplify leasing workflows that adapt to your current processes no matter how your leasing teams operate—whether they’re specialized, centralized, or property-specific. From single-property management to portfolio-wide task navigation, the Knock platform was designed with flexibility in mind.
Join industry experts Krista Hurley, Industry Principal and Bronte Beaird, Director of Product Management at Knock, as they explore the features of Knock CRM’s new centralized leasing platform. Through an engaging live demo, they’ll showcase how the platform enables seamless collaboration, enhances prospect management and delivers comprehensive oversight of leasing tasks.
Here’s what you’ll learn:
  * How centralized and non-centralized agents can effortlessly manage leasing tasks and processes across multiple properties.
  * To accelerate follow-ups and simplify messaging with bulk communication tools that cover more of the renter journey.
  * To eliminate workflow bottlenecks using collaborative features like centralized scheduling and shared calendars.
  * To leverage aggregated guest cards and centralized communication for personalized, high-touch interactions with prospects and residents.
  * To retain more qualified leads by accessing sister properties’ pricing, availability, leasing binders, and tour scheduling—all in one place.

This webcast is perfect for property management professionals looking to streamline their leasing operations and deliver exceptional experiences.
