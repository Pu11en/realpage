**Protecting Your Multifamily Business in an Unpredictable Environment**
Risk management is essential in today’s world of multifamily housing. Diminished rent growth and increased competition for residents raises the financial stakes of potential threats to revenue, expenses, retention and reputation.
Join us for our latest Innovation Day, a virtual event featuring industry and technology experts who will provide insights into the multifamily market, the latest best practices, and how you can leverage your RealPage® products to address new challenges and opportunities.
At the event, we will:
  * Reveal groundbreaking new research into multifamily renter fraud
  * Examine managing risks due to rental application fraud and property damage
  * Introduce you to new offerings that will help you combat ever more sophisticated fraudsters as well as insurance options that protect residents and property owners from financial loss

With ever-increasing threats to multifamily NOI, you cannot afford to miss this informative event. Learn what technology innovations you can enlist to reduce exposure to protect your business.
