# Market Intelligence: Identifying Risks and Potential Solutions
Aired on Wednesday, September 25, 2024
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/market-intelligence-identifying-risks-potential-solutions/#form7)

A focus on three risks facing the multifamily industry in 2024 — fraud, high supply, and resident retention. We’ll dive into the impact fraudulent applications, record levels of supply, and a challenging renewal environment are having on owners and operators.
The good news? A winning resident satisfaction and retention strategy can go a long way in mitigating all three risks.
Markets covered in this webcast:
  * Houston
  * Nashville
  * Chicago
