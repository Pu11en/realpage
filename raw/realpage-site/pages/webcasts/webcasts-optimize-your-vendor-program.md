Locating and vetting vendors has always been a time-consuming task — until now. Join VP of Spend Management Solutions Sara Jones and Product Marketing Director Lindsey Cade as they unveil RealPage Vendor Marketplace — our free online hub that gives you a single, convenient source for finding new, reputable vendors. With RealPage Vendor Marketplace, the vendors you need are always just a click away.
During this introduction, we’ll discuss:
  * Leveraging larger vendors for group purchasing opportunities
  * Searching, vetting and evaluating potential vendors
  * Types of vendors included in Vendor Marketplace
  * Current and planned scope of our vendor network
