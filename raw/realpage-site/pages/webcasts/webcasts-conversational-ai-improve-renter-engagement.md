Across the multifamily landscape, CRM-embedded Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming onsite communication with prospects and residents alike. Join us for this informative webcast as we explore how the powerful AI features of Knock® CRM utilize Conversational and Generative AI to enhance communication effectiveness. Hear directly from industry experts, partake in a live Q&A and experience our first-ever live AI product demonstration.
This webcast will also cover:
  * Communication preferences of today’s prospects and residents
  * Conversational and Generative AI and their significance in multifamily
  * How CRM-embedded AI helps deliver personalized, timely, strategic communication
  * How to measure AI communication impact and ROI
