The apartment market is reaching a critical inflection point. With shifting supply patterns, evolving investment strategies and operational challenges reshaping the landscape, property owners and investors need clear, data-driven insights to navigate uncertainty and identify opportunity.
Join RealPage® rental housing experts as they deep-dive into the most pressing questions facing the industry today. From regions and metros bucking national trends to operational strategies driving success, this interactive session will provide actionable intelligence to help you make informed decisions in the current complex environment.
Key discussion topics include:
  * Which multifamily markets are outperforming national trends
  * Urban vs. suburban performance dynamics
  * Lease-up velocity and concession strategies
  * Expense pressures that operators are facing now
  * Technology investments that help deliver ROI faster

### Adam Couch
Research Analyst, RealPage, Inc.
Adam Couch specializes in residential housing research and advanced analytics, with a focus on risk assessment, emerging trends, capital markets, and demographic shifts across the multifamily sector. He develops forward‑looking research into strategic recommendations that guide executive decision‑making across investment, portfolio strategy, and operations through market cycles and changing economic conditions. Adam has spoken at commercial real estate conferences, been cited in The Dallas Morning News, and previously advised Fannie Mae on multifamily asset management strategy.
### Dustin Weaver
Market Analyst, RealPage, Inc.
Dustin Weaver is a Market Analyst with RealPage's Data Analytics division, where he focuses on conventional apartment market analysis, research and data methodology. Dustin leverages his background in partnering with institutional investors and REITs to enable easier consumption and application of multifamily data and analysis.
