# Why SmartSource Accounting Is the Smart Choice Now
Aired on Wednesday, March 31, 2021

In today’s retooled workplace where remote employees are commonplace, many property management companies are looking for winning strategies to contain costs for non-revenue producing functions like accounting.
Learn how to leverage this blueprint to exceed your financial goals in an informative webcast hosted by Annette VanDuren, RealPage® VP of Managed Services & Commercial.
Joining Annette will be customers who will speak to the benefits of a partnership with the SmartSource™ Accounting team:
  * Nehal Patel, VP of Finance at Olympus Properties
  * MJ Trujillo, Founder and Managing Member of PRAXM Management

Discover the competitive advantage a highly skilled and tenured team of property management accountants can deliver for your organization now.
