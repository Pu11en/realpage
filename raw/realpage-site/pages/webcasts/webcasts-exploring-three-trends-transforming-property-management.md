Property management is evolving rapidly. The demands of modern-day residents, a stretched workforce and the escalating need for cutting-edge technology are all reshaping operations. Join experts Paul Rosenstein, GM/SVP of Property Management, and Adam Ortiz, VP of Product Management at RealPage, for an in-depth exploration of how innovation can empower your operations.
This exclusive, live webinar will provide actionable strategies to:
  * Empower Residents: Elevate their living experiences with centralized operations
  * Leverage Technology: Harness the unmatched efficiency of AI to drive operations and deliver seamless experiences
  * Automate Decision-Making: Elevate productivity through intelligent automation and cutting-edge tools

Why Should You Attend?
  * Gain insights into industry-leading tools and technology proven to streamline operations and enhance NOI
  * Gather expert advice from seasoned professionals who understand the challenges in property management
  * Discover how to future-proof your operations with scalable solutions designed for a rapidly evolving industry

### Paul Rosenstein
SVP, GM, Property Management, RealPage, Inc.
Paul has over 20 years of product leadership, strategy and general management experience. Most recently, he was Vice President Product with CoreLogic, North America’s leading integrated solution provider for real estate, mortgage and insurance providers. Before joining CoreLogic, he spent 14 years in leadership positions at Mitchell International, a provider of software and software-enabled services to clients within the property insurance and collision repair industries. Paul has experience in growing enterprise software solutions, scaling organizations and driving strategic transformation.
### Adam Ortiz
Director of Product Management, On-Site, RealPage, Inc.
With over 17 years of industry experience, Adam Ortiz has been on both the owner and vendor partnership side of multifamily property management. Adam's career started in student housing, working his way from Resident Assistant up to the General Manager of a 1000 bed, mixed used property. After spending time in conventional multifamily, supporting marketing, recruiting, and system administrations, he joined On-Site as an Account Manager. Taking his industry experience on the owner side, Adam was able to provide relatable ownership of client accounts and understand the SaaS side of the industry. Since RealPage's acquisition of On-Site, Adam has pivoted into several critical roles including as product leader for Online Leasing and Cross-Property Operations. He's currently the Vice President of Product Management, focusing on the OneSite platform.
