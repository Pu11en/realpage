# Drive Faster Demand From Advertising to Leasing: Client Panel
Aired on Thursday, April 11, 2024
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/drive-faster-demand-client-panel/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/drive-faster-demand-client-panel/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/drive-faster-demand-client-panel/#hosts)

Join our webcast for valuable insights into the capabilities of DemandX™. This solution leverages AI to optimize the entire demand funnel, connecting the marketing, leasing and pricing functions to enable companies to spend smarter, lease faster and price right.
Through firsthand experiences shared by RealPage® clients who successfully implemented DemandX, you will gain actionable takeaways and insights into how DemandX advertising and leasing transform multifamily demand operations.
Hosted by RealPage industry experts – and joined by client guests – this webcast will:
  * Explore real-life case studies and results from clients leveraging DemandX so you can spend smarter with monthly advertising recommendations using predicted exposure
  * Offer best practices and tips from experienced users on the benefits of pricing right and leasing faster by connecting revenue and CRM for prioritized leads
  * Address common challenges associated with adopting solutions that cross separate functional areas in multifamily and how to overcome them

### Kadi Mancuso Rice
Vice President of Sales and Marketing, JVM Realty Corporation
Kadi is the Vice President of Sales and Marketing, where she leads tactical marketing strategy, brand development, corporate communications, public relations and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR). Kadi's team is responsible for the martech stack that drives campaign development, lead generation, marketing operations, and resident retention to achieve optimum occupancy and financial performance. Kadi unites the creativity of visual storytelling and brand development with the ability to use data analytics to refine marketing strategies across the JVM portfolio.
Over the course of her career, Kadi has overseen 20,000+ multifamily units with a demonstrated track record leading corporate and site level brand development, go to market new construction, supporting stabilized assets and assisting with asset transactions. Kadi's broad multifamily experience has helped her and her respective companies’ notable successes, such as the RE Journals CRE Future Leaders recognition, multiple J Turner Research ORA Power Rankings and CAMME awards. She is a member of CHIEF executive women's network as well as multiple industry committees, including, but not limited to, Revyse CMO Roundtable and RealFoundations CX Council Advisory Board.
### Kortney Balas
Senior Vice President of Strategic Services, JVM Realty Corporation
As SVP of Strategic Services at JVM Realty, Kortney Balas oversees and manages the company’s system implementations, support services, company information databases, and strategic solutions. During her 23 + years in property management, Kortney has served in roles spanning from leasing and training to revenue and compliance in both affordable and conventional markets, allowing her to approach improving operational processes and products holistically.
Kortney is an active member of NMHC’s Data Privacy, Security, and Information Management and Cyber Security Committees, a past member of RealPage’s Executive Steering Committee, and has been a featured speaker at NAA Apartmentalize, NMHC OpTech, RealPage Real World, AIM, and more on the topics of implementation and vendor selection best practices, and network security in multifamily.
