# Alex Stanton

Vice President, Solution Engineers and Sales Enablement

Vice President, Solution Engineers and Sales Enablement, RealPage, Inc.

Alex Stanton leads the Solution Engineering team at RealPage. He has spent 25 years of his career working in the real estate solutions and services industry. Based in Richardson, Texas, Alex works with clients and prospects to establish strategic direction-leveraging technology solutions. Most recently, Alex has been focused on the application of data strategy and the use of artificial intelligence to further enhance clients’ NOI. He holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science.
