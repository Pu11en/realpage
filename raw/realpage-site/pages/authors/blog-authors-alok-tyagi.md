# Author Posts: Alok Tyagi | RealPage Blog

RealPage

Chief Technology Officer, RealPage Inc.

Alok Tyagi brings more than 30 years of hands-on Enterprise Product leadership experience in product/technology strategy, product management, design, development, and deployment of market leading global enterprise software products.

Alok frequently engages with customers of all size from Fortune 100 to SMB, often establishing a digital business agenda. Alok has introduced several new enterprise SaaS/Cloud products as well as re-innovated existing product portfolios to spur high-growth and improve the customer experience.

Alok is currently Chief Technology Officer at RealPage overseeing our Product Development organization. He joined RealPage from GTreasury - a high growth startup in the FinTech space - where he was the Chief Product and Technology Officer.Previously, Alok has filled executive product & technology leadership positions of industry-leading companies such as Oracle, Peoplesoft, JD Edwards, Sage, and CA Technologies. In those roles, he has led large product businesses and organization.

Tyagi is driven by a founder mindset and a strong sense of purpose, which lead to an increase in shareholder value and stronger technology business. A champion of Digital Business Transformation in every industry – Alok regularly invests and advises various start-ups companies and mentor entrepreneurs on how to build Scale-Ups.

Alok Tyagi has an MBA from the University of Colorado, and he is a Computer Engineering graduate from Regional Engineering College in India. You can connect with him on Twitter @aloktyagi or LinkedIn.
