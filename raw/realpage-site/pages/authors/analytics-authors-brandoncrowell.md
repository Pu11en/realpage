# Brandon Crowell: Author Profile | RealPage

Realpage Analytics Blog Authors

Senior Market Analyst

Brandon Crowell is a Senior Market Analyst for RealPage, Inc., focused on providing actionable real estate and economic insight for today’s data-driven economy. Prior to joining Realpage in 2012, Brandon worked for L&B Realty Advisors, a privately owned real estate investment manager in Dallas. While at L&B, he worked in asset management, specializing in the repositioning of value-add opportunities in primary and secondary apartment markets. Brandon is an active member of NCREIF, the Pension Real Estate Association and The Real Estate Council of Dallas/Fort Worth. He received his bachelor’s degree in real estate analysis and MBA in corporate finance from the University of North Texas.
