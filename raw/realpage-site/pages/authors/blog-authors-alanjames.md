# Author Posts: Alan James | RealPage Blog

RealPage Blog Authors

SVP, Commercial & Investment Mgmt, RealPage, Inc.

Alan James is a Senior Vice President with RealPage. Over the last three decades he has worked with many of the top fund and institutional managers in providing technology solutions to help achieve goals and objectives directed toward increasing shareholder value. Previously, Alan served in different consultative and sales capacities at Yardi Systems, Oracle Corporation, PeopleSoft and J.D. Edwards focusing on solutions for the large real estate owners, managers and REITS. Prior to beginning his career in real estate technology, he was the Vice President of Finance for a large Real Estate developer that worked very closely with Institutional owners in Joint Venture developments of commercial and residential real estate. Alan holds a BS degree from Portland State University in Accounting and Finance Law.
