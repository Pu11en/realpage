# Shamik Mukherjee: Company Profile

Shamik Mukherjee serves as Chief Marketing Officer at Realpage, where he leads the development and execution of marketing strategies that align the company’s technology vision with innovative product narratives. His focus is on driving engagement, empowering sales teams, and delivering strong business outcomes.
With over 25 years of experience in technology leadership, Shamik has spearheaded business transformations across marketing, strategy, research and development, and customer service. Before Realpage, he held multiple leadership positions at Motorola Solutions, including Chief Marketing Officer, overseeing global marketing efforts across branding, product strategy, verticals, channels, and digital analytics.
Earlier in his career, Shamik was a strategy consultant at Deloitte, advising clients in the technology, media, and telecommunications sectors. He holds a master’s and bachelor’s degree in engineering from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.
