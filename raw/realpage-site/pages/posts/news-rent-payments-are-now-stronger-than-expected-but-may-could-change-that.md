# Rent Payments Are Now Stronger Than Expected. But May Could Change That
April 28, 2020
**Forbes**(April 28, 2020) -- By April 19, 89% of American rental households made a full or partial payment toward their monthly rent. Analyzed through the [National Multifamily Housing Council’s rent payment tracker](https://www.nmhc.org/research-insight/nmhc-rent-payment-tracker/ "https://www.nmhc.org/research-insight/nmhc-rent-payment-tracker/") of 11.5 million professionally managed units, that figure accounts for a payment rate of 95% compared to the same time last month, when 93% of tenants had paid rent. It also lags only slightly behind rent collection for the same period a year ago.
