# Raleigh is a top market for investing in rental property. Why that isn’t a surprise.
January 29, 2020
**The News and Observer** (January 29, 2020) — Rising home prices and an increase in renters have placed the Raleigh metropolitan area at number six on a list of the top 10 rental investment markets in 2020, according to a study by Millionacres, a real estate investment company that is part of the financial advice company The Motley Fool.
