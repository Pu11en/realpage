# $500M in new apartments designed to help Seattle’s Middle Class
June 10, 2017
**THE SEATTLE TIMES** (June 10, 2017) – Two Seattle firms are putting $500 million into building “workforce apartments” rather than the luxury units now sprouting all over. Spectrum Development and Laird Norton Properties will focus on areas like First Hill and Pioneer Square where rents are becoming out of reach.
