# Nine CRE Experts Weigh in on the Impact of the Fed’s Likely Interest Rate Increase
September 14, 2018
**NATIONAL REAL ESTATE INVESTOR** (SEPTEMBER 14, 2018) – For investors in the apartment sector, which has increasingly become challenged as deals have been tougher to find at this stage in the cycle, the rate hike will make deal-finding even harder, says Greg Willett, chief economist at RealPage Inc. “It cuts off the number of doable deals a little bit more and makes it a little bit more challenging environment to get deals across the finish line,” Willett says.
