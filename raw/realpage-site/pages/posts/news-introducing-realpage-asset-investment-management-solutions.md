# Introducing RealPage Asset & Investment Management Solutions
March 10, 2017
**REDIRECT CONSULTING BLOG (March 10, 2017)** – We’re honored to announce REdirect as one of six distinguished teams of experts selected by RealPage to become certified Asset & Investment Management (AIM) software solutions consultants. In January, REdirect received extensive product training at RealPage’s Dallas headquarters to become authorized to implement and provide consulting services for RealPage’s new AIM solutions, including two innovative components: Investment Accounting and Portfolio Asset Management.
For more info, visit: http://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/introducing-realpage-asset-investment-management-solutions
