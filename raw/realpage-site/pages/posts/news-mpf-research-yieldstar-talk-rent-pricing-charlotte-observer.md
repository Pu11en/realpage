# MPF Research and YieldStar Talk Rent Pricing with Charlotte Observer
April 28, 2014
**(April 28, 2014)** — _The Charlotte Observer_ posted an article, “Why You Pay More Rent Than Your Neighbor.” This article discusses reasons why rents vary from person to person and property to property. To help provide some validation on their reasoning, they turned to [MPF Research](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/) expert Jay Parsons and [YieldStar](https://www.realpage.com/insights-analytics/) senior vice president Keith Dunkin for their insights. Read the full article for more information on how renters, property managers and investors look at rents.
