# RealPage Group VP to Speak at Crittenden Multifamily
March 1, 2015
### _MPF Research’s Parsons to also speak March 9-11_
**(March 1, 2015) —**[Crittenden Multifamily Conference](https://www.crittendenmultifamily.com/), held in Fort Worth, Texas, will have two representatives from RealPage and MPF Research serve on panels.
Group Vice President of Marketing Solutions Jamie Clymer will sit on the panel “Data Held Hostage: The Truth Behind Unit Availability” on March 10. He will share insights on this topic, along with other panelists that include:
  * Moderator: Christopher Erickson – Apartment List
  * Jennifer Staciokas – Pinnacle
  * Israel Carunungan – Greystar
  * Brian Zrimsek – MRI Software LLC

[MPF Research’s](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/) Director of Research & Analysis Jay Parsons will moderate the panel “Urban vs. Suburban: Is the Urban Hype Supported by Data and Experience?” alongside other panelists, including Al Palamara – UBS Realty Investors LLC
