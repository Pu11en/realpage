# RealPage Honored with 2014 MHN Tech Choice Awards
September 8, 2014
### _Company earns six awards in six different categories_
**(September 8, 2014) —** Multi-Housing News recently announced its [2014 Tech Choice Award winners](http://www.multihousingnews.com/awards/mhn-2014-tech-choice-awards-winners/1004107285.html). These awards are given to “companies who provide flexible customization, competitive pricing, and the most innovative solutions, all backed by superior customer support. The industry users have voted these firms as those that stand out among the rest.”
RealPage was honored six times in six different categories. This is the fourth year in a row RealPage’s products and services have been recognized by these awards.
  * Property Management Software: [OneSite® Leasing & Rents by RealPage](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/)
  * Lead Management: [Lead2Lease by RealPage Inc.](https://www.realpage.com/solutions/front-office/)
  * Resident Portals: [RealPage Portals](https://www.realpage.com/resident-portal/)
  * Marketing Portals/Websites: RealPage Portals
  * Mobile App: [MyNewPlace.com iPhone app](http://www.mynewplace.com/)
  * Revenue Management: [RealPage YieldStar™](https://www.realpage.com/insights-analytics/)
