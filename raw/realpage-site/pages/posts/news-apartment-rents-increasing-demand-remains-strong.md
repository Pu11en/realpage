# Apartment rents increasing as demand remains strong
July 7, 2017
**SUN SENTINEL** (July 7, 2017) –South Florida apartment dwellers are still feeling the pinch when it comes time to write that monthly rent check.
Average apartment rents in Broward and Palm Beach counties have been rising steadily in recent years, and the latest figures show landlords still have the upper hand.
