# RealPage to acquire Dallas-based apartment research firm for $75M
January 24, 2017
**DALLAS BUSINESS JOURNAL** (January 24, 2017) – Software provider RealPage Inc. (Nasdaq: RP) has agreed to acquire Axiometrics, a Dallas-based apartment research firm, for $75 million and combine it with its MPF Research division, which will help deepen its research capabilities.
