# RealPage Economy Express Episode 1
![Photo of Arben Skivjani, Deputy Chief Economist and Director of Forecasting](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/blog/authors/images/2022/09/arben-skivjani.jpg)
_by_ [ Arben Skivjani](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/authors/arbenskivjani/)
RealPage Deputy Chief Economist and Director of Forecasting [Arben Skivjani](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/authors/arbenskivjani/) makes the vast and complicated world of the U.S. economy more accessible and understandable in our RealPage Economy Express series. In these short, bi-weekly videos, RealPage breaks down the most important economic indicators to cover everything from GDP and employment to the Consumer Price Index and consumer sentiment.
Catch these bite-sized videos to better understand how various elements of the economy impact the multifamily world and our RealPage forecast – and all in episodes under five minutes. Whether you’re a real estate investor, developer, analyst or simply interested in the multifamily industry, RealPage Economy Express is the video series for you.
#### Episode 1: What do current economic reports say?
  * Only 4.4 million existing U.S. homes sold in March, as [mortgage rates](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/mortgage-interest-rates-hit-two-month-low/) continue to increase.
• Consumers don’t have much confidence in the U.S. economy right now.
• Real [GDP](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/slow-growth-us-gdp/) grew by 1.1% in 1st quarter.
• A mild recession could be coming later in the year.
