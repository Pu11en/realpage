# Apartment Survey Finds August Rent Payments Held Up Fairly Well
August 14, 2020
**GlobeSt** (August 14, 2020) -- Apartment rental payments are holding up fairly well—at least for now.
The National Multifamily Housing Council reported that 79.3 percent of surveyed households made a full or partial rent payment by August 6. That’s slightly better than last month’s 77.4 percent, and just shy of the August 2019 level of 81.2, according to [NMHC’s Rent Payment Tracker](https://www.nmhc.org/research-insight/nmhc-rent-payment-tracker/).
“Over the past few months apartment residents have largely been able to meet their housing obligations,” NMHC Chair David Schwartz said in a written statement.
