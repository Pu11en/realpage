# Charlotte among top U.S. metros for new apartments in Q3; Here’s where they’re going
November 8, 2017
**CHARLOTTE BUSINESS JOURNAL** (November 8, 2017) – Charlotte’s apartment boom isn’t showing signs of a slowdown.
As metro Charlotte’s population continues to grow — up 9.7% from five years ago to 2.47 million, according to the latest U.S. Census Bureau figures — multifamily developers remain vigilant in keeping up supply across the Queen City to meet demand. And a recent report from real estate analytics firm RealPage Inc. (NASDAQ: RP) shows the metro area posting the nation’s third-highest growth in apartment inventory during this year’s third quarter. The report covers the 50 largest U.S. metros.
