# A surprising 87% of D-FW apartment residents made their May rent payments
May 8, 2020
_The share of renters around the country who made full or partial payments as of May 6 was even better than a month ago._
**Dallas Morning News**(May 8, 2020) -- More than 80% of America’s apartment renters have made their May payments. And in the Dallas-Fort Worth area, about 87% of apartment residents so far have paid their rent for the month.
That’s better than some forecasters had expected in a month with record unemployment and pay cuts caused by the pandemic.
