# RealPage to Speak at RealShare Student Housing
August 25, 2015
### _Vice President Amye Baker to serve as panelist for operations session._
**(August 25, 2015) —** RealPage’s Vice President of [Velocity](https://www.realpage.com/utility-management/expense-management/) Amye Baker will speak at the RealShare Student Housing event held September 1-2, 2015 in Dallas, Texas.
On September 2, she will serve as a panelist for the “Top Tips from Operations as Tech Takes Over” alongside Alexis Krisay, Owner & President of Marketing, Serendipit Consulting; Marc Treitler, General Counsel & Vice-President – Regulatory Affairs, Conservice Utility Management and Billing; and Danny Soule, Property Management Specialist, PayLease.
The session discusses how [property management](https://www.realpage.com/student/) is ever changing as technology becomes a top strategy for operators. New tech strategies are helping property managers keep up with maintenance, payment and organization. Students are now demanding everything from digital rent payments and key-less room entry, to tech-equipped study areas and even digital fitness. How are these new student demands being managed? Find out what works, what’s changing and what to avoid.
RealShare Student Housing is in its eighth year. Catch the sector’s top voices when they cover the latest trends in financing, investment, brokerage, development and property management. Hear what to expect for 2015 and beyond.
