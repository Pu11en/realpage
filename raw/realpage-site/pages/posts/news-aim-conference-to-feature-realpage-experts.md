# AIM Conference to Feature RealPage Experts
April 26, 2016
### _Will share insights on sentiment, focus groups and virtual reality._
**(April 26, 2016) —** Three of RealPage’s top experts will speak at this year’s Apartment Internet Marketing Conference held May 1-4 in Huntington Beach, Calif.
Chief Economist Greg Willett will moderate the session, “Focus Group in Action” which shares insights and learnings on how to conduct effective focus groups.
Head of Data Science Rich Hughes will lend his expertise to the session, “Sentiment Analysis Surveys, Social, and Suggestion Boxes” sharing how to move beyond having a process for responding and put the data to work in driving revenue and resident satisfaction.
Finally, Vice President of Content Services John Gorman moderates the panel session, “Virtual Reality and the Future of Presenting 3D”, which will demo virtual reality software and how to use it for the leasing office and online.
To learn more about the conference, visit: [www.aimconf.com](http://www.aimconf.com).
