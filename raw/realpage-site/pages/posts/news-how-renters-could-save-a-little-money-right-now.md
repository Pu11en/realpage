# Right on the Money: Here's how renters could save a little money right now
March 31, 2020
_With a drop in potential new renters, there are a few ways you could save. Current renters could try renegotiating by shopping around for competitive prices._
**WFAA**(March 31, 2020) -- There is a new change.org petition calling for a rent freeze in Dallas because of closures and financial uncertainty caused by the COVID-19 outbreak.
Apartment landlords are facing a lot of uncertainty too.
Richardson-based RealPage, which conducts research in this area, said in a recent article there's been a 15% drop in apartment website visits by potential new renters. And they predict interest in apartments will drop even further with so many people under stay at home orders.
