# MPF Research’s Parsons Talks Urban Dwelling
August 20, 2014
### _Director shares insights with the Dallas Business Journal_
**
(August 20, 2014) —** Jay Parsons, MPF Research’s director of analytics & forecasts, served as a thought leader in the Dallas Business Journal’s cover story titled, “Is the apartment DFW’s new first home?” This article discusses the trend of millennials in North Texas dwelling in apartment living longer due to the amenities offered, prime location, and more. Mr. Parsons provides his insights into the trends he is seeing with millenials and why we can expect apartment living to only increase in the upcoming years.
For more of the story, head here: <http://www.bizjournals.com/dallas/print-edition/2014/08/08/cover-story-is-the-apartment-dfw-s-new-first-home.html>.
