# Velocity Announces Online Access to All-in-One Resident Bills
August 26, 2008
**_Residents and property manager benefit from Velocity Convergent Billing_**
**(August 26, 2008) —** [Velocity](https://www.realpage.com/client-login/utilities/), a leading provider of resident utility billing and invoice processing services for the multifamily industry, today announced that its popular revenue-enhancing convergent bill is now available for online review and payment by apartment residents.
With Velocity Convergent Billing Services, residents can now go to their online portal to review and pay their rent and other charges, including utilities (electricity, gas, water and sewer), service fees, amenity fees, and [vacant unit cost recovery](https://www.realpage.com/utility-management/expense-management/), on a single online bill. This type of self-service approach also allows residents to see their utility consumption, enabling them to make lifestyle adjustments and better control their expenses.
From the property management perspective, the integration of convergent billing and online access gets all charges in front of residents in a timely, unified manner and improves total collections, while the ability for residents to pay online improves collection times.
“It’s about improving the resident experience and property operations at the same time,” stated Ashley Glover, president, Velocity. “Velocity Convergent Billing is an amenity for residents — proven to increase resident satisfaction. Additionally, our studies have shown that properties lift overall resident collections an average of three to five percent by providing a convergent bill that includes resident ledger and utility charges.”
The online presentment of the Velocity convergent bill is powered by CrossFire, another solution from RealPage. CrossFire serves more than 4,000 communities with integrated systems to manage marketing and leasing over the phone, online and in the office. CrossFire offers a powerful resident portal that provides online rent payments, online service requests, online renewals and now integrated convergent bill presentment.
“Velocity’s Convergent Billing, combined with the Online Living system from CrossFire, presents property operation teams a state-of-the-art platform to facilitate resident self-service, increase resident satisfaction and improve NOI,” declared Michael Fortinberry, president of CrossFire.
Communities can also use CrossFire Online Living to communicate resident notices and community events. With Velocity Convergent Billing, the online experience has just gotten richer for residents and property managers.
