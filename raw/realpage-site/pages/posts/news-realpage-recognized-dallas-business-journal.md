# RealPage Recognized by the Dallas Business Journal
July 8, 2014
### Named as a fastest growing company in North Texas
**(July 3, 2014) —** The [Dallas Business Journal ](http://www.bizjournals.com/dallas/)released it’s yearly report on the fastest growing companies in North Texas and the largest North Texas public companies. RealPage was named to both of these lists. For the “Fastest Growing Companies in North Texas”, RealPage was listed at No. 25 and came in at No. 79 for the “Largest North Texas Public Companies.”
For the complete list of the fastest growing companies, click here: <http://www.bizjournals.com/dallas/subscriber-only/2014/06/27/fastest-growing-public-companies.html>.
For the complete list of the largest North Texas public companies, click here: [http://www.bizjournals.com/dallas/subscriber-only/2014/06/27/public-companies.html ](http://www.bizjournals.com/dallas/subscriber-only/2014/06/27/public-companies.html).
