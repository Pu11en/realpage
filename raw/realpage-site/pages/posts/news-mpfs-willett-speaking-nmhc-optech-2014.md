# MPF’s Willett Speaking at NMHC OpTech 2014
November 5, 2014
### _Panel to discuss industry trends in the apartment market_
**(November 6, 2014) —** MPF Research’s vice president Greg Willett will serve as a speaker at this year’s [National Multifamily Housing Council’s OpTech Conference](http://www.nmhc.org/optech/) held November 17-19 in Orlando, Fla.
Mr. Willett will serve as a panel expert in the “Industry Trends – Operator Opportunities, Challenges and Priorities: A Discussion with Apartment Leaders” session held on November 18 from 3:15-4:15 p.m.
For more information about the conference, visit: <http://www.nmhc.org/optech/>.
