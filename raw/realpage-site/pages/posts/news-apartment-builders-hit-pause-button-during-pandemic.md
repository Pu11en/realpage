# Apartment builders hit pause button during the pandemic
June 15, 2020
_Dallas-area multifamily building permits are down by almost 9% as of_ April.
**Dallas Morning News** (June 15, 2020) -- Apartment builders hit the brakes as the COVID-19 pandemic hit.
Nationwide multifamily building permits were at a four-year low in April, according to a new report by RealPage.
And construction starts across the country plunged 40% from April 2019 totals.
“Multifamily permitting dipped to 373,000 units on a seasonally adjusted annual basis in April, the lowest annual rate since April 2016,” RealPage’s Chuck Ehmann said in the latest report. “That number was down 12.4% from March and was 22.6% lower than the rate from the same period in 2019.”
