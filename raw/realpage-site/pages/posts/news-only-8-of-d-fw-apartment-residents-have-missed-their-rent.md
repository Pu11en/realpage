# Only 8% of D-FW apartment renters have missed April payments
April 22, 2020
_Analysts expect the number to go way up in May._
**Dallas Morning News** (April 22, 2020) -- Only 11% of renters nationwide have skipped their April payments, but apartment industry analysts expect May’s rent delinquency rate to be much higher.
As of April 19, 89% of apartment renters around the country had made a full or partial payment, according to the National Multifamily Housing Council.
The rent payment rate is even higher in Dallas-Fort Worth — 92%, according to analysts at RealPage.
