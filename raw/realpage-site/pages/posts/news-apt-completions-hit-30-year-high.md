# Apt. Completions Hit 30-Year High
January 30, 2018
**GLOBEST.COM** (January 30, 2018) – Even as the construction pipeline has emptied out to a degree, new apartment supply reached a 30-year high in 2017. RealPage data show 395,777 completed units in the 150 largest US metro areas, a 46% increase over 2016 completions. However, on the whole demand continues to keep pace with the new supply.
