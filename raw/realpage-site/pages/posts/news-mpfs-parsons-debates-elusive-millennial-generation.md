# MPF’s Parsons Debates The Elusive Millennial Generation
October 22, 2014
### Parsons RealShare Apartments panel is recapped by Globe Street
**(October 22, 2014) —** Globe Street recently recapped the RealShare Apartments conference panel, “Next-Generation Renter” held on October 15. In this article, [“Be Cognoscente of Your Property’s Reputation”](http://www.globest.com/news/12_971/national/multifamily/Be-Cognoscente-of-Your-Propertys-Reputation-351682.html), discussions around the millennial generation was as varied as the panelists that included MPF’s Jay Parsons, Bel Air Internet COO Joel McIntyre, PayLease vice president of management and marketing Ben Truehart and Yardi’s executive sponsor.
Parsons remarked about this generation that “we learn how these consumers will affect the economy—and real estate—in material ways. We see all these contradictions about this generation; it is diverse group that is very complicated.”
Read more about millennials and what these panelists had to share here: <http://www.globest.com/news/12_971/national/multifamily/Be-Cognoscente-of-Your-Propertys-Reputation-351682.html>.
