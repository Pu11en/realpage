# How much will local apartment rents rise in 2017? Here’s what one firm says
December 16, 2016
**SACRAMENTO BUSINESS JOURNAL (December 16, 2016)** – Rental growth for Sacramento region apartments is likely to slow to 5.6 percent in 2017, according to one tracking firm, but that may still be two percentage points faster than the national average.
