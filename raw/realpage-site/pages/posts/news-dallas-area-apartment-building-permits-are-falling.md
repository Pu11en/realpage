# Dallas-area apartment building permits are falling
July 14, 2020
**Dallas Morning News**(July 14, 2020) -- With the pandemic, permits for new apartment projects are down in six of the 10 top U.S. markets – including Dallas.
Multifamily building permits in the Dallas area fell 18.5% for the 12-month period ending in May, according to the latest data from RealPage.
Dallas’ percentage decline was the largest of any of the 10 largest metro areas that RealPage looked at for its apartment construction snapshot.
