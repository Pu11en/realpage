# What, Us Worry? Multifamily Developers Sanguine About Supply.
June 29, 2020
**Bisnow** (June 29, 2020) -- Booming multifamily housing production, a pandemic and increasingly difficult restrictions on tours and amenities don't have the sector's biggest developers worried, they say. In fact, what may have been viewed as oversupply before the recession in some of the country's rapidly growing markets like Dallas and the East Bay Area is now being seen as a boon.
