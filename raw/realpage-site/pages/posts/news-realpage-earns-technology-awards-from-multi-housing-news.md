# RealPage Earns Technology Awards from Multi-Housing News
August 19, 2008
**(August 19, 2008) —** RealPage, Inc., a leading provider of on-demand software and services to the multifamily industry, today announced it received seven 2008 Technology Choice Awards from Multi-Housing News for products in its property management portfolio.
The Technology Choice awards are held annually by Multi-Housing News magazine, with the results based on votes from its readers: multifamily industry executives and professionals. Multi-Housing News currently has a readership of more than 85,000 per month.
“This vote of approval from our customers is a true honor; especially earning these significant awards for consecutive years,” stated Steve Winn, president and chief executive officer of RealPage. “We will continue to improve the technology and enhance the services in the RealPage portfolio to meet our customers’ needs for property management efficiency, accuracy and profitability.”
These RealPage products have received a 2008 Technology Choice Award:
  * OneSite — Property Management Systems category
  * OneSite Payments — Resident Payment Systems category
  * CrossFire — Lead Management, Resident Portals and Marketing Websites categories
  * LeasingDesk Screening — Resident Screening Systems category
  * Velocity — Utility Management category

While each RealPage solution provides valuable services on its own, most RealPage customers find extra value in using a combination of these components to reduce up-front integration efforts, simplify operations with one user-friendly interface, reduce time-consuming manual processes and improve profitability with reduced operational expenses and capture of additional revenue. At the same time, RealPage is fully committed to being Open for Business, maintaining each product line as a stand-alone solution that is open to standards-based integration with other software venders.
