# LeaseStar President Talks Lead Management with Multi-Housing News
July 8, 2014
### _Shares how to spend your marketing dollars effectively_
**(July 7, 2014) —** [LeaseStar](https://www.realpage.com/solutions/front-office/) President Eric Broughton was interviewed by [Multi-Housing News](http://www.multihousingnews.com/) for its July issue regarding how to be the most effective with your lead management. In this article, “Spend Smarter: Obtain a range of market intelligence with powerful lead management technology”, Broughton discusses how to adequately track your leads, spend marketing dollars and use social to reach prospect.
