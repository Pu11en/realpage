# MPF Research’s Parsons Selected to Speak at ULI Multi-Family Council
September 29, 2014
### _Director of analytics and forecasts to serve as expert on current economic conditions_
**(October 7, 2014) —** MPF Research’s Director of Analytics & Forecasts Jay Parsons will serve as a speaker at the Urban Land Institute’s (ULI) Fall Meeting held October 21-23 in New York City.
During this session, Mr. Parsons and three other panelists will discuss current economic conditions and the state of the multifamily sector. Other panelists include Ron Witten, President of Witten Advisors, Rim Cornwell, Principal at The Concord Group and Steve Eggert of St. Anton Partners.
For more information about the fall meeting, visit: <http://fall.uli.org/>.
