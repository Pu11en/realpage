# Houston’s high-rise apartment rents fell the most nationally in 2016
December 22, 2016
**HOUSTON BUSINESS JOURNAL (December 22, 2016)** – It’s no secret that Houston apartment rents are falling amid the oil slump.
However, high-rise apartments are feeling the brunt of the rent slide as developers deliver several new apartment towers across the Bayou City, according to a new national report.
