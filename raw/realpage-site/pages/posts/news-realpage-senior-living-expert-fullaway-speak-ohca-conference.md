# RealPage Senior Living Expert Fullaway to Speak at OHCA Conference
September 9, 2014
### _Vice president of business development to discuss staffing in presentation_
**(September 11, 2014) —** Doug Fullaway, vice president of business development for [RealPage Senior Living](https://www.realpage.com/senior/), will be speaking at the Oregon Health Care Association Conference held September 22-23 in Portland, Ore.
Mr. Fullaway’s presentation, “Staffing to Acuity at Assisted Living Communities” will share with attendees ways of determining staff levels based on resident needs, pros and cons of methods used to determine staffing, and who to build an acuity-based staffing plan.
To find out more about the conference, visit: <http://convention.ohcaonline.com/Web/ConventionRegistration/ConventionHome.asp>.
