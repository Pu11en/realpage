# MPF Talks Apartment Markets with Multifamily Executive
October 21, 2014
### _Market intelligence group shares insights on new construction for 2015_
**(October 14, 2014) —** Multifamily Executive Magazine recently turned to apartment market experts MPF Research to provide their insights into where new construction will take place in 2015. In the article “[Top 10 New Construction Metros of 2015](http://www.multifamilyexecutive.com/business-finance/top-10-new-construction-metros-in-2015_o)“, the top metros include Houston in first with 19,662 expected completions in 2015, followed by Dallas and New York City.
