# CrossFire® Launches Portals for Mobile Devices
September 7, 2010
### _Renters and residents can now access information, transact business while on the go_
**(September 7, 2010)** – [CrossFire](https://www.realpage.com/contact-center/), a division of RealPage, Inc. ([www.realpage.com](https://www.realpage.com/)), today announced the launch of the [mobile device portals](https://www.realpage.com/contact-center/) for both prospects and residents, following a period of successful testing at several Camden Property Trust sites.
The Mobile Prospect Portal allows prospects who are out searching for an apartment to locate nearby communities via GPS and view full property details on their handheld devices; the Mobile Resident Portal gives residents the same mobility for paying rent, submitting service requests and staying informed about community issues.
“It’s not enough to have just a website anymore,” said Michael Munoz, Vice President of CrossFire Portals. “Your prospects and residents are becoming increasingly dependent on their mobile devices; they expect to be able to find the information they need and transact business while on the move.”
**Attracting more prospects with Mobile Prospect Portals**
The [Mobile Prospect Portal](https://www.realpage.com/contact-center/) has been in use at several communities belonging to Camden Property Trust. GPS technology helps prospects with mobile devices locate communities closest to their location — for example, when they’re in a car searching for an apartment. They can also view floor plans, access pricing and real-time availability, submit a guest card and even reserve a unit, all from their mobile device.
“Since June, we’ve had a 16% increase in mobile traffic according to site analytics, said John Selindh, Camden’s vice president of marketing. “With the assumption that more prospects are hitting our site due to the online campaigns, we can infer that prospects are comfortable with shopping for apartments using their mobile devices.”
**Mobile Resident Portal is a convenient new amenity**
The [Mobile Resident Portal](https://www.realpage.com/contact-center/) has also been in use at several Camden test sites, and is designed to deal with the primary concerns of most residents, such as receiving important community updates, submitting service requests, and paying rent online.
“During the first quarter of 2010, we processed over 165,000 transactions through our resident portal,” said Kristy Simonette, Camden’s senior vice president of strategic services and chief information officer. “We are pleased to now offer on-the-go access to our portal, allowing even more flexibility for our residents.”
In addition to providing powerful new capabilities for prospects and residents, the Mobile Prospect and Resident Portals extend CrossFire’s effort to promote self-service and reduce labor for onsite professionals. “The easier you make it for prospects and residents to do things for themselves, the greater the participation,” said Michael Munoz. “This means more renters engaging with your properties at any place and time.”
