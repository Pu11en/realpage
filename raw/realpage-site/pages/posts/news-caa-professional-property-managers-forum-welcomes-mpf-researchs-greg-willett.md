# CAA Professional Property Managers’ Forum Welcomes MPF Research’s Greg Willett
January 7, 2015
### _MPF Research’s Vice President to discuss area’s economic outlook_
**(January 7, 2015) —** Greg Willett, [MPF Research’s](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/) vice president, will serve as the guest speaker at the California Apartment Association’s [Professional Property Managers’ Forum](http://myaccount.caanet.org/CAA/EventDetail?EventKey=29PMF0129) held January 29 in Newport Beach, Calif.
During this luncheon, Mr. Willett will share insights into what the Southern California apartment market can expect in the upcoming year. He will delve into the Orange County Rental Housing Economic Outlook for 2015. See how Orange County compares to other counties in Southern California. And share how occupancy, rent, supply, and demand, etc., compare with others in your area, and what the expectations are for 2015.
