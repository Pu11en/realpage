# MPF Research’s Parsons and Willett to Speak at NMHC Apartment Strategies Outlook Conference
January 5, 2015
### _Pair will moderate discussion on suburban strategies_
**(January 5, 2015) —** On January 20, [MPF Research](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/)‘s Vice President Greg Willett and Director of Analytics & Forecasts Jay Parsons will sit on a panel at the NMHC Apartment Strategies Outlook Conference. Their panel, titled “Penny Lane” – Suburban Strategies: What Works Where?, will also have the following speakers:
  * Dean Henry, CEO, Legacy Partners
  * Brian Natwick, President – Multifamily, Crescent Communities
  * Carter Siegle, Director, Wood Partners
