# Apartment Market Data Firm Axiometrics Acquired
January 25, 2017
**MRWEB DAILY RESEARCH NEWS ONLINE** (January 25, 2017) – In the US, real estate software and data analytics specialist RealPage has acquired apartment market data firm Axiometrics for an undisclosed sum. Axiometrics will join RealPage’s MPF Research division.
Axiometrics surveys and researches properties in more than 500 apartment markets and submarkets, along with 680 colleges and universities nationwide.
