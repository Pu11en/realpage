# RealPage® Releases Public Housing-50058 Functionality for Affordable Housing Property Managers
March 8, 2012
### _American Baptist Homes of the West among first to implement new feature in the public housing market_
**(March 8, 2012)** — RealPage, Inc. (NASDAQ: [RP](http://finance.yahoo.com/q;_ylt=Ap5Wn9L7nK9q1JAadLn0p9axcq9_;_ylu=X3oDMTBzYm05a3ZkBHBvcwMxBHNlYwNuZXdzYXJzdGFydARzbGsDcnA-?s=rp)), a leading provider of on-demand software and software-enabled services to the rental housing industry, today announced its public housing-50058 functionality for OneSite® Affordable customers, enabling property owners to expand the use of their OneSite Affordable application within their public housing portfolio.
OneSite Affordable client, American Baptist Homes of the West (ABHOW), is using the new 50058 product for its first foray into public housing. “We’ve had a great experience using OneSite Affordable 50058 with our first public housing property. Our staff has found it very simple to use, with very few training requirements needed,” said Susan Ruan, corporate compliance specialist for ABHOW. “We’ve worked closely with RealPage, who has been a great partner as we enter a new area of affordable housing.”
Public Housing Authorities (PHAs) and property management companies managing public housing on behalf of PHAs are required to qualify and certify residents using the HUD-50058 form, while also reporting that information by electronic transmission to HUD’s PIC system. In some cases, these properties are also layered with tax credits financing, making the compliance requirements even more complex. RealPage’s solution takes into consideration all these complexities and places special emphasis on the ease-of-use and flexibility of this new feature.
“We’re providing this new functionality to our existing customers as well as prospects that have been looking into potentially adopting OneSite Affordable. This is a growing market as the privatization of the management of public housing has become more prevalent across the country” said Gustavo Sapiurka, vice president and general manager of Affordable Housing at RealPage. “We are now able to support all flavors of affordable housing programs supported at the site level by complementing our existing offerings for HUD Multifamily, USDA Rural Housing and the Low Income Housing Tax Credit programs.”
“RealPage built the new functionality from the ground up, and by doing so, was able to maintain the same ease-of-use customers are accustomed to, making it easy to deploy across portfolios while keeping consistency,” said Sapiurka.
