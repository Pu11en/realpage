# Richardson’s RealPage buys American Utility Management for $70 million
June 19, 2017
**THE DALLAS MORNING NEWS** (June 19, 2017) –Richardson-based apartment software and services firm RealPage Inc. has made its third purchase so far in 2017.
RealPage said Monday that it’s paying $70 million to buy Illinois-based American Utility Management, a provider of utility and energy management systems for the apartment industry.
