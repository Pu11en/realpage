# Rents are rebounding in cities like Nashville, Tampa, Columbus and Kansas City
June 30, 2020
_But big cities like New York, Boston, LA and San Jose see double-digit drops in executed rent amounts_
**HousingWire** (June 30, 2020) -- In April, [apartment retention](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/as-more-renters-stay-put-apartment-retention-breaks-records-but-revenue-suffers/) broke records as renters had to stay put due to the pandemic. Come May, apartment operators across the country began to [drop rent](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/rent-is-dropping-across-the-country/).
Now, in the week ending June 20, executed rents – a real-time indicator that includes concessions and lease term lengths – for new leases rose 0.08% year over year, according to **RealPage**.
Although this number is small, it’s a sign of apartment demand rebounding after the last three months of rent declines. In the week ending June 20, total new lease volumes soared by 18.6% year over year.
