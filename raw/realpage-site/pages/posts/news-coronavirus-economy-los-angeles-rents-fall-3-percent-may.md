# Los Angeles’ average rent fell 3% in May from a year earlier as coronavirus infected the economy
June 5, 2020
**Los Angeles Times** (June 5, 2020) -- The average asking rent for an apartment in Los Angeles County fell last month from a year earlier, according to RealPage, a major real estate data provider.
The 3.3% decrease to an average of $2,254 for all sized units followed a 0.8% drop in April and reflects how the coronavirus-related economic downturn is sweeping through the[ rental housing market](https://www.latimes.com/homeless-housing/story/2020-05-27/thousands-of-struggling-l-a-residents-could-get-rent-relief-under-100-million-city-council-proposal).
Some landlords and property managers said they’re giving concessions such as a month of free rent or dropping their asking price to get units filled as [concern over the economy](https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2020-05-22/california-april-jobless-rate-jobs-disappear) grows.
