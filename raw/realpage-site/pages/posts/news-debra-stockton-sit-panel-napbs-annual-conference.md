# Debra Stockton to Sit on Panel at NAPBS Annual Conference
October 9, 2014
### _RealPage senior vice president speaking October 21_
**
(October 9, 2014) —** RealPage senior vice president of LeasingDesk Screening Debra Stockton will sit on a panel at this year’s National Professional Background Screeners Annual Conference. She will be joined by two other panelists –Jay Harris, CoreLogic National Background Data; Scott Ledbetter, Resident Verify, LLC — to discuss “Tenant Screening: Tackling Questions Regarding Data Sources and Laws that Govern Tenant Screening”
For more info on the conference, visit: <http://www.napbs.com/events/annual2014/program/schedule.cfm>.
