# RealPage Contributes to Units Article on Collections
November 5, 2014
### _Provides insights into how to alleviate missed rent payments_
**(November 6, 2014) —** In the November issue of Units Magazine, RealPage provides insights and advice for the article, “Avoiding Collections Complications.” The article covers ways that apartment managers can avoid skips and evictions due to non-payment of rents from developing specific strategies around “anti-collection” rent payments to new technology solutions to implement.
