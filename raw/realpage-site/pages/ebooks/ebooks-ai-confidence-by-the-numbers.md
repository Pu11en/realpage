#### What Do Leasing Teams Really Think About AI?
Leasing teams don’t just want any AI; they want tools they can trust. New research from RealPage's UX Research Center of Excellence reveals a growing sense of optimism among leasing professionals, alongside a clear takeaway: AI should simplify workflows, not complicate them; assist, not replace.
Explore how AI is currently used in leasing and where it’s headed. From concerns over accuracy and reliability to the excitement around streamlined processes, the findings reveal what matters most to leasing teams regarding artificial intelligence.
This study digs into:
  * What tasks leasing agents are _ready_ to delegate to AI
  * The _top benefits_ leasing agents see from AI-powered tools
  * Where human connection still matters most
  * The _biggest challenges_ slowing down adoption
  * What’s next for AI across the renter journey, from lead to resident
