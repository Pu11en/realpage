# Unlocking NOI: Consolidating Your Smart Building Stack
[ Download eBook ](https://www.realpage.com/ebooks/unlocking-noi-consolidating-smart-building-stack/#form-wrapper-148)
Fragmented property technology creates more than operational inconvenience. When Wi-Fi, access control, and smart devices are managed across multiple vendors and disconnected systems, operators face duplicated costs, slower workflows, inconsistent resident experiences, and reduced visibility across the asset. In today’s multifamily environment, that kind of fragmentation directly limits efficiency, scalability, and net operating income.
In this eBook, discover how consolidating your smart-building stack can help your portfolio by:
  * **Reducing Operational Inefficiency:** Unify disconnected systems under one platform to automate routine workflows, simplify daily management, and help on-site teams operate more proactively.
  * **Eliminating Redundant Costs:** Replace overlapping infrastructure, contracts, and support models with a centralized smart-building approach designed to reduce waste and improve accountability.
  * **Improving Resident Experience and Asset Performance:** Deliver reliable, instant-on connectivity and a more cohesive smart-home experience while building a flexible technology foundation that supports long-term portfolio value.

Learn how a unified smart-building platform can streamline operations, strengthen resident satisfaction, and create a more profitable, future-ready portfolio.
