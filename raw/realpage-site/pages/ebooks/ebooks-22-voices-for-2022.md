#### Explore executive interviews and invaluable insights on pivotal moves from industry peers.
Given the variables that will be ongoing in 2022, RealPage® set out to interview a diverse group of 22 rental housing leaders ― gathering strategic perspectives from different geographies, portfolio types, sizes and job roles ― to cover 22 timely topics that we hoped would inform and engage professionals across the industry.
This guide is the result of generous, videotaped conversations with all 22 leaders who agreed to share their best tips and strategies for success in shifting market conditions. Our goal was simple: deliver the same current and relevant information and analysis that you would glean from gurus speaking at major industry events—but in the most succinct way possible.
You’ll find those insights and invaluable commentary summarized here. And if there is a theme to these 22 conversations, it’s this: _Focus on controlling what you_ can _control._
Read what your peers across the apartment industry have to say as they open up their playbooks.
