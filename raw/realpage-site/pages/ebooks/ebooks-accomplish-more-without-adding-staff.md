#### Affordable Housing Playbook
Hiring and retaining qualified Affordable Housing accounting, information technology (IT) and compliance team members for your organization can be daunting, time consuming and expensive. And thanks to the Great Resignation, turnover in the multifamily industry is high — 32% annually, according to the NAA*.
The constant struggle to source quality talent means you don’t always have the help you need. And that puts your business continuity at risk. You can navigate the uncertainty by tapping into managed services, which can help you gain accounting, IT and compliance expertise you can count on — experts with deep knowledge of property management and the Affordable sector.
Our Playbook gives you the info you need to get started.
