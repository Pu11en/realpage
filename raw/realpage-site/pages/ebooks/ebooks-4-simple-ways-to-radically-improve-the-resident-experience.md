# 4 Simple Ways To Radically Improve the Resident Experience
[ Download eBook ](https://www.realpage.com/ebooks/4-simple-ways-to-radically-improve-the-resident-experience/#form-wrapper-133)
Today’s residents live in a world rich with smart technologies and a near-endless array of user-friendly apps. Property management companies looking to outperform their competitors embrace this new reality and build their businesses accordingly. When it comes to the overall rental process and resident experience, they’re using new systems and technologies to address four critical areas:
  * Replacing outdated, cumbersome systems
  * Streamlining the move-in/move-out processes
  * Creating flexible online payment solutions
  * Enhancing and strengthening their community engagement

Gone are the days of paper applications, payments made with hand-written checks, and phone calls to a maintenance hotline. Renting today is more like an Amazon® purchase or a Netflix® subscription, and residents expect – even _demand_ – that their rental experience does the same.
Download this timely eBook and discover what constitutes a “good” resident experience today and how you can apply that across all your properties.
