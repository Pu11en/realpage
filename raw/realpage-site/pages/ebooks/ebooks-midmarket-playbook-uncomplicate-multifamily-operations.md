# Midmarket Playbook: 3 Steps to Uncomplicate Your Multifamily Operations
[ Download eBook ](https://www.realpage.com/ebooks/midmarket-playbook-uncomplicate-multifamily-operations/#form-wrapper-106)
Multifamily owners are buying and selling at a faster pace than usual, rent is unpredictable as markets continue to rapidly shift, renters are more demanding than ever, and opportunities are nipping at the heels of your top talent. Your path to growth is complicated, to say the least. You need a way to stay ahead of the curve as you scale — to keep up with the demands of the market, your staff and your residents.
This Midmarket Playbook picks up where our webcast, Overcoming the Big Challenges Facing Smaller Multifamily Operators, left off by detailing 3 steps to uncomplicating your operations so you can win against larger portfolios, even as you face big growth challenges.
Learn how to
  * Leverage technology and get creative with staffing
  * Automate mundane tasks to gain efficiency, accuracy and insights
  * Avoid disruptions and future-proof your operations as you scale
