# Apartment Tours
Elevate Your Apartment Tours
Exceed prospect expectations from the moment they schedule a tour with a comprehensive touring platform available right from a tablet. With fast, accurate and user-friendly ID-scanning and identity verification tools, Knock Tours helps mitigate rental fraud and gives you peace of mind.

## Keep communities safe from fraud
With a guest-card integrated ID scanner that validates more than 16,000 documents from 200 countries and territories and a selfie scan tool that allows prospects to verify their identity quickly, Knock Tours delivers a flexible way to help mitigate rental fraud. By integrating agent workflows, community content and business intelligence into a mobile, user-friendly experience, we give leasing teams the tools to level up the touring experience and provide management with data to improve the sales process continuously.
## Empower leasing teams to give the best tours possible
Knock Tours gives leasing teams the tools to always give a standout tour. Our user-friendly dashboard gives teams a quick overview of the day’s touring schedule, while the Tour Itinerary highlights the important property information. During the tour, interactive property maps, neighborhood maps, digital floor plans and photos showcase the best parts of your community. And at the end of a tour, leasing teams can send a quote to prospects with just the click of a button.
## Match the needs of any prospect
Allow prospects to tour the way they prefer with video, live-agent or self-guided tour options for prospects.
