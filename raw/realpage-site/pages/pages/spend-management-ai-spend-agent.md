# AI Spend Agent – Powered by Lumina
## Smarter procurement starts with visibility
Realpage AI Spend Agent validates approved invoices against your preferred vendors, purchase-order and receipt-of-goods requirements, budget, and the contract terms you have entered. It surfaces potential compliance exceptions, purchasing patterns, and savings opportunities based on category assumptions you set, helping your team reduce maverick spend, improve purchasing consistency, and inform future decisions across the portfolio.

##  Your AI-powered partner for ongoing spend visibility
Property teams make purchasing decisions every day across sites, categories, vendors, invoices, approvals, and contracts. Without ongoing visibility, teams may have difficulty identifying potential exceptions, comparing purchasing patterns across properties, and evaluating contract-related savings opportunities.

AI Spend Agent works behind the scenes monitoring procurement on an ongoing basis to help you keep purchasing compliant, efficient, and aligned with portfolio-wide standards. It helps teams understand purchasing behavior across the portfolio and make more informed future decisions.

Built on Realpage's data platform, AI Spend Agent works with OpsTechnology or Spend Management in Accounting (subscription required). It brings invoice-based spend visibility and surfaces potential compliance exceptions within everyday purchasing workflows.
##  What the AI Spend Agent delivers:
## Spend compliance monitoring
  * Monitors procurement activity on an ongoing basis through approved invoice review
  * Checks approved invoice line items against your preferred and compliant vendors by category
  * Identifies catalog vendors submitting offline invoices or operating outside their provisioned properties
  * Surfaces potential noncompliant activity and exceptions for your team’s review

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/spend/images/2026/07/spend-compliance-screen.png?v=1785436734)
## Contract compliance that helps you capture negotiated savings
  * Reviews contracts for preferred terms, pricing rules, and discounts
  * Detects on- and off-contract spend across properties and portfolios using customer-entered contract data
  * Surfaces potential contract and policy exceptions for team review
  * Estimates potential savings based on the category assumptions you set

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/spend/images/2026/07/contract-compliance-screen.png?v=1785436734)
## Actionable insights
  * Monitors spend trends across properties and portfolios
  * Identifies portfolio-wide savings opportunities
  * Improves oversight with actionable spend intelligence

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/spend/images/2026/07/insights-screen.png?v=1785436737)
## Procurement automation with less friction – Planned Functionality*
  * Automates requisitions, approvals, and ordering
  * Captures and processes invoices; supports timely payments
  * Identifies invoices missing purchase-order or receipt-of-goods approval
  * Coordinates with Lumina® AI Facilities agent to support broader operational workflows

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/spend/images/2026/07/procurement-screen.png?v=1785436737)
## Vendor management built into the workflow – Planned Functionality*
  * Recommends preferred vendors for sourcing and bidding
  * Accelerates vendor contracting by identifying the relevant client template and incorporating commercial terms from invoice data
  * Assists with vendor credentialing and compliance requirements
