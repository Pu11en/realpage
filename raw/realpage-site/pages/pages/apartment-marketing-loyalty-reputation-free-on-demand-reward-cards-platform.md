Deliver reward cards for free – without ever leaving the leasing office.

The Community Rewards™ Incentives program enables apartment communities to deliver on-demand, virtual Reward Cards directly to residents. Besides reducing the time spent managing and buying cards—saving you up to $12/unit/year in staff productivity*—you can manage the entire process from a single dashboard.
Offer your residents instant rewards that are redeemable for their choice of over 100 virtual reward card brands, including virtual VISA®. There are no activation fees, conserving cash flow and potentially replacing rent concessions.
Plus, you’ll gain full accountability and reporting for incentives sent, as well as money back on all unclaimed incentives. And you can employ multiple user access levels for increased security, while reducing fraud and theft.
