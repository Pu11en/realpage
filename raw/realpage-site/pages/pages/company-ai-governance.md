# AI Governance
Responsible AI Governance for Multifamily
## Our Approach
###### Responsible AI at Realpage® is built on three core pillars:
## 1
#### AI Assurance
We evaluate and test AI systems throughout their lifecycle, including model assessments, security reviews, performance monitoring, and ongoing validation. Our goal is to help ensure AI solutions operate as intended and continue to meet defined performance and safety expectations.
## 2
#### AI Compliance
We translate evolving regulatory requirements, fair housing obligations, customer commitments, and industry standards into practical controls and governance processes that support responsible AI deployment.
## 3
#### AI Governance Operations
We maintain the processes, oversight, and accountability structures needed to govern AI at scale. This includes AI inventories, review workflows, risk assessments, control management, and ongoing monitoring.
## Built for Multifamily
The multifamily industry presents unique considerations for AI adoption. Solutions that interact with residents, operators, and housing-related decisions require thoughtful oversight and careful attention to fairness, transparency, and accountability.
Our Responsible AI program is designed with these realities in mind. We work to ensure our AI solutions support customer trust, align with industry obligations, and deliver meaningful business outcomes while maintaining appropriate safeguards.
## Our Commitment to Governance
Responsible AI is not a one-time initiative. It is an ongoing commitment to governance, transparency, and continuous improvement. As AI capabilities continue to evolve, Realpage remains focused on helping customers adopt and benefit from AI with confidence, backed by a governance framework designed specifically for the multifamily industry.
