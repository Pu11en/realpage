## Key Components of Renters Insurance
Renters insurance is designed to protect what matters most—your belongings, your finances, and your peace of mind. Most policies are built around a few core coverages that work together to help you recover quickly from unexpected events like theft, damage, or accidents.
#### Property Coverage
Protect the things that make your space feel like home. Property coverage helps reimburse you for personal belongings—like furniture, clothing, and electronics—if they're damaged or stolen due to covered events such as fire or theft.
#### Liability Coverage
Accidents happen. Liability coverage helps protect you financially if someone is injured in your home or if you accidentally cause damage to someone else's property—covering medical bills, legal fees, and more.
#### Additional Living Expenses
Stay on track, even when life is disrupted. If your rental becomes temporarily uninhabitable due to a covered loss, this coverage helps pay for essentials like temporary housing, meals, and other day-to-day expenses while you get back on your feet.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Renters Insurance
#### How much does apartment renters insurance cost?
Renters insurance is typically very affordable. Policies in the U.S. range depending on your location, coverage limits, and deductible. Your exact cost will also vary based on how much coverage you choose and the level of protection you need.
#### Will we receive proof of renters insurance from residents automatically?
Yes. With Realpage’s integrated insurance solutions, proof of coverage is handled automatically. This eliminates the need for site teams to manually collect or verify insurance documents, while ensuring policies remain active and compliant throughout the lease term.
#### What is not covered in renters insurance?
eRenterPlan policies cover many everyday risks, but some limitations apply. Coverage may be restricted for high-value items, certain events requiring add-ons, and belongings away from your home. Reviewing your policy helps ensure you have the right protection.
