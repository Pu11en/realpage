## Take a deep dive into current renter needs and expectations
####  97% of renters say they’d be more likely to renew their lease if working with their property manager was as easy as interacting with Amazon®.
####  98% of renters think property managers should offer a loyalty program for on-time rent payments that would provide points for rent payments, groceries, gas, personal care, etc.
####  77% of renters have struggled with credit and would like help with flexible payment schedules and a financial wellness program offered by their property management company.

## The research
As a leading software and data analytics provider to the real estate industry, Realpage partnered with Dimensional Research®, which provides practical market research to help technology companies make smarter business decisions.
#### Participation
  * The survey included 2,000 U.S.-based participants ages 18 to 55 who are currently multifamily home renters.
  * A mix of genders, household incomes, regions and demographics were captured to provide a reliable snapshot of the U.S. population.
  * The research study was conducted online in June 2024.
