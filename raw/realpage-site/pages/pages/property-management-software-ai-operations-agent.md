##  Your always-on AI-powered operations specialist
Your onsite and property operations teams juggle hundreds of recurring tasks — lease audits, certifications, renewals, move-outs, ledger updates, exceptions, billing accuracy, and compliance. These tasks matter, but they often create bottlenecks, inconsistencies, and missed revenue.

The AI Operations Agent takes on operational details. As your policy driven, fully embedded Realpage® assistant, it works behind the scenes 24/7 to audit, verify, escalate, and keep every task on track. It is purpose built for accuracy, compliance support, and transparency — while reducing manual effort and improving execution across your entire portfolio.
##  What the AI Operations Agent delivers
## Portfolio-wide lease audits — done continuously, not periodically
  * Scans leases vs. PMS ledgers to detect missed revenue and compliance risks
  * Flags discrepancies in rent, fees, concessions, deposits, and charge schedules
  * Surfaces renewal packet inconsistencies and move-in/renewal charge issues
  * Supports both Conventional and Affordable portfolios

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/pages/ai-operations-agent/images/2026/07/ai-operations-product-page-section-1b.png?v=1785559145)
## Affordable file audits that reduce risk and save hours
  * Reviews certifications and recerts for errors and inconsistencies
  * Identifies missing forms, missing signatures, dual income files, and documentation issues
  * Summarizes findings and routes compliance exceptions for follow-up

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/pages/ai-operations-agent/images/2026/07/ai-operations-product-page-section-2b.png?v=1785559145)
## Move-out, renewal, and unit occupancy processing without the back-and-forth
  * Presents staff-side tasks for renewals, transfers, and moveouts
  * Helps keep rent rolls accurate for reporting and compliance
  * Reduces manual clicking and administrative workload

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/operations/images/2026/07/move-out.png?v=1785434402)
## Charges, payments & ledger accuracy — With real time anomaly detection
  * Detects charge discrepancies, unusual adjustments, and billing inconsistencies
  * Responds to staff inquiries about ledgers, charges, and payments
  * Supports reduction in delinquencies with improved account accuracy

![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/operations/images/2026/07/payments-ledger-accuracy.png?v=1785434407)
## Daily operational briefings & performance reporting
  * Summarizes all completed audit actions, flags, and required follow-ups
  * Delivers performance dashboards to help teams quickly resolve discrepancies
  * Provides transparent, documented activity logs
