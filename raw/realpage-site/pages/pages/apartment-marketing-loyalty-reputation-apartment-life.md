### Apartment Life + Community Rewards
Realpage® is thrilled to join the Apartment Life mission. Our vision is to deliver best-in-class technology that enhances the productivity and results of Apartment Life coordinators and teams so coordinators can spend more time building resident connections and experiences while leveraging the Community Rewards platform to support continuous resident engagement that is enhanced with personalized rewards.
“I love that our Apartment Life Coordinators are on Community Rewards! Using the Community Rewards platform has become the best way to share information directly with our residents and gives our coordinators easy access to residents virtually and in between events. Since our Apartment Life Coordinators have transitioned, they’ve been able to leverage the app to increase resident engagement and decrease the administration for our onsite team.” - Property Manager, Linq at North Springs
Together with Apartment Life, we’re able to help multifamily property owners reach more residents and drive more value for you and them:
  * Attract new leases more quickly
  * Gather more resident feedback

  * Retain residents for longer periods of time
  * Enhance operational efficiency
