#  RealPage and SimpleBills® team up to meet Student demands.
## Less stress for students.
More savings for you.
Now part of the RealPage® family, [SimpleBills](https://www.simplebills.com/) provides RealPage Utility Management (RUM) customers with expanded options that make billing and payments even more convenient for students by enabling them to split water, electric, gas, cable bills and other shared utilities among their roommates. Students love the quick-and-easy experience, your site staff will love their increased productivity—because SimpleBills frees them from tedious billing tasks—and you'll love the improved overall payment performance across your campus assets.
