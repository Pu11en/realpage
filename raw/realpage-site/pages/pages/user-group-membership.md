Any Realpage® client may serve on a user group subcommittee. In order to join, a client—as well as his or her company—must agree to the terms as outlined in the Subcommittee Charter.
  * Attend one annual meeting to be held during the RealWorld conference.
  * Participate in one conference call per quarter.
  * Pay the costs associated with the annual meeting (i.e., travel, etc.).
  * Commit the time necessary to perform subcommittee business.
  * Serve a two-year term.

Please note: We also request that you are currently using the product or service of the subcommittee you want to join.
