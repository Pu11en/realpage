## Realpage data access is only the beginning
Moving records from one property management system to another does not make them ready for analysis or AI agents. Raw transactional tables still require cleansing, modeling, definitions, relationships, and ongoing maintenance before teams can use them confidently. This challenging work burdens data teams – in fact, 57% of analytics professionals spend more time preparing data than conducting analysis1.
Traditional replication and batch delivery may move data closer, but they leave customers to build and maintain the pipeline and reconstruct the business meaning behind the records. Lumina Connect provides governed access to Realpage data with the context needed to support more consistent analysis and more reliable AI answers, and without ongoing maintenance of data pipelines.

##  Choose the Realpage data connection that fits the work
Both solutions reduce the work required to turn Realpage records into information your property teams and technology can use with confidence.
#### Lumina Data Cloud
Provides zero-copy access to analytics-ready Realpage data through Snowflake, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, Google Cloud Platform and BI tools such as Power BI. Build dashboards, create custom KPIs, and blend Realpage data with enterprise and third-party sources without building and maintaining another ingestion pipeline for Realpage data.
#### Lumina Model Connect
Gives customer AI agents governed access to Realpage data through an MCP connector. Curated data models and consistent multifamily context help agents answer complex questions the first time, without repeated prompts and unnecessary token burn.
## Explore Lumina Data Cloud
Lumina Data Cloud provides zero-copy access to authorized Realpage data through Snowflake, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, or Google Cloud Platform. Begin working with current, analytics-ready data without waiting for replicated files or building and maintaining a separate ingestion pipeline for Realpage data.
Because Realpage maintains the source data and understands the business logic behind it, your teams can begin with curated tables, predefined metrics, consistent definitions, and time-stamped updates.

  * Use Realpage data with BI tools such as Power BI or through your existing cloud data platform.

## Explore Lumina Model Connect
Lumina Model Connect gives your AI agents governed access to Realpage data modeled and curated specifically for how agents query, reason, and analyze. Through MCP, agents can ask questions in plain language, analyze connected operational and financial data, rebuild or combine reports, and create custom analysis.
Instead of reverse-engineering joins, definitions, report logic, and business relationships from raw tables, agents begin with the multifamily context needed to reach supported answers in fewer steps.
  * Help agents produce more reliable answers and reduce unsupported responses.
  * Reduce repeated prompts, correction cycles, and unnecessary token use.
  * Move beyond a fixed set of prebuilt questions and narrow use cases.
