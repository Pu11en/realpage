## Benefits of an Online Rent Payment Portal
#### Improve efficiency and decrease processing cost
Reduce time spent collecting and processing leasing and rent payments each month and reduce revenue recognition issues resulting from processing mistakes. As a result, site staff can spend more time on revenue-driving activities such as leasing and renewals.
#### Reduce time spent understanding and ensuring compliance
Realpage has heavily invested in ensuring compliance and paying attention to complex and changing federal and state laws governing payments. We’ve done the groundwork, so you don’t have to with built-in payment compliance and security standards.
#### Reduce risk of fraud associated with payment collection
By taking checks and money orders out of the office, you remove the opportunity for theft and fraud, reducing risk. Some properties report having to replace 8-12 site managers per year due to money order fraud.
## Payment Options for Easier, Faster Rent Collection
#### Online and Mobile Card Payments
Empower residents to pay online or from their smartphone—automatically—with a bank account (ACH payment) or a credit or debit card.
#### Digital Shared Wallet
Simplify the payment experience with a shared digital wallet that allows residents to securely use saved payment methods across accounts, creating a faster, more seamless checkout while enhancing payment security.
#### Flexible payments (Livble)
Give residents the ability to split rent into smaller, manageable installments with Livble, improving on-time payments while reducing financial stress and increasing satisfaction and retention.
#### Cash/Money Order
Replace in-office, paper money order payments with cash payments made at nearby retail facilities and automatically see payments posted in the ledger.
#### Lockbox
Give residents the convenience of paying by check or money order without paper payments coming into your office. Residents mail checks to a designated P.O. box. Payments are received at our secure, remote facility where they are automatically scanned, extracted and routed to post in real time.
#### Check Scanning
Allow residents to pay by check while you reduce manual data entry and enable funds to be sent electronically to the bank with check scanning technology. Scan checks one-by-one or up to 50 at a time.

## FAQ About Online Rent Payment Portals
#### What is the best rent payment app for large multifamily portfolios?
For large multifamily portfolios, the best option is usually one that integrates payments with property management operations, reporting, and resident services. Portfolio scale often requires more than a standalone payment tool.
#### How do rent payment apps help reduce late payments?
They reduce late payments by offering auto-pay, scheduled transactions, reminder notifications, and easier mobile access. These features make it easier for residents to pay on time without manual follow-up from staff.
#### Do rent payment apps integrate with property management software?
Some do, and that distinction matters. Integration helps reduce duplicate work, improve reporting accuracy, and connect payment activity to broader property operations.
#### What security features should I look for in a rent payment application?
Look for secure payment processing, encryption, fraud controls, data protection standards, and clear administrative controls. Strong security supports both compliance and resident trust.
