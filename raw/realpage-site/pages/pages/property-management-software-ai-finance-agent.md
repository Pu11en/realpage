##  What the AI Finance Agent delivers
## Invoice processing – Automated, accurate, and scalable
  * Extracts invoice data from emails or uploads
  * Applies GL coding based on historical activity
  * Prepares invoices for review with pre-filled details

**Result:** Faster processing, fewer errors, and reduced manual entry
![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/finance/images/2026/07/invoice-processing-screen.png?v=1785431467)
## Bank reconciliation – Simplified and continuous
  * Connects directly to banks or ingests bulk statements
  * Matches bank activity to GL transactions
  * Creates journal entries for unmatched items

**Result:** Faster reconciliation workflows and stronger close readiness
![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/finance/images/2026/07/bank-reconciliation-screen.png?v=1785431481)
## Recurring expenses – Managed proactively
  * Identifies recurring vendors and standard expenses
  * Flags missing invoices for current and prior periods
  * Supports accrual creation and timely payment of critical vendors

**Result:** Fewer missed expenses, improved accuracy, and better cash flow control
![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/finance/images/2026/07/recurring-expenses-screen.png?v=1785431476)
## Contract & task automation – Without the manual work
  * Extracts key contract terms, dates, and obligations
  * Automates workflows like approvals, escheatment, and vendor maintenance
  * Tracks critical deadlines and financial responsibilities

**Result:** Reduced administrative burden and stronger contract oversight
![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/finance/images/2026/07/contract-task-automation-screen.png?v=1785431467)
## Financial exceptions & anomaly detection – Before they escalate
  * Flags discrepancies in invoices, reconciliations, and expenses
  * Surfaces anomalies for quick review and resolution
  * Supports close readiness with visibility into open items

**Result:** Fewer errors, faster resolution, and reduced financial risk
![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/pages/ai-finance-agent/images/2026/07/contract-and-task-automation-2.png?v=1785557890)
## Financial reporting & insights – Delivered in real time
  * Summarizes financial activity and flagged exceptions
  * Generates reports to support better decision-making
  * Provides visibility into trends and performance

**Result:** Smarter decisions with real-time financial intelligence
![](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/next-ui/lumina/finance/images/2026/07/financial-reporting.png?v=1785431472)
