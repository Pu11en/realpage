##  Why the AI Analytics Agent is Different
The Analytics Agent combines Realpage data, multifamily business logic, and [Lumina® Ascent](https://www.realpage.com/solutions/lumina-ascent/ "Lumina Ascent") performance intelligence to help teams move from question to action with confidence.
  * Understands market, asset, and portfolio context
  * Interprets proprietary Market, Asset, and Portfolio Signals
  * Explains the drivers behind performance
  * Prioritizes opportunities with the greatest potential impact
  * Guides teams toward informed action

Backed by Realpage data, business logic, and a quarter century of rental real estate experience, the AI Analytics Agent gives executives, asset managers, and operators a better way to explore performance and align on what action to take next. It turns complex data into narratives, visuals, and supporting detail so your team can move from question to action.

##  What the AI Analytics Agent delivers
#### Conversational
Analytics
  * Ask performance questions in plain language
  * Explore performance without advanced analytics training
  * Ask follow-up questions to move quickly beyond initial findings

#### Performance
Diagnostics
  * Understand why a Signal is strong, weak, improving, or declining
  * Explore the drivers and supporting data behind each Signal
  * Get recommendations for actions with the highest impact

#### Narrative
Summaries
  * Generate concise summaries for portfolios, markets, and individual assets
  * Generate stakeholder-ready insights for leadership updates, portfolio reviews, and operating meetings

#### Data
Visualization
  * Create reports and visualizations based on plain language requests
  * Answer questions about Lumina Ascent reports
  * Build, recreate, and modify reports based on user templates
