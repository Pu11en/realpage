## Are You Looking for the Total Package?
That’s fair. So are we.
###### A Total Package
Our benefits packages offer teammates quality health plans, discount programs and financial planning options. We know – who doesn’t? But, at Realpage®, our packages come with teams of support to help you access, learn and understand your benefits.
###### Something for Everyone
Coffee. Quiet Rooms. Playful Amenities. Medical Plans. Dental Plans. Vision Plans. Pharmacy Plans. Retirement Plans. PTO. Paternity Leave. Maternity Leave. Adoption Assistance. You can have it all.
###### Well-Rounded Well-Being
Realpage takes wellness seriously. Physical wellness and mental wellness. Our health partners offer tools and resources to assist with some of life’s hardest issues. Our goal is to help all employees achieve life balance.
###### Physical Benefits
Work doesn’t need to be stressful. Need to walk? Walk. Need to take a break. Break. Want to work out? Go for it! Some office locations offer on-site game rooms or fitness facilities. Or, take advantage of our fitness reimbursement programs, where available.
###### Time for You
Realpage offers competitive time-off packages that include vacation and sick time, plus floating holidays. New fathers can enjoy paternity leave the same as new mothers.
###### Medical Necessities
Want to set aside money for medical needs? Flexible spending and health spending accounts are available in some locations. These healthcare dollars help you manage medical costs now and into the future.
