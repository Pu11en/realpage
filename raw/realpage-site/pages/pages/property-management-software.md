OneSite property management software consolidates leasing, maintenance, accounting, and operations into a centralized platform. With AI-enabled workflows and cross property capabilities, it simplifies processes, reduces manual tasks, and boosts NOI across your property portfolio.

Lumina AI copilot supports OneSite users by automating routine property management tasks, like month-end close and move-out processing, often saving teams up to 80% of their time. It enables intelligent execution of common tasks through prompts, actions, and insights.

OneSite is designed for multifamily owners and operators, property management companies, onsite teams, regional managers, centralized operations teams, and portfolio leaders. It supports operators managing conventional multifamily, student, affordable housing, tax credit, military, and senior living properties.
