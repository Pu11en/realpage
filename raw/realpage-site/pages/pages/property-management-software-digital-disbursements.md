## Comprehensive Digital Disbursement Multifamily Solutions
### Virtual Refunds
Modernize your refund operations with Virtual Refunds. Residents can receive refunds via virtual prepaid cards, eliminating the hassle of paper checks.
#### Benefits:
  * Reduce manual paperwork and processing time.
  * Save $3-$12 per refund.
  * Improve resident satisfaction with faster, trackable refunds.

How It Works
Refunds are sent digitally to tenants, where they can access their funds instantly. Residents select how they would like to receive their refund: Check, ACH, virtual prepaid card or via mobile wallet.
### Utility Reimbursement Debit Cards
Simplify utility reimbursements for the Affordable Housing market with reloadable prepaid debit cards.
#### Benefits:
  * Eliminate paper checks and escheatment processes.
  * Funds are added to the card monthly, ensuring timely payments.
  * Residents can use the card for purchases or bill payments.

How It Works
Payments are processed through Realpage Accounting and sent directly to residents’ cards, which they can activate via phone or app.
### Petty Cash Prepaid Cards
Replace traditional petty cash with prepaid cards for better control and visibility.
#### Benefits:
  * Track and code expenses in real-time.
  * Reduce the risk of lost receipts or misuse of funds.
  * Simplify reimbursements with automated workflows.

How It Works
Cards are issued to properties or property management staff with preset limits, and transactions are automatically recorded in Realpage Accounting.
### Vendor Payments
Take control of the procurement-to-payment cycle with Realpage Vendor Payments.
#### Benefits:
  * Automate invoice processing and payment workflows.
  * Reduce cycle times from 1 month to 10 days.
  * Earn cash-back incentives on electronic payments.

How It Works
Vendor payments are processed through Realpage Accounting, with options for Virtual Card, Direct Pay, or Check.
