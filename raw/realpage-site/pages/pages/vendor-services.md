## Frequently Asked Questions
#### What is vendor credentialing and what does it help me manage?
Vendor credentialing ensures vendors meet required insurance, licensing, and business standards before working with property managers. Realpage helps vendors manage documents, track requirements by customer, and stay current with automated renewal alerts, keeping everything organized, accurate, and up to date.
#### How does Vendor Credentialing help me get approved faster and avoid delays?
Realpage shows exactly what is missing, tracks requirements per customer, and sends proactive alerts for updates or expirations. Direct coordination with insurance agents helps resolve issues quickly, reducing delays and enabling faster, even same-day, approvals when requirements are met.
#### What is a vendor marketplace and how does it help me win more business?
A vendor marketplace is a platform where service providers showcase offerings and connect with property managers. Realpage helps vendors discover new opportunities, respond to sourcing requests, and grow their network of property management clients.
#### How does Realpage help vendors get paid faster?
Real-time invoicing and streamlined payment workflows reduce processing delays, enabling faster and more predictable payments.
#### Who can use Realpage Vendor Services?
Contractors, service providers, and vendors that work with property management companies.
#### What is the vendor experience and how does it benefit me?
A single, centralized platform to manage credentials, documents, and customer requirements—eliminating multiple logins and disconnected tools while simplifying day-to-day operations.
#### What is a Credential Key and why is it valuable?
Credential Key is an insurance-based program that allows qualified vendors to establish a master insurance profile within Realpage Vendor Credentialing that can be leveraged across participating customers. By leveraging the master insurance profile, vendors reduce duplicate submissions and, when required information is complete, can enable a faster approval.
#### What is the Insurance Agent Portal and how does it work?
Realpage Insurance Agent Portal gives insurance agents a faster, more transparent way to submit documentation on behalf of vendors. Using a secure, customized link tied to the vendor and property management company, agents can upload documents accurately, access sample certificates of insurance to confirm coverage requirements, view real time document status, and resolve compliance issues faster. Submissions through the portal are prioritized for quicker processing and turnaround.
