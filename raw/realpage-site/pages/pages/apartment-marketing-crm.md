## Knock CRM FAQs:
#### What is an apartment customer relationship management (CRM) platform?
An apartment CRM helps multifamily teams manage leads, prospect communications, follow-ups and leasing activity in one place. It helps leasing teams respond faster, stay organized and convert more leads into leases.
#### What is Knock CRM?
Knock CRM, a Realpage Company, is a centralized customer relationship management platform designed specifically for multifamily property management. It unifies marketing, leasing, communication, and analytics tools so property teams can attract high-quality leads, convert faster, and improve the renter experience.
#### What makes Knock CRM different from other leasing CRMs?
Unlike generic CRMs, Knock CRM is purpose-built for multifamily operations. It connects directly with your Property Management System (PMS), incorporates AI tools for leasing automation, and integrates with other Realpage products to help you manage the entire leasing lifecycle from a single platform. Its gamified user interface for leasing teams provides a dynamic experience that boosts agent engagement, productivity, and adoption of the CRM system, turning everyday leasing activities into interactive, goal-driven workflows that keep teams motivated and performance-focused.
#### What are the key features to look for in an apartment CRM?
Key apartment CRM features include centralized lead management, automated follow-ups, tour scheduling, lead source tracking, reporting and property management software integrations. The right CRM should help leasing teams reduce manual work and improve the renter experience.
#### How does a CRM help multifamily property managers?
A multifamily and apartment CRM automates lead follow-up, centralizes communication, and gives leasing teams real-time insights into marketing and leasing performance. By managing every renter interaction in one place, property managers can reduce manual work, increase occupancy rates, and make smarter decisions about marketing spend.
#### Does Knock CRM integrate with my Property Management System (PMS)?
Yes. Knock CRM integrates with most major PMS platforms, including Realpage, Yardi, Entrata, and more, enabling seamless data sharing between leasing and back-office systems. This eliminates duplicate entry, keeps pricing and availability accurate, and ensures property staff always have the most current information.
#### How can AI Agents enhance CRM performance?
When paired with Realpage AI solutions, Knock CRM can automate up to 86% of prospective inquiries through chat, email, voice, and text1. AI ensures that leads receive fast responses while freeing up leasing teams to focus on personalized engagement and closing leases.
#### What is the ROI of using Knock CRM for property management?
Knock CRM generates strong ROI for multifamily portfolios by improving operational efficiency, maximizing lead conversion, and reducing marketing costs. Its integrated CRM suite helps teams focus on qualified leads, automate outreach, and track results in real time. The outcome is measurable: higher leasing speed, improved team productivity, and a clear lift in net operating income (NOI).
