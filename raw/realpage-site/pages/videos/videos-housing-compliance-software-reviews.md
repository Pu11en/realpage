# RealPage Affordable Streamlines Compliance and Simplifies Complexities for Mercy Housing
“Impressed” is how VP of Mercy Housing Jeff Kohler describes his experience with [RealPage OneSite Affordable software](https://www.realpage.com/affordable/). With about a dozen regulatory funding layers requiring compliance, Mercy needed two things—a software platform that could handle it all, and a collaborative team of experts to work with.
That’s why Mercy chose RealPage OneSite Affordable software and [Compliance Services](https://www.realpage.com/compliance-services/) (formerly Windsor Compliance). The combination of unified software and dedicated professionals ensures a comprehensive solution _and_ “a second set of eyes”—so everyone is on the same page and pays attention to all the details.
Thanks to this successful partnership, Mercy has drastically reduced the time it takes to approve residents for their subsidized units—and aligns perfectly with their mission to help the most vulnerable across the United States.
RealPage compliance professionals are experts at helping to mitigate risk, maximize occupancy and ensure property compliance with federal, state and local government regulations. With compliance monitoring and asset management for all property types, this full-service offering takes the burden off of Mercy staff while mitigating risk and keeping properties in line with changing regulations.
