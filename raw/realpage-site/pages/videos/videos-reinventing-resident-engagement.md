# Community Rewards Reinvents Resident Engagement
[ Contact Us ](https://www.realpage.com/videos/reinventing-resident-engagement/#form1)
#### Learn how to increase retention, improve online reputation, and drive better operational efficiency.
Community Rewards by RealPage® is specifically designed to engage residents, increase renewals, grow referrals and boost your multifamily property’s Online Reputation Assessment (ORA) score by incentivizing resident actions. Through automated campaigns, residents earn reward points redeemable for digital gift cards from $5 to $4,900, which they spend at 100 businesses nationwide, including virtual VISA and American Express. Residents can begin earning reward points from the moment they sign their lease: rent payment, lease renewal, amenity bookings, attending community events, participating in surveys and polls, submitting reviews, and more. You create the incentives—residents earn points and choose how they’ll spend them.
For more information on Community Rewards please visit <https://www.realpage.com/apartment-marketing/loyalty-reputation/>.
