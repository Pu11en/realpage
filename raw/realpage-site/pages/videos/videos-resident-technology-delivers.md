# SmartSource™ Resident Technology Delivers on New Property Vision
When Live LMC envisioned a unique, all-outsourcing approach to high-quality multifamily property development, they needed an experienced provider who could provide end-to-end services to create state-of-the-art communities nationwide—with built-in amenities to attract and satisfy today’s tech-savvy residents and a new delivery model to pay off for tech-hungry investors. Bringing the property industry and tech delivery experience together in one package, RealPage® SmartSource™ Resident Technology (SSRT) is Live LMC’s lead partner of choice to design, develop and source technology infrastructure from start to finish for more than 32 brand-new multifamily properties. Live LMC is all-in from the beginning, starting with land purchase and finishing with the final product, so balancing quality with efficiency is critical to their success. Based on Live LMC’s total outsourcing approach, Chris Acker is excited with the results of SSRT leading the technology effort with all-inclusive design services, saving the costs of 2-3 staffers and leaving no question about the economic value as a partner. Competent and skilled at what they do, SSRT brings together RealPage’s multifamily experience and the SmartSource technology depth to expertly and effectively design the full range of on-property technology services, from video and data infrastructure to audio/visual components to security and access control. SSRT’s right-sizing approach ensures they can scale the performance Live LMC needs based on their budgetary requirements, ensuring the right fit for their communities and their company.
