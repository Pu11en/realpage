# Properties Share Benefits of Modern Message Community Rewards
In this enlightening video, executives from top multifamily properties talk about the benefits of engaging residents and building loyalty with Modern Message Community Rewards. Hear managers from companies such as Lincoln Property Company, Tribridge Residential, Bell Partners, Avenue 5 Residential and Westdale Real Estate share their stories of success with Community Rewards.
For instance, Brittany Barclay, Regional Property Manager of Tribridge Residential, says, “By building a community, we’ve seen a huge increase in the amount of reviews online. They want to express how much they love our communities, and we’ve seen so many positive comments. It’s been uplifting for our teams to see, too.”
As the multifamily industry’s only resident engagement and loyalty platform, Community Rewards empowers properties to connect with and incentivize residents, offering digital reward points for interactions such as contests, surveys, online rent payments, and more.
