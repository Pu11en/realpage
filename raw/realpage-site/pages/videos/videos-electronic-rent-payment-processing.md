# Accept Credit Card Rent Payments with ResidentDirect™
Learn how to give your residents all the rent payment options without the hassles using RealPage multifamily rent payment solutions.
Today residents have come to expect options when it comes to paying their rent. That’s where ResidentDirect and bill payment services come in. With the new ResidentDirect service offered by RealPage Payment Services, your residents can pay rent and other fees with a debit card, credit card or even with ACH accessed online through your resident portal, and they pay the service fee directly to RealPage Payments Services!
That’s right, there are no transaction fees for you to manage! Now you can give your residents the convenience they want while avoiding the painful transaction fees that come with credit card processing.
ResidentDirect is a win-win for everyone and it uses the same RealPage Payments engine that has processed over $70 billion in 2019 alone.
