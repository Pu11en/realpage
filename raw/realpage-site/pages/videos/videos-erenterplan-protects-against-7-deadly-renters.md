# eRenterPlan® Renters Insurance Helps Protect Against 7 Deadly Renters
eRenterPlan doesn’t just protect renters from themselves, but also from the mishaps of others. Offered through LeasingDesk Insurance Services, a licensed insurance agency, eRenterPlan was specifically designed to cover nearly all the ways a renter can cause damage, including:
  * Full fire coverage for all injured parties including the property management company
  * Full coverage reimbursement if renters are forced to evacuate due to a covered peril such as water or fire damage
  * Pet liability coverage
  * Full theft coverage at today’s replacement cost
  * Property damage coverage for up to 5 lessees
