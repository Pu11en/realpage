# SimpleBills Simplifies Utility Billing for You and Your Residents
Discover how you can reduce move-in stress for your residents by eliminating deposits and replacing them with a one-time online signup process with SimpleBills.
This video shows you how SimpleBills removes the burden of utility setup, management, billing and support. You’ll see how with SimpleBills, you can redefine the move-in and apartment living experience, allowing your staff to focus on what’s most important — your residents.
SimpleBills retrieves all property utility bills, runs reporting and audits for missing bills, and invoices and collects directly from residents to ensure payment to utility providers are on time — guaranteeing no provider late fees! And when you have access to your total property utility data, you can make real time decisions that can help make your communities more sustainable.
