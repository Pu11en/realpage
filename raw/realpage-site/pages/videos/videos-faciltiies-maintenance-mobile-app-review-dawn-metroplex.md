# Mobile Service Ticket App Review by Dawn Pilja
With RealPage mobile facilities app, your maintenance team can tackle resident service requests faster, for improved resident satisfaction.
Simplify your maintenance workflow by eliminating paper-based maintenance requests. By making maintenance trickets mobile, you can easily check the status of orders and know exactly when they are requested and when they are completed. Plus, emergency work orders automatically appear at the top of the request list on your team's smartphones.
As Dawn Pilja of Metroplex Inc. says, "It takes a lot of the work and worries off your staff."
