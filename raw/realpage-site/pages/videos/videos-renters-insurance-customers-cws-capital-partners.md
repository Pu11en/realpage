# Reduce Liability and Risk with Renter's Insurance
Shanna Berrien, Director of Insurance and Risk at CWS Capital Partners talks about how CWS complements their required renters insurance program with RenterProtection.
Exclusive to the multifamily industry, this supplemental renters insurance helps properties manage risk by allowing them to put limited property coverage in place in the event a resident allows his or her renters insurance policy to lapse or cancel.
RenterProtection can also be utilized as an apartment community blanket insurance policy that provides coverage limited to resident-caused damage under the named perils to as many units as necessary – either the whole portfolio or specific properties.
