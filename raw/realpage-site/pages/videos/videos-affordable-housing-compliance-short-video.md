# How to Simplify Affordable Housing Compliance
### Get Complete Management of Your Affordable Properties
OneSite Leasing and Rents Affordable is an integrated property management solution for resident management, accounts receivable, and compliance that allows you to manage your revenue opportunities more effectively. It keeps you in compliance with HUD, Tax Credit and Rural Housing regulations, as well as the 50058 Public Housing program, and at the same time allows for conventional workflow for market-rate units.
One seamless integrated system manages rent collection and features easy-to-use tools to automate and streamline:
  * Move-in process
  * Certification processing
  * Compliance reporting
  * Drive Compliance at the Site Level

Simplify your management processes by enforcing policies into daily workflows for your sites:
  * Step-by-step certification process includes an interview wizard that helps to ensure the correct questions are asked during the move-in and re-certification process
  * Fast analysis of prospect eligibility assists in determining a prospect’s qualification for a program
  * Customization of letters, notices and leases so you can be sure they fit your state and company requirements
  * Verification letters are managed though the process with complete audit trail capabilities
