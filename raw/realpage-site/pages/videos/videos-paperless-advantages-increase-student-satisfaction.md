# RealPage Student Paperless Advantages Increase Student Satisfaction
When Courtney Gordon joined Rise Real Estate as Senior Vice President, he made it his mission to transform it into a paperless office operations. By switching to RealPage® Student, Rise gained efficiency in their leasing office and saved time not having to file over 500 yearly leases by hand, giving them the ability to lease 24/7. Going paperless also meant reducing their work order handling time, allowing them to meet their goal of 24-hour completion more consistently. Not only does RealPage Student save time and money for Rise but going paperless also means their properties are environmentally friendly, a big priority for today’s conservation-conscious students. The RealPage Student team was there for every step of implementation, ensuring changes were integrated into properties smoothly across all the Rise properties. Their Client Success and Project Managers were integral to the methodical rollout process. RealPage Student completely changed Rise’s processes and helped them to become a more ecological and efficient company.
