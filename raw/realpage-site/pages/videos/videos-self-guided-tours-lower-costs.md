#### Allow prospects to tour a property—at their convenience—regardless of leasing agent schedules.
RealPage® self-guided apartment tours allow you to provide the convenient, always-on service today’s prospects demand while reducing costs and leasing more apartments.
Now prospects can tour a specific apartment, or an entire community, at their convenience without being limited by leasing agent schedules—even when your leasing office is closed.
RealPage self-guided tours can be easily supported by multiple options for access to gates, buildings and corridors, and are available for both retrofit and new developments, regardless of the technology you’re using. It’s incredibly easy to set up and it’s safe and easy for prospects to book appointments—all at a low cost.
Prospects can also view and engage with high-quality digital content throughout their tour via QR codes—tapping into helpful interactive site maps, apartment and community lifestyle information, pricing and availability…they can even access and complete an Online Leasing Application.
And with integrated ID Document Validation, you can verify the prospect’s identity prior to touring through a contactless, multi-level, government-issued ID validation process.
