# RealPage Student Leasing Helps The Preiss Company Grow and Compete
RealPage® Student On-Site Leasing delivers a true competitive edge to many student housing owners and operators, including Adam Byrley, COO of The Preiss Company (TPCO) in Raleigh, North Carolina. He describes in this video how RealPage has helped their company grow from 7,000 properties in 2006 to 37,000 in 2019. Because the student housing market is highly competitive—drawing from a finite pool of students in just one geographic area—TPCO recognized that they needed a software technology provider to help them outshine and outperform the market. Understanding that the student leasing process takes place at the student’s own pace—anytime day or night from anywhere they choose—they knew their leasing solution had to be flexible, comprehensive and easy to use. TPCO recognized On-Site as the leader in the student leasing space, so they knew RealPage Student would deliver the technology they needed to compete. By committing people, resources, time and money, RealPage has been able to help The Preiss Company maintain their competitive edge.
