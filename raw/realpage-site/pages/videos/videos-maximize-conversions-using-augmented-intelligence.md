# Maximize Conversions using NEW Augmented Intelligence – Chat and Text
Beyond _Artificial_ Intelligence, the RealPage® AI Chatbot is _Augmented_ Intelligence – a cost-effective, high-quality solution that ensures a great experience and maximizes conversion.
#### Capture and Convert Leads
The RealPage AI Chatbot is engineered to complement rather than replace human intelligence by automatically warm transferring leads to a live Contact Center agent if, at any point, the request is outside the standard capabilities of the chatbot.
#### Fully Integrated
Integrated leasing agent calendar allows a prospect to self-schedule an appointment that syncs directly with the Property Management System. The AI Chatbot also gives prospects real-time access to pricing and floorplan availability. Free up agents to spend more time with current residents and the highest-potential prospects.
