# Get Real-time Visibility to Budget and Spend
Vicky Yoo, eProcurement Systems Manager for Avalon Bay, talks about how RealPage multifamily spend management software with online procurement gives her company real-time visibility into their spending compared to budget.
With spend management software, a portfolio’s complete P.O. and approval process is automated witth real-time visibility into spending against budget. Online purchasing, through a custom catalog with approved vendors, offers the benefit of purchase consolidation and standardization for cost savings. Paperless invoice processing software reduces the cost to process invoices and speeds up cycle times for reduced late fees. Ultimately, the complete apartment purchasing solution also allows properties to manage renovation projects and track transaction documents against budgets, making it easy to stay informed..
