# RealPage Makes Electronic Rent Payment Processing Easy
[ Contact Us ](https://www.realpage.com/videos/make-electronic-rent-payment-processing-easy/#form1)
RealPage Payments can make rent week hassle-free and convenient for both your residents and staff with our electronic rent payment processing solution. Our solution helps your staff reclaim up to 16 hours per month and provides your residents with multiple options for paying rent. Residents can choose to pay via credit and debit cards, ACH, Tel IVR and eMoney orders. If residents prefer to pay via credit card, we even offer a service that allows you to offer credit cards without incurring that expensive credit card transaction cost!
We’ve even made accepting checks easier. Your staff simply scans the checks and quickly verifies that the dollar amounts have been automatically filled in and match the check image. Our payments system automatically converts the check to an electronic format and attaches it directly to the resident ledger. We’ve removed the time-consuming scanning, copying, and filing to give you more flexibility and better accuracy in rent collections.
And it doesn’t end there. RealPage [Payments](https://www.realpage.com/online-payments/) is the only payment solution that is fully integrated with [OneSite Leasing & Rents](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/), from payments to late fees and NSFs, all are automatically posted in real-time to the resident ledger.
Give your residents the ability to pay rent with ease, while your staff sees how automatic posting to the resident ledger, bulk check scanning, and no credit card transaction costs saves time and improves collections.
