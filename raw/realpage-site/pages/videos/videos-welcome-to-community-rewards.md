# Welcome to Community Rewards by RealPage
Community Rewards by RealPage® enables property owners and managers to deliver a rewarding resident experience in their multifamily communities. Residents can begin earning reward points from the moment they sign their lease for rent payment, amenity bookings, attending community events, and more. They can redeem their points via digital gift cards for over 100 brands, including Foot Locker, Express, Jiffylube, Macy's, Walmart, and Hulu.
