# RealPage EasyLMS Expands Staff Engagement and Development
When Wingate Management needed a more comprehensive, versatile solution for employee education, Meaghan Fahey recognized that RealPage EasyLMS offered all the options in one robust platform. Starting with the unique RealPage product training for their new hires, EasyLMS delivers self-guided online instruction to lay the foundation before in-person training. Industry-specific content from RealPage, along with custom coursework, keeps Wingate leasing and property management staff engaged, current, and motivated to learn new things. Most importantly, Meaghan values the capability to set up course tracks in career development for team members that want to move forward and grow with the company through promotion or a new position. With EasyLMS, she knows Wingate can give them the professional tools they need to reach their goals and drive the business forward.
For more information, visit [RealPage® Learning Management Systems](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/learning-management-system/).
