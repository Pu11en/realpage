# RealPage Business Intelligence Delivers a Clearer View of Portfolio Performance
As Southern Colorado’s largest manager of commercial and multifamily properties across the country, Griffis/Blessing, Inc. measures their success by the results they produce for their individual, corporate and institutional real estate owners. When SVP Kerry Egelston sought to replace Excel spreadsheets and manual consolidation of reporting data for a better understanding of property performance, RealPage® Business Intelligence delivered a comprehensive, flexible and integrated solution for more efficient analysis and reporting across the organization.
Along with the capability to instantly pull up customized reports, Griffis/Blessing has gained chart, graph and drill-down options across all required performance indicators — and even more data points than they envisioned. With one click of the mouse, her team can access individual site-level results or pull together multiple property reports for a complete portfolio review. Different roles have customized dashboards—from marketing to operations to maintenance—to focus on their departmental priorities, and upper-level teams can see an overview of the entire organization on demand.
Within the first 18 months of using RealPage Business Intelligence, the firm has seen performance exceed expectations with improved quarterly reporting reviews, and each quarter gets better. With more than 8,000 apartment units and over 5 million sf of non-residential space for the Griffis/Blessing team to manage, Egelston credits the success from using RealPage to their improved focus on real data and visibility into the numbers that drive real results.
