# Technology Stack Advances Prospect Marketing and Planning
#### See what the RealPage® Tech Stack can do for you
Kelly Tang, president of IEC Property Services Corporation, discusses how the RealPage Technology Stack enables precision prospect marketing and marketing planning.

IEC also is using the RealPage Technology Stack to assist in creating marketing plans for every specific asset during the entire hold of the asset.
