# RealPage® Adds Spend Control and Purchasing Visibility Across Properties
Todd Girgus has a birds-eye view of the challenges that United Apartment Group faces in controlling costs across multifamily properties in 6 states throughout the southern US. With RealPage® OpsTechnology for Spend Management, United Apartment Group has introduced vendor control, pricing consistency and real-time purchasing visibility that is the key to controlling spend throughout their entire portfolio. By standardizing their site-level operations with centralized eprocurement and approved vendor catalogs, Todd’s organization ensures that all United Apartment Group properties are purchasing from the vendors they want at prices they’ve negotiated—and achieving the strategic business results they expect.
