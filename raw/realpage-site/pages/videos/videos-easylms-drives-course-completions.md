# RealPage EasyLMS Drives 34K Course Completions in 5 Months
Introducing a new training solution for BH Management was a priority for Christi Weinstein to support significant staff growth in recent years, but she had concerns about repeating her experience with long and difficult software transitions and ineffective systems. After a smooth and easy rollout with EasyLMS, training on “The BH Way” was fully functional within 30 days of rollout, and associates across the country had completed more than 34,000 courses within 5 months—exceeding previous results over a year with other solutions. The EasyLMS platform helped standardize the property management firm’s training, processes and procedures through a modern, customized education program that builds on existing skills and strengths to provide direction, increase performance, and improve retention for their associates in different locations. Built with the RealPage team’s expertise in property management systems and solutions, EasyLMS makes it easy for property management organizations to educate and motivate employees to learn faster, achieve more results, and gain more satisfaction in their roles with the company.
