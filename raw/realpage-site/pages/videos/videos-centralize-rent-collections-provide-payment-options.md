# Centralize Rent Collections & Provide Payment Options
**Erica Reinke, VP Property Management at IRT Living** , shares her insights:
COVID-era payment plans and flexible payment schedules shouldn’t disappear even as the economy continues to improve, rent collections normalize and eviction bans expire. But relieve your on-site team of the collections burden, and instead consider forming a centralized team to focus on collections, flexible payment schedules, and assisting renters applying for rental aid programs.
