# Multifamily Rent Payment Solution Review by Oden Hughes
The ResidentDirect™ payments solution has made it even easier for Amada Phelps of Oden Hughes to[ process online credit card rent payments](https://www.realpage.com/online-payments/) because there are no transaction fees to manage.
Their residents can continue to pay rent and other fees via debit and credit cards as well as ACH through the resident portal.
With ResidentDirect, residents pay the associated service fee directly to RealPage. This means Oden Hughes no longer pays convenience fees or manages card association fees. And they can still process checks and cards on-site.
Amanda says she saw a big increase in the amount of online rent payment by credit card in just the first two months!
Using ResidentDirect, Oden Hughes now provides their residents with the flexibility and convenience they have come to expect in apartment living.
