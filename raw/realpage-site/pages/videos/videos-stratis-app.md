# STRATIS App
[ Contact Us ](https://www.realpage.com/videos/stratis-app/#form3)
The STRATIS App is connected to a central communication hub for each community that monitors the performance of every device in the building 24x7 and takes calls from residents or property staff if there is an outage. The STRATIS App enables renters to be instantly provisioned to access community and unit locks, smart devices and secure personal Wi-Fi networks anywhere on the property. The STRATIS App is licensed to the property owner on a per unit per month basis with affordable subscription fees tied to which STRATIS solutions are deployed and the number of smart devices activated in each community.
