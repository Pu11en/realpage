# Blue Ridge Completes Inspections in the App
Properties managers know having to go to different systems and steps to complete work orders consumes time that could be used completing more work orders. Gina Carter, Vice President of Operations, Blue Ridge Companies uses the RealPage Mobile Facilities App because it can allow her service staff to clock in, log their time spent on the work and complete the service request in one tool. This allows them to complete work orders sooner and be more efficient.

Blue Ridge Companies was formed in 1997 in the heart of North Carolina. Blue Ridge founders envisioned a company that could integrate all phases of real estate - development, construction and management - in order to operate more efficiently on behalf of its clients. Operating under this approach, Blue Ridge has been able to utilize creative solutions in order to solve the challenges that face our partners and clients in the industry for well over two decades.
As the Vice President of Portfolio Operations, Gina is responsible for the development and implementation of programs that promote the optimum performance of Blue Ridge’s multifamily portfolio. Gina has experience in all levels of property management, from Leasing to Systems Administration. Gina holds Certified Apartment Manager (CAM), Certified Apartment Portfolio Supervisor (CAPS) and National Apartment Leasing Professional (NALP) designations through the National Apartment Association, and is an instructor for the PTAA’s Advanced Instructor Program – Train the Trainer.
