# RealPage EasyLMS Supports Training from Onboarding to Advancement
For System Administrator Lisa Noll, employee training at Barrett & Stokely involved too much paperwork, too little visibility, and not enough learning. RealPage EasyLMS introduced online training technology with the flexibility and usability to support employee onboarding, policy compliance and career development from the same portal for all leasing and property management team members. After launching this expanded education solution, Lisa saw new hire packets going digital, required training being completed on plan, and employees pursuing extra coursework to expand their skills and knowledge in the industry. The business benefits Barrett & Stokely gained with RealPage EasyLMS went beyond eliminating manual processes and streamlining training to result in shared access to business knowledge that improved the team’s work and their individual opportunities at the company.
