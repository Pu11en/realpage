# RealPage EasyLMS Gives Employees the Tools to Succeed
Before RealPage® EasyLMS™, Sharon Ruttenbur of Lyon Living found her current learning management system lacking in real product training capabilities. With the switch to EasyLMS, Sharon and her team finally received the all-in-one product, compliance and industry training they needed in an innovative, engaging format to keep her busy team learning. EasyLMS learning track programs allow managers to prepare employees for future positions and give them the tools they need to succeed with courses they can take at any time. Managers find it easy to see who is ready to learn and advance their careers, revealing more advancement opportunities and reducing the need for outside recruitment. EasyLMS also increases retention by demonstrating the care and trust Lyon Living has for its employees. With RealPage EasyLMS, Sharon keeps her associates up-to-date on products and invests in their future.
