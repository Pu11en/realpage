# RealPage STARS and Spend Management Help Dayrise Drive Performance
Tomiko Chavis, Director of Business OPS for Dayrise Residential, uses RealPage® Spend Management to reduce costs with electronic invoicing and online vendor catalogs, and she uses RealPage STARS to reveal portfolio performance internally and against peers. She shares how the Spend Management solution has helped NOI by facilitating more cost savings, streamlining procurement processes and freeing up more time for the managers to focus on leasing. Tomiko also shares her enthusiasm for RealPage STARS, the value creation tool that helps Dayrise identify, quantify and realize opportunities to grow ROI. With RealPage STARS, Tomiko can easily identify the top performers across the Dayrise portfolio. It also reveals where their properties can improve performance, in addition to letting them know how they rank externally against their peers.
