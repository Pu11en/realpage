# RealPage Affordable Online Leasing Changes the Game
[ Contact Us ](https://www.realpage.com/videos/affordable-housing-online-leasing-improves-efficiency/#form3)
RealPage® Affordable Online Leasing is a comprehensive, end-to-end online leasing solution designed specifically for Affordable Housing. Now, you can automate the leasing process, optimize staff activities and increase digital application adoption with a digital, mobile-responsive experience that enables prospects to submit applications, pay fees, get screened and sign lease documents — from anywhere.
Qualifying Affordable Housing applicants has traditionally been paper- and staff-intensive, requiring multiple rounds of document reviews, long applicant interviews, back-and-forth between applicants and property managers, and lines out the door. Manual processing increases the likelihood of errors and missing or incomplete documents and information. RealPage Affordable Online Leasing reduces all that, so you minimize vacancy days.
