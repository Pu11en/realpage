# EasyLMS Makes Learning at WinnResidential Fast, Simple and Effective
## EasyLMS helps your staff hit the ground running
EasyLMS® provides 15-minute classes that increase employee engagement and adoption for clients like WinnResidential. According to WinnResidential Vice President Jennifer Coberth, her staff can return to their properties and truly be able to use the software and systems. And putting new hires through the EasyLMS process helped make sure that with just four hours of initial training, they could get into the system and get started faster, increasing overall efficiency.
