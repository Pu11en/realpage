What do acquisition managers look for when purchasing buildings, and what sustainability upgrades do they consider when making purchasing decisions? Join Zach Goldman, Chief Operating Officer of TGM Associates, as he talks about the one thing he always looks for and how you can get a quick read on opportunities.
He’ll also give his take on what efficiency really means—in terms of energy as well as your team’s time and your residents’ experience.
