Join RealPage®’s Carl Whitaker as he sits down with Michael Bull, CEO of Bull Realty, to talk about regional opportunities and investment trends he’s anticipating in 2024, keys to surviving and thriving through unavoidable market swings and what he’s learned over the years as the host of the nation’s leading podcast on commercial real estate.
### Carl Whitaker
##### Vice President & Chief Economist
##### RealPage, Inc.
Carl Whitaker is RealPage’s Chief Economist, leading the company's team of economists and market analysts. Carl and the Market Intelligence team specialize in analyzing and disseminating economic and market trends for the rental housing industry. He primarily focuses on presenting trends in the rental housing industry to a wide audience, including investors, asset managers, operations professionals and media, among other personas. Carl has been cited in the Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg and GlobeSt and is a frequent keynote speaker for industry events. Carl holds degrees from the University of North Texas, including a Master of Science in Applied Geography.
### Michael Bull
##### CEO
##### Bull Realty
Michael Bull, CEO, Bull Realty/Host, _America’s Commercial Real Estate Show_
Bio: Michael Bull founded Bull Realty 26 years ago with the intention of creating a company of advisors known for integrity and the most effective disposition marketing in the nation. His now full-service firm has teams focused in each property sector providing disposition, acquisition, project leasing, tenant representation and consulting. Michael personally leads the office investment sales division.
Michael also hosts the nation’s leading show on commercial real estate topics – _America’s Commercial Real Estate Show_. Every week since 2010 economists, analysts & industry leaders, including Michael, share market intel, forecasts & strategies. The show was on 60 radio stations around the country.
Michael holds a degree in Business from Georgia State University and is an avid enthusiast of high-performance boating.
