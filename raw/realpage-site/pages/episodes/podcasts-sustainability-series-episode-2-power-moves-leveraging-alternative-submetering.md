Alternative submetering can reduce resident utility consumption between 15% - 29%. California has implemented submeter mandates statewide and the number of cities following suit across the country continues to multiply year over year. It may not remain an alternative for long and what you don’t know about submetering could cost you thousands in expenses and penalties for non-compliance for those cities where it is mandated. Join experts from the leading provider of multifamily submeter services as they discuss:
  * How to implement submetering
  * Maximizing savings with benchmarking and cost recovery
  * Successful maintenance practices
  * How to stay in compliance
