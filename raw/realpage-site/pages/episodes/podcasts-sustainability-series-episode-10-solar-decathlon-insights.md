Want to know more about the future of sustainability? Find out what one team learned when they competed in the U.S. Department of Energy Solar Decathlon®. It’s a collegiate competition that inspires student teams to design and build highly efficient buildings powered by renewables while optimizing for key considerations, including affordability, resilience and occupant health.
Meet the winners of the 2020 Solar Decathlon Design Challenge, Mixed-Use Multifamily Division—a team from The University of Arizona—and hear their ideas on community, affordability and sustainability.
