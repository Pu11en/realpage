Join Mary Nitschke as she introduces the RealPage Sustainability Success Series. Sustainability is a growing trend in multifamily and there is a thirst for information on this topic. This series was created to fill that information void by presenting timely content addressing the sustainability related topics you want to know more about. You’ll hear from industry thought leaders and practitioners as they describe the latest innovations and evolving best practices in this exciting market segment. In this episode Mary provides an overview of what you can expect from the series and shares the 12 things you can do right now to gain control over your utilities and promote sustainability.
### Mary Nitschke
##### Vice President, Sustainability
##### RealPage, Inc.
Mary Nitschke joined RealPage as Vice President of Sustainability in 2019. Previously she was the Director of Ancillary Services for the award-winning Prometheus Real Estate Group, Inc, where she had the privilege of managing both the resident costs and expense sides of the utility wheelhouse.
Mary is passionate about utilities, and should perhaps switch to decaf. She was the first president of the Utility Management Advisory board, possesses an Energy Resource Management Certificate from UC Davis, and two Bachelors of Art from UC Berkeley.
