2020 has been a challenging year—one that has changed the patterns of utility consumption in the Multifamily industry.
Join Craig Haglund, Program Manager for the EPA, for insight into the ways COVID-19 has changed reporting, as well as the benefits of reporting annually and what owners and operators should expect going forward.
### Mary Nitschke
##### Vice President, Sustainability
##### RealPage, Inc.
Mary Nitschke joined RealPage as Vice President of Sustainability in 2019. Previously she was the Director of Ancillary Services for the award-winning Prometheus Real Estate Group, Inc, where she had the privilege of managing both the resident costs and expense sides of the utility wheelhouse.
