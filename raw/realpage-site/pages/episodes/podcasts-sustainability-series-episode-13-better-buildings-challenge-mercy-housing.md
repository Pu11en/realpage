Earlier in the series, we talked about The Better Buildings Challenge, which involves a voluntary commitment to reduce portfolio-wide energy consumption by 20% within 10 years. In June, the DOE and HUD recognized Mercy Housing for Energy Efficiency Leadership.
Join Caitlin Rood, National Environmental Sustainability Director for Mercy Housing, for a discussion about her work and her company’s success with the Better Buildings Challenge. Plus, get insight into:
  * How Mercy achieved this goal
  * How participation in the Better Buildings Challenge has benefitted Mercy
  * What Mercy Housing learned from the process

### Mary Nitschke
