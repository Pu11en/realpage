How can you keep the day-to-day activity of the Multifamily Industry from derailing your long-term goals? Western Wealth’s Jennifer Staciokis says it comes down to embracing the importance of stillness in daily life. Hear how she uses it to slow down, reflect and thoughtfully strategize — and how it helps her personally and professionally.
### Andrew Bowen
##### Vice President & Industry Principal
##### RealPage, Inc.
Andrew Bowen serves RealPage customers and partners as a Vice President and Industry Principal dedicated to the Institutional and Build-to-Rent spaces. For over a decade, his focus has been on the tools that help investors and operators of multifamily real estate drive yield through better leveraging data and analytics.
Considered one of the experts on revenue management in multifamily, he is a regularly featured speaker on both the webcast and conference circuit, bringing a passion backed by over 20 years of operational experience to the conversation.
Andrew holds a Bachelor of Science in Aquatic Biology from the University of California at Santa Barbara. When not pontificating on the future of the industry, you can usually find him either in the gym or on the lake with his two grown “boys” and his wife of 25 years.
### Jennifer Staciokis
##### President
##### Western Wealth Communities
Jennifer Staciokis joined Western Wealth Capital to launch their in-house property management company, Western Wealth Communities, in 2020. Jennifer was responsible for building out the property management platform and hiring the team to support the growing business of over 15,000 units. In 2023, she broadened her role to include strategic direction of Western Wealth Capital’s people and technology departments in addition to her existing oversight of operations, property marketing, training, revenue management, human resources and technology for Western Wealth Communities.
Beginning her career in the multifamily industry nearly 25 years ago, Jennifer has served at two of the nation’s largest property management firms focusing on all areas of property performance.
Jennifer received a Bachelor of Arts in French from West Chester University and a Master of Arts in International Commerce & Policy from George Mason University. Jennifer is a regular guest speaker, facilitator and moderator at many of the industry’s most renowned conferences as well as an active participant in NAA, NMHC and AZCREW. She gives her time to various industry advisory boards such as Zillow Rentals, Knock, LeaseLock, Shelters to Shutters (Phoenix Chapter), Nurture Boss and Connexus Living. Jennifer is recognized among the GlobeSt. Women of Influence (2016, 2021), the AMA Tributes Executive of the Year (2022) and the 2023 AZ Big Media Women Leaders.
Learn more about Western Wealth Communities at <https://www.westernwealthcapital.com/western-wealth-communities/>
