Keeping up with the highly regulated environment of utilities can be challenging without robust legal resources. Making a wrong turn as you try to navigate this complicated terrain can have disastrous results. Join us as industry experts who have advised the most successful owners and operators in multifamily discuss:
  * Critical insights on the current legislative environment
  * An examination of rent control highlighting New York, California and Oregon
  * Practical and cost-effective measures you can take to stay in compliance

### Mary Nitschke
##### Vice President, Sustainability
##### RealPage, Inc.
Mary Nitschke joined RealPage as Vice President of Sustainability in 2019. Previously she was the Director of Ancillary Services for the award-winning Prometheus Real Estate Group, Inc, where she had the privilege of managing both the resident costs and expense sides of the utility wheelhouse.
Mary is passionate about utilities, and should perhaps switch to decaf. She was the first president of the Utility Management Advisory board, possesses an Energy Resource Management Certificate from UC Davis, and two Bachelors of Art from UC Berkeley.
