Are your smart apartments smart enough to lower investment and operating expenses, reduce outages, increase efficiency, enable rate optimization, optimize conservation activity and drive strategic initiatives? The answer to this question will vary according to the capabilities of your building management system (BMS). In this webcast, industry experts will discuss the advancements in smart apartment technology and its wholistic management, and how the most innovative of these technologies positively impact the factors that matter most to investors, owners and operators including:
  * Strategic capital planning
  * Predictive maintenance
  * Energy management and sustainability
  * Monitoring as a Service (MaaS)
