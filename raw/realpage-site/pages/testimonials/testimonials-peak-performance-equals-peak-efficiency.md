# Peak Performance Equals Peak Efficiency at Winn Residential
Jennifer Coberth, Vice President of Management Operation Software for Winn Residential, talks about how Affordable Housing property managers need to know much more about residents than other multifamily property managers do because they must maintain compliance with government regulations.
