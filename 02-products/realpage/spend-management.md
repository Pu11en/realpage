# RealPage Spend Management Software

**What it is:** This software helps automate and centralize processes for purchasing, invoicing, and managing vendors with Lumina-powered guidance. It provides real-time visibility into spending against a budget and streamlines activities.
**Who it's for:** It is for properties, management companies, and multifamily operators managing their portfolio's purchasing, invoicing, and vendor activities.
**Also called:** Realpage Source to Pay, Spend Management Suite

**Parts of it:**
- OpsBuyer copilot
- Vendor Credentialing
- Mobile App for Approvals
- AI Spend Agent
- OpsBuyer
- OpsInvoice

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Uses Lumina-powered guidance.
- Realpage Source to Pay enables scaling operational efficiencies across a portfolio.
- AI Spend Agent validates approved invoices against preferred vendors, purchase-order and receipt-of-goods requirements, budget, and contract terms.
- AI Spend Agent surfaces potential compliance exceptions, purchasing patterns, and savings opportunities.
