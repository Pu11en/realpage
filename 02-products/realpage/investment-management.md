# RealPage Realpage Investment Management

**What it is:** It provides an end-to-end suite of front-office, data-driven software and services for real estate investment management. It uses AI to ensure data quality, offering portfolio-wide visibility and enhancing investor interactions for informed decision-making.
**Who it's for:** Real Estate Fund/Portfolio Managers, Real Estate Asset Managers, and Real Estate Investment Firms/Syndicators, including growth-focused firms managing multi-asset class portfolios (e.g., commercial, multifamily, hotels).

**Parts of it:**
- Data Management Gateway
- Data Management Services (DMS)
- Asset Investment Management (AIM)
- Investment Accounting
- Investor Management Services (IMS)
- Financial Modeling

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Saves hundreds of hours annually in manual data collection, normalization, and analysis.
- Compatible with major property management and accounting systems.
- The Data Quality dashboard measures data quality across three dimensions: Consistency, Completeness, and Timeliness.
- Scales across asset types and strategies.
