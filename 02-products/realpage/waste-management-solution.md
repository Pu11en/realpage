# RealPage Multifamily Smart Waste Management Solution

**What it is:** This is a camera and AI-based waste management tool for multifamily properties. It helps optimize waste pickups and reduce costs by assessing dumpster fullness, capacity, content, and contamination levels.
**Who it's for:** Multifamily properties and apartment properties.
**Also called:** Smart Waste

**Parts of it:**
- Contamination Alerts
- Fullness Optimization
- Built-In Compliance
- Performance Analytics
- Sustainability Impact

**What RealPage says about it:**
- cuts costs for managing waste up to 40%
- reduces trash contamination fees by up to 80%
- saves up to $1800 per dumpster per year
- first cloud-based platform capable of assessing the location of each dumpster on a property
