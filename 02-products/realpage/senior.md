# RealPage Realpage Senior Living Software

**What it is:** Realpage Senior Living property management software helps manage senior properties, from prospect management to payment collections, resident screening, and real-time reporting. It is designed to increase staff efficiency, productivity, and resident satisfaction across various senior living facility types.
**Who it's for:** It is for operators of assisted living, independent living, memory care, and dementia care facilities.
**Also called:** Senior Living Software, Realpage Senior Living

**Parts of it:**
- Boost Leads
- Care Management
- Community Management
- Dedicated Contact Center
- Accurate Census Management
- Incident Management

**What RealPage says about it:**
- The dedicated contact center is the largest in the U.S.
- The software provides an integrated lead-generation platform.
- It accesses real-time medication information and alerts through an interface with an eMAR.
- It includes automatic calculation of care fee charges based on the acuity score of assessments.
