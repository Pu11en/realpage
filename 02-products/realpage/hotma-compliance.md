# RealPage OneSite

**What it is:** It provides updates to help customers comply with the Housing Opportunity Through Modernization Act (HOTMA) and TRACS 2.0.3.A requirements. It integrates HOTMA rules and calculations for various housing programs.
**Who it's for:** Multifamily Housing (MFH) owners, and customers managing Tax Credits, HUD Project Based programs, Public and Indian Housing systems, and Rural Development (USDA) programs.

**Parts of it:**
- OneSite Affordable
- OneSite Tax Credits
- HUD Project Based programs (TRACS 2.0.3.A)
- Public and Indian Housing systems
- Rural Development (USDA) updates
- Tenant Income Certification forms

**What RealPage says about it:**
- OneSite Affordable has been updated to meet TRACS 2.0.3.A/HOTMA requirements.
- HOTMA rules have been enabled for all Tax Credit programs in OneSite Tax Credits.
- HOTMA values and calculations for Rural Development (USDA) were implemented on July 1st, 2025.
- Updates are set to be released in line with HUD's deadlines for HUD Project Based programs and Public and Indian Housing systems.
