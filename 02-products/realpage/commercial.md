# RealPage Realpage Commercial Property Management Software

**What it is:** Realpage Commercial Property Management Software is a fully integrated platform engineered to drive transparency and efficiency across the entire asset lifecycle. It provides a singular system for managing back-office accounting to front-office tenant services.
**Who it's for:** It is for owners, managers, and developers managing Industrial, Office, and Retail properties, from a single asset to diverse multi-property portfolios.

**Parts of it:**
- Centralized commercial lease and tenant management
- Streamlined accounting and financial automation
- Advanced Lease Administration Software
- Realpage Tenant Portal
- Realpage Financial Suite
- Realpage Commercial Property Reporting

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Provides a secure, 24/7 platform for tenants.
- Systematically manages CAM reconciliations, CPI-based escalations, and percentage rent calculations.
- Integrates property, corporate, and investment accounting in one system.
- Can be configured to meet unique demands of Industrial, Office, and Retail asset classes.
