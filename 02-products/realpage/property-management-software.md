# RealPage OneSite Property Management Software

**What it is:** OneSite is an industry-leading property management system designed to maximize operational efficiency, empower decision-making, and deliver NOI growth. It consolidates leasing, maintenance, accounting, and operations into a centralized platform with AI-enabled workflows.
**Who it's for:** It is for multifamily owners and operators, property management companies, onsite teams, regional managers, centralized operations teams, and portfolio leaders. It supports conventional, student, affordable, tax credit, military, and senior living properties.

**Parts of it:**

- OneSite Core Property Operations
- AI-powered automation
- Lumina AI copilot
- Maintenance Management Visibility
- Role-based dashboards
