# RealPage Affordable Housing Software

**What it is:** This software simplifies compliance, reduces expenses, and optimizes efficiency for affordable housing operations. It makes it easier to process move-ins, certifications, and manage compliance requirements.
**Who it's for:** It is for staff and management of affordable housing properties and portfolios. This includes those managing low-income housing tax credit (LIHTC), HUD, Rural Housing, Section 50058 Public Housing, Section 8, HOME, and tax-exempt bond programs.
**Also called:** OneSite® Affordable, ActiveBuilding®

**Parts of it:**
- Realpage Affordable Housing Property Management Solutions
- Affordable Housing Online Leasing
- Affordable Housing Screening
- LOFT™
- Business Intelligence
- Managed Services

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Utilizes the industry’s largest lease transaction database spanning 13.5 million units.
- 1 million Affordable units rely on Realpage Affordable Housing Screening.
- OneSite® Affordable is at the core of the property management solutions.
- Affordable Housing Screening seamlessly integrates with Affordable Online Leasing.
