# RealPage Realpage® Contact Center

**What it is:** The Realpage Contact Center is a 24/7 leasing solution that acts as an extension of a property's leasing and marketing team. It provides a customized prospect experience and aims to generate more visits and leases per call.
**Who it's for:** It is for leasing and marketing teams and maintenance personnel at apartment properties.

**Parts of it:**
- Contact Center All Calls and Emails
- Contact Center Missed Calls and Emails
- Chat and Text
- Answer Automation
- Instant Call Reply
- Contact Center for Maintenance

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Generates up to 20% more visits and leases per call.
- 40% of leads resulted in scheduled on-site visits.
- 88% of calls are answered in 25 seconds or less.
- The new Answer Automation 2.0 platform is now live.
