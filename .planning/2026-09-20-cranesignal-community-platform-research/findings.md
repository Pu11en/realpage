# Findings & Decisions

## Requirements
- Research public posting targets for CraneSignal.
- Focus audience: people selling into apartment owners, property managers, multifamily operators, apartment investors, proptech founders, multifamily service providers.
- Exclude DMs, private outreach, private Slack groups, and email blasts.
- Communities and sections should be separate from platforms.

## Research Findings
- Multi-Housing News Viewpoint has strong audience fit because its editorial guidelines name multifamily brokers, owners, investors, developers, and managers. Risk: it does not accept product-vendor submissions and requires company-neutral 800 to 1,000 word pieces.
- Multifamily Dive covers development, operations, regulation, tech, transactions, and multifamily industry news. Good for opinion, tech, press release, or event/resource promotion, but not a casual community post.
- Multifamily Executive reaches top multifamily decision makers including owners, developers, and managers. Good as a thought-leadership/media target, not first organic post.
- BiggerPockets forums have public real estate investing categories including Landlording & Rental Properties, Managing Your Property, Real Estate Technology, and Multifamily. Strong for discussion-first posts.
- Reddit r/realestateinvesting has a monthly self-promotion thread and multifamily/property-management discussions. Use only allowed threads for links.
- Reddit r/PptyMgmtSoftware is small but directly relevant to property-management software, landlords, PMs, and founders. Good for transparent feedback posts.
- Reddit r/PropertyManagement has the exact audience, but rules ban self-promo, software ads, data farming, and direct software discussion. Use mainly for listening or no-link operational questions.
- Reddit r/CommercialRealEstate includes commercial real estate operators and asset managers, but rules ban self-promotion and advertising. Use mainly for listening or no-link industry questions.
- CREtech reaches proptech vendors, owners/operators, brokers, investors, and innovation leaders. Better for proptech/AI market angle than apartment-operator feedback.
- Substack and LinkedIn support public newsletter/article style posting and can hold the reusable long-form version.
- Background research also identified MultifamilyBiz, GlobeSt, and PropTechBuzz as secondary candidates. MultifamilyBiz and GlobeSt made the card. PropTechBuzz is plausible but needs sign-up/public visibility checks before adding to the primary list.
- Best practical order: Multifamily Insiders, BiggerPockets, CraneSignal Blog, LinkedIn, then editorial/publication pitches, then careful Reddit tests.

## Technical Decisions
| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Make a concise ranked Markdown deliverable | Drew needs a clear posting map, not a giant source dump. |
| Include rule risk per target | Some exact-audience places are dangerous if CraneSignal sounds promotional. |
| Keep Facebook groups out of the first ranked list | Search evidence was weaker and many groups are private or approval based. |

## Issues Encountered
| Issue | Resolution |
|-------|------------|
| Reddit pages expose useful current rules but posting norms vary by thread | Treat Reddit as high-risk and require rule checks before each post. |
