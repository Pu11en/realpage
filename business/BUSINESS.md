# CraneSignal — Business

## What we sell

CraneSignal is a free early-access lead finder for anyone selling to apartment owners.

**The core insight:** Most apartment buildings run property-management software.
When a building changes ownership, is newly constructed, or is under new management,
they're more likely to switch software. The buildings you want to reach are the ones
your competitors haven't already sold to.

CraneSignal shows you which buildings run which software, which ones just changed hands
or ownership structure, and why each building is a good lead — with sources linked
to public filings, news, and SEC documents.

## Who buys

- **Property-management software companies** (Yardi, Entrata, AppFolio, RealPage, and others)
  selling to new markets or chasing defection opportunities
- **Service providers** (marketing, HR, accounting, payroll) selling to multi-unit properties
- **Commercial real-estate firms** finding tenants or landlords to pitch
- **Franchisees** (small chains, regional operators) looking for local expansion targets

## The business model

All data is **free with no paywall**. The offer is early access and feedback in exchange
for telling us what works and what doesn't. Revenue comes from the companies that use it.

**First customers** are property-management software companies who want:
- Early, evidence-backed leads about which buildings might switch software
- A finished, working product they can use on day one
- Public-source transparency (no insiders, no stolen data, no gray zones)

**Next wave** are service providers and real-estate firms who want to find and filter
multi-unit properties by installed software, transaction history, or construction stage.

## Why it works

1. **Outside-in only** — everything comes from public sources. No proprietary sales data,
   no product logins, no customer databases, no insider information. That makes it
   bulletproof and defensible.
2. **Sources everywhere** — every claim links back to the original web page, SEC filing,
   or news article. Customers can verify and trust the data.
3. **One finished product** — the website works now. No "coming soon", no betas,
   no "sign up for early access and we'll email you in 6 months". It runs locally,
   maps every area, and answers questions with sources.

## What's hard

- **Finding the owners** — most apartment building websites don't list who owns the property.
  We scrape USPS/Google/Zillow/Census records and cross-reference ownership filings.
- **Detecting software** — we can't log into every building's management interface. We look at
  job listings, news articles, public filings, vendor case studies, and domain registration data.
- **Scoring leads** — which buildings are actually likely to switch? We surface recent changes
  (new ownership, construction, management company change) and let customers decide what matters.

## Growth plan

**Phase 1 (now):** Free MVP showing it works. Customers use it to find leads. Feedback tells
us what to build next.

**Phase 2 (later):** Once we know which features save customers time and money, premium features
for teams (saved searches, lead alerts, bulk export, API access, or team collaboration).

**Phase 3 (year 2):** Deeper data (maintenance alerts, financing changes, board turnover)
and targeting (API for third-party tools, Slack integration, CRM sync).
