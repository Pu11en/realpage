Apartment buildings that are about to need something

CraneSignal tracks 1,702 apartment buildings across Texas — 263,462 units — that are planned, permitted, under construction, leasing, or have just changed owner. It is for people who sell to apartment owners rather than to renters: the question it answers is which buildings are about to need something, and why now. Updated Sep 26, 2026.

By stage today: 190 under construction, 522 permitted, 190 planned, 1 leasing, 799 sold. 122 have an expected opening date this year or later and 794 have a recorded sale — the two moments when a building reviews its software, its suppliers and its service contracts.

What each building carries

Where the public record says so: name, street address, city, unit count, stage, permit date, expected opening date, sale date, buyer, developer and office phone — 221 of the 1,702 have a published office number. Every row names the public record it came from. A blank cell means that record is silent, never that we guessed, and nothing here is estimated or modelled.

Where it comes from

State and city construction records, county appraisal-district sale records, and public announcements. No proprietary feeds, no purchased lists, no logins. Some source links open a building’s own record; most open the county or city source it was published in, where the address finds it.
