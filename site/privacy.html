Privacy Policy

PropertyStack · Last updated September 12, 2026

PropertyStack shows public information about apartment buildings and the software they run, plus an AI assistant you can ask about it. This page explains what we collect when you use it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics when you sign in: your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to sign you in and keep your chats yours.
  • Your chats: the questions you ask and the answers you get, so you can come back to them.
  • Basic usage counts per account (how many questions, rough cost), used only to apply fair-use limits.

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Who else sees it

  • Your questions are sent to an AI model provider (DeepSeek) to generate answers.
  • When you ask the assistant to research a building, it sends search words and public web addresses to a web-reading service (Jina AI). Your name and email are not sent.
  • The site runs on Railway, which hosts the servers and stored data.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

The building data

Building, owner and software information comes from public sources such as county appraisal records, city filings and the buildings' own public websites. Each fact links to its source.

Deleting your data

You can delete any chat inside the chat app. To delete your account and all your chats, contact us at the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
