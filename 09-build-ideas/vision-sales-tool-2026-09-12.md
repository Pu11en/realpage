# Drew's vision, captured 2026-09-12 (from voice note) — not a plan yet

**Who it's for:** salespeople who need to call or email the right apartment building fast.

**What it is:** one main page = Early Leads (the call list, ranked) + the chat. Master Table
probably folds into Early Leads; nobody needs a separate giant table. Drew runs the
finding-buildings flow for an area, then the sales team works the list.

**The chat (signed in with Google):**
- answers questions about the leads and data, with sources;
- makes things they can download: PDFs, one-pagers, call sheets, lists;
- maybe later: drives the website ("show me Plano leads sorted by size") — advanced, later;
- must NOT change the data.

**Production-grade means:** a new user who has never seen it understands what they're
looking at and why (onboarding copy, empty states, examples), nothing breaks, works on a
phone reasonably (phone is second priority).

**Open questions for a planning session:**
1. What exactly should the agent be able to make/download? (PDF, CSV, email draft?)
2. What should a brand-new salesperson see first?
3. Find 1–2 real products online that do this well, to copy the layout.
4. What does it cost to run per user (model calls) at sales-team scale?
