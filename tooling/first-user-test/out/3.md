# Persona 3

You are Priya, a marketing lead at a package-locker company, on your PHONE, very busy. Your goal: get a list of leads for Houston you can share with your team.

## Pages visited
https://cranesignal.com
https://cranesignal.com/
https://app.cranesignal.com/index.html
https://app.cranesignal.com/index.html?region=Houston

## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

STEPS:
1. Landed on cranesignal.com. Saw a hero offering a "Free Lead Pack: download every lead in your area as a PDF", with a sample lead for Richardson, TX (Lead #1 of 10) and two CTAs: "Start free →" and "or book a 15 minute call".
2. Scrolled down through the page and read the "We dig. You call." service sections and sample lead tables, including a sample "Your call list. Sample data · Austin, TX metro" table.
3. Scrolled back to the top to find the CTA again.
4. Clicked "Start free →". It opened a new tab to app.cranesignal.com (CraneSignal -- Early Leads), a lead database with region filters: All Texas 592, Dallas-Fort Worth 307, Houston 69, Austin 84, San Antonio 41, Rest of Texas 91.
5. Clicked the "Houston (69)" filter; the URL changed to app.cranesignal.com/index.html?region=Houston and the table updated to Houston leads. Seen Houston-area leads included: "Groves Apartments" (Humble, 336 units, under construction, opens 2026-09-30, software not chosen yet), "Pelican Pearland Town Center Multifamily" (Pearland, opens 2027-01-15, software not picked yet), and "The Oaks At Carrell Street" — each row with a "Get contact →" button. The page header showed "Data from Sep 15, 2026".
6. Clicked "Download Lead Pack (PDF)". No PDF was produced. Instead an "Ask CraneSignal" chat panel opened with the gated message: "Make a free account to download your Lead Pack — A small window opens. Use Google or your email. It closes by itself and the chat appears here." with buttons "Make a free account" and "Already have one? Sign in".
7. I did NOT sign up. I stopped at the account wall.

CONFUSING:
- "Free Lead Pack: download every lead in your area as a PDF" vs later being told to "Make a free account to download your Lead Pack" — the word "free" made me unsure whether an account was required.
- "A small window opens. Use Google or your email. It closes by itself and the chat appears here." — I didn't understand whether this was a signup window or a chat; it was unclear what would happen next.
- The sample shown on the landing page (Richardson, TX) vs the "Austin, TX metro" sample table vs my needing Houston — I was unsure whether "your area" was auto-detected or something I'd have to choose.
- "Software not chosen yet" / "Not chosen yet" column value — I wasn't sure if this meant the building's property-management software wasn't selected yet (i.e., a buying signal).

MISSING:
- A visible way to get Houston leads WITHOUT creating an account. The download was fully gated behind "Make a free account".
- Clear upfront messaging that an account is required (it's only revealed at the download step).
- Any pricing / what "Start free" actually includes without an account.

FIRST 5 SECONDS:
A lead-generation tool that gives sales people (like me, selling to package-locker / multifamily buildings) a downloadable list of prospective buildings/leads in my area — nearly free, PDF-based, for busy people.

WOULD SIGN UP: No. I'm a first-time visitor who's never heard of CraneSignal, I don't have an account, and I'm on my phone and very busy. Hiding the download behind a mandatory signup before I can see any real value is a trust blocker — I understand why (capturing my email as a lead), but I wouldn't hand over my details on the first visit without seeing the actual Houston list first. If the site let me preview a few Houston leads and then asked me to sign up for the full pack, I probably would.

SCORE: 6/10. The value prop was clear and the Houston filter worked instantly (69 leads, contact buttons per row), which is genuinely useful and fast on mobile. Points off because the promised "free" PDF turned out to be gated behind a signup that wasn't mentioned until the last moment, and the account prompt appeared as a chat-panel pop-over I found confusing rather than a clean, obvious signup flow.
