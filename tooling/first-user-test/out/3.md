GOAL REACHED: partly

CONFUSING:
- "Leads for companies that sell to apartment buildings" — took a beat to realize this is a lead-gen tool FOR vendors, not an apartment listings site.
- "A free account unlocks who to call, the agent and your Lead Pack PDF" — unclear what "the agent" is exactly (an AI calling agent?) before signing up; I never found out.
- "Lead Pack PDF" — no explanation of what's inside or how big it is.
- The "Get contact →" button was ambiguous — I expected a phone number to appear, not a sign-up wall.
- Region labels mixed cities ("Houston") with suburbs (Humble, Pearland, Tomball, Webster, Cypress) — as a Houston lead list they're all in metro Houston, but that wasn't spelled out.
- The phone numbers listed next to each building look like generic office/project record numbers (several repeat, e.g. 512-220-7816 appears twice) — unclear if these are the real decision-maker contacts or just permit filings.

MISSING:
- No Houston sample lead on the landing page (samples were Richardson and Austin).
- No clear explanation of what "the agent" and "Lead Pack PDF" actually do/what's in them.
- No pricing page or explanation of what happens after early access / what a paid tier costs.
- No indication until I clicked that the actual "who to call" was gated; the early leads page shows enough to look free.
- No way to export/save the lead list (e.g. CSV) without an account.
- No direct link from the landing page to the app or the leads table.

FIRST 5 SECONDS:
"This is a lead-gen service that finds companies selling to apartment buildings, showing me buildings that are being permitted/built so I can pitch them early." The headline and the sample lead cards made that clear almost instantly, though it looked more like an Austin/Richardson tool until I found the region filter.

WOULD SIGN UP: no (in this session) — the task said not to sign up, and I'm a busy first-timer. Would I understand why? Yes: the free account is what unlocks the contact names and the Lead Pack PDF, and it's free with no card, so the ask is fair and low-risk. Honestly, if I actually wanted the contacts I would likely sign up — it's quick and free — but I'd want to know what "the agent" and the "Lead Pack PDF" give me first. If I were evaluating for my team, the visible 69 Houston leads alone were enough to convince me it's worth a look.

SCORE: 7/10 — landing page communicated the value fast and the Houston filter worked in one click, but the contact gate was a surprise, the phone numbers shown aren't obviously the real contacts, and there's no explanation of "the agent"/"Lead Pack PDF" before the wall.
