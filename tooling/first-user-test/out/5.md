## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

STEPS:
1. Landed on cranesignal.com. Saw a hero, a sample lead (Richardson, TX), and testimonials. It looked like a lead-gen tool for companies selling to apartment buildings.
2. Scrolled the landing page. Read "Your call list" (sample lead Austin, TX) and "Built for property management sales." It gives call lists of apartment buildings based on public records/permits. No Tulsa mention.
3. Searched the page for "Tulsa" — not found. Scrolled to the bottom; found CTAs "See the leads free", "Browse every lead free", "Pick your state and region", and "Don't see your area? Ask and we'll add it."
4. Clicked "See the leads free" and it opened the leads app (app.cranesignal.com) in a new tab.
5. On the leads page I saw coverage: Texas (592 leads) and Arizona only — every region listed was Texas. A note said "A free account unlocks who to call, the agent and your Lead Pack."
6. Searched that page for "Tulsa", "Oklahoma", "Oklahoma City" — 0 matches. So Tulsa is NOT covered.
7. Clicked "Ask for it" (the "Don't see yours? Ask for it" option), which took me to a request-area page with a US map and a form: "Tell us the city or county and we'll add its buildings."
8. Filled the area field with "Tulsa, OK" and clicked "Ask for it."
9. Page confirmed: "Got it. We'll add Tulsa, OK." A chat panel opened offering a free account (I did not sign up).

CONFUSING:
- "Span 1" / unlabeled click targets: my first two attempts to click "Browse every lead free" and "See the leads free" hit an unlabeled element ("Clicked span 1") and did nothing — I was unsure what I had actually clicked.
- "Early Leads" and "Under the Hood" nav labels — I didn't immediately know what they contained.
- "Lead Pack" — not defined anywhere I could see; I assumed it's a bundle of leads.
- "The states we cover so far" with grey vs. highlighted states on the map — no legend explaining the shading.
- "A free account unlocks who to call, the agent and your Lead Pack" — "the agent" was unclear (contact person for the building?).

MISSING:
- Any mention of Tulsa or Oklahoma anywhere on the site.
- A clear coverage/markets list on the landing page (I had to dig into the app to find Texas + Arizona only).
- Pricing or what a "Lead Pack" actually contains.
- A visible "available in your state" search or state/region selector on the landing page itself.

FIRST 5 SECONDS: I thought it was a lead-generation / sales-intelligence tool that hands you call lists of apartment buildings (with software detected) so you can sell to property managers.

WOULD SIGN UP: No. It's a free account that unlocks the actual contact info, but it only covers Texas and Arizona — neither is my market (Tulsa, OK), so an account would give me nothing actionable. Instead I used the "Ask for it" form to request Tulsa, OK; the site confirmed "Got it. We'll add Tulsa, OK." That's the actionable path for a first-time visitor in an uncovered market. I'd reconsider signing up once Tulsa is added (I left no email, so I'll need to check back).

SCORE: 6/10 — the purpose was clear and the ask/language was friendly, but the missing-coverage situation wasn't obvious from the landing page (no Tulsa/Oklahoma signal, no coverage list), and two CTA clicks failed on unlabeled elements before I reached the right one.
