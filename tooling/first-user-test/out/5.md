## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

STEPS:
1. Landed on cranesignal.com (first-time visitor, no account). Above the fold it presented itself as a "lead" source for "companies selling to apartment buildings," showing an example lead (an apartment building in Richardson, TX).
2. Scrolled down and read the whole landing page: testimonials from people selling to apartments, a sample data table (3 of 14 rows in an Austin, TX metro), a "Sample Lead Pack" for Dallas–Fort Worth, and a CTA for "free early access, no card needed."
3. Clicked "See the leads free" — it opened a new tab into the actual leads app (app.cranesignal.com).
4. In the leads app I could only find leads for two states: Texas and Arizona. Tulsa, Oklahoma was not there. The page said grey states "aren't covered yet" and offered an "Ask for it"/"ask for yours" link.
5. Clicked "Ask for it" → went to the map page with a "Don't see your area?" form. A chat/sign-in panel popped up over it; I closed it ("Close chat").
6. The map showed only TX and AZ highlighted as covered; all other states grey.
7. Filled the "Don't see your area?" form with "Tulsa, OK" and clicked "Ask for it."
8. Confirmation appeared on screen: "Got it. We'll add Tulsa, OK." — so the site acknowledges Tulsa but does not yet have leads for it.

CONFUSING:
- "CraneSignal" as a name gave me no clue what the product was until I read the body copy — for a few seconds I was "unsure what this company is."
- "Early Leads," "Under the Hood," and "Lead Pack" — I didn't know if a "Lead Pack" was a paid bundle or a free sample.
- The leads app list said "The states we cover so far" and "Grey states aren't covered yet" — I had to work out whether Tulsa was grey/covered or not; it wasn't explicitly named.
- A sign-in/chat modal ("Make a free account to see who to call") appeared unexpectedly over the request form — I hesitated over whether the form would work without an account.

MISSING:
- Any Oklahoma or Tulsa content at all — no OK leads, no "Tulsa" mention anywhere on the site.
- A clear coverage list/I could not find a plain statement of which states are covered besides reading the map.
- Contact data (names/phone numbers) without signing in — the site says you need a free account to "see who to call."
- Pricing for the full product (it only advertised "free early access," no published price).

FIRST 5 SECONDS: I thought it was a data/lead-generation service that sells verified lists of apartment buildings (and the decision-makers at them) to vendors who sell into the multifamily/apartment market — i.e., exactly a lead source for someone like me selling apartment internet.

WOULD SIGN UP: No, not on this visit. I understood the reason for it — a free account (Google or email, ~20 seconds) is required to unlock the actual contact details ("see who to call"). But as a first-time visitor whose market (Tulsa, OK) isn't covered yet, signing up gives me nothing right now: no Tulsa leads exist in the product. I did the correct first step instead — requested Tulsa via the "Don't see your area?" form (confirmed: "Got it. We'll add Tulsa, OK."). I would sign up later once Tulsa leads appear, or if I expanded into TX/AZ where coverage exists.

SCORE: 7/10 — clean, fast to understand what it is and what to do for an uncovered market, and the request flow worked immediately. Points off for the vague name/hero copy, the unexplained "Lead Pack"/coverage terms, and the sign-in modal appearing over the request form.

BOTTOM LINE FOR DANA: CraneSignal does NOT currently cover Tulsa, OK (only TX and AZ). The actionable move was taken: Tulsa was submitted via "Don't see your area?" and acknowledged as "we'll add Tulsa, OK." Until Tulsa is added, there are no leads for your market on this site.
