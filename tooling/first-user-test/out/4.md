## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

STEPS:
1. Landed on cranesignal.com — hero page saying it shows "new apartment buildings worth a call right now"; a sample lead card (Richardson, TX) and nav items "Why early" and "The list".
2. Clicked "The list" — saw the sample data section with a table including a "Recently sold" example (Austin, TX) and a CTA "See the leads free" (FREE EARLY ACCESS · ONE METRO).
3. Clicked "See the leads free →" — opened a new tab at app.cranesignal.com showing the Early Leads table with 592 leads, State filters (Texas, Arizona) and Lead-type filters (All leads, New builds, Opening soon, Recently sold).
4. Clicked the "Arizona" state filter — table showed Arizona leads (Phoenix, Tempe, Mesa, Gilbert, Scottsdale), 279 leads, but all displayed were "Upcoming" new-build permits.
5. Clicked the "Recently sold" lead-type filter — now saw Arizona apartment buildings with sold dates and buyer entities. Examples I read on screen:
   - 4030 N 44th Ave, Phoenix — sold 2026-08-01 · to LEGACY MULTIFAMILY HOLDINGS LLC · 256 units
   - 1 W Campbell Ave, Phoenix — sold 2026-07-01 · to 1 WEST CAMPBELL AVENUE LLC · 254 units
   - 5727 N 7th Street, Phoenix — sold 2026-06-01 · to SEVENTH STREET OWNER LLC · 258 units
   (Buyer/owner entity names ARE visible; manager name and direct phone are locked.)
6. Clicked "Get contact →" on the first sold row — a panel appeared: "Make a free account to see who to call... Takes about 20 seconds, with Google or your email. No card." with Sign in / Sign up options. This confirmed the sign-up wall. Per instructions I did NOT sign up.

CONFUSING:
- The landing headline "new apartment buildings worth a call right now" made me first assume it was ONLY about new construction; I wasn't sure it also covered sales until I found "Recently sold" in the table. The word "Early Leads" and "why-now brief" were a little unclear at first.
- "ONE METRO" in the "FREE EARLY ACCESS · ONE METRO" CTA was ambiguous — I didn't know if that met my need (I wanted a specific metro/state).
- "Under the Hood" and "Ask the agent" — I wasn't sure if the agent could access contact info without an account.
- Term "Lead pack" / "Download Lead Pack (PDF)" wasn't obviously defined until it was explained as manager name + phone + why-now brief.

MISSING:
- I expected the contact info (manager name + direct phone) to be shown with the sold data, but it's locked behind a free account.
- No clear way to sort/filter by "new owner" type or by buyer entity without an account.
- I could not see ALL Arizona recently-sold leads — only the table rows shown, and the contact details were gated.

FIRST 5 SECONDS:
"This is a lead-generation tool that tells you which apartment buildings are being built or just sold, so you can call them and pitch your service." I understood it was aimed at companies selling to apartment buildings (like mine), and that it covered both new construction and recently-sold buildings.

WOULD SIGN UP: no — only because you told me not to sign up (I'm a first-time visitor without an account). I understood WHY the site asks: manager names/direct phone numbers and the "why-now" brief are the site's paid value, so they gate it behind a free account. If I were actually doing this job, I WOULD sign up (it's free, no card, ~20 seconds with Google) because the buyer entity names alone aren't enough — I'd want the contact info.

SCORE: 7/10 — easy to find the right filters (Arizona + Recently sold) and the UI was clean, but the sign-up wall on contact details and the somewhat ambiguous "new construction" framing on the landing page cost a couple points.
