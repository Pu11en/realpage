CONFUSING:
- "CraneSignal" itself — the name gives no clue; I had to read the tagline to understand it was about apartment-building leads (not e.g. market data or a telescope/signal product).
- "Signal" column and the "Why" column — the header labels were terse ("Signal," "Why") and only made sense after reading the row text.
- "Software" column showing "Unknown" — I didn't understand what software is being tracked, or why "Unknown" (was it unknown whether they use software? something else?).
- "Unnamed project" rows (e.g., 290 and 140 units in "Maricopa County") — unclear why there's no address.
- "LEAD #1 / LEAD #2" labels on the sample table — didn't immediately understand these were just sample rows, not a rank.
- "The list" menu item — clicking it just scrolled the same page; I expected it to lead to a page of available leads/metros.
- "See the leads free" vs "The list" — two similar-sounding CTAs; I wasn't sure which one led to more data.

MISSING:
- Any mention of Arizona on the public landing page (only Austin, TX sample data was shown publicly).
- A clear explanation of what you get for FREE vs paid on the landing page.
- Buyer CONTACT details (who to call, phone/email, the deed/agent name) — these are behind the account wall. I could only see the buying entity's LLC name from the table, not the person to call.
- The "Lead Pack PDF" that was advertised on the landing page never became visible without signing up.
- No obvious filter I could find for city/metro other than the State buttons and the "Describe the leads you want" agent box.

FIRST 5 SECONDS: I thought it was a real-estate/proptech data product — specifically a tool that alerts salespeople to new or newly-sold apartment buildings so they can target them, pitched at vendors (e.g., software/landscaping/leasing-services) who sell into multifamily. The tagline "know which new apartment buildings are opening" and the "See the leads free" + "book a 15 minute call" CTAs made me think "free lead sample, paid full list."

WOULD SIGN UP: No. I understood WHY it wants a signup — it's a lead-gen product that gates the actual value (contact names/phone/email, the Lead Pack PDF) behind a free account to capture leads and let you taste the data. For Tom's goal (a one-time look at AZ sold buildings + buyers), the LLC buyer names were already visible in the table without an account, so the free signup wasn't necessary. Also, as instructed, I'm a first-time visitor with no account and didn't want to create one. I would sign up only if I needed the actual contacts for a real prospecting list.

SCORE: 6 — The path from landing page → Arizona → Recently sold was quick and the buyer LLC names were visible for free, which is great. But it cost me several steps to even find Arizona (hidden behind the app CTA), contact info is gated, and the product name/columns were unclear to a first-time visitor.
