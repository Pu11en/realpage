## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

(START PAGE) cranesignal.com → clicked 'See the leads free →' → leads app at app.cranesignal.com → clicked 'San Antonio (41)' region filter → clicked 'Download spreadsheet (CSV)' → account gate appeared (did not sign up) → extracted the on-screen rows → saved them to san_antonio_leads.csv.

STEPS:
1. Landed on cranesignal.com. Read the hero: it's about early leads for new apartment buildings, aimed at "companies selling to apartment buildings." Nav offered 'Why early', 'The list', 'See the leads free', 'book a 15 minute call'.
2. Clicked 'The list' → landed on a '#monday' sample section showing AUSTIN, TX metro sample data (sample lead = Richardson, TX). No San Antonio anywhere.
3. Scrolled down → found pricing/comparison (Free, no account vs Free account) and an 'Open a sample call list' link; sample call list preview was Dallas–Fort Worth. No city selector visible.
4. Scrolled to bottom → found 'Browse every lead free' and a 'Pick your state and region' teaser.
5. Tried clicking 'Browse every lead free' (twice) — nothing happened (it's a span without a link). Also tried 'Pick your state and region' — nothing happened. Hesitated here: the text looked clickable but wasn't.
6. Clicked the actual nav link 'See the leads free →' [631] — it opened a NEW TAB to app.cranesignal.com (the real leads app).
7. In the leads app: Texas selected, 592 leads total. Region filter buttons showed: All Texas (592), Dallas–Fort Worth (307), Houston (69), Austin (84), San Antonio (41), Rest of Texas (91).
8. Clicked 'San Antonio (41)' → URL became ?region=San+Antonio. Header showed: 41 Leads, 7,969 Units in play, 'Opening soon', 'Data from Sep 15, 2026.'
9. Clicked 'Download spreadsheet (CSV)' → instead of a file, a sign-in/free-account panel appeared: 'Make a free account in the panel to start your download.' A free account unlocks manager name, direct phone, 'why call now' note, the call list (PDF) and the spreadsheet.
10. Per instruction, did NOT sign up. Closed the account/chat panel.
11. Extracted the visible San Antonio rows (building name, city, units, status, opening date, why-now note, office phone).
12. Wrote them to san_antonio_leads.csv (34 rows) and verified the file is well-formed and openable.

DATA FRESHNESS: The leads app stamps the list 'Data from Sep 15, 2026.' Today is 2026-09-19, so the data is about 4 days old — quite fresh. Every row I captured carried a List Date of 2026-09-15.

CONFUSING:
- 'The list' — I expected a full browsable list of leads; instead it jumped to a single '#monday' sample block for Austin metro. I didn't understand why it was called 'The list' for one city's sample.
- 'Browse every lead free' and 'Pick your state and region' — these read like buttons/links but clicking did nothing (they're plain spans). I couldn't tell what I was supposed to click.
- 'See who to call →' buttons on each row — I assumed they'd reveal the manager, but each opens the same free-account gate. Unclear whether there's any account-free way to see a contact.
- 'Software' / 'No software yet' column — I wasn't sure what 'software' refers to here.
- 'Score' (0–100) column with an aria tooltip 'How good this lead is right now' — the scoring basis isn't explained on the page.
- 'Ask the agent →' and the Chat button — a second way to get leads; unclear if it does anything without an account.
- The filter buttons showed counts like 'San Antonio (41)' yet the table rendered only ~34 rows with rank numbers skipping (11 shown, but 11 had New Braunfels; gaps at 15, 28, 32, 35, 37). I was unsure whether 41 was the true count or whether some rows were filtered out.

MISSING:
- Street addresses. The Property column shows only a building name; there is no address, so the file has name + phone but no street address (I noted addresses as blank, not 'Not available', to be honest).
- A no-account CSV/PDF export. The site advertises 'Free during early access, no card' but the actual spreadsheet download is behind a free-account login, so I could only hand-build the CSV.
- Manager name and direct phone — explicitly listed as account-gated, so my file only has the public office phone.
- A clear way to see all 41 rows without an account (table seemed to show ~34 with rank gaps).
- San Antonio was not reachable from the marketing homepage at all; it only exists inside app.cranesignal.com.

FIRST 5 SECONDS: After landing, I thought CraneSignal was an early-warning lead service for B2B salespeople who sell into apartment buildings — like a heads-up feed of newly built/opening apartment complexes so reps can call before competitors do. The 'monday' sample and 'why early' framing reinforced that.

WOULD SIGN UP: No. Reason: I'm a first-time visitor doing a one-off pull, and the gate appeared at the exact moment I clicked download, before I could confirm the data was worth an email. The page does say it's free and no card, which is low-friction, but there's no privacy/terms link visible on the gate and no way to preview a full row first. If I needed this list regularly I probably would sign up — the value proposition (fresh, dated, early-stage leads) was clear. But for a single pull, the friction stopped me.

SCORE: 5/10 for ease. Marketing site is clean and the value prop is obvious, but: (a) the actual leads live in a separate app the homepage never links to directly, (b) two prominent 'browse/pick' teasers were dead spans that did nothing, (c) the CSV I was told was 'free' is gated, and (d) the table omitted street addresses and appeared to show fewer rows (34) than the filter's stated count (41).
