After landing, I thought this was a B2B sales-intelligence / lead-gen tool for property-management companies — it promises "New apartment buildings. Before your competitor calls." i.e. a feed of brand-new apartment developments so you can call the owner before anyone else, with a "free account" step to unlock contact info.

WOULD SIGN UP: No — not during this evaluation. I understood why the site gates: the list shows property/stage/software/why and even phone numbers, but the "who to call" contact info (and presumably verified decision-maker) sits behind "Make a free account to see who to call." It's low-friction (Google/email, ~20 seconds, no card), so it's a reasonable gate — but as a first-time visitor evaluating, I would not sign up just to unlock, since most of what I needed (Austin list, software "Not chosen yet", and even the Developer field on detail pages) turned out to be visible without an account.

SCORE: 7/10 — easy to get to the Austin list and read qualifying leads, and developer info is free on detail pages; points off for the ambiguous sample block, the unexplained Score/Signal/Software columns, the silent Austin-filter reset, and having to open many detail pages one-by-one to see developers.
