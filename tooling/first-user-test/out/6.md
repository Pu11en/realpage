## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

STEPS:
1. Landed on https://cranesignal.com. Headline read: "CraneSignal — New apartment buildings. Before your competitor calls. Leads for companies that sell to apartment buildings." The sample lead shown was Richardson, TX (not Austin).
2. Scrolled the landing page. Found a sample lead table with Austin buildings (Rainey Street Flats, Parmer Crossing, South Lamar Yards) and a "Why early" / pricing section. Site says it covers Texas and Arizona.
3. Clicked "See the leads free" → opened app.cranesignal.com leads app in a new tab (592 Texas leads).
4. Clicked the region filter "Austin (84)" → table showed 84 Austin leads, 10,187 units. Columns: Score, Property, City, Units, Why now, Software, Why.
5. Clicked "See who to call →" on a building → a chat panel opened with a sign-in/sign-up wall requiring a free account.
6. Tried the "Recently sold" tab → table went empty ("Nothing found"); re-clicked "All leads" to restore the 12 visible rows.
7. Clicked a "State project record" link → opened the Texas TDLR record for Bluff Springs Apartments (Austin, 241 units), showing owner RPMD 7333 Bluff Springs Road Owner, LLC and design firm Meeks + Partners.
8. Opened the CraneSignal detail page for Bluff Springs (7333 Bluff Springs Rd Bldg 3, Austin, 20 units) → openly showed Developer: RPMD 7333 Bluff Springs Road Owner, LLC, phone (512) 762-9541, Under construction, opens 2027-02-01, no software yet.
9. Went back and opened Belmont Apartments (4401 E Slaughter Lane, Austin, 348 units) → openly showed Developer: Belmont Apartments Ltd., phone (913) 439-8117, Under construction, opens 2027-04-30, no software yet, lead score 96.

FINDINGS:
- Austin Under-construction buildings with NO manager attached (no manager/software picked yet): Bluff Springs Apartments (20 units — Dev: RPMD 7333 Bluff Springs Road Owner, LLC) and Belmont Apartments (348 units — Dev: Belmont Apartments Ltd.).
- Manager name / "who to call" is gated behind a free account signup wall.
- The DEVELOPER name IS shown openly on each building's detail page (no account needed).
- Open source links provided (Texas TDLR project records, TDHCA permit data).

CONFUSING:
- "Why now" column — unclear what it means.
- "Signal" (e.g. "Opens: 2027-04-30") — unsure if it is a date, a status, or an indicator.
- "Lead score 96" — did not understand how it is calculated.
- Clicking "Recently sold" emptied the table ("Nothing found") — I did not understand why the Austin leads disappeared; I had to re-click "All leads" to recover.
- "See who to call" — I expected contact info but got a signup wall instead.

MISSING:
- A manager / property-management column or field anywhere in the list (the whole point of my search).
- Developer name in the leads table itself (only on the individual building detail page).
- Any indication in the list of which buildings have no manager yet.
- Free visible contact info / phone numbers on the list.

FIRST 5 SECONDS:
I thought CraneSignal was a lead-generation / prospecting tool that surfaces new and upcoming apartment building projects early, aimed at companies selling to apartment buildings (e.g. property managers, vendors).

WOULD SIGN UP: no. My reason: the site is clearly built for sellers/prospectors, not for a property-management company looking to onboard new buildings; and the data I actually needed (whether a building has no manager yet) was not visible in the list, while "who to call" was locked behind an account. So signing up did not appear to give me the specific "owner hasn't picked a manager yet" list I needed. I understood the signup (free account unlocks who-to-call / agent / call-list PDF), but it was not worth it for my goal.

SCORE: 5/10. Reasonable to navigate and it openly shows developer + source records, but the key manager/who-to-call data is walled, the "Recently sold" filter emptied the list, and several column labels ("Why now", "Signal", "Lead score") were unclear.
