## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

STEPS (as a first-time visitor, working only from what the site showed me):
1. Landed on cranesignal.com. Hero said it's a lead-gen tool for pre-construction apartment buildings; offered a "Free Lead Pack" PDF. Example lead shown: Richardson, TX (Legacy Arapaho, 443 units).
2. Scrolled the landing page. Saw a sample data table (Austin, TX metro) with columns BUILDING / UNITS / STAGE / SOFTWARE / WHY CALL NOW, plus source tags: Public records (CITY), Government housing data (GOV), News and company sites (NEWS).
3. Scrolled further. Found CTAs: "Start free today", "FREE EARLY ACCESS · ONE METRO", "5 early access spots". Footer: "Built from public records only." Read that it's free during early access, no card needed, no sign-in to browse, account needed to "use the agent".
4. Clicked "Open CraneSignal" → new tab to app.cranesignal.com. This is the actual product.
5. Early Leads tab: State = Texas, 592 leads, 91,276 units in play, 41 opening soon, "Last updated: Sep 15, 2026". Table columns: Rank, Score, Property, City, Units, Signal, Software, Why. Each row had a "Get contact →" button and a source link ("State project record" / "City permit record"). Also a "View as" dropdown (Everyone / RealPage / Yardi / Entrata) and a "Download Lead Pack (PDF)" button.
6. Clicked "Under the Hood". It described CraneSignal as a "sales research assistant for property-software teams" and showed self-test metrics: 92/100 test questions correct, 94% AI-grader agreement with humans, ~14s answer time, 10/10 software matches hand-confirmed via proof links.
7. Expanded "How the lead finder works" and "Known limits". Limits admitted: 31% of software unknown, coverage only TX + AZ, ~873 leads total. A sample (Plano/Richardson) listed 256 buildings, 227 with sites, 177 with software identified.
8. Clicked "Get contact →" on a lead. Instead of contact info, it opened an "Ask CraneSignal" chat panel asking me to sign in (email/password or Google) or "Make a free account". (I did NOT sign up.)
9. Opened a "State project record" source link → it went to a real Texas TDLR government page (tdlr.texas.gov, project TABS2025003997, Groves Apartments). This confirmed the data traces to genuine public records.
10. The property detail page for that lead (app.cranesignal.com/property.html?id=tx-1) showed: Groves Apartments, 336 units, Humble TX, developer RK Commercial Properties, Inc., stage "Under construction", opens 2026-09-30, permit date Oct 24 2024, software "Not chosen yet", lead score 100 — and it displayed a phone contact (210) 326-1119 alongside the state source link.

CONFUSING (moments I hesitated / didn't understand):
- "CraneSignal" name: I first thought it was about construction crane activity, not software/leads. Took a few seconds to re-orient.
- "Signal" column meaning — unclear what a "Signal" value represents.
- "40+ signals" / weighting not explained on the landing page.
- "Use the agent" — didn't know what "the agent" was until I hit the chat.
- "View as: Everyone / RealPage / Yardi / Entrata" dropdown — I didn't understand why I should pick a property-software vendor ("Highlight the buildings a Yardi / Entrata / AppFolio seller would win") as a lock company.
- Why "Get contact →" opened a sign-IN chat instead of showing the phone (and yet the property page did show a phone) — inconsistent, so I wasn't sure if contact is gated or not.
- "Free Lead Pack (PDF)" vs "Open CraneSignal" — which is the quote-tool? Unclear CTA hierarchy.

MISSING (what I expected but didn't find):
- No plain-language "What is this?" / "Who is it for?" one-liner up front; I had to reverse-engineer it.
- No explicit price page or "after early access, it costs $X" statement — only "free during early access."
- No stated data-refresh cadence or how "Last updated Sep 15, 2026" is produced.
- No clear explanation of the "Why call now" scoring methodology or what "lead score" weights.
- No visible free-trial limits (how many leads/contacts, how many chats).
- No About/Team/Company page — who runs CraneSignal is unknown.
- Contact info is behind an account, yet some contact appears on a detail page — the rules weren't stated.

FIRST 5 SECONDS: I read "CraneSignal" and assumed it was about construction/crane activity tracking. After the hero headline about pre-construction apartment leads with a sample lead (Richmond/Richardson, TX, legacy buildings), I concluded it's a B2B lead-gen / sales-prospecting tool that finds apartment buildings (20+ units) BEFORE they pick their property-management software, so a vendor can call them early.

WOULD SIGN UP: No (as instructed I didn't sign up). Even if I weren't instructed, I'd hesitate: the value prop wasn't instantly legible (name mismatch), the contact gate vs. visible phone was inconsistent, and there's no pricing or company/trust info. What WOULD move me: the verifiable TDLR source link per lead (real public records) and the honest "Known limits" (31% software unknown, TX+AZ only). If my team sold into TX apartment pre-construction, I'd likely try the free early-access account to see contacts unlocked.

SCORE: 6/10. The tool is genuinely useful-looking and the data checks out against a real government source, but the first-time experience is muddled: the name doesn't describe the product, CTAs compete ("Free Lead Pack PDF" vs "Open CraneSignal"), contact gating was inconsistent, and pricing/company info is absent.
