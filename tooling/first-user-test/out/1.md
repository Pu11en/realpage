# Persona 1

You are Maria, a sales rep at a company selling property-management software. Your goal: find one apartment building in the Dallas area that is about to pick software, and find out who you should call there.

## Report
GOAL REACHED: partly

STEPS:
1. Landed on cranesignal.com. Hero said "See the leads free" and "A free account unlocks who to call." A sample lead was shown (Legacy Arapaho, Richardson TX) with software "Not picked yet."
2. Scrolled the landing page. Saw a sample lead table (mostly Austin-area) and a "Built for property management sales" section. The site is a lead board for vendors selling to apartment buildings.
3. Clicked "See the leads free →". It opened a new tab: the Early Leads board at app.cranesignal.com, showing 592 Texas leads.
4. Used the region filter "Dallas–Fort Worth (307)" to narrow to DFW.
5. Browsed Dallas leads with software status "Not chosen yet": Bowser Multifamily (28 units, opens 2027-04-05), Inwood Senior Living (opens 2027-05-31), Mockingbird Station East Ph2 (395 units, opens 2027-07-23), Oram Apartments (opens 2027-08-30).
6. Clicked "Get contact →" on a Dallas lead. A panel appeared: "Make a free account to see who to call." Contact names are gated behind signup.
7. Closed that panel and clicked the "State project record" link on the Mockingbird Station East Phase 2 lead. It opened the Texas TDLR project page, which revealed the owner WITHOUT any account.
8. On TDLR I found: Project "Mockingbird Station East Phase 2 Multifamily," 5399 Twin Sixties Dr, Dallas TX 75206, 395 units, completion 7/23/2027. OWNER: Trammell Crow Company, 2121 N Pearl St Suite 300, Dallas TX 75201, Owner Phone (806) 224-3380, Owner Contact Name: Conner Best. (Design firm GFF Design; RAS contact Sam Stribling.)

RESULT: One Dallas building about to pick software = Mockingbird Station East Phase 2 (395 units, software "Not chosen yet" on CraneSignal). Who to call = Conner Best at owner Trammell Crow Company, (806) 224-3380. I found this via the public state record, not CraneSignal itself.

CONFUSING: "Not chosen yet" vs "Not picked yet" — same thing said two different ways on the landing page vs the board. "Get contact" didn't say it would require an account until after I clicked it, which felt like a bait-and-switch. On the leads board I wasn't sure whether the office phone shown was the building's or the developer's. "State project record" wasn't obviously a link I should click to find a contact — I guessed. On the TDLR page the abbreviations "RAS" and "RAS Name/RAS #" were opaque, and it wasn't clear which of the several people listed (Sam Stribling, Conner Best, etc.) was the one to call.

MISSING: The actual decision-maker's name and title attached to the lead on CraneSignal itself (it's locked). No way to sort/filter by "who's choosing software soon" beyond the software-status column. No visible explanation of what a "free account" includes beyond unlocking contacts. No way to see a contact without either signing up or leaving the site to a state database.

FIRST 5 SECONDS: I thought it was a directory/list of new apartment developments, like a construction-leads or permit-tracking site, because the first thing shown was a specific building with units, opening date and a "not picked yet" software field.

WOULD SIGN UP: No — because the user told me not to, but honestly also because I could reach the same person (owner contact) for free via the public TDLR record. If I genuinely needed the exact phone/inbox that CraneSignal had already verified, and the free tier were confirmed no-strings, I might consider it. I did understand WHY they ask: the contact field is their paid value, so they gate it. The gate was clearly explained ("Make a free account to see who to call").

SCORE: 6/10. The board was easy to browse and the region filter worked instantly, but the key payoff (who to call) was locked with no upfront warning, and I had to leave the site to actually get a contact.
