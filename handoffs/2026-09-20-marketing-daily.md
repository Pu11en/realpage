# Handoff: CraneSignal day-to-day marketing (new thread)

Written 2026-09-20, right after the product went live. This thread's job is **daily marketing**:
plan the day, write the posts, and ship them. Not product work — the Texas data audit has its
own thread.

## What CraneSignal is, in one line

A free list of U.S. apartment buildings that **just did something worth a call** — filed permits,
opening soon, or sold to a new owner — plus an AI agent that finds **who to call** at any of them.

Live now: **cranesignal.com** (landing) and **app.cranesignal.com** (the list).

## Who it is for

**People who sell software or services into apartment buildings**: property-management software,
resident tech, leasing and marketing services, maintenance and turn services.

Why they care: a building that just got permitted or just changed owner has **not picked its
software yet**. Calling it before that decision beats calling an incumbent's customer.

The pain, in a real seller's own words from r/PropertyManagement: *"the gatekeepers are really
good at not letting me through."* CraneSignal's angle is to skip that fight by calling earlier.

## Hard rules — do not break these in any public post

- **Never name the states covered.** The product is "U.S. apartment leads". Say size and freshness
  instead. The live line is: *"1,876 buildings tracked — 938 being built or planned, 938 sold to a
  new owner. 175 with a permit or sale in the last 6 months."*
- **Never say "scrape".** Say "check" or "run".
- **No prices anywhere.** The offer is **free early access in exchange for feedback**.
- Browsing is free with no login. A **free account** unlocks who-to-call, the call-list PDF and the
  CSV. Say that up front, in plain words, before anyone hits it.
- **Reddit is for helpful answers only**, never a pitch — r/sales and r/PropertyManagement.
  LinkedIn is the main channel.
- Every claim must be one a visitor can check on the site in ten seconds.

## What is true about the data today (use these, they are verified)

- **1,876 buildings** tracked, up from 1,462 this morning.
- Every Dallas-area lead (449) has a **real building name, a street address and a unit count**.
- Houston: 365 of 378 carry a real unit count.
- Sale dates show only what the county actually recorded — a month-precision county reads
  "Sold Aug 2026", never a made-up day.
- One verifiable example to use publicly: a 256-unit Phoenix building the list flagged as sold in
  August 2026 really sold for **$22.6M on 2026-08-14**, and the unit count matched exactly.

Do **not** claim the whole list is fresh news. Only 175 of 1,876 moved in the last six months, and
the site now says exactly that. Honesty here is a selling point — most lead lists do not admit it.
