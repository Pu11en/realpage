# What the chat can and can't use

Checked by reading the chat's setup and data files only. No tests, no paid calls.

## ✅ The chat can use
- **Every lead on the site:** Texas (about 590), Arizona 279 and New York 2. It has name, city, address, units, stage, why it's a lead, permit, opening and sale dates, buyer, developer, phone, website, software and source links.
- **Plano and Richardson in full detail:** all 204 buildings, their websites, their software (with proof links), 28 sales, 14 upcoming projects, the 42 ranked leads with score reasons, and phone and email contacts.
- **Street Talk:** 73 Reddit and YouTube posts (quote, mood, city, which building, "warm lead" flag).
- **Research notes:** company, products, reviews, Reddit, social, news, competitors, voice of customer and AI Visibility, text files only.
- **Live web search**, but only when the web-search key is set on the server.

## ⚠️ Only partly
- **Software vendor is only known for Plano and Richardson.** For every other area it's blank, and the chat is told to say so.
- **Research files that aren't text** (pictures, spreadsheet-style data files) are dropped.

## ❌ The chat can't use
- **The whole Dallas area folder that was "parked"**: buildings, websites, software, sales and contacts. Its files are named differently, so they never get loaded.
- **Map data:** per-state lead counts, top cities and pin locations. The chat can count from the leads, but it can't see the map itself.
- **The detailed AI Visibility scores** (per-AI results, history, action list). It only has the written summary.
- **Software market share** and the "reach" proof list shown on the site.
- **Building-page extras** for Plano and Richardson: owner, how sure we are about the website, and why something is unknown.
- **The lead finder's working files:** scout runs, the address lookup cache and the client-map files.
- **CraneSignal's own stats:** build steps, chat speed, eval results. It can't answer "how was this built?" from real numbers.

## What matters most for customers
- **Biggest gap: the parked Dallas data.** It includes software and contacts, the most valuable fields, and the chat can't see any of it.
- **Next: software for areas beyond Plano and Richardson.** It's blank everywhere else, on the site too, so it's missing data, not a chat problem.
- The rest (map pins, AI Visibility details, build stats) matters less for sales questions.
