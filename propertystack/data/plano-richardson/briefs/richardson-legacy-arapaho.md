# Deep dive: Legacy Arapaho (upcoming)

## Bottom line
- **Call: yes, early.** A 443-unit new build by Legacy Partners at 250 E Arapaho Rd, Richardson; permit issued June 2026, so **no software has been chosen yet**.
- Legacy Partners manages its own buildings (60+ communities), so the software decision is made at the company, not by a third-party manager.
- Ask for **Brian McNally, VP of Development**, who presented this project to Richardson City Council.

## Who they are
- Legacy Arapaho, 250 E Arapaho Road, Richardson TX 75081, 443 units in five buildings (1–3 bedrooms, townhomes, average ~850 sq ft), replacing a vacant office building.
- Developer: Legacy Partners (Dallas office; owns, develops and manages multifamily nationwide).
- Expected opening: about November 2028 (our upcoming-projects list); the city said completion "nearing October 2029" at approval, so the date is unconfirmed.
- Current software: not chosen yet.

## Why now
- Permit issued June 2026 after council approval in Feb 2025; lease-up systems get picked well before opening: https://communityimpact.com/richardson/government/443-unit-apartment-complex-approved-by-richardson-city-council/
- Plan commission and council both approved unanimously, so the project is moving: https://citizenportal.ai/articles/6667755/Texas/Dallas-County/Richardson/Council-unanimously-approves-Legacy-Partners-plan-for-443unit-Arapahoe-multifamily-project-with-limited-flexibility-to-preserve-trees
- Legacy Partners runs management in-house, so one company decision covers this building: https://legacypartners.com/news/legacy-partners-grows-southern-u-s-presence-with-three-major-hires/

## Who to ask for
- Brian McNally, Vice President of Development, Legacy Partners (named as the project's representative): https://communityimpact.com/richardson/government/443-unit-apartment-complex-approved-by-richardson-city-council/
- Matt Brendel, Senior Managing Director Southwest, Legacy Partners (runs development and operations for Southwest properties, per a 2020 release; may be out of date): https://legacypartners.com/news/legacy-partners-grows-southern-u-s-presence-with-three-major-hires/

## 30-second opener
Hi, this is [your name] with [your company]. Congratulations on getting Legacy Arapaho through Richardson council and permitting. With 443 homes to lease up, the leasing and resident systems are easier to set up now than to change after opening. Could we get on your list for when you choose the lease-up and resident tools?

## 3 questions
1. Will Legacy Arapaho run on the same systems as your other Legacy communities, or is it a chance to re-evaluate?
2. When in the build schedule do you usually lock in leasing and resident software?
3. Who besides you weighs in on that choice: operations, IT, or your capital partners?

## 2 objections + answers
- "We already have a company-wide standard." Understood; new builds are often where standards get tested, and we can pilot here with no change to your other buildings.
- "It's too early, we open in 2028." That's why we're calling; we'd like to be in the review when it happens, not after it's decided.
