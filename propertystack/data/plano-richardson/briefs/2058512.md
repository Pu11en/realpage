# Deep dive: Creekside At Legacy Apartments

## Bottom line
- **Call: yes, but it's the "at risk" case.** This 380-unit Plano building already runs **RealPage** (resident portal on RealPage's Loft), and it changed hands on 2024-09-26 to Key Creekside LLC, managed by **Key Real Estate Company**.
- For a RealPage seller this is a keep-and-grow account; for a competitor it's a switch target, since new owners review their stack.
- Ask for **Caitlin Roniger**, Key's Director of Systems & Strategic Initiatives, who vets and rolls out new software.

## 30-second opener
Hi, this is [your name] with [your company]. I saw Key took over Creekside at Legacy, and I know you look after the systems across Key's portfolio. Now that the property is settled in, I'd like to show you how we handle resident payments and portal work, so you can compare it with what the building runs today. Would ten minutes next week work?

## 3 questions
1. Is Creekside on the same systems as the rest of Key's portfolio, or still on what came with the building?
2. You already added Flex for payments; what made you add a separate tool instead of using what the portal offers?
3. When is the next time Key reviews its property software, and who joins that review?

## 2 objections + answers
- "We're happy with RealPage." Great; then let's make sure you're using everything you pay for, and if anything is missing we can fill the gap without a full switch.
- "We standardize across the whole portfolio." That's exactly why we'd talk to you and not the site; one building can be the test before any bigger decision.
