# Masiate Construction: First Research Results

## What You Received

- ✅ **A finished 63-page PDF with 49 detailed property profiles**, drawn from 267 collected public-record entries.
- Each profile describes the location, proposed or permitted work, why it may fit Masiate, dates, project value when available, named businesses, linked sources, and facts still needing verification.
- The report has a clickable property index and a source-by-source coverage appendix.
- Repeated records were combined where the evidence supported it; different project phases were kept separate when necessary.
- **267 records does not mean 267 different properties or sales-ready leads.** The 49 profiles connect to 58 supporting record entries.

- Examples include a fire-damaged maintenance building remodel, a commercial roof replacement, an office tenant buildout, and paving and drainage improvements.
- The ordering favors work that matches Masiate's services, supporting evidence, and recency; it is not a prediction of who will hire Masiate.

## What Still Needs Checking

- ⚠️ **No currently open Masiate trade package was confirmed.** A permit, planned project, or registration does not prove that a contractor is still being hired.
- **Six profiles have a sourced business phone number, email, or website.** Other profiles name project participants or say the contact is unknown; a business name is not a callable lead.
- An architect, engineer, or accessibility specialist is not necessarily the person buying construction services.
- Three out-of-area registrations were removed, and **98 additional Brazos registrations were not deeply reviewed** in this first pass.
- Lorca Apartments, Iola's wastewater project, and Pecan Grove Phase 2 were moved to a short watchlist because of an unresolved address, expired tender, or future meeting decision.
- The short watchlist shows these three review changes, not every weaker record in the collection.
- Project values describe whole projects or source-specific work; they are not amounts Masiate could expect to earn.

## Coverage And Next Research

- **76 source checks** record what was accessible, partially covered, blocked, or unproductive.
- Useful evidence came from local permits, development-review documents, meeting materials, and state accessibility registrations.
- Remaining gaps include Bryan's account-gated permit portal, College Station's dynamic permit listings, TCEQ stormwater searches, and fuller coverage in smaller counties.
- Coverage differs by source and date; this was not a complete search of every property or government record in the seven counties.
- Nothing was sent to property owners or government offices, and no new paid-data service was purchased.
