# Multifamily Insiders Comment Patterns

## What Commented Posts Have In Common

- **They ask for a copy or resource.** The 2026 MFI Tech Adoption Research post had visible comments from people asking for the report.
- **They give people something specific to add.** The amenity measurement post got a comment adding tracking as another element.
- **They use a concrete example people can react to.** The Delta apartment community post got a comment quoting the strongest analogy.
- **They invite a follow up question.** The RETCON sessions post got a comment asking whether the sessions lived up to the author's expectations.
- **They use human stakes.** The property management leadership post got comments on a hard story, not on a generic concept.

## What To Copy For CraneSignal

- Ask one question people can answer from experience.
- Offer a short checklist and invite people to add or remove one signal.
- Avoid asking for general opinions.
- Avoid a product CTA.
- Make the post useful even with no link.
- If we want comments, include a sentence like: **What would you add or remove from this list?**

## Comment First Draft Direction

Best discussion prompt:

> When a property changes hands, what is the first sign that the new owner may actually change vendors or software?
>
> Recent sale alone feels too broad. The signals I would trust more are:
>
> 1. Management company change
> 2. Active permits or renovation work
> 3. Leasing or website changes
> 4. Public records showing repositioning
> 5. Old software or vendor traces disappearing
>
> For people who sell into owner/operators or work inside property management teams, which signal has been most reliable?
>
> What would you add or remove from this list?
