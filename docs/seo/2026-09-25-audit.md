## What changed

1. **Titles cut to ≤ 60 characters.** Was `Apartment Buildings Under Construction and
   Recently Sold in Grand Prairie, Texas | CraneSignal` (94). Now `Grand Prairie Apartment
   Construction and Sales | CraneSignal` (59). `fit_title()` drops the least informative
   words in a fixed order rather than hand-trimming one page.
2. **Share image.** There was no `og:image` anywhere — every share and every AI preview was
   faceless. Added `site/img/og-default.png`, 1200×630, built from the site's own blueprint
   blue and amber with the real totals on it (`tooling/seo/make_og_image.py`, run by hand).
3. **FAQPage schema and a visible FAQ** on all 18 pages: 4–5 questions per page answered from
   that page's own rows ("67 of the 443 apartment buildings CraneSignal tracks in Houston are
   under construction, as of Sep 20, 2026"). A test asserts the LD text appears verbatim on
   the page — LD that says something the page does not show risks a spam verdict.
4. **BreadcrumbList schema**, generated from the same crumb links the page renders.
5. **One Organization entity.** `creator` and `publisher` now reference `@id: /#org` instead
   of each page declaring a fresh `Organization`.
6. **robots.txt covers all three crawler purposes** — training (GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
   Google-Extended, CCBot), search index (OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot),
   live fetch (ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User). 15 agents named.
7. **llms-full.txt** with per-state totals, stage splits, sales and openings by year, and the
   metro breakdown, so an engine can take the numbers without crawling 18 pages.
8. Caddyfile serves `/llms-full.txt`; `og:image` added to index, under-the-hood and privacy.

## Not done, and why

- **Bing Webmaster Tools.** The skill puts this first in the AEO lane, because Copilot and
  ChatGPT search both lean on the Bing index. It needs David's account; the steps are in
  `docs/seo/GOOGLE-SETUP-GUIDE.md` Part 2. IndexNow is already wired
  (`tooling/seo/indexnow.py`) and will push to Bing the moment the pages are live.
- **Per-page share images.** The checklist calls dynamic generation ideal. The repo ships only
  woff2 fonts, which Pillow cannot read, so per-page cards would fall back to different system
  fonts on Windows and Linux and churn the generator's `--check` on every run. One committed
  card until a TTF is vendored.
- **`sameAs` entity links.** Nothing to link yet. When a public repo, wiki entry or social
  profile exists, add them to the Organization node; LLMO depends on it.
- **Content operations (Phase 5).** No blog, no RSS, no question backlog. This is the part
  agencies bill monthly for, and it is a real gap, but it needs a publishing decision from
  Drew rather than a code change.
- **Backlinks.** Not pursued. The skill's first rule is legitimate methods only, and the
  measurement run already showed what actually earns citations here: trade press, city and
  census data, and vendor documentation.
- **`/static/*` routes to the chat app, not the site.** Pre-existing: every page's favicon and
  apple-touch-icon are served by Open WebUI rather than by CraneSignal. Out of scope for SEO,
  but worth someone checking which icon a search result ends up showing.
