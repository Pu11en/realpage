# CraneSignal Worker Call Sheet

## ✅ What This Is

- A **spreadsheet** and **PDF call sheet** for a worker calling apartment maintenance/facilities software vendors.
- The list is built around the ICP we decided is best right now: **software/service companies selling maintenance, inspections, work orders, make-ready, or facilities tools to apartment owners and operators**.
- The goal is not to sell on the first call.
- The goal is to get permission to send a **free Texas apartment maintenance-pain sample**.

## ✅ Best Call Opener

- **Say:** “Hi, this is Drew with CraneSignal. We are mapping Texas apartment communities showing public maintenance pain signals. I pulled a few accounts your sales team may want. Who is the best person to send that to?”
- **Do not say:** “We found apartments that need software.”
- The stronger angle is: **“We found timely accounts with evidence-backed maintenance pain.”**

## ⬜ How The Worker Should Use It

- Start with **Tier A** companies.
- Mark each row with a call status.
- If someone asks for the sample, mark **Send sample**.
- If the main line is bad, mark **Find named sales/BD**.
- Use the notes column for the person’s name, title, direct number, and next action.

## ⚠️ Important Caveat

- The phone numbers and emails are public-source contacts from the research pass.
- Before high-volume dialing, verify any number that looks like support instead of sales.
- The next best asset is a **10-account Texas sample lead pack** to make these calls much stronger.
