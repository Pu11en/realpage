- The [College Station permit-report page](https://www.cstx.gov/your-government/departments/planning-development-services-department/building-permits-issued/) loaded its documents in a browser, exposing the actual spreadsheets and a **CONTRACTOR NAME** column.
- This was a newly working access route compared with the pilot's static-page attempt. The public browser request to `/umbraco/surface/DocumentManager/GetDocumentsByFilters` returned 200; no authentication bypass was used.
- Checked January through September 2026 workbooks, all sheets, including project descriptions and site addresses. Duplicate city/CDN links represent the same monthly reports, not extra coverage.
- These are issued-permit lists, not every pending application, signed private contract or subcontract bid.
- **The latest available September workbook contained issue dates only through September 11, 2026**, leaving a gap before this September 20 check. It cannot rule out a later permit or hiring decision.
- Date ranges in monthly files cross calendar boundaries: March includes April 2, April includes May 1, July begins June 29, and September begins August 31.
- College Station's [detailed eTRAKiT permit search](https://cd.cstx.gov/eTRAKiT/Search/permit.aspx) redirected to its registered-user login; no account was created.
- Search-engine results and TDLR project registrations alone cannot establish that a contractor has not been hired.
- No currently open invitation for a main contractor was verified for these three projects.
- The next decisive evidence would be the exact current permit/application naming the builder, a project-specific award, or an owner/tenant explicitly confirming that estimates are still being accepted. No such outreach is authorized or performed in this check.
