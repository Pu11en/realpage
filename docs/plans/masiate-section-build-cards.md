# Masiate: Turning The Research Into A CraneSignal Section

## What The Company Will Get

- A regular **Masiate Construction** section inside CraneSignal, open like the other public pages.
- The **49 reviewed properties**, ranked and searchable, with county and contact-available filters.
- Useful project details and source links, plus the existing **63-page PDF** to download.
- The report date, missing coverage and unknown job availability remain visible.
- The PDF stays fixed; this build does not start another crawl or silently update the research.

## What The Agent Will Know

- Masiate's construction services, seven-county research area and saved property evidence.
- An Ask action for the company and for each property, carrying the correct project into chat.
- Existing sign-in and saved conversations stay in place; public property browsing does not require a new company account.
- Later research stays in that person's chat, not in the PDF or another user's history.
- No automatic search through unrelated old chats, automatic outreach or invented contacts.
- Existing research tools remain available under their current account limits; no new subscriptions or paid test calls.

## Six Small Build Tasks

- ⏳ The task-loop request is submitted; **the bot is waiting for your AI choice** before starting workers.
- Its **A option** chooses a Codex model for each task and is the recommended continuation.
- **1. Shared data:** turn the saved research into matching website and agent data, with the original PDF unchanged.
- **2. Public section:** add the page, filters, navigation, source links and download.
- **3. Agent knowledge:** package the same evidence into the agent's existing searchable data.
- **4. Chat connection:** make each Ask action carry the right company/property context through sign-in.
- **5. Browser checks:** test the whole experience on desktop and phones, including errors and empty searches.
- **6. Local release check:** verify deployment packaging, start a safe preview and report anything still unverified.
- Tasks 2 and 3 can run in parallel after task 1; the rest follow their dependencies.
- Initial estimate: **roughly 75-140 minutes after workers start**, subject to integration problems, not a promised deadline.

## What Ready Means

- Navigation, property counts, filters, links and PDF download work locally.
- The agent can retrieve the saved evidence without mixing it into apartment-software totals.
- No promise of confirmed available work: the pilot found **zero confirmed open trade packages**, and only **six profiles with a business phone, email or website**.
- Desktop and mobile checks pass, and the existing site/chat behavior is not broken.
- The website and agent deployment packages both include their required data; any unavailable verification is called out.
- You receive a local preview and a short testing walkthrough before publishing.
- **Nothing is pushed or deployed by this build**, and the old fifty-task plan stays stopped.
