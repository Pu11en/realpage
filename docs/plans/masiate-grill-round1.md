# Masiate Early-Stage Leads: Holes To Close (Round 1)

## Q1: Is Masiate the main builder or a helper trade?
- **Why it matters:** "get in before contractors are picked" only helps if Masiate bids to run the whole job. If they do fencing, concrete or roofing for other builders, the person to call is the **general contractor**, and that name usually shows up only **after** the permit.
- **A)** Main builder on small to mid jobs
- **B)** Helper trade (sub) under other builders
- **C)** Both, depending on the job
- **D)** Don't know, ask Masiate
- ➡️ **Recommend D.** This decides who we call on every lead.

## Q2: What job size can Masiate actually take?
- **Why it matters:** most of our early leads are **state project filings, which only start at about $50,000**, and some are large (a **$2.5M bank**, school campuses). Nobody has confirmed Masiate's smallest or biggest job.
- **A)** Under $250k
- **B)** $50k to $1M
- **C)** Anything, they'll sub out
- **D)** Ask Masiate
- ➡️ **Recommend D**, and until they answer, sort by size instead of hiding jobs.

## Q3: Do they want homeowner jobs too?
- **Why it matters:** houses don't go through site plans. Home remodels only show up as **permits**, and the contact is a **private person**, which we've kept out of the list so far.
- **A)** Businesses only
- **B)** Include homes, business contacts only
- **C)** Include homes with homeowner names
- **D)** Ask Masiate
- ➡️ **Recommend A for now.** That's the site plan to permit path they described.

## Q4: How old is too old for an "early" lead?
- **Why it matters:** we pulled up to **24 months of planning records**. A site plan from a year ago has probably already picked its builder, so it's not early anymore.
- **A)** Last 90 days only
- **B)** Last 6 months
- **C)** 12 months, labeled by age
- **D)** Keep all, sort newest first
- ➡️ **Recommend B.** It keeps the list honest without making it too thin.

## Q5: Is this a one-time list or a service that keeps updating?
- **Why it matters:** the plan so far was a **one-time collection**. The best moment Masiate named, when a site plan turns into a permit, **only shows up if we check again** later.
- **A)** One-time list, done
- **B)** Monthly re-check
- **C)** Weekly re-check (paid service)
- **D)** One more re-check in 30 days, then decide
- ➡️ **Recommend D.** It proves the value before we build a regular re-check.

## Q6: Are they paying, and how will we know it worked?
- **Why it matters:** we have no agreed result. Without one, we can't tell if the list is good or just long.
- **A)** Free pilot, success = they call 10 and land 1 meeting
- **B)** Free pilot, success = they say it's useful
- **C)** Paid, success = one bid within 60 days
- **D)** Not decided yet
- ➡️ **Recommend A.** It's a clear yes or no you can use as a case study.
