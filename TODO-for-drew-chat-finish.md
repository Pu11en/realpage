# What you need to do: chat finish

All 6 chat tasks are built and saved on your computer only. Nothing is on GitHub yet. Your part is short.

## 1. ⏳ Try it yourself (about 1 minute)
- **Start it:** run `bash tooling/dev.sh` (it may already be running), then open **http://localhost:8765** and click **Ask**. No sign-in needed locally.
- **Check A:** the chat stays in the side panel. There is no "Open in full page" link. A green **Past chats** button opens your saved chats; **Back to chat** closes them.
- **Check B:** ask "Which buildings sold recently?" You should get about 40 words, 3 bold bullets, a **Next:** line and **Sources:**, with no codes or file names.
- **Check C:** click **Deep dive** on any Early Leads row twice. The second time is instant and says "Saved deep dive from <date>. Press ↻ to redo it." Press ↻ then **Try Again** to redo it fresh.

## 2. ⬜ Tell me if it's good
- If something looks wrong, just say what you saw in plain words and I'll fix it.
- If it's all good, say so. Only then I ask: **"Put it on GitHub?"** Nothing gets pushed before your OK.

## 3. ⬜ Small leftovers you may want to decide on (optional)
- ⚠️ **Date on saved deep dives** uses the server's clock, so an evening save in Texas can show tomorrow's date. Easy fix if you want it.
- ⚠️ **Follow-up suggestions** under an answer can sometimes offer a "call opener", which you said you don't want. Can be turned off.
- ⚠️ **Redo after a restart:** if the bot restarts, pressing ↻ on an old deep dive shows the saved copy instead of redoing it. "Fresh deep dive on …" always redoes.
- ⚠️ **"Which buildings sold recently?"** sometimes lands near the 60-word limit.
- Dates like "July 2026" (no day) are not auto-bolded; full dates are.

## 4. ✅ Things you do NOT need to do
- No keys, logins or payments needed.
- Automatic checks pass today (panel check and readable-answers check).
- Not in this round, by your choice: call scripts, shared team memory, speed work, auto-expiring saved deep dives.
