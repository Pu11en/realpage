# The PropertyStack demo video: one real call, start to finish

## 💰 What it's worth to RealPage, in one line
- **A RealPage salesperson finds out who is about to change software, and gets a call written for them, in about 2 minutes instead of about an hour of digging.**
- Right now a rep has to guess who to call. They spend a long time on Google, county records and apartment websites before every call, and most of their cold calls land at the wrong moment.
- PropertyStack catches the **moments when a building is most likely to switch software**:
  - ✅ **The building just sold.** New owners often bring their own software. We already find these from county sale records.
  - ✅ **The building is still being built.** It hasn't picked software yet. We already find these from city zoning filings.
  - ✅ **It runs on a competitor.** We already detect Yardi, Entrata, ResMan, AppFolio and others from the building's own website.
- **Money angle for the video:** one large building is hundreds of units paying monthly software fees for years. Winning one extra building a month pays for this tool many times over. Say it as "one building", not as made-up dollar figures, unless we check real pricing first.

## 🎬 The video, scene by scene (about 3 minutes)
### Scene 1: the problem (20 sec)
- You on camera: "I'm a RealPage rep. There are 204 apartment buildings in Plano and Richardson. Who do I call today?"
### Scene 2: the map of the market (30 sec)
- Show the software-share page: **66 buildings on Yardi, 36 on RealPage, 22 on Entrata, 52 unknown.**
- Line: "Every Yardi building is a building we could win."
### Scene 3: the call list (30 sec)
- Open Early Leads. The top is ranked by size and timing, and every row says **why** and links to the proof.
- Pick the star lead: **Vantage At Spring Creek, Richardson. 420 units. Sold June 2026 to a new owner (TREA Routh Creek TX Owner LP). Currently on Yardi.**
- Click the proof link so viewers see it's real public data, not made up.
### Scene 4: the AI prep (60 sec), the heart of the video
- In the chat you ask: "Prep me to call Vantage At Spring Creek."
- The AI answers with a **personalized call sheet**:
  - who they are, how big, who bought it and when, what software they run today;
  - a **30-second opener** that mentions the sale ("congrats on the Vantage acquisition, new owners usually review their software in the first 6 months…");
  - 3 questions to ask, 2 likely objections with answers ("we're locked into Yardi"), and the ask: a 20-minute meeting;
  - every fact has a source link.
- You click **Download** and get a one-page PDF call sheet.
### Scene 5: the call (30 sec)
- You role-play the first 20 seconds of the call, reading the script. (Don't call a real business on camera.)
### Scene 6: the close (20 sec)
- "This works for any city. Same thing for buildings still being built, like **Legacy Arapaho, 443 units, software not chosen yet.**"

## ⚠️ What's real today vs. what we still need to build

- **Film it on your computer (localhost)** first. Nothing goes on GitHub until you've tried it and said it's good.

## 🧭 How to pitch it to RealPage
- **Option A: a tool for their own sales team.** It finds who to call and preps the call. This is the easiest story for the video and it fits the job posting.
- **Option B: a new product they sell.** "Market intelligence" for property owners and investors, like which software their competitors use and what's being built nearby. It's bigger, but harder to prove in one video.
- Suggested order: lead with A in the video, and mention B in one sentence at the end as "where this could go".
