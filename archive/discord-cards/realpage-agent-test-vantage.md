# I asked the dashboard's AI to prep a real cold call. Here's what it did.

## ✅ The short answer: yes, it already works
- I typed one sentence into the AI that runs on your computer: **"Prep me to cold call Vantage At Spring Creek."**
- It came back in **19 seconds** with a full call sheet. Every fact had a source attached.
- **Nothing had to be built for this.** It's the chat you already have, running on your computer.

## 📋 What it gave back (shortened, but every point kept)
### What we know
- **420 units**, 2810 Routh Creek Pkwy, Richardson. Built in 2017.
- **Runs on Yardi.** We know because its resident login page is a Yardi page, and it gave the link.
- **Sold on June 11, 2026.** The seller was JDFW (Vantage) LLC and the buyer was TREA Routh Creek TX Owner LP. Source: county records.
- **Phone: (844) 493-7356.** No email found.
### Why they might switch
- New owners often re-pick their software, so **this is the window**.
- Yardi has its own support complaints in our review research.
- **RealPage's headquarters is in Richardson, the same city.** That's a friendly "we're your neighbor" hook.
- It warned itself: "Don't claim they're unhappy. We only know they use Yardi."
### The 30-second opener
- "Hi, this is [you] with RealPage. We're right here in Richardson. I saw Vantage At Spring Creek changed hands in June. New ownership usually means a fresh look at the software, and since you're on Yardi today, I wanted to ask how the transition is going. Do you have two minutes?"
### 3 questions to ask
- "Since the ownership change, has the new owner asked you to look at your software again?"
- "What bugs you most about Yardi: reporting, support, or something else?"
- "How much of your day goes into manual work the software should handle?"
### 2 objections, with answers
- **"We're happy on Yardi, switching is painful."** Every system has a pain. The real question is which pain you'd rather live with.
- **"Isn't RealPage the company in the government pricing lawsuit?"** Acknowledge it plainly: there's a settlement that stops RealPage from using competitors' private pricing data. Then steer back to the rest of RealPage's software (leasing, payments, accounting, and its AI tools).
### Its own warning at the end
- Our research notes are summaries of web searches, so treat them as medium confidence. Check which Yardi product they use before promising anything about moving them over.

## ⚠️ What it can NOT do yet
- **It can't look anything up online.** It only reads our building data and our RealPage research notes.
- So it can't find:
  - **who the new owner really is** (for example, whether "TREA" is a big investment firm and what else they own);
  - **the building's online reviews from residents.** Complaints like "maintenance never answers" are the best pitch material.
  - **the name of the manager or regional person** to ask for.
- It also can't **save the call sheet as a file** yet. You'd copy and paste it.

## 💰 What you can show a business, strongest first
- **1. "Who do I call today, and what do I say?"** The ranked list, then one click, then a call sheet. This works today. **Makes money**: more meetings booked.
- **2. "Know the buyer before the call."** The AI looks up the new owner and the building's resident reviews online. Needs a web-lookup tool added. This makes the pitch much more personal.
- **3. "Catch buildings before they pick software."** Buildings still being built (like Legacy Arapaho, 443 units). This works today with the same call-sheet flow.
- **4. "See the whole market at a glance."** Which software each building uses: 66 on Yardi, 36 on RealPage, 22 on Entrata. This works today and is good for managers, less so for reps.
- **5. "Take it with you."** Download the call sheet as a one-page file. Nice, but not needed for the tool to be useful.
