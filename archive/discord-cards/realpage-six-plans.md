# PropertyStack: six plans, every hole filled

## ✅ What's decided
- **Scout:** about 25 Sun Belt metros (not the Dallas area), scored on growth plus RealPage weakness, recent sales, and competitor pain. This computer's AI writes the "why this city" cards. Searches are capped at 1,500 per run.
- **Map:** a plain US map replaces the Master Table. Dots show RealPage's public reach; click one for a small proof card. Markers show where the scout is looking.
- **Deep-dive button:** on every building page and every Early Leads row. It fills in the chat, and you press send.
- **Deep dive:** always the same 8 parts, **Bottom line first**. It names the actual decision-maker from public pages (never guessed contact details). You take it away as text with the Copy button.
- **Chat:** normal answers **end** with a short Bottom line, with bigger and airier text.
- **Memory:** one shared memory for the whole team, no rules.
- **New areas:** always built. Missing county records get a "not available here" note. Limit of 400 buildings.
- 🅿️ **Parked:** "ready to sell" copy (welcome text, proof, price) until you've used the finished flow. Also Builder mode and the "At Risk" section.

## 📋 The six plans
- **1. Chat readability:** 4 tasks.
- **2. Map and deep-dive button:** 5 tasks, about 100 searches.
- **3. Shared memory:** 3 tasks.
- **4. Scout:** 6 tasks, up to 1,500 searches.
- **5. New area end to end:** 6 tasks. ⚠️ Waits for your pick from the scout.
- **6. Deep dive:** 4 tasks. ⚠️ Stops after 3 sample briefs for your review.

## 🏎️ Build order
- **Start now, all at once:** 2 (map and button), 4 (scout), and the first 3 tasks of 6 (deep dive, built here).
- **One after another** (they all change the AI's instructions): 1 (readability) → 3 (memory) → last step of 6 (deep dive into the site chat).
- **After you pick an area:** 5.
- Everything lands on **localhost only.** You try it, and after your OK it goes to GitHub and live.
