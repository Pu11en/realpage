# PropertyStack: five small plans, built fast

## 🏎️ How builds go fast
- Each plan is a short list of **small tasks**. One fresh AI session finishes each task in about 15–30 minutes, and every task gets tested automatically.
- They run with **/gowork**, and plans that don't depend on each other **build at the same time.**
- Everything lands on **localhost first.** You try it there, and after your OK it goes live.
- The planning is what makes building fast: every hole gets answered before a build starts, so the builders never stop to ask questions.

## 📋 The five plans
### 1. Chat readability (tiny)
- Every answer **ends with a short, clear summary** of what matters.
- **Bigger text, more spacing, less clutter** in the chat panel.
### 2. Site: map and "Deep dive" button
- A **simple US map** replaces the Master Table page. Dots show **where RealPage visibly is** (offices, case studies, known customers, buildings we've proven). Markers show **where the scout is researching.**
- A **"Deep dive in chat" button** on each building page drops that building into the AI chat.
### 3. Shared team memory (small)
- One memory for everyone, **no rules**. What one person's chat learns, every chat knows.
### 4. The scout (on your computer)
- Scans **Sun Belt metros** (Texas, Florida, Arizona, the Carolinas, not the Dallas area).
- Scores each city on **growth plus RealPage weakness, recent building sales, and competitor pain**, and writes a plain reason with links for each.
- Produces **3–5 area cards** for you to pick from, and puts markers on the map.
### 5. A brand-new area, end to end
- Build the area you pick: every building, its software, sales and new builds. That needs **new county record sources**, which is the hardest part.
- Its **Early Leads** show up on the site, and the deep dive works from there.

## ⏱️ Order
- ⏳ **1, 2 and 3 can build at the same time** right away.
- ⬜ **4 comes next.** The scout needs your Jina and web tools and runs on your computer.
- ⬜ **5 waits** for you to pick an area from the scout.
- 🅿️ **Parked:** Builder mode (editing the site and bot from the chat) and the "At Risk" section.
