# What the PropertyStack AI can and can't do today

## ✅ What it can do
- **Answer questions about all 204 buildings** in Plano and Richardson: their software, owners, sales, new builds and ranked leads. Every fact comes **with a source.**
- **Make tables** and comparisons, like "Yardi buildings that sold, biggest first."
- **Research one building online:** who manages it, resident reviews, news. About 1 to 2 minutes.
- **Write call prep:** an opener, questions, answers to objections and voicemail scripts, for a building you name.
- **Remember what you said earlier in the same chat.**
- **Use our RealPage research:** complaints, competitors, news and the lawsuit.

## 🚫 What it can't do yet
### Make things you can take away
- ⬜ **No downloadable files:** no PDF call sheet, no CSV list, no email file. You copy the text.
### Take action in the world
- ⬜ **Can't send emails, book meetings, or add leads to a CRM** like Salesforce or HubSpot.
- ⬜ **Can't save notes on a lead** ("called, no answer, try Tuesday"). Nothing tracks your progress.
### Know more
- ⬜ **Only Plano and Richardson.** It can't run the building finder for a new city by itself.
- ⬜ **No names of decision-makers.** It finds the management company, but not "Jane Smith, VP of Operations" or her email.
- ⬜ **Some websites block it** (Reddit and ApartmentRatings, for example).
### Remember and see
- ⬜ **No memory between chats.** A new chat starts from zero.
- ⬜ **Doesn't know which page or building you're looking at.** You have to name it.
- ⬜ **Can't look at images or screenshots.**
### Change things
- 🔒 **Can't change the data or the site.** That's on purpose. Builder mode would be the admin-only exception.

## 🏆 Which gap is "the winning thing"
A salesperson's real day is: **pick who to call → learn about them → call → write down what happened → follow up.** Today the AI covers the first two steps very well. Filling the rest turns it into a tool someone would use every day:
- **1. "Prep this call" button plus a one-page PDF:** one click on any lead gives a finished call sheet to download. It's the fastest thing to show, and it makes a great demo moment.
- **2. Find the decision-maker:** the person at the management company, with their public LinkedIn and company page. **This is the step every salesperson actually gets stuck on.**
- **3. Lead notes and follow-ups:** "called, left voicemail, try Tuesday." The AI then reminds you and writes the follow-up. That turns it from a research tool into a **daily work tool.**
- **4. "Add a city" from the chat:** it runs the finder and the new area shows up. That proves it scales past one city.
- **5. Buildings at risk for RealPage:** a daily list of RealPage buildings that just sold, for the team that keeps existing customers. It's the angle RealPage probably doesn't have yet.
