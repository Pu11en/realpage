# Would RealPage already have this? What's new, and what to show

## 🏢 What RealPage (and others) almost surely already have
- **Market data on every apartment building:** rents, occupancy, who owns and who manages it, and new buildings coming. RealPage bought a company called **Axiometrics** in 2017 to do exactly this, and sells it as "Market Analytics."
- **Building sales records:** RealPage partners with a sales-data firm (Real Capital Analytics). So they can see **"this building just sold."**
- **Normal sales tools:** almost every big software company uses Salesforce plus contact databases like ZoomInfo, and many now have AI tools that research a company before a call.
- **Their own customer list.** They know every building that runs RealPage.
- ⚠️ **So on their own, "new builds", "recent sales" and "AI call prep" are probably NOT new to them.** Your worry is right.

## 💎 What we found nobody sells (research from Sep 10)
- We checked **14 data companies**: RealPage's own, Yardi Matrix, CoStar, ALN, HelloData, 6sense, Enlyft, BuiltWith and others.
- **None of them advertise "which software each individual building runs."** The closest ones only know it per company ("Greystar uses Yardi"), not per building.
- **None of them track when a building switched software.**
- A separate search found **no public sign that RealPage tracks competitors' buildings** internally either: no job titles, no products, no press about it.
- ⚠️ **Honest limit:** "not public" doesn't prove "they don't have it." A sales rep's notes in Salesforce might hold some of it. But nobody has it **complete, for every building, with proof, kept up to date.**
- **That's our real asset:** in Plano and Richardson we know the software for 152 of 204 buildings, each with a proof link: **66 on Yardi, 36 on RealPage, 22 on Entrata**, and more.

## 🎯 The value we can show, strongest first
### 1. 🛡️ "Buildings you're about to lose" (protects money they already make)
- **4 buildings that run RealPage just sold** (1,071 units): Creekside At Legacy (380 units), The Ludlow (326), Woodlands Of Plano (232) and Alta Vista (133).
- New owners often switch software. That's RealPage revenue at risk, and the **customer-success team** can call them first.
- Keeping a customer is cheaper than winning a new one. This angle is rarely pitched, and it uses data we already have.
### 2. 🗺️ "Who runs what, building by building" (the thing nobody sells)
- A map of every building's software, with proof. It shows exactly where Yardi and Entrata are strong in each city.
- Useful for sales leaders, marketing and strategy, not just reps.
### 3. 🔁 "Who switched, and when" (not built yet)
- Old copies of websites (the Internet Archive) show **when a building's software changed.** That tells you who is leaving whom, and how fast.
- This is the strongest "nobody has this" proof. It's about one to two build sessions.
### 4. 🤖 "The AI that turns it into action" (works today)
- Ask about any building and get a researched brief in about 1.5 minutes, with a source on every fact.
- On its own it's not new. **Sitting on top of #1–#3, it is.**

## 💼 Remember what the job is
- The RealPage job you showed me is **AI Developer IV**: building AI tools and agent workflows that their own teams actually use, done safely.
- So this project wins you the job even if RealPage already has part of the data. It proves you can build a **real, safe, tested AI agent on real data**: read-only, sources on every answer, web research with guardrails.
- **For the job:** show how it's built. **For selling it:** show #1 and #3.
