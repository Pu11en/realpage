# All five PropertyStack plans are written and ready to build

## ✅ Your answers, locked in
- **Map dots:** clicking one shows a small card with the city, how many RealPage signs we found, and proof links.
- **Deep-dive button:** on every building page **and** on every Early Leads row. It fills in the chat, and you press send.
- **Scout:** about **25 Sun Belt metros** per run, not the Dallas area. Crawl4ai reads the pages for free, and **Jina searches are capped at 1,500 per run.**
- **Missing county records:** the area gets built anyway, with a clear "not available here" note. There's a limit of **400 buildings per area** unless you say otherwise.

## 📋 The five plans (each is small tasks, tested automatically)
### 1. Chat readability: 4 tasks
- Longer answers end with a bold **"Bottom line"** and 2–4 short bullets. Bigger text and more space.
### 2. Map and deep-dive button: 5 tasks
- A US map replaces the Master Table, with dots for RealPage's public reach and markers where the scout is looking.
- A "Deep dive in chat" button on buildings and on Early Leads rows.
- 💲 About 100 web searches to find RealPage's public reach.
### 3. Shared team memory: 3 tasks
- One memory for everyone, no rules. It survives restarts.
- 💲 A few cents per test run.
### 4. The scout: 6 tasks
- Scores about 25 metros on growth plus RealPage weakness, recent sales, and competitor pain.
- Produces the **top 5 area cards** and puts markers on the map.
- 💲 Up to 1,500 searches for the full run.
### 5. New area end to end: 6 tasks
- ⚠️ **Waits for you to pick an area** from the scout's cards.
- Builds every building there, its software, sales and new builds, and its Early Leads. An area picker on the site, and the chat can answer about it.
- 💲 About 2–4 lookups per building.

## 🏎️ Fastest order
- **Start together right away:** Plan 1 (chat readability), Plan 2 (map and button), Plan 4 (scout). They touch different files.
- **After Plan 1 finishes:** Plan 3 (memory). Both change the AI's instruction file, so they go one after the other.
- **After you pick from the scout's cards:** Plan 5 (new area).
- Everything lands on **localhost only.** You try it, and after your OK it goes to GitHub and live.
