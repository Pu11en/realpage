## Identity

- **Legal name:** RealPage, Inc. HQ Richardson, Texas.
- **What:** Cloud property management software for real estate owners/operators:
  leasing, property management, resident experience, payments, financials,
  analytics. Claims 24M+ rental units worldwide; positions AI as platform center.
- **Ownership:** Private — Thoma Bravo took it private Dec 21, 2020 at
  ~$10.2B enterprise value (~$9.6B equity per Fortune). Was NYSE-listed before.

## Product lines (to detail in 02-products/)

- **AI Revenue Management** — successor to YieldStar; algorithmic rent pricing.
  Legally constrained post-DOJ settlement (see 06-news/).
- **Lumina AI Suite** — new umbrella for RealPage AI capabilities.
- **Property Management Platform** — core PMS: leasing, ops, resident, payments,
  analytics, financials.
- **OneSite** — leasing/property management line (G2 lists it separately).
- Also: marketing/reputation management, utilities, AI leasing assistant.

## Legal posture (the defining public narrative)

- DOJ antitrust suit over rent-pricing algorithm (Sherman Act §1).
- Proposed settlement Nov 24-25, 2025: RealPage must stop using real-time
  confidential competitor data in pricing. Stipulation entered Mar 26, 2026;
  DOJ response to comments May 2026. See `06-news/doj-antitrust-timeline.md`.
- Separate private class action: In re RealPage Rental Software Antitrust Litig.
- Nov 2025: RealPage sought injunction vs New York's algorithmic-pricing ban.
