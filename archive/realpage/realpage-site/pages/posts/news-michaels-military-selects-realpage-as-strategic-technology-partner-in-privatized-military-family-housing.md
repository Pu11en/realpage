# Michaels Military Selects RealPage as Strategic Technology Partner in Privatized Military Family Housing
June 21, 2010
**(June 21, 2010)** —RealPage, Inc.([www.realpage.com](https://www.realpage.com)),
a leading provider of on-demand software to the rental housing industry, and Michaels Military Housing LLC, a leader in privatized military family housing, today announced that RealPage has been selected as strategic technology partner for Michaels Military Housing. RealPage will provide comprehensive software and technology services throughout Michaels Military’s portfolio of privatized military family housing.
The agreement includes providing property management software, 24×7 contact center services, web portals, and payments processing services to Michaels Military’s growing portfolio serving Army and Air Force installations throughout the country. Michaels Military is among the most experienced private partners providing family housing on military installations within the United States.
“Michaels Military Housing’s leadership in serving the housing needs of military families is based on our unyielding commitment to providing the best service possible to our residents, and choosing RealPage as a technology partner will enhance our capability for management and reporting.” said Ronald Hansen, senior vice president, Michaels Military. “I was so impressed with the visibility RealPage provides in all aspects of operations and management with easy, almost intuitive, software. RealPage will give us the most powerful platform for management and reporting that we have ever had.”
“The scope of the products and services involved in the partnership is exciting to RealPage,” said Dave McKenna, director of military housing for RealPage. “Michaels Military Housing serves multiple service branches on bases of all sizes. Its portfolio is like a microcosm of the privatized military housing market under one management team. It is an impressive organization and RealPage is proud to work with Michaels Military Housing.”
To find out more about RealPage Military Housing, please visit www.realpage.com/property-management-software/military-housing, or call our sales representative at 1-87-REALPAGE. **About RealPage**
Based in Carrollton, Texas, a suburb of Dallas, RealPage provides on-demand software solutions and services to the rental housing industry including a wide range of multifamily property types as well as single-family properties. Its six on-demand product lines include OneSite® property management systems that are tailored to the specific needs of conventional single-family, conventional multi-family, affordable HUD, affordable tax credit, privatized military housing, student housing and senior living properties; CrossFire® sales and marketing systems that manage marketing and leasing operations and enable owners and managers to originate, capture, track, manage and close more leads; YieldStar® asset optimization systems that enable owners and managers to optimize rents to achieve the overall highest yield, or combination of rent and occupancy, at each property; Velocity™ utility management systems that offer a complete range of billing and utility management services; LeasingDesk® risk mitigation systems that enable rental housing property owners and managers to reduce delinquency, liability and property damage risk; and OpsTechnology™ spend management systems that enable owners and managers to better control costs. Through its Propertyware subsidiary, RealPage also provides on-demand software solutions to single-family rentals and small, centrally-managed multi-family properties. Through its recent acquisition of certain assets of Domin-8 Enterprise Solutions, Inc., RealPage is adding additional property management systems with Tenant Pro, Spectra, i-CAM and other software solutions. Finally, RealPage also offers tightly integrated shared cloud services. For more information, call 1-87-REALPAGE or visit [www.realpage.com](https://www.realpage.com).
**About Michaels Military Housing, LLC**
Michaels Military Housing, LLC is solely owned, managed and operated by Michaels Development Company and its affiliate, Interstate Realty Management, companies of The Michaels Organization, based in Marlton, NJ. Michaels Military Housing (MMH) has been expertly staffed, organized and resourced to be a long-term partner with the Department of Defense in implementing the Military Housing Privatization Initiative passed by Congress in 1996. MMH is deeply committed and has the industry leading experience in developing, financing, construction, property management, and family support services to create communities that reflect a vivid sense of place, a strong environmental ethic, and a rich quality of life for military families.
