# RealPage Heads Back to Vegas for 2008 Conference
August 1, 2007
**(August 1, 2007)** —RealPage, Inc., a leading provider of products and services to the multifamily industry, today announced that its annual user conference will return to the Wynn Resort in Las Vegas for 2008. The conference will be held July 20—22.
The Wynn, which hosted the 2006 RealPage User Conference, is the only resort in the world with both a Mobil 5 Star and AAA 5 Diamond rating. Its lavishly appointed rooms and international shopping boutiques are just a few of the many amenities that attract businesses each year.
“We had a tremendous response from our attendees in 2006, which was our largest conference to date,” said Andrea Massey, senior vice president of marketing for RealPage. “We have already experienced the world-class accommodations and superb meeting rooms that the Wynn has to offer, and we are excited about returning there next year.”
RealPage recently wrapped up its 2007 conference, which was held July 22—24 at the Westin La Cantera Resort in San Antonio. Among the highlights were:
  * **Keynote Presentation** — CEO Steve Winn offered his view of the multifamily “ecosystem” and how it is the role of managers to create a healthy, balanced living environment that is profitable for owners, supportive of suppliers and beneficial to prospects and residents.
  * **Document Management System Launch** — A new system for providing storage, retrieval, security and archiving of all documents and forms associated with multifamily business processes and procedures was unveiled.
  * **New eSignature Technology** — Attendees were able to see the company’s new eSignature technology, which allows property management companies to conduct legal transactions over the Internet through a unique “ID, Seal & Store” process that offers validity and legal enforceability of residential leasing agreements.
  * **Rural Housing Product Launch** — The newest addition to RealPage’s affordable line of products—OneSite Leasing & Rents Rural Housing—was introduced. The system supports the Rural Development Section 515 program and works with the company’s HUD and Tax Credits systems to provide owners of affordable properties with a full solution for administering multiple subsidy portfolios.

The conference offered more than 30 sessions on property management, affordable property management, and marketing. More than 550 industry professionals attended the three-day event.
For more information on the 2008 RealPage User Conference, visit www.realpage.com or call Bernay Porter at 972-820-3370.
