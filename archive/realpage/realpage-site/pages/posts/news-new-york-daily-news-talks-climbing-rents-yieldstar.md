# New York Daily News Talks Climbing Rents and YieldStar

### _Publications shares insights into revenue management_
**
(June 27, 2014) —** In the _New York Daily News_ ‘ article, “High-tech, airline-style pricing is pushing New York City rents to new altitudes,” the reporter shares her ideas about why rents in New York City might be climbing. One reason, the use of revenue management systems, such as [YieldStar](https://www.realpage.com/insights-analytics/). These systems use a several statistics, including seasonal trends, leasing velocity and supply, as well as an apartment’s particular traits, to come up with the optimal asking price for a unit. And at times, can increase revenue for property management companies, by 7%.
