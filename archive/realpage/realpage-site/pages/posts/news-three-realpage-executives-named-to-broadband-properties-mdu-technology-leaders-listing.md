# Three RealPage Executives Named to Broadband Properties MDU Technology Leaders Listing
June 2, 2010
**(June 2, 2010)** —RealPage, Inc.([www.realpage.com](https://www.realpage.com/)), a leading provider of on-demand software to the rental housing industry, today announced that three company executives have been recognized for their contributions to multifamily broadband services by the Broadband Properties MDU Hall of Fame. Steve Winn, RealPage chairman and CEO, and Henry Pye, vice president of Velocity Advisory Services, have been named as Top 25 Leaders, and Keith Dunkin, vice president of YieldStar & MPF Research, has been recognized as one of 10 Rising Stars.
“The MDU Hall of Fame, a distinction bestowed every four years, is a list of key people in the multi-dwelling unit (MDU) industry and its suppliers who led, or provided input to, forward-looking management and technology teams,” said Scott DeGarmo, president and CEO, Broadband Properties LLC. “Our main criterion for being named to the list was cutting-edge impact on MDU broadband deployments in 2009-2010. We judged that without the participation of the Top 25 and the Rising Stars results would have been significantly different and probably less exciting.”
“We feel honored to be recognized by Broadband Properties for our service to our customers in helping them realize the highest value from the voice, video and Internet services that they provide to their residents,” said Winn. “We also congratulate our customers who made the Top 25 and Rising Star lists.”
RealPage industry experts focus on maximizing the value of residential technology assets and services that its customers provide to their residents. The Velocity Advisory Services team has worked with multifamily owners, managers, developers and general contractors to help them maximize revenue

**About RealPage**
Based in Carrollton, Texas, a suburb of Dallas, RealPage provides on-demand software solutions and services to the rental housing industry including a wide range of multifamily property types as well as single-family properties. Its six on-demand product lines include OneSite® property management systems that are tailored to the specific needs of conventional single-family, conventional multi-family, affordable HUD, affordable tax credit, privatized military housing, student housing and senior living properties; CrossFire® sales and marketing systems that manage marketing and leasing operations and enable owners and managers to originate, capture, track, manage and close more leads; YieldStar® asset optimization systems that enable owners and managers to optimize rents to achieve the overall highest yield, or combination of rent and occupancy, at each property; Velocity™ utility management systems that offer a complete range of billing and utility management services; LeasingDesk® risk mitigation systems that enable rental housing property owners and managers to reduce delinquency, liability and property damage risk; and OpsTechnology™ spend management systems that enable owners and managers to better control costs. Through its Propertyware subsidiary, RealPage also provides on-demand software solutions to single-family rentals and small, centrally-managed multi-family properties. Through its recent acquisition of certain assets of Domin-8 Enterprise Solutions, Inc., RealPage is adding additional property management systems with Tenant Pro, Spectra, i-CAM and other software solutions. Finally, RealPage also offers tightly integrated shared cloud services. For more information, call 1-87-REALPAGE or visit [www.realpage.com](https://www.realpage.com/).
