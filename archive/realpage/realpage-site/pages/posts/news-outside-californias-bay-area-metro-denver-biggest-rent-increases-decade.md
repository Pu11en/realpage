# Outside California’s Bay Area, metro Denver had biggest rent increases this decade
March 20, 2018
**DENVER POST** (March 20, 2018) – After the San Francisco Bay Area, metro Denver experienced the largest apartment rent increases this decade. But plenty of new supply should put future gains closer to the national average, according to a new report from RealPage, a real estate research firm.
Between 2010 to 2017, metro Denver apartment rents rose 469 percent, the fourth-highest increase in the country after three northern California metros: San Jose at 51.3 percent, Oakland at 51 percent and San Francisco at 48.6.
