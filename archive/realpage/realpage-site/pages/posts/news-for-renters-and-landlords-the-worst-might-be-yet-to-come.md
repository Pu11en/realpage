# For Renters And Landlords, The Worst Might Be Yet To Come
May 10, 2020
**Forbes** (May 10, 2020) -- Even though 80.2% of renter households paid rent fully or partially in the first week of May, [according to the National Multifamily Housing Council](https://www.forbes.com/sites/brendarichardson/2020/05/08/rent-payments-reach-80-in-may-amid-a-record-surge-in-unemployment/#40ea79076f87 "https://www.forbes.com/sites/brendarichardson/2020/05/08/rent-payments-reach-80-in-may-amid-a-record-surge-in-unemployment/#40ea79076f87"), pressure is starting to simmer for tenants as well as landlords already struggling to cover their expenses due to the financial repercussions of the pandemic.
