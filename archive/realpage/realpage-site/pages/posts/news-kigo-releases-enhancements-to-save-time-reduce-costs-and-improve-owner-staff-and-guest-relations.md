# Kigo Releases Enhancements to Save Time, Reduce Costs and Improve Owner, Staff and Guest Relations
October 27, 2015
### _New solutions increase efficiency for property managers of short-term and vacation rentals_
**(October 26, 2015) —** [Kigo](https://www.kigo.net), a leading provider of [vacation rental booking and channel management solutions](https://www.kigo.net/?utm_source=web&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=kigo-new-features-10-2015), recently expanded its online booking and channel management system to deliver owners a real-time view of their properties’ performance, vacation rental managers a more efficient way to operate, and guests an easier way to keep track of all reservation details.
These new solutions include a more robust operations management system, automated guest portals, owner-friendly dashboards, and a fully-integrated financial management system
“The expanded Kigo product, especially the operations management addition, has completely changed the way we operate and, ultimately, grow our business,” said Johnathan Browning, Owner of BeachWalk Vacation Properties. “Our staff can save up to 20 hours per week thanks to the automation of many of our processes, from sending out e-rental agreements to scheduling our property turns. Now, we can almost double our unit management without adding any additional staff thanks to Kigo’s newest round of product solutions.”
Users of the expanded Kigo product will receive:
  * **Comprehensive View of Your Operations:** Gain a 360-degree view of your operational performance, simplify the scheduling of cleaners and inspectors, immediately respond to guest issues, easily manage the dispatch of maintenance work, and efficiently monitor daily progress.
  * **Easy-to-Use Guest Portal Solution:** Automate the entire reservation process from sending contracts, property access details and reminders about check-ins, payments, etc., to helping your staff communicate more successfully with guests.
  * **Advanced, Fully-Integrated Financial Management System:** Sync your financials and accounting system based on history and rules with the QuickBooks® integration. This vacation rental accounting software also tracks the performance of each individual unit to provide a more impactful, actionable view for owners and managers.
  * **Real-time, Mobile-Friendly Owner Portals:** Share more in-depth insights into your properties faster and easier with those most important to your business through real-time statements that are both printer- and mobile-friendly.

“We want to empower our property managers, owners and guests of short-term and vacation rentals through our product, and with these newest solutions we now offer a more robust yet simplified end-to-end solution, one which will improve efficiencies and benefit all those involved in the vacation rental booking process,” said Rob Käll, senior vice president, Kigo.
To learn more about Kigo’s newest features, stop by booth #702 at the [VRMA Annual Conference](http://www.vrma.com/annualconference) held October 26-28 in New Orleans, La., for a product demonstration.
Kigo offers vacation rental websites, revenue management, online pricing and availability, booking software, automated reservation management, payment processing and insurance sales. Currently, Kigo is used by more than 1,300 vacation rental management companies worldwide. For more information on Kigo, visit: [https://www.kigo.net](https://www.kigo.net/).
