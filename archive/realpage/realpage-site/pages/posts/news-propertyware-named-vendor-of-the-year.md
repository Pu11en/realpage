# RealPage’s Propertyware Division Named Vendor of the Year by Property Management Inc.
October 18, 2017
RICHARDSON, Texas–([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/portal/site/home/))– [RealPage, Inc.](http://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.realpage.com&esheet=51700577&newsitemid=0&lan=en-US&anchor=RealPage%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=3a41d116e9e35d9aaf3ad830dd4886a5) (NASDAQ:[RP](http://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.realpage.com%2F&esheet=51700577&newsitemid=0&lan=en-US&anchor=RP&index=2&md5=f7f33b2cb6e48a7a285642ca6f56e9d1)), a leading global provider of software and data analytics to the real estate industry, announced today that its single family property management division, Propertyware, was awarded Vendor of the Year by Property Management Inc. (PMI).
**“The PMI Vendor of the Year award is chosen directly by franchisees and, for the second year in a row, they chose Propertyware as their top vendor,” said Steve Hart, President of PMI. “The partnership we have with Propertyware has been a key piece of our commitment to providing leading-edge tools to our network of property managers.”**
PMI franchisees selected Propertyware as vendor of the year based on the value the company offers single family property management companies. PMI franchisees participate in an educational program designed by Propertyware that enables them to standardize processes, implement tools and scale their businesses.
**“Propertyware helps my business by automating many essential tasks and communications with tenants, owners and vendors,” said Jason Evanchak, Owner of PMI Nevada. “It is essential to our efficiency and effectiveness and allows each member of my team to communicate, collaborate and serve our clients well.”**
The partnership between Propertyware and PMI began in 2011. It has since evolved to support the growing needs of vendors, owners and tenants, with a heavy emphasis on training and education.
The award was delivered at the 2017 PMI Summit in Snowbird, Utah, which took place September 20-22.
