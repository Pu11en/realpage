# RealPage Chief Economist to Speak to Arizona Multihousing Association
September 18, 2015
### _Greg Willett to talk at 2015 Perspectives & Projections Conference_
**
(September 28, 2015) —** RealPage’s Chief Economist and Vice President of [MPF Research](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/) Greg Willett will speak on October 9 at the Arizona Multihousing Association’s 2015 Perspectives and Projections Conference.
In this session, he will discuss apartment market trends and the behaviors of Arizona renters.
