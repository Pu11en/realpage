# InstaManager’s Hoffman Published in VRMA Magazine
July 11, 2014
### _Article discusses ways to get more bookings and increase revenue_
**(July 11, 2014) —**[Vacation Rental Manager’s Association (VRMA)](http://www.vrma.com/) magazine’s summer issue featured an article written by InstaManager’s Sales and Business Development Director Matt Hoffman. In his article, “Five Easy Steps to More Bookings and Increased Revenue,” Hoffman shares the many challenges faced by today’s professional property management and concludes with practical solutions that drill-down on ideas like: consolidating your marketing channels, optimizing search engine listings and moving toward the cloud, to name a few.
To read the full article, you will need to become a member of VRMA. To become a member, visit: <http://www.vrma.com/membership>.
