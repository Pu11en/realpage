# RealPage Rental Housing Experts to Speak at NMHC Research Forum
March 16, 2016
### _Jay Parsons and Rich Hughes to talk about market-rate apartments_
**(March 17, 2016) —** MPF Research’s Director of Analytics and Research Jay Parsons and RealPage’s Head of Data Science Rich Hughes will speak at this year’s NMHC Research Forum held April 6-7 in Chicago, Ill.
The pair will discuss various topics related to market-rate apartments, such as demographics and affordability, as well as provide attendees with a preview of some of the newest technology coming to the industry related to heat maps.
To learn more about the industry, visit: <https://nmhc.org/Research16/>.
