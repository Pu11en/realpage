# Propertyware® Selected to Present at NARPM® Annual Convention and Trade Show
October 4, 2013
### _Highlighting their knowledge and insight on the latest web trends, Propertyware will present two sessions at this year’s NARPM® Conference_
**(October 3, 2013) —** Propertyware, an industry leader in property management solutions for single-family and low density rental units through a full suite of self-provisioning on-demand tools, will present two sessions at this year’s National Association of Residential Property Managers (NARPM®) Annual Convention and Trade Show at the Hyatt Regency La Jolla in San Diego on October 16-18, 2013.
Celebrating its 25th year as the leading professional and educational event for the residential property management industry, the NARPM® Conference will present experts from business management, operations and technical fields from around the country who will be leading sessions and showcasing products designed to help property managers improve and grow their businesses.
The two Propertyware sessions to be presented are:
**Back to Basics with SEO 101 (Thursday, October 17, 2013)** : This session will look at the ever-changing landscape of search engine optimization (SEO) and how property managers can leverage SEO techniques in their Internet marketing plans. Led by Keith Brown, director of SEO for RealPage, the session will take a look at the core parts of SEO and how it works together with today’s customer-focused web.
**Facebook® Changes and What They Mean to You (Friday, October 18, 2013)** : This session will explore two major changes Facebook® made to its platform ̶ Graph Search and the over haul of the News Feed. Michael Cunningham, social media manager for RealPage, will take a look at how these changes impact property managers and share steps for optimizing your Facebook® page for these new updates.
“Having the opportunity to share our knowledge and insight on the latest web trends is another way that Propertyware can positively impact our industry,” said Jamie Clymer, president of Propertyware. “Our clients rely on Propertyware and we will continue to participate in events such as NARPM® to share our knowledge, build relationships and understand how we can better serve the needs of our clients to optimize their success.”
In addition to these sessions, Propertyware will also be exhibiting at this year’s event. Attendees can visit Propertyware in booths 35 and 50 during the published exhibit hall hours, view a live demonstration of Propertyware and learn more about anticipated new product offerings, including Propertyware Contact Center, eRenterPlan and the highly-anticipated Propertyware Inspections for Android™.
Prior to the convention opening, Propertyware is sponsoring the 4th Annual Past Presidents’ Charity Golf Tournament, which takes place on Tuesday, October 15 at the Twin Oaks Golf Course in San Marcos, California. This tournament benefits the American Cancer Society® and the Nice Guys of San Diego, Inc., an organization that has been dedicated to helping out needy San Diego citizens since 1979.
