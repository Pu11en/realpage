# Huntsville rents rise as city keeps growing
December 9, 2019
**AL.com** (December 9, 2019) — Apartment rents in Huntsville grew by 7.6 percent in July, “well ahead of the 2.9 percent national norm,” according to RealPage, a national software and data analytics provider to the real estate industry.
