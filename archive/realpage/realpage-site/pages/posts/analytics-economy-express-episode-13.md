# RealPage Economy Express Episode 13
![Photo of Arben Skivjani, Deputy Chief Economist and Director of Forecasting](https://s.realpage.com/storage/files/blog/authors/images/2022/09/arben-skivjani.jpg)
_by_ [ Arben Skivjani](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/authors/arbenskivjani/)
#### Episode 13: Will we have another rate hike?
  * U.S. employment was up by a surprisingly strong [336,000](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/us-job-growth-beyond-expectations-september/) jobs in September, notably surpassing economists’ expectations, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
  * Average hourly earnings rose by 4.2% over the past year, outpacing[ inflation](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/inflation-holds-steady-september/).
  * Inflation reports showed a mixed picture, with producer and consumer prices increasing, including a notable 4.1% annual rise in Core CPI.
  * Retail Trade Sales saw a 3% annual increase, reflecting steady consumer spending, according to the U.S. Census Bureau’s Advanced Monthly Sales Report.
  * The Federal Reserve anticipates continued improvement in the labor market but does expect to increase prices during the holiday shopping season.
