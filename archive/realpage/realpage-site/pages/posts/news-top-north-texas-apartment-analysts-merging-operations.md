# Top North Texas apartment analysts merging operations
January 24, 2017
**DALLAS NEWS** (January 24, 2017) – Two North Texas apartment analysts are merging.
Richardson-based RealPage Inc. – which owns MPF Research – said Tuesday it is buying apartment market data firm Axiometrics in a deal valued at $75 million.
