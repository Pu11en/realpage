# Multifamily market permits continue to decline
April 3, 2020
_The month-to-month declines started before the spread of COVID-19_
**HousingWire**(April 3, 2020) -- Prior to stay-at-home orders and businesses closing, the multifamily market was already seeing a decline in both permits and starts, a new report from **RealPage** said.
Permits fell to 415,000 units on a seasonally adjusted annual basis in February, marking the second-lowest annual rate in 17 months, down 20.2% from January and 5% from February 2019.
