# MPF Research & YieldStar Speakers at Marcus & Millichap Forum
September 24, 2014
### _Experts Dunkin and Willett will speak on NOI and the apartment market_
**(September 24, 2014) —** This year’s Marcus & Millichap Multifamily Forum DFW will be held on October 7. RealPage and MPF will have representation as a speaker and panelist with MPF Research’s Greg Willett and YieldStar’s Keith Dunkin serving as experts.
Mr. Dunkin, RealPage senior vice president, YieldStar, will be part of the “Boost Your NOI: Best Practices for Property Management” panel starting at 11 a.m., while Mr. Willett, MPF Research’s vice president, research & analysis, will share with audiences the apartment market trends at 2:15 p.m.
To find out more about the conference, visit: <http://mmdallasforum.com/>.
