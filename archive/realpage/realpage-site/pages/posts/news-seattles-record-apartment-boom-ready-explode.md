# Seattle’s record apartment boom is ready to explode
December 30, 2016
**THE SEATTLE TIMES (December 30, 2016)** – The apartment boom in Seattle has already reached historic heights — more units opened in each of the past four years than ever before.
Now, the real boom is about to begin.
Seattle is set to see almost 10,000 new market-rate apartments open in 2017, nearly twice as many as in any other year in the city’s history.
