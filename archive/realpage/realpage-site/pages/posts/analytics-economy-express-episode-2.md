#### Episode 2: To default or not to default?
  * The U.S. economy added [253,000 jobs in April](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/job-growth-surprisingly-high-april/), bringing total gains this year to about 1.1 million.
• Average hourly earnings only grew by 0.5% in April.
• The [Consumer Price Index](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/cooling-rent-growth-finally-shows-in-cpi/) rose by 40 basis points in April.
• Retail sales only increased by 0.4% in April, falling short of expectations.
• Consumer sentiment index dropped by 9%, indicating a lack of confidence among consumers.
