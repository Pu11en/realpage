# MPF Research Drives Real Estate Discussion in Media
June 24, 2016
### _Findings in WSJ, Yahoo Finance, among others_
**(June 24th, 2016) —** Proving to be a pertinent resource, publications spotlight MPF Research findings in order to analyze the current real estate market. The articles illuminate that in a booming rental market, concern still stirs within low-income renter households as the landlord’s pockets grow deeper.
