# MPF Vice President to Speak at PCBC Conference
June 10, 2015
### _Greg Willett to discuss the economy and multifamily sector_
**(June 10, 2015) —** Greg Willett, [MPF Research’s](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/) vice president, will serve as a guest speaker at this year’s PCBC Conference held June 24-25 in San Diego, Calif.
In the session, “The Economy and the Multifamily Sector” held June 25 from 9 a.m.-9:45 a.m., Mr. Willett will discuss current economic issues and the impacts on national and regional multifamily housing fundamentals. An expert in tracking and evaluating trends in the multifamily market, Willett will address the pending issues of oversupply in the urban submarkets and highlight new development trends in the suburbs.
