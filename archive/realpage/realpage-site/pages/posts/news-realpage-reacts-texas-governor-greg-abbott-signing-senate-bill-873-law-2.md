# Texas Senate Bill 873 Media Coverage
June 9, 2017
RICHARDSON, Texas–(BUSINESS WIRE)– RealPage, Inc. (NASDAQ:RP), a leading global provider of software and data analytics to the real estate industry, shares that Texas Governor Greg Abbott has signed Senate Bill 873 into law. The new law, which modifies the Texas water code, will benefit apartment owners, operators and their residents throughout the State of Texas.
