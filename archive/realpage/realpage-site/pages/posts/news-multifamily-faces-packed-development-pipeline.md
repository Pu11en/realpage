# Multifamily Faces a Packed Development Pipeline
February 4, 2020
_The sector is expected to add 330,000 new units in 2020, up from 304,000 a year ago._

And early indications are that 2020 will be another boffo year. The question is after several big years of hefty additions whether the market can digest the units that are in the works.
