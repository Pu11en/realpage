# RealPage eMoney Technology Gives Residents Another Payment Option
December 14, 2015
### _Houston Chronicle, Houston Business Journal and Style Online Feature Company’s eMoney Order Software_
**(December 14, 2015) —** With [RealPage eMoney Order](https://www.realpage.com/online-payments/), residents are provided another payment option—being able to pay with cash at thousands of retail locations. Residents don’t have to purchase multiple money orders for rent and don’t have to worry about fraud or theft associated with paper items. Each payment is validated in real time to ensure that partial payments and payments from residents in eviction are not accepted.
