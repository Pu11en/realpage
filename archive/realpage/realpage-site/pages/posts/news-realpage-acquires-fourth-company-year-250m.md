# RealPage acquires its fourth company this year for $250M
August 3, 2017
**DALLAS BUSINESS JOURNAL** (August 3, 2017) – In its fourth company acquisition this year, Richardson-based RealPage Inc. (Nasdaq: RP) has decided to purchase On-Site, a leasing platform for property managers and renters, in a deal valued at about $250 million.
The leasing platform pairs well with RealPage’s business by working in the multifamily and single-family housing industries.
