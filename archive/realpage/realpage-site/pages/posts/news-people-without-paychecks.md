# People without paychecks: April 1 rent bills loom
April 1, 2020
**Newsday (April 1, 2020)** - Long Island tenants, many of them devastated by job losses and health concerns because of the coronavirus pandemic, now face a new crisis as their rent comes due Wednesday for the first time since the state ordered non-essential businesses to shut down.
Erica Prince, 49, was just about to start a new job as an aide at a home health care service in Hauppauge last month when the company suspended all training for new hires.
