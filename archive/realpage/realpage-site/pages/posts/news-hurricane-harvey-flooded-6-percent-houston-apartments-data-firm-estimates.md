# Hurricane Harvey flooded 6 percent of Houston apartments, data firm estimates
October 4, 2017
**HOUSTON CHRONICLE** (October 4, 2017) – About 6 percent — or 43,000 units — of Houston’s apartment supply were affected by Hurricane Harvey’s flooding, according to data from Richardson-based RealPage, a real estate data firm.
