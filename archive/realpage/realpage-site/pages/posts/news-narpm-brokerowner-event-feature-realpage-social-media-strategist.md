# NARPM Broker/Owner Event to Feature RealPage Social Media Strategist
February 2, 2015
### _Jade Smith to speak on visual technologies and online reputation_
**
(February 5, 2015) —** RealPage’s social media strategist Jade Smith will speak at the [NARPM Broker/Owner Retreat](http://www.narpmbrokerowner.org/schedule), held February 16-18 in Las Vegas, Nev.
Ms. Smith will be speaking on two topics: “Establishing, Managing and Protecting Your Company’s Online Reputation” and “Let’s Get Visual: Attract Tenants with New Technology”
For more information on the event, visit: <http://www.narpmbrokerowner.org/>.
