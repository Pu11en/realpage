# RealPage Snaps Up Another Company Toward Goal Of Being A $1B Powerhouse
June 20, 2017
**BISNOW** (June 20, 2017) – On the heels of purchasing Axiometrics in January and Rainmaker’s LRO in February, Richardson, Texas-based RealPage has acquired another multifamily company. RealPage snapped up American Utilities Management, the Chicago-based multifamily utilities and energy management solutions platform, for approximately $70M.
