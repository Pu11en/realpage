# Why developers aren’t building affordable apartments in Houston
December 9, 2016
**HOUSTON BUSINESS JOURNAL (December 9, 2016)** – In 2016, developers delivered more than 20,200 new apartment units across Houston.
About 96 percent of these new units — 19,400 of them — were Class A apartments, charging on average about $1,030 per month, according to Marcus & Millichap’s year-end multifamily report.
