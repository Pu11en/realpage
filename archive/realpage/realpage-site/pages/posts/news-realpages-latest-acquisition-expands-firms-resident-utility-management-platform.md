# RealPage’s Latest Acquisition Expands Firm’s Resident Utility Management Platform
June 19, 2017
**MULTIFAMILY EXECUTIVE** (June 19, 2017) – Real estate software and data analytics firm RealPage announced its acquisition of American Utility Management (AUM) today in a move that expands the company’s resident utility management (RUM) platform providing billing, invoice processing, and bill payment services to more than 2.5 million rental units.
