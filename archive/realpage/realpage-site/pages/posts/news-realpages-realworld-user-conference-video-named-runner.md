# RealPage’s RealWorld User Conference Video Honored by AIM
May 8, 2014
### _Da Stache video earn second place honors from the Apartment Internet Marketing Video Awards_
**(May 8, 2014)** –The [Apartment Internet Marketing (AIM) conference](https://www.aimconf.com/), held May 5-7, hosted their awards ceremony for the annual AIM Video Awards. RealPage was on-hand as a sponsor for the event, as well as a nominee for the AIM Video Awards in the category of “Supplier/Partner Company Culture.” Their video, [“Da Stache”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lxe7qftT7b0&list=PLVzNHy6rYs4Zw7ablYwXaUJT7zWwOlQSn) promoting the company’s RealWorld User Conference keynote speaker Mike Ditka, was awarded runner-up. Congrats to our marketing team!
