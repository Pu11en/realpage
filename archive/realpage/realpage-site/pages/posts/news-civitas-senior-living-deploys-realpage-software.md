# Civitas Senior Living Deploys RealPage Software to Streamline Business Processes and Improve Resident Experience
November 9, 2017
RICHARDSON, TEXAS (November 7, 2017) – [RealPage, Inc.](https://www.realpage.com/) (NASDAQ: RP), a leading global provider of software and data analytics to the real estate industry, announced that Civitas Senior Living is implementing RealPage’s [Community Management](https://www.realpage.com/senior/), Accounting, OneSite Budget, Resident Payment and Business Intelligence software solutions across its portfolio of 42 communities that include 4,288 units.
Civitas Senior Living, based in Fort Worth, Texas, chose RealPage’s solutions to consolidate systems and improve accounting functions and reporting, company officials said. The package will eliminate duplicate data entry from the applicant stage to move-in to scheduled billing and financials. Before working with RealPage, Civitas used multiple, non-integrated systems.
“RealPage solutions will be great for Civitas,” Chief Operating Officer Cooper Vittitow said. “Thanks to the system’s real-time reporting, we’ll be able to manage our accounting process with pin-point accuracy. Our month-end reporting will be more efficient, so our organization can be more effective.”
President Wayne Powell said the RealPage solutions will enable onsite staff to improve the resident experience.
“Civitas Senior Living operates with passion,” he said. “RealPage solutions will allow us to handle business processes in a more efficient way so we can focus on bringing that passion and quality of life to the seniors and families who trust us to care for them and their loved ones.”
RealPage’s Community Management software is a multi-faceted platform that mobilizes maintenance processes while measuring performance and managing conditions. The software also creates accounting and budgeting transparency and promotes efficient [document management](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/document-management/), featuring electronic signature provided through integration with DocuSign®. RealPage [Business Intelligence](https://www.realpage.com/insights-analytics/business-intelligence/)™ accelerates profitability through increased revenue and reduced expenses. The solution offers valuable insight and clarity into portfolio performance, and immediately identifies risk, opportunity and contextualized analysis impacting metrics that drive performance.
