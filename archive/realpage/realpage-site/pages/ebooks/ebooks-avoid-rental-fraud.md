#### AI screening and prequalification help multifamily housing safeguard operations against rental fraud
Advanced screening technology and comprehensive prequalification equip property managers with the tools and knowledge to make informed business decisions. Factors like accurate scoring, predictive outcomes, income verification, identity verification and early detection can give multifamily property companies a more complete picture of rental applicants.
In this short eBook, learn how multifamily property managers can avoid leasing fraud by:
  * Implementing advanced screening technology that offers the ability to predict an applicant’s ability and willingness to pay rent
  * Conducting comprehensive prequalification with updated identity intelligence and pattern recognition to detect fraudulent applications
  * Enhancing the property leasing process across teams with a property leasing workflow that outsmarts fraudsters before they move in
