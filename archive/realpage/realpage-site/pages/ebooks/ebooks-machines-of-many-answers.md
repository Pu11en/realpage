## Why AI Analytics Disagree and What Multifamily Leaders Must Do About It
AI has changed multifamily analytics forever. Owners and operators now have access to powerful AI tools capable of generating instant insights, forecasts, and recommendations. But when every stakeholder has their own AI analyst, what happens when the answers don’t match?
In this new research paper from RealPage AI Strategy & Analytics, Rich Hughes and Julie Chow explore the growing challenge of competing AI-generated analyses and why the future of trust in multifamily depends on shared definitions, transparency, and coordination.
Inside the paper, you’ll discover:
  * Why AI tools can produce conflicting “truths” from the same data
  * The hidden risk of AI overconfidence and analytical drift
  * The “Magic Test” framework for evaluating AI-assisted analysis
  * A maturity model for navigating the multi-agent world
  * Practical steps for creating shared analytical standards across owners and operators

As AI adoption accelerates across the industry, the organizations that win won’t simply have better tools they’ll have better coordination.
