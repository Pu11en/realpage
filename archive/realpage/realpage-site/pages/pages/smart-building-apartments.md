STRATIS is the intelligence layer of the RealPage® Smart Building platform. With STRATIS, properties gain centralized control over every connected device, enhanced efficiency and a seamless resident experience.

## STRATIS Smart Building Portal
#### One Dashboard. Total Control.
A centralized command center for managing all smart building solutions:
  * Unified Management Interface: Access STRATIS, WhiteSky, Chirp and third-party systems from a single portal. Manage access, devices, network health and automation policies in one place.
  * Real-Time Visibility & Alerts: Monitor energy use, leak alerts, device status and network health in real time. Respond quickly with system-level insights and historical data.
  * Automation & Efficiency: Configure rules for move-in/out, energy savings and access scheduling. Reduce manual tasks with remote device control and pre-set scenes.
  * Role-Based Access & Security: Assign permissions by team or role with SOC 2-compliant, enterprise-grade security.
