Formerly Axiometrics, Realpage Market Analytics is a comprehensive market intelligence platform specifically designed to empower all roles within the multifamily industry. It provides insights into market, submarket, niche and asset performance, giving stakeholders the insights needed to make informed decisions quickly.
#### What are the benefits of using the Market Analytics solution from Realpage?
Realpage Market Analytics offers discuss intelligence on supply, demand, rent, concessions, occupancy trends, forecasts and competitor analysis. Owners, investors, developers, operators, lenders, and property managers use Market Analytics to make key business decisions, determine strategy, improve performance, allocate resources, and identify opportunities and potential risks.
#### What data sources does Realpage Market Analytics utilize?
Realpage Market Analytics integrates Realpage’s proprietary historical apartment data, publicly available unit-level data (updated daily), MSCI Real Capital Analytics’ sales transaction data, and precise econometric modeling to provide comprehensive market intelligence.
#### What are Realpage Daily Property Updates?
Realpage daily property updates are a Lumina AI-powered feature in Market Analytics that enables critical business decisions based on the latest, most accurate information. Each day, Lumina AI finds, acquires, and processes publicly available property data, capturing and intelligently deriving key information such as asking rents, concessions, floor plans, availability, and occupancy.
