### Compliance Services for Affordable Sites
Realpage Compliance Services provides compliance services for all types of Affordable programs, including Low Income Housing Tax Credit (Section 42), tax exempt bond, Section 8, HOME, HUD and Rural Development. Our services include:
#### Compliance Subscription
Using our years of expertise in the various federal, state and local Affordable Housing programs, we review your move-in certifications for pre-approvals within 1 business day and annual recertififications within 2 business days, minimizing compliance delays and ensuring ongoing compliance.
#### Bulk Audits
Realpage will provide an unbiased review and assessment of move-ins and annual recertifications for each unit, certifying those that are in compliance. If we determine you are not in compliance, we can provide the advice and tools you need to get back on track - and stay on track.
#### On-Site Compliance Services
Our compliance professionals arrive on-site, complete an audit, provide training, or assist in certifying applicants and/or existing residents for eligibility as low-income residents under the Low Income Housing Tax Credit program guidelines or other affordable housing program guidelines. RPCS has reviewed over 300 federal, state and local level programs across the country.
