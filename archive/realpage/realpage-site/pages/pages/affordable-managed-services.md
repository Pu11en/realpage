## Realpage Compliance Services
The Realpage Compliance Services team can help you stay ahead of compliance issues to mitigate risk, maximize occupancy and stay in compliance with federal, state and local government Affordable Housing regulations. Our compliance experts assure the safety and security of personally identifiable information (PII). Realpage’s proprietary compliance portal gives you access to real-time status updates on files and the ability to maintain an audit trail for file handling.
Our compliance team has extensive knowledge of Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) Section 42, tax-exempt bonds, Section 8, HOME, Rural Development and other local housing programs. Their unbiased assessment of applicants ensures you only get qualified residents. They’ll also assist with state agency correspondence, required reporting and training your in-house compliance staff.
