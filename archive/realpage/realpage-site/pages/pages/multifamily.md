## What is Property Management Software?
Property management software is a specialized digital platform designed to help owners, property managers, and investors efficiently oversee and streamline daily operations across multifamily portfolios.
### Key Features of Property Management Software:
#### Leasing Management
Create and manage leases, screen tenants, and track applicants throughout the leasing process.
#### Financial Management
Automate rent collection, process [online payments](https://www.realpage.com/online-payments/ "Online Payments"), and generate detailed [financial reports](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/accounting-software/ "Accounting Software") for better decision-making.
#### Maintenance Management
Handle [maintenance requests](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/maintenance-management/ "Maintenance Management"), track progress, and communicate seamlessly with tenants and vendors to ensure timely resolutions.
#### Renter Communication Tools
Facilitate clear and efficient communication through online portals for residents, owners, and property managers.
By automating repetitive tasks and leveraging AI-powered, data-driven insights, multifamily management software helps boost operational efficiency, increase net operating income (NOI), and simplifies property management for teams of all sizes.
