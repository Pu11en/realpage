## Maintenance Management FAQ
#### What is maintenance management software?
Maintenance management software streamlines property operations by automating service requests, scheduling work orders, tracking vendor activity, and providing mobile tools for on-site technicians.
#### How does Realpage Maintenance Management improve facilities operations?
Realpage centralizes maintenance requests, compliance, and asset tracking across all properties, helping operators reduce response times, cut costs, and enhance resident satisfaction.
#### Can maintenance technicians use the software on mobile devices?
Yes. Realpage includes a mobile facilities app with offline functionality, voice-to-text features, and real-time updates to keep maintenance teams productive anywhere on property.
#### How does Realpage improve resident satisfaction?
Faster response times, real-time service updates, and streamlined workflows ensure maintenance issues are resolved quickly, leading to higher resident retention and satisfaction.
#### What makes Realpage different from other maintenance management solutions?
Realpage combines facilities management, compliance, asset tracking, mobile tools, and portfolio-wide analytics into one unified solution purpose-built for the property management industry.
#### Why do businesses need facilities management software?
Facilities management software helps property management companies streamline maintenance operations, reduce costs, and improve efficiency across properties. By centralizing work orders, asset tracking, and vendor coordination, apartment teams can respond faster to issues and prevent costly downtime. It also improves visibility into property operations, enabling better decision-making and higher resident satisfaction.
#### What are the key features and benefits of a facilities maintenance management software?
Facilities maintenance management software typically includes features like work order management, asset tracking, preventive maintenance scheduling, mobile access, and maintenance reporting dashboards.
These features help property teams:
  * Track and complete maintenance requests efficiently
  * Automate preventive maintenance to reduce unexpected repairs
  * Monitor asset performance and lifecycle
  * Improve technician productivity with mobile tools
  * Gain real-time insights through reporting and analytics

The result is faster response times, lower operating costs, and improved resident experiences across multifamily portfolios.
