###### SmartSource IT Health Assessment
# Holistic Security Best Practices for Your Property Management Company.
Ensuring your property management business has covered all the risks of the rental industry sounds impossible. But it’s not.
Realpage® SmartSource IT Health Assessment (SSIT Health Assessment) is the holistic approach to measuring your security posture by covering topics across people, processes and technology. In addition, the SSIT Health Assessment Tool is a risk audit designed to provide information and recommendations about best practices for security within your company.
## Prescriptive guidance and recommendations
SmartSource IT evaluates your Microsoft licenses to determine if you’re utilizing the most beneficial license, checks your email configuration and identifies security risks. Then, we review the findings and make best practice recommendations that either you can implement or SmartSource IT can execute.
You’ll get a:
  * Security score
  * Action items
  * Roadmap to mitigate risk
  * Deployment plan

## Peace of mind in just moments
We’ll explain the process, review the deliverables and request system access. Then your Realpage Solution Architect will ask you questions to evaluate risk and setup. Finally, the Solution Architect will do a deep dive that takes all of this into consideration:
  * Microsoft tenant configuration
  * Hardware
  * Microsoft license
  * Antivirus/malware
  * Single Sign-On, Multifactor Authentication & Mobile Device Management
  * Outstanding patches
  * Microsoft Modern Workplace model

## Actionable Security Plans on the spot
Once your assessment is done, the Solution Architect will share results and recommendations right then. They will also answer any questions to help you choose the right post-assessment for you from 3 available options:
1. Utilize your in-house IT team or current IT provider to execute recommendations.
2. SmartSource IT can execute recommendations with a one-time SOW project.
3. SmartSource IT can execute recommendations as part of an ongoing end-user support contract.
Easy, fast, reliable and actionable. Secure your property management company in hours, not months.
