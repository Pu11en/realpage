##  LOFT (formerly ActiveBuilding) Saves Time and Lowers Costs
Once leasing is complete, applicants can begin transitioning to residency, even before move-in day, with LOFT — the resident portal that provides mobile access to every aspect of apartment living. LOFT’s convenient, in-app payment options help reduce the risk of fraud and theft. And the rent gets paid that much sooner.
Self-service maintenance requests allow efficient tracking and dispatching of staff and streamline resident management — all while relieving staff to focus on higher-value tasks. It’s also easy to keep residents and staff connected and informed in real time, all via the channel they prefer.
##  Streamline Operations & Reward Residents for Completing Important Tasks
Streamline your affordable housing operations and reduce staff overhead by launching a loyalty rewards program right from LOFT You can offer points to residents to encourage them to:
  * Complete paperwork for recertifications
  * Pay rent online
  * Comply with ESG programs like recycling
  * Provide feedback through resident polls
  * Report maintenance issues and keeping units in good condition
