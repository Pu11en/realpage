## A great renter experience starts with one platform that does it all.
Student renters demand more service with less interruption. For multifamily operators, that means maximizing technology to build a connected living experience. Realpage Student Living is a one platform approach to delivering an amazing student living experience without adding to overhead.
## Simplify Renter Services
Figuring out living on your own can be stressful for students (and their parents) and often time consuming for your staff. With Realpage Student Living, students can manage their renter services 24-hours a day such as:
  * Online Payments
  * Utility Billing
  * Renewals
  * Amenity Reservations
  * Package Tracking
  * Guest Access
  * Building Communication
  * Renters Insurance
  * Renter (and Guarantor) Communication

Simplifying everyday apartment life for students, while streamlining tasks, provides time-savings for staff and improves resident satisfaction and retention.
