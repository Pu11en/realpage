## FAQs
#### What is a resident portal and LOFT ?
A resident portal is a digital platform that allows renters to manage rent payments, maintenance requests, communication, and leasing activities in one centralized place. Realpage LOFT Resident App is a resident portal for multifamily operators that centralizes the entire resident lifecycle, from leasing and moving to payments and community engagement, all in one seamless platform. Instead of managing multiple disconnected tools, LOFT gives your team and residents one app, one login, and one connected experience.
#### What features should a resident portal software include?
Key features include payments, maintenance tracking, communication tools, applicant workflows, and integrations with property management systems.
#### How does a multifamily resident portal improve resident retention?
By simplifying the resident experience, improving communication, and offering incentives like rewards, resident portals increase satisfaction and reduce turnover.
#### Can a resident portal increase on-time payments?
Yes. Features like flexible rent payment options, reminders, and financial tools help residents pay on time more consistently.
#### How does LOFT integrate with property management systems and other tools?
LOFT Living is built to work seamlessly within the Realpage ecosystem, connecting with property management systems, payment solutions, screening and verification tools, and utility providers. This integration ensures that operators can manage the entire resident lifecycle without relying on disconnected systems. The result is a more efficient workflow for onsite teams and a smoother, more consistent experience for residents.
#### Does LOFT include AI to support residents and staff?
Yes, LOFT includes an AI-powered Resident Agent designed to enhance both the resident and operator experience. The AI assistant can answer common resident questions instantly, automate routine communications, and reduce the volume of manual support requests. By handling repetitive tasks, it allows onsite teams to focus on higher-value interactions while delivering faster, more responsive service to residents.
