## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
#### What is the Realpage Tenant Portal?
The Realpage Tenant Portal is a secure online platform that provides tenants with self-service capabilities. They can make rent payments, submit and track maintenance requests, view important documents, and communicate with property management, improving convenience and satisfaction.
#### How does Realpage handle complex commercial lease terms like CAM reconciliations?
Our platform is built to optimize complex financial calculations specific to commercial real estate. It systematically manages CAM reconciliations, CPI-based escalations, and percentage rent, reducing manual error and ensuring accurate billing in accordance with lease agreements.
#### Is this CRE solution suitable for different types of commercial properties?
Yes. Realpage Commercial Property Management is designed with the flexibility to serve Industrial, Office, and Retail portfolios. Its robust feature set can be configured to meet the unique operational and financial demands of each asset class.
#### Can tenants submit maintenance requests through the portal?
Absolutely. Tenants can create new service requests 24/7 through the portal, include details, and monitor the status from submission to completion, providing full transparency and improving communication with maintenance staff.
