## Demand Marketing (formerly G5) FAQ
#### What is Demand Marketing?
Demand Marketing, formerly G5, is an end-to-end solution built for the future of how renters find homes in multifamily. As an integrated multifamily digital marketing solution, Demand Marketing connects Agentic Websites, Agentic Search Optimization, Smart Digital Advertising, Reputation Management and Performance Reporting to help apartment operators get found by AI, chosen by renters, and proven by the lease.
#### How does Smart Digital Advertising work with Demand Marketing?
Smart Digital Advertising reaches and converts the renter who's ready to act. By leveraging predictive technology, it ensures your advertising spend is focused on campaigns that are more likely to convert, optimizing daily, and resulting in up to 63% more calls with intent to rent that convert to leases up to 50% more often.2 When paired with Demand Marketing websites, call transcripts that improve ad targeting can also feed the FAQ content that helps your website get cited by AI.
#### What is included in Demand Marketing websites?
Demand Marketing apartment websites are machine-readable and citable with structured data, schema and a mobile-first, fast architecture. They are WCAG compliant and accessible from the ground up, including fee transparency options. They also include interactive 3D property maps and integrations with leading property management systems for current pricing, availability, and lead information. Together, these capabilities help properties improve online visibility and create a clearer path from search to lease.
#### How does Demand Marketing help boost visibility in search for multifamily properties?
Demand Marketing improves multifamily search visibility through Agentic Search Optimization, or ASO. This approach brings together technical SEO, GEO, AEO, local optimization, answer-first content, and consistent property information across websites, listings, profiles, and directories. It helps search engines and AI systems better understand, trust, and surface a property when renters ask about pricing, fees, pet policies, availability, and lease terms.
#### How does Demand Marketing's AI Call Scoring improve lead management?
Demand Marketing’s AI Call Scoring analyzes incoming phone calls, identifies renter intent, and scores each call so property teams can better understand lead quality. Those insights help optimize marketing spend toward the campaigns, audiences, and creative most likely to drive higher intent renter demand.
#### How does Demand Marketing help manage online reputation for multifamily properties?
Reputation Management, powered by Yext, helps multifamily teams manage their online reputation and off-page credibility from one place: listing syndication across 50+ directories, Google Business Profile management, and review generation and response. It goes further with AI sentiment analysis, competitor insights, and renter behavior trends across every location. This matters more than ever, as 86% of citations in AI-generated answers come from brand-managed sources, including listings and reviews.3
#### What creative services does Demand Marketing Creative Studio provide?
Demand Marketing's in-house creative and branding agency turns brand stories into a presence that performs with services including brand identity, copywriting, and design that make a property feel distinct, not generic. Every creative deliverable is built on real estate content informed by expert apartment digital strategists, so the same creative that elevates an operator's brand, also earns the citation and converts the renter who finds it.
#### What sets Demand Marketing apart from other digital marketing platforms?
Demand Marketing combines depth, data, and multifamily-specific expertise most digital agencies don't have. Backed by 20+ years of digital marketing expertise and a decade-long Google Partnership, Demand Marketing is one connected suite where the website gets properties cited, the reputation signals build trust, the advertising converts, and the reporting proves it. As part of the RealPage software ecosystem, Demand Marketing connects marketing performance to leasing outcomes, giving teams clearer visibility into which campaigns, sources, and strategies are driving signed leases.
