# Institutional Solutions
Institutional Investment & Portfolio Solutions
Leveraging technology to enable data-driven insights is key to managing an institutional real estate portfolio. Through its Asset & Investment Management (AIM) and Investor Management Services (IMS) platforms, Realpage® empowers institutional investors, REITs, and private equity firms to streamline data management, improve data quality, accelerate capital raises, and enhance portfolio transparency. Realpage’s relevant, timely data and robust analytics enable institutions to identify opportunities, navigate market shifts confidently, and make more informed, data-driven investment decisions.
