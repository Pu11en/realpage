## FAQs
#### What is property management accounting software?
Property management accounting software helps apartment owners and operators manage financial tasks like rent collection, expense tracking, budgeting, and financial reporting, all within a centralized system.
#### How is Realpage Accounting & Financial Suite different from generic accounting software?
Realpage is purpose-built for multifamily property management, offering features like site-level visibility, automated month-end close, and integrated budgeting across portfolios.
#### Can Realpage integrate with my existing property management system?
Yes. The finance and accounting suite integrates seamlessly with other Realpage solutions and can connect with third-party property management and banking systems, reducing duplicate entry and manual reconciliation.
#### How does the Financial Suite improve efficiency for property managers?
By automating expense management, vendor payments, financial close, and reporting, property managers save time, reduce errors, and gain real-time insights into portfolio performance.
#### Can Realpage Accounting support both corporate and property-level financials?
Yes. It handles corporate accounting, property accounting, and owner-level reporting within one platform, removing the need for multiple PMS systems.
