## FAQs
#### What is vendor credentialing in property management?
Vendor credentialing is the process of verifying that vendors meet required compliance standards before performing work at a property. This can include validating insurance and license documents, collecting W 9s, completing background checks when required, and confirming vendors are approved to work. Realpage process submitted documents and tracks requirements by property management company, helping ensure vendors are, insured, and ready to work.
#### Why is vendor credentialing important for property management companies?
Vendor credentialing helps reduce liability, helps prevent uninsured or unqualified work, and supports owner, resident, and regulatory expectations. By verifying insurance and licenses, proactively tracking expirations, and maintaining real time approval status, property management companies can reduce risk and accelerate onboarding, so work can begin faster, even in urgent situations.
#### What documents and requirements are typically included in vendor credentialing?
Most vendor credentialing programs include:
  * Certificate of Insurance (COI) with required limits
  * General liability, workers’ compensation, and auto liability (if applicable)
  * Trade licenses, as required by state
  * W 9 for vendor setup and tax reporting
  * Background checks ability to customize requirements, if required

Realpage also provides sample insurance certificates so vendors can see the exact insurance requirements and limits by property management company, reducing back and forth during onboarding.
#### How does Realpage handle vendor compliance and communication?
Realpage uses proactive notifications to help keep vendors and insurance agents informed throughout the approval process. Vendors and agents can receive regular evaluation status updates until approval is complete, as well as insurance renewal notices before expiration. With vendor permission, Realpage can contact the vendor’s insurance agent directly and provides an insurance agent portal where agents can upload documents and receive expiration and missing item notices, helping vendors stay compliant.
#### How does Realpage Vendor Credentialing support faster approvals and emergency readiness?
Realpage Vendor Credentialing accelerates approvals by validating documents, tracking requirements by property management companies, and leveraging a master insurance profile evaluated against each customer’s unique business standards through Credential Key; priority vendor lists support faster activation when it matters most, while mobile access lets vendors submit documents, track status, and complete tasks quickly, helping teams stay ready for urgent or emergency needs.
#### Does vendor credentialing control spend?
Vendor credentialing alone does not control spend. Used alongside spend management and source-to-pay processes, credentialing helps reduce risk, speed of time-to-work, and support better financial outcomes through improved compliance and vendor readiness.
