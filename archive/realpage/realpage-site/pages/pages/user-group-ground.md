# Ground Transportation Options to Headquarters

## DFW Airport
### Rental Cars
The following companies offer rental car services at DFW.
  * Advantage 800-777-5500
  * Alamo 800-327-9633
  * Avis 800-331-1212
  * Budget 800-527-0700
  * Dollar 866-434-2226
  * Enterprise 972-586-1100
  * Hertz 800-654-3131
  * National Car Rental 800-227-3876
  * Thrifty 972-453-5555

### Taxi/Cab Service
Taxis are available in all terminals at designated taxi stands, curbside on the upper level.
  * Express Cab Service 214-763-CABS (2227)
  * Carrollton Cab Taxi Service 972-687-0061

**SuperShuttle Service From DFW to Realpage ®**
After collecting your luggage, locate the "Information Board/Phone" at baggage claim. Press the appropriate number for SuperShuttle. After giving your location (the terminal and section are on the phone) to the Guest Service Representative proceed outside to the orange 'Shared Ride' area and look for the blue and gold SuperShuttle van on upper level. 817-329-2000
## Dallas Love Field
### Rental Cars
The following companies offer rental car services at Dallas Love Field. Most have a rental counter in the airport terminal with a shuttle to the car lot.
  * Hertz
  * Avis
  * Budget
  * Dollar
  * Enterprise
  * National
  * Alamo
  * Thrifty
  * Advantage
  * E-Z Rent-A-Car

### Taxi Cab Service
Taxi Cab service is provided on the upper level across the street from the baggage claim wing of the main terminal building.
**Airport Shuttle Services**
  * City Shuttle (214) 760-1998
  * Yellow Checker Shuttle (972) 222-2000
  * Super Shuttle (800) 258-3826 At baggage claim, locate the phone and push the button marked 'SuperShuttle.'
