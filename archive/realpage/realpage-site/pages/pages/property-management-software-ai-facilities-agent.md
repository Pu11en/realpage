##  How it works: End-to-end facilities orchestration
The AI Facilities Agent connects and automates the full maintenance lifecycle:
#### Turn management
Optimizes make-ready plans and prioritizes unit turns based on move-ins, floor plan demand, and technician availability
#### Task orchestration
Assigns, prioritizes, and rebalances work based on urgency, skills, schedules, and resources
#### Performance intelligence
Surfaces daily briefings, exceptions, trends, and performance dashboards so teams know what happened and what needs attention
#### Field execution
Captures service requests and guides inspections in real time
#### Vendor & spend coordination
Coordinates vendor scheduling, communication, and purchasing handoffs through connected Realpage workflows
