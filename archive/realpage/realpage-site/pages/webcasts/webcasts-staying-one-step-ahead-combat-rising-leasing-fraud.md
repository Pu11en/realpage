Savvy rental fraudsters have more tools at their disposal than ever before. It’s an ever-increasing source of frustration for multifamily operators across the country for whom mitigating risk is top of mind. As the ability to perpetrate leasing fraud gets easier, property managers face ever-increasing challenges in their attempt to detect it in its many forms, including identity and income verification fraud.
Our webcast tackles the task of mitigating fraud risk in multifamily communities. Gain insight into how fraud has evolved in the apartment industry in recent years and the tools and strategies property managers can adopt to stay in front of the industry’s fastest-growing concern.
Join RealPage® experts Ed Tonarelli, Industry Principal, Screening, and Carl Whitaker, Senior Director, Research and Analytics, as they discuss fraud in multifamily, how operators are handling the threat and best practices for protecting your properties — and your bottom line — from fraud.
### Ed Tonarelli
Industry Principal, RealPage, Inc.
Ed has been in the multifamily industry for over 25 years concentrating on sales and business development for residential screening products. He currently serves as the Industry Principal for RealPage Screening.
### Carl Whitaker
Vice President & Chief Economist, RealPage, Inc.
Carl Whitaker is RealPage’s Chief Economist, leading the company's team of economists and market analysts. Carl and the Market Intelligence team specialize in analyzing and disseminating economic and market trends for the rental housing industry. He primarily focuses on presenting trends in the rental housing industry to a wide audience, including investors, asset managers, operations professionals and media, among other personas. Carl has been cited in the Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg and GlobeSt and is a frequent keynote speaker for industry events. Carl holds degrees from the University of North Texas, including a Master of Science in Applied Geography.
