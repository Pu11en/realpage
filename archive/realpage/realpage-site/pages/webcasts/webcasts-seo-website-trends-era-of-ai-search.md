# SEO & Website Trends in an Era of AI Search
Aired on Thursday, July 11, 2024
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/seo-website-trends-era-of-ai-search/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/seo-website-trends-era-of-ai-search/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/seo-website-trends-era-of-ai-search/#hosts)

For multifamily companies, a website built upon expert SEO strategies is critical for attracting people looking for apartments and converting them into leads. But how will the advent of AI-powered search — like the recently rolled out AI Overviews by Google® — impact how prospects search more broadly and shop for apartments? How does your website design impact the goal of converting researching renters into leads by showcasing your brand and answering renters’ key questions?
Join Dayna Gardner (RealPage® Industry Principle); Audrey Myre (Manager, Brand Strategy); and Megan Giess (Director, Digital Strategy) as they talk with Justin Choi (Sequoia Equities) and Courtney Bastian (Dayrise Residential) about:
  * How Google AI Overviews search results impact renters in multifamily
  * Why longtail search is an important aspect of multifamily SEO, and how it will likely evolve
  * How website design combined with hyperlocal SEO strategies drive traffic, engagement and conversion
