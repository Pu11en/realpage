The nation's Northeast region has seen steady demand support tight occupancy in recent years. For many markets in this region, that is a reflection of limited supply delivering, a regional trend that has held true for the past few decades. However, there are handful of Northeast markets – such as Newark-Jersey City and parts of Boston and greater New York City – experiencing a local construction renaissance.
As the summer leasing season heats up, will demand be able to maintain pace with supply in these select neighborhoods? And for other regional markets, will demand be able to continue at its steady pace as economic pressure remains elevated?
Join RealPage® experts as they recap early 2025 performance statistics in the Northeastern U.S. and share expectations for the remainder of 2025.
