# Discover How RealPage Commercial is Just the Right Fit for Today’s Market
Aired on Wednesday, May 5, 2021
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/realpage-commercial-just-the-right-fit/#form7)

Today’s commercial property landscape is changing even as you read this.
That’s why you need commercial property management software that will allow you to customize dashboards, pull reports in real time and more, enabling you to review data when and how you like it.
RealPage® Commercial Property Management is here to take the complexity out of managing your commercial assets through full integration—from acquisition to disposition. From managing daily tasks to reporting and analyzing even the most complex leases, RealPage Commercial has every square foot covered, making us the perfect fit for your business.
Join RealPage Commercial Property Management experts Kim Maddox, Vice President Commercial, Steven Schwinger, Sales Engineer, and Abbie Adams, Product Marketer for part one of our Commercial Property Management webcast series. They’ll showcase how we can provide you with the easy-to-use operational, financial and reporting functionalities that scale as you go, so you can keep pace with the market today and tomorrow!
Webcast highlights include:
  * “Test driving” the software
  * Learning how to easily and efficiently grow and scale your operations
  * Understanding how we provide near-total customization to perfectly fit your business

### Kim Maddox
Vice President, Product Management - Commercial, RealPage, Inc.
Kim has 30+ years real estate industry experience serving Commercial and Corporate Real Estate markets. She has been a Strategy and Product Manager in the technology industry with JD Edwards, PeopleSoft, Oracle, MRI, Yardi and in her current role at RealPage where she is the Vice President of Product Management responsible for the strategy and development of Commercial Property Management products.
She has delivered growth through product innovation for the global market, and has lead projects through end-to-end new product launches, market research, product strategy, competitive analysis, business systems analysis, product requirement documentation, and client relationship management.
Kim has also directed organizations consisting of product, design, and engineering leaders to define and deliver SaaS and hosted solutions for existing and new products. Kim is a graduate of the University of Nebraska, Omaha - Bachelors of Science in Accounting. Her professional experience also includes accounting in the banking and property management industries.
Kim has served on numerous OSCRE standards development workgroups and committees since 2001. She has been a board member since 2015. Kim is currently a member of the OSRE Council of 100.
### Steve Schwinger
Senior Sales Engineer, RealPage, Inc.
Steve joined RealPage in 2011 and spent three years on the sales engineer team helping create financial suite solutions. In 2014, he accepted the position of controller for a realty investment group in Boulder, Colorado, working directly with owners and investors. There, he focused on scaling corporate and property operations to meet acquisition growth, which included a system conversion from Yardi to RealPage solutions. In 2017, Steve rejoined RealPage as a senior sales engineer for the corporate and enterprise sales team.
With a bachelor’s degree in finance and an MBA in accounting, he has also served as both a controller and chief financial officer for multi-family and commercial owner/managers. Steve lives with his family in Denver and enjoys spending his free time rock climbing near his home and exploring destinations outside of Colorado.
