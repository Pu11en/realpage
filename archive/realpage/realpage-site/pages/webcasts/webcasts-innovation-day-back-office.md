Ignite Your Competitive Advantage With RealPage Back Office Innovations
In slow-growth market conditions, gaining competitive advantage lies in increasing efficiency through automation, optimizing the deployment of resources, and identifying and acting on opportunities and threats quickly. New integration, automation and AI innovations will help you survive and thrive by creating efficiencies, automating cross-property workflows and uncovering hidden opportunities to scale operational investments. This is the future of back-office operations!
Join us for RealPage® Innovation Day, a unique, **virtual** event featuring industry trends and cutting-edge RealPage innovations designed to help you:
  * Use automation and exception-based handling of administrative tasks to create efficient cross-property workflows
  * Optimize, automate and centralize purchasing, invoicing and vendor engagement processes that are critical to controlling expenses
  * Leverage data advantages to streamline utility expense management, identify performance improvement opportunities and ensure regulatory compliance

Engage with the brightest minds in the industry as they outline key challenges, describe innovations and measurable outcomes, and answer your questions during a live Q&A.

### Danielle Rivers
Director of Business Services, Camden
Danielle Rivers began her career with Camden Property Trust in 2006. Through her 17 years in the Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) arena, Danielle has served in multiple capacities within Operations, Camden’s Contact Center and Business Services. In her current role as the Director of Business Services, Danielle is a liaison between Camden and RealPage, and is responsible for the implementation of new products. Danielle’s projects have included the implementation of DocuSign with Document Management, Facilities Mobile, Active Building and streamlining Centralized Task. Danielle earned a Bachelor in Science in Business Administration from the University of Phoenix.
### Gina Carter, CAM, CAPS, NALP
Vice President of Portfolio Operations, Blue Ridge Companies
As the Vice President of Portfolio Operations, Gina Carter is responsible for the development and implementation of programs that promote the optimal performance of Blue Ridge’s multifamily portfolio. Gina began her career at Blue Ridge in 2001 and transitioned to the corporate headquarters five years later.
Gina has experience in all levels of property management from Leasing to Operations. Gina’s intimate knowledge of the leasing process and expertise in all facets of property management systems administration is as a vital element in Blue Ridge’s management platform. Gina has taken the lead role in the portfolio-wide deployment of programs, such as Online Leasing, Spend Management and, most recently, our Paperless Office initiative to ensure that Blue Ridge’s onsite management teams have the necessary resources to achieve the goals set by owners, investors and third-party management, clients.
Gina holds the Certified Apartment Leasing Professional (CALP), Certified Apartment Manager (CAM) and Certified Apartment Portfolio Supervisor (CAPS) credentials through the National Apartment Association (NAA) and is a member of the National Apartment Association Educational Institute (NAAEI) Faculty, as well as an Education Advisory Board (EAB) appointee (2023). Gina is also a graduate of the NAA Leadership Lyceum Program and serves on the NAA Technology Committee as the Education Subcommittee Chair. Besides being an active member of the Apartment Association of North Carolina, Gina also serves as co-chair of the RealPage Steering Committee.
When Gina married her high school sweetheart, instead of taking a honeymoon her whole family went fishing. She and her husband have two beautiful boys, who are active in their local sports teams. Gina has now worked at Blue Ridge for more than half of her life.
### Robin Flagler
President, AION Management
Robin Flagler is President of AION Management and oversees all operations of over 21,000 apartments across 72 properties throughout seven states with a focus of bringing value to AION’s investors and partners. Robin has over thirty years of experience in asset repositioning and development projects with third party management companies, REIT’s, and owner/managed companies. Previously, Robin was Vice President of Operations at Kettler Management, where she oversaw a portfolio a portfolio of 42 properties in the mid-Atlantic region.
Robin serves on the Board of Directors for PAA and NJAA as well we the Real Estate/Property Management boards for Virginia Tech, Drexel University and Ball State. She is a NAAEI Faculty Member for the National Apartment Association where she, not only, assists in development of designation curriculums but also teaches many students annually on obtaining their designations. She is a 2016 NAA Leadership Lyceum graduate. Robin holds both CAPS and CPM designations and has positioned AION Management to be an AMO (IREM Accredited Management Organization) Company.
