# Modern Maintenance Strategies to Lower Property Costs
Aired on Thursday, March 13, 2025
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/modern-maintenance-strategies-lower-costs/#form7)

Property maintenance can be a logistical challenge, with manual processes, disjointed systems and rising resident expectations. Today’s multifamily maintenance management faces inefficiencies and increasing demands, such as:
  * High turnover: Recruiting challenges and scarcity of entry-level skills
  * Tech acceptance: Experienced workforce may resist adopting new technology, including mobile
  * Management across properties: Difficulty in managing technicians across multiple properties
  * Unit turnover: A need to turn units faster to minimize downtime

Join RealPage® experts to learn how to centralize and transform this complexity into simplicity by connecting maintenance teams to intuitive tools, establishing mobile workflows and gaining actionable insights. By automating service requests, integrating mobile devices and delivering real-time visibility, your properties will run efficiently, residents will stay satisfied and costs will be controlled. You can work smarter, respond faster and spend better – all from a centralized maintenance platform.
Learn how to overcome these challenges with:
  * A centralized, integrated ecosystem: Delivering speed and accuracy across systems
  * Advanced mobile functionality: Enabling maintenance technicians to manage work orders and perform inspections remotely
  * Real-time insights: Using advanced analytics and reporting tools to provide actionable data to improve decision-making
