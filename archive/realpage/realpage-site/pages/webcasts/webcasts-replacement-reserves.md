Replacement reserves, a key yet time-consuming business function, is now easier. Available in RealPage Job Cost, this formerly manual process has been automated to help increase efficiency, tap unused funds and gain better visibility.
Join Dan Newbern, Vice President RealPage Financial Suite for a demonstration of RealPage’s new and innovative approach to managing and reporting replacement reserves.
Learn how to give your team back hours of time in creating draw packages and tracking replacement reserve limits. Plus, get information on how your organization can set up reserves for multiple lenders, capture invoices that have been coded to reserve accounts and build the documents you need to reclaim your funds.
