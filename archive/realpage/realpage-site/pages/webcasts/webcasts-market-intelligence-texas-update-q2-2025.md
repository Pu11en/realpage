Texas remains a favored destination for employer relocations and remarkable population expansion. Along with substantial demographic and economic growth, the nation’s second-largest state by area has attracted a wave of apartment development and investment to maintain pace with housing demand.
While supply in Texas generally outpaced demand during the past few years, 2025 introduces a shifting narrative with demand once more matching supply as new apartment starts ease to their lowest level in a decade.
Join RealPage® experts as they discuss key apartment market themes across the Lone Star State, including a focus on Texas’ near-term outlook.
