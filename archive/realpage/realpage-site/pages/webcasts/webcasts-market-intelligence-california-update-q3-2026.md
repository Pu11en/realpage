Apartment market performance across California continues to diverge at both the regional and product level. Northern California—particularly the San Francisco Bay Area—is seeing a sharp rebound in demand, supported in part by the ongoing AI-driven economic expansion. In contrast, Southern California metros are experiencing more tempered demand at a time when supply levels remain modestly elevated relative to historical norms.
Adding another layer of complexity, performance trends vary meaningfully by product type, with Class A, Class B, and Class C segments following distinctly different trajectories across the state.
Is Northern California positioned for continued demand acceleration in the months ahead? Will Southern California stabilize as supply pressures ease? And which economic indicators will be most critical to watch as conditions evolve?
This webcast will provide a comprehensive assessment of supply, demand, and performance trends across California's major apartment markets, with the following markets featured:
  * Anaheim-Santa Ana-Irvine, CA
  * Los Angeles-Long Beach-Glendale, CA
  * Oakland-Fremont-Berkeley, CA
  * Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario, CA
  * Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA
  * San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad, CA
  * San Francisco-San Mateo-Redwood City, CA
  * San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara, CA
  * Oxnard-Thousand Oaks-Ventura, CA
