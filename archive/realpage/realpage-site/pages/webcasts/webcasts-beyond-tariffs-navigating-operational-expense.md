# Market Intelligence: Beyond Tariffs: Navigating Operational Expense
Aired on Tuesday, June 24, 2025
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/beyond-tariffs-navigating-operational-expense/#form7)

  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/beyond-tariffs-navigating-operational-expense/#hosts)

While the apartment industry has adapted to tariff uncertainties, a new wave of cost pressures is reshaping the operating landscape in ways that demand immediate attention. Insurance premiums are skyrocketing across key markets, utility costs are creating regional disparities, and labor market disruptions are driving unprecedented expense growth that outpaces rent increases. For operators, the challenge is not just managing individual cost categories but navigating the cascading effects that threaten cash flow stability and long-term profitability.
Join RealPage® rental housing experts as they examine the evolving expense landscape and provide actionable strategies for cost management with discussion topics including:
  * Examining how expense growth is outpacing rent performance and creating potential cash flow challenges
  * Analyzing the insurance crisis by the numbers and its geographic spread from Florida to Texas and California
  * Reviewing how leading operators are leveraging technology adoption to navigate rising expenses
  * Evaluating consumer spending patterns and warning signs in Class C and affordable segments
