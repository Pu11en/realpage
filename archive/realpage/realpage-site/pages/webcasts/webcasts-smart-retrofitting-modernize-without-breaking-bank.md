# Smart Retrofitting: How to Modernize Without Breaking the Bank
Aired on Thursday, November 13, 2025
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/smart-retrofitting-modernize-without-breaking-bank/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/smart-retrofitting-modernize-without-breaking-bank/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/smart-retrofitting-modernize-without-breaking-bank/#hosts)

Modernize your properties with precision without major capital costs. Join RealPage® experts for a dynamic webcast focused on smart retrofitting strategies, with a spotlight on IoT device integration and Wi-Fi infrastructure upgrades. Whether you manage multifamily, commercial or mixed-use properties, this session will show you how to unlock the value of existing buildings by layering in intelligent technology that enhances operations, resident experiences and long-term asset value.
Discover how smart retrofits create the foundation for connected living, enabling seamless interactions between residents, devices and building systems. Learn how to bring older properties up to modern smart building standards by cost-effectively deploying in-unit IoT devices, smart access controls and high-performance Wi-Fi without gut renovations or major disruptions.
Key takeaways:
  * The IoT and Wi-Fi technologies driving connected living and retrofit innovation
  * Scalable, budget-friendly strategies for modernizing legacy buildings
  * Case studies showcasing successful smart retrofits in action
  * Tips to future-proof assets with minimal disruption
