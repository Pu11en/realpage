# Managing Risk for Modern Real Estate Investors
Aired on Wednesday, March 22, 2023
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/managing-risks-for-modern-investors/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/managing-risks-for-modern-investors/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/managing-risks-for-modern-investors/#hosts)

In an increasingly challenging environment for real estate investment, investors need accurate portfolio data coupled with timely market information to drive faster and more precise decision making than ever. Investors who can access the latest performance data and comprehensive analysis will outperform the market in their capability to manage risk.
In this webcast, we will explore how real estate investors are working towards adopting technology solutions that enable automation of continuous valuation and actionable analysis of their real estate assets.
Join experts in institutional markets as RealPage® hosts a discussion of the advantages of a modern, data-driven approach to investment management, including how advanced systems and proven processes improve support for navigating changes and headwinds with a better understanding of optimizing cash flow forecasting and valuation:
  * Collecting, validating and sharing the right data
  * Continuous portfolio assessment, analytics and modeling
  * Connecting reporting with risk management and reforecasting

### Jeff Phaneuf
VP Finance & Planning, BXP
Jeff Phaneuf is the VP of Finance & Planning at BXP, where he oversees Budgeting & Planning, Corporate Lease Administration, and Accounts Receivable & Collections. BXP is one of the largest public real estate investment trusts (REITs) in the U.S., with a significant presence in Boston, Los Angeles, New York, San Francisco, Seattle and Washington, D.C.
BXP, a fully integrated real estate company organized as a REIT, has more than 50 years of experience developing, owning, managing and acquiring exceptional properties in dynamic gateway markets. Including properties owned by joint ventures, BXP’s portfolio totals 54.1 million square feet and 192 properties with 12 under construction or redevelopment. BXP assets include office, retail (including one under redevelopment), residential (including one under construction) and hotel properties.
Jeff joined BXP in 1996 and since the company’s public offering in 1997, he has focused on staffing, operational practices and procedures, and process improvements, including the integration of technology in the Budgeting & Forecasting, Lease Administration, and Accounts Receivable departments.
Jeff has a Bachelor of Arts in Marketing from Assumption College. He also serves on the Board of Directors for Canton Co-operative Bank in Canton, Mass., as well as the Board for Canton Youth Hockey.
### John Cronin
VP Budgeting & Planning, BXP
John Cronin is the VP of Budgeting & Planning at BXP. As one of the largest public REITs in the United States, BXP has a significant presence in six markets: Boston, Los Angeles, New York, San Francisco, Seattle and Washington, D.C. Including properties owned by joint ventures, the BXP portfolio totals 54.1 million square feet and 192 properties with 12 under construction or redevelopment. BXP assets include 173 office properties, 12 retail properties, six residential and one hotel.
A dedicated BXP employee since 1999, John’s passion lies in using technology to improve the budgeting and planning process. He has achieved significant success using software development and technology adoption to facilitate real-time information flow throughout the organization. John has extensive experience leading software development projects from initial strategic decision-making through development, testing, deployment, rollout and user adoption. John has a degree in Accounting from City and Islington College.
