When you complete your RealPage ® Vendor Marketplace profile, your business gets exposure to thousands of property management companies and you open the door to hundreds of opportunities — for less than 15 cents a day! Join VP & GM of Spend Management Solutions, Sara Jones and Lindsey Cade, Senior Director, Product Marketing for a tour of RealPage Vendor Marketplace — our online hub where property management companies connect with reputable, qualified vendors like you. Instead of finding new business, let new business find you.
We’ll discuss:
  * Building an online profile that stands out and attracts Property Managers
  * What questions to anticipate from Property Managers
  * How to turn your first job into a long-term relationship
  * The current and planned scope of our vendor network

Register now for this webcast on a smarter, easier way to market yourself.
