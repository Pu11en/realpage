# RealPage Insurance: Optimize Coverage, Empower Operations
Aired on Wednesday, December 3, 2025
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/realpage-insurance-optimize-coverage-empower-operations/#form7)

A well-structured insurance program does more than just mitigate risk - it enhances your bottom line, streamlines site operations, and improves the resident experience. With rising commercial insurance premiums and increasing operational expenses, optimizing your insurance strategy is no longer optional; it's essential for protecting your properties and maximizing asset value.
Join us for a strategic discussion on how property management companies and owners can transform their approach to insurance. We will explore how to build a comprehensive program that not only ensures compliance but also drives higher resident adoption and reduces the administrative burden on your teams.
## **In this session, you’ll learn how to:**
  * **Improve your bottom line** by mitigating expenses and reducing claims against your commercial policy.
  * **Streamline site operations** by automating compliance tracking and removing the burden of managing third-party policies.
  * **Boost resident adoption** of your preferred insurance options to ensure maximum coverage and protection.
  * **Enhance property protection** with gap coverage that addresses policy cancellations and non-compliance.

Discover how our innovative insurance solutions can protect your properties and streamline your property management strategy today.
