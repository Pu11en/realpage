In a cost-conscious multifamily real estate environment, it is more important than ever for property managers to get smart about spend and vendor management.
Taking a strategic approach to these areas offers onsite teams more insight and control to identify opportunities and enhance processes that help save money, improve efficiency and reduce risk.
Join operations leaders for an executive discussion about the specific considerations for expenses and purchasing— from procurement and payments to vendor credentialing and more — informing strategy differentiation and optimization of asset efficiency in a highly competitive and uncertain apartment market.
Topics explored include:
  * The value of both spend management and vendor management in multifamily real estate
  * Operational and financial factors for property managers developing a spend and vendor management strategy
  * Implementing the processes and procedures to ensure an effective spend and vendor management strategy
  * How a strategic approach to spend and vendor management can protect revenue, improve efficiency and reduce risk
