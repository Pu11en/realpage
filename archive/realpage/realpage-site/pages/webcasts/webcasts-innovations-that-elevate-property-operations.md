# Innovations That Elevate Property Operations
Aired on Wednesday, October 16, 2024
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/innovations-that-elevate-property-operations/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/innovations-that-elevate-property-operations/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/innovations-that-elevate-property-operations/#hosts)

Multifamily property management companies face many challenges that include slowing rent, growing expenses, evolving resident expectations and changing to a centralized workforce. What if your business had the latest tools that optimized, automated and centralized your property operations workflow to create process efficiencies, reduce expenses and drive savings?
Join RealPage® hosts Krista Hurley and Paul Rosenstein and their panel of experts as they talk about:
  * Property management innovations to OneSite® that address staffing challenges, fraud, delinquency and expense reduction
  * How AI technology advancements create operations efficiencies and reduce manual workloads in property, spend and vendor management
  * Planning for reduced energy consumption with the power of AI and data

### Krista Hurley
Industry Principal, Agentic AI Workforce, Realpage, Inc.
Krista Hurley is the Industry Principal for Agentic AI Workforce at Realpage, where she leads sales, business development, launches, and beta initiatives for innovative multifamily technologies
With more than 14 years in the multifamily industry, Krista began her career as a leasing consultant in a call center before moving into implementations, account management, and industry leadership. Her experience has given her a deep understanding of product integrations, client needs, and the connectedness of the multifamily technology ecosystem.
Krista is the host of Multifamily Talks a Realpage Podcast and winner of GlobeSt. Women of Influence for Proptech. She is also a member of the Upper State Apartment Association in Greenville, South Carolina, and participates in Realpage employee resource groups, including EMPOW-HER and BE WELL.
### Chris Cranfill
Director, Sustainability & Smart Water Solutions, RealPage, Inc.
Chris Cranfill is the Director of Sustainability and Smart Water Solutions at RealPage and has been with the company since 2012. Prior to RealPage, Chris spent 17 years at IBM and 2.5 years at CloudOne, a SaaS startup, building and leading technical sales teams and go-to-market initiatives across the United States and Canada. In his current role, Chris is responsible for product development, implementation, and account management of RealPage’s Sustainability Services and Smart Water solutions which includes SaaS, IoT, and AI-based technologies that identify utility cost/usage anomalies and alerts property staff of potential water leaks and wasted energy loads across the multifamily market. With over 25 years of experience in building and delivering Cloud, SaaS, and IoT solutions, Chris is very passionate about building and delivering ESG & Sustainability solutions for the multifamily market that enables customers to achieve their ESG & Sustainability goals and provide actionable data insights that allows portfolios to track trending, reduce energy usage, and quickly address leaks with IoT alerts.
### Paul Rosenstein
SVP, GM, Property Management, RealPage, Inc.
Paul has over 20 years of product leadership, strategy and general management experience. Most recently, he was Vice President Product with CoreLogic, North America’s leading integrated solution provider for real estate, mortgage and insurance providers. Before joining CoreLogic, he spent 14 years in leadership positions at Mitchell International, a provider of software and software-enabled services to clients within the property insurance and collision repair industries. Paul has experience in growing enterprise software solutions, scaling organizations and driving strategic transformation.
### Sara Jones
SVP, GM Spend & Vendor Management, RealPage, Inc.
Sara is responsible for product management and operations for all RealPage Spend and Vendor Management solutions including OpsTechnology, Vendor Credentialing, Vendor Marketplace, eSupply GPO, Vendor Compliance Services and SmartSource Accounting Services. Sara has been with RealPage for over 13 years and has over 18 years of experience in the multifamily industry. She’s focused on leading product and operations teams to achieve company goals and delivering strategic metrics to maintain workflow success for clients.
### Dawn Major
