With the start of 3rd quarter right around the corner, it's time to revisit early year predictions (and outcomes) for the nation's South Central states of Arkansas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, and Texas. Demand has held firm through the first half of the year, but has supply afforded enough relief for a more stable revenue growth environment to return? And what other key trends are economists and analysts closely monitoring as the summer months hit their peak?
This webcast will deliver a comprehensive look at supply, demand, and performance. Included will be a deep dive into how these markets are evolving and what operators need to know as we move towards peak leasing season.
##### Key Topics:
  * Supply/Demand Fundamentals: Supply is cooling, but headwinds are affecting demand in some of the region’s major metro areas. Is supply easing enough to support stronger performance? And how are macroeconomic demand drivers affecting absorption and accompanying performance metrics?
  * Performance Metrics: Review occupancy, rent growth, and renewal trends across major metros. Which metros are leading, which are lagging, and where are we seeing signs of softening (or strengthening)?
