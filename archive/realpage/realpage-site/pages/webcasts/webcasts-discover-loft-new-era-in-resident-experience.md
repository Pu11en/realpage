# Discover LOFT™: A New Era in Resident Experience
Aired on Wednesday, October 2, 2024
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/discover-loft-new-era-in-resident-experience/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/discover-loft-new-era-in-resident-experience/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/discover-loft-new-era-in-resident-experience/#hosts)

Renters now demand the same cutting-edge tech they use in their daily lives, with 97% stating they are more likely to renew a lease if interacting with their property manager is as easy as interacting with Amazon. The message is clear: Renters demand and expect a modern resident experience.
That’s why RealPage introduced **LOFT** , a fully integrated resident experience platform that simplifies every stage of the apartment rental process — from application and leasing to moving and living — all within a single app.
In this webcast, we’ll take an in-depth look at the LOFT ecosystem and demonstrate how it can transform your resident journey while enhancing operational efficiency and opening new revenue streams. LOFT is the modern platform multifamily property managers need to boost net operating income (NOI) and maximize return on investment (ROI).
Join RealPage Industry Principal Dayna Gardner and Vice President/General Manager of Living and Loyalty Mike Ivey for a deeper dive into LOFT, exploring how we are redefining the resident experience for the multifamily industry with everything resident in one app. We’ll also show you the data revealing how impactful LOFT can be.
Topics covered in this interactive, information-packed session include:
  * Walkthrough of key capabilities in the new LOFT platform
  * Recap and response from the LOFT launch at RealWorld 2024
  * Market insights from the 2024 National Multifamily Renter Survey
  * Customer results from LOFT Moving and Loyalty beta customers
