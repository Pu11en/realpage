# Are Your Commercial Operations Too Hot or Too Cold?
Aired on Wednesday, May 26, 2021
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/commercial-operations-hot-cold/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/commercial-operations-hot-cold/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/commercial-operations-hot-cold/#hosts)

Ever wonder if not using the same property management software (PMS) across all your commercial properties could be limiting your abilities to unlock yield, achieve organizational efficiency and who knows what else? Does your current PMS leave you cold with limited scalability and features, and raise your temperature because it’s hard to use and doesn’t deliver the data you need in a way that works best for you?
RealPage® Commercial Property Management will show you the true importance of having a PMS that can not only scale but expand its capabilities when and where you need them.
Join RealPage Commercial Property Management experts Kim Maddox, RealPage Vice President Commercial; and Bruce Hezlep, RealPage Senior Sales Engineer, for part two of our Commercial Property Management webcast series. They’ll showcase how we can provide you with consolidated reporting, common area maintenance (CAM) recoveries, corporate accounting, dashboards, chart types and more so you can see how our software can be the perfect temperature.
Webcast highlights include:
  * “Test-driving” the software
  * Learning how we help you deliver the right data at the right time, when and how stakeholders need it
  * See why you should never have to switch from property accounting to corporate accounting

### Kim Maddox
Vice President, Product Management - Commercial, RealPage, Inc.
Kim has 30+ years real estate industry experience serving Commercial and Corporate Real Estate markets. She has been a Strategy and Product Manager in the technology industry with JD Edwards, PeopleSoft, Oracle, MRI, Yardi and in her current role at RealPage where she is the Vice President of Product Management responsible for the strategy and development of Commercial Property Management products.
She has delivered growth through product innovation for the global market, and has lead projects through end-to-end new product launches, market research, product strategy, competitive analysis, business systems analysis, product requirement documentation, and client relationship management.
Kim has also directed organizations consisting of product, design, and engineering leaders to define and deliver SaaS and hosted solutions for existing and new products. Kim is a graduate of the University of Nebraska, Omaha - Bachelors of Science in Accounting. Her professional experience also includes accounting in the banking and property management industries.
Kim has served on numerous OSCRE standards development workgroups and committees since 2001. She has been a board member since 2015. Kim is currently a member of the OSRE Council of 100.
### Bruce Hezlep
Senior Sales Engineer, RealPage, Inc.
With over 30 years of accounting and enterprise sales experience, Bruce brings his technical savvy and customer-focused leadership to the Financial, Commercial and Institutional solutions at RealPage.. Previously, Bruce held various consulting and sales roles at Yardi, Oracle Corporation, PeopleSoft and JD Edwards, as well as serving as Controller/Division CFO for a publicly held corporation. Bruce holds a bachelor’s degree from Grove City College in Business Administration and Financial Planning.
