# Financial Suite – Scaling With Technology
Aired on Thursday, March 20, 2025
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/financial-suite-scaling-with-technology/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/financial-suite-scaling-with-technology/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/financial-suite-scaling-with-technology/#hosts)

Controlling costs while scaling your multifamily business is key to staying competitive in today’s property management industry. Join Dan Newbern (RealPage® Vice President of Financial Suite) and special guests Char McCurdy (President) and Leslie Loyd Malaise (CFO) of Summit Property Management as they explore how technology can drive efficiency and transform financial processes.
This webcast will equip you with actionable insights on:
  * Using advanced technology to scale your property management business intelligently
  * Tackling challenges associated with adopting online invoicing and payments
  * Enforcing compliance with vendors through paperless processing

The session will also feature an exciting demonstration of AI-powered invoice processing by Amanda Traylor, Sales Engineer at RealPage, followed by an interactive Q&A with Dan Newbern.
Don’t miss this opportunity to discover how leveraging these innovative tools can help maximize financial performance and operational efficiency in property management.

### Dan Newbern
Vice President, Financial Suite, RealPage, Inc.
With over 20 years in technology and operations, Dan Newbern joined RealPage in 2016. As the Vice President of the RealPage Financial Suite, Dan brings innovative and customer-focused leadership to the Accounting and Budgeting products for our customers. Prior to RealPage, Dan led multinational ERP teams for one of the largest financial system companies in the United States and EU.
### Amanda Traylor
Solutions Engineer, RealPage, Inc.
Amanda joined the RealPage SE team in 2012. She came to us with 12 years of experience in the multifamily property management industry where she gained hands-on experience with many of the RealPage solutions, including OneSite Leasing & Rents, Payments, and YieldStar. Amanda has a background in marketing with a focus on systems implementation and training for new construction sites.
