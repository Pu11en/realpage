Low volatility has largely defined apartment market performance across Upper Midwest and Great Plains metros in recent years, with modest—but stable—demand matched by similarly restrained construction activity. However, sluggish job growth introduces an added layer of demand uncertainty across many markets, while seasonal patterns may play an outsized role in shaping near-term performance expectations throughout the nation’s breadbasket states.
If job growth remains soft into late 2026, what factors will underpin demand, and how might that carry into the 2027 outlook? Will expected supply in the year ahead diverge from the subdued trends seen in recent cycles? And which key economic indicators are most critical for tracking the trajectory of apartment markets across the Upper Midwest and Great Plains?
This webcast will provide a comprehensive assessment of supply, demand, and performance trends across the Upper Midwest & Great Plains region's major apartment markets, with the following markets featured:
  * Chicago-Naperville-Elgin/Kenosha, IL-IN-WI
  * Des Moines-West Des Moines, IA
  * Kansas City, MO-KS
  * Madison, WI
  * Milwaukee-Waukesha/Racine, WI
  * Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington, MN-WI
  * Omaha, NE-IA
  * St. Louis, MO-IL

The webcast will be followed in the coming days by a shortened webcast focused on the region's secondary & tertiary markets, including the following areas:
  * Champaign-Urbana, IL
  * Davenport-Moline-Rock Island, IA-IL
  * Springfield, MO
  * Fargo, ND-MN
  * Lawrence, KS
  * Lincoln, NE
  * Sioux Falls, SD-MN
  * Wichita, KS
