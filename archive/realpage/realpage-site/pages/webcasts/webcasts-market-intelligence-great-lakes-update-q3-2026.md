Driven by steady, measured demand, apartment market performance across major metros in the Great Lakes and Ohio Valley has remained more stable than the national average in recent years. While local construction pipelines introduce some market-level variation, the broader regional narrative continues to center on a relative balance between supply and demand.
What demand drivers are supporting—or constraining—local market performance? Do current construction pipelines point to any near-term shifts in fundamentals? And to what extent does performance vary across different product types within these markets?
This webcast will provide a comprehensive assessment of supply, demand, and performance trends across the Great Lakes & Ohio Valley region's major apartment markets, with the following markets featured:
  * Cincinnati, OH-KY-IN
  * Cleveland, OH
  * Columbus, OH
  * Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek, OH
  * Detroit-Warren-Dearborn, MI
  * Grand Rapids/Muskegon/Holland, MI
  * Indianapolis-Carmel-Greenwood/Greencastle, IN
  * Louisville/Jefferson County, KY-IN
