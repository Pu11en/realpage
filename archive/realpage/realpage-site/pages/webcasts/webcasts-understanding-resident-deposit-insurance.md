# How Covered Are You, Really? Understanding Resident & Deposit Insurance
Aired on Thursday, March 25, 2021
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/understanding-resident-deposit-insurance/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/understanding-resident-deposit-insurance/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/understanding-resident-deposit-insurance/#hosts)

Providing resident insurance and deposit alternatives is now the industry standard. But providing the right resident insurance and deposit alternative solutions will truly limit risk, simplify operations and drive ancillary revenue.
Join RealPage® industry experts Adam Falkauff, SVP of Insurance Services, and Tom Schickel, VP of Deposit Alternatives, as they discuss how property management companies are leveraging resident insurance and deposit alternatives to provide the protection you and your residents need, while generating ancillary revenue and leveraging technology to drive efficient and seamless experiences for you and your residents.
Webcast topics include:
  * Identifying optimal solutions for resident insurance and deposit alternatives tailored to your objectives
  * Limiting risk by optimizing resident insurance and deposit alternatives to ensure both properties and residents are protected
  * Uncovering material benefits from resident insurance and deposit alternative programs
  * Leveraging technology to improve program performance and ensure coverage

### Adam Falkauff
SVP, Insurance Services, RealPage
Adam has over 15 years of strategic leadership experience in both the Insurance and technology sectors. Adam joined RealPage in 2015 as Vice president of Insurance and is now responsible for RealPage’s Insurance & Deposit Alternative products. Prior to joining RealPage, Adam was a Director at Liberty Mutual, responsible for leading organizational transformation, improving processes and implementing technology. Adam received his BA from the University of Michigan and his JD from New York Law School.
### Tom Schickel
VP/General Manager, Deposit Alternatives, RealPage
Tom founded DepositIQ in 2011, which was later acquired by RealPage, Inc. in 2017. He currently serves as Vice President & General Manager for Deposit Alternatives. Prior to founding DepositIQ, Tom was a Vice President with CoreLogic and led a national team to retain and grow NOI for all existing accounts. Tom received his BA from Colorado State University.
