# 10 Mistakes Management Companies Make with Vendors
Aired on Wednesday, June 9, 2021
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/10-mistakes-management-companies-vendors/#form7)

Here are the top three of the 10 most common mistakes property management companies make with their vendors:
1. Adopting a Risk Elimination vs. Risk Management Mindset
2. “Set It and Forget It” Vendor Set-Up Procedures
3. Vendor Agreements or Contracts Authored by the Vendor
10 Mistakes Management Companies Make with Vendors delivers insights on these top three mistakes, reveals what the other seven are, and shows you how to avoid making any of them. Whether you’re using internal corporate staff, external service partners or property site teams to manage your vendor compliance, you’ll learn how to minimize costs associated with vendor liability risk, better manage compliance and ensure vendors satisfy all of your requirements.
This live webcast also includes a Q & A with industry experts who can answer your questions. Highlights include:
  * How to best track the correct types of policy endorsements
  * Understanding the details of insurance certificates to ensure you have appropriate insurance
  * The 5 steps you need to take to find the right vendors for you
  * Learning more about how RealPage® can help through Vendor Credentialing
