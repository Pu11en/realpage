# How Bundling Your Accounting Processes Really Adds Up

Reporting to investors, preparing monthly draws and managing your monthly close is a never-ending challenge for every property management company.
However, with the RealPage® Accounting Accelerator Bundle, you get essential add-on solutions for the RealPage Financial Suite that give your team the tools to save valuable time and expense by supercharging your monthly accounting processes.
Join RealPage’s Dan Newbern, Vice President/Financial Suite, and Victoria Sicula, Business Analyst/Accounting, as they discuss how the Accelerator Bundle can streamline processes for booking general ledger entries, automate tasks and prepare necessary reporting for better visibility into your assets and progress throughout the month, highlighting these specific solutions:
  * Financial Close Management
  * Replacement Reserves
  * Investor Tracking
  * Fixed Assets

See how to boost your team’s productivity and save dollars with the RealPage Accounting Accelerator Bundle.
### Dan Newbern
Vice President, Financial Suite, RealPage, Inc.
