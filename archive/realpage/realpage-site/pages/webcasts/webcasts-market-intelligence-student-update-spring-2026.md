## **Spring Leasing, International Enrollment Declines & the Student Housing Outlook**
Spring leasing season is gaining momentum—but for student housing, this year looks very different than last fall. International student enrollment is dropping sharply, creating real pressure on occupancy and pricing in markets that have historically depended on that demand. As this shift reshapes leasing outcomes, operators are being forced to compete differently, rethink pricing strategies, and adjust expectations market by market.
Join **Arben Skivjani, Deputy Chief Economist at RealPage** , and **Carl Whitaker, VP & Chief Economist at RealPage**, for a data-driven webcast focused on what’s happening right now—and what it means for your portfolio.
RealPage’s market experts will break down:
  * Spring lease-up performance and summer pre-lease momentum
  * Rent growth, occupancy, and pre-lease velocity across key university markets
  * The impact of declining international enrollment on demand and pricing
  * Which regions are holding steady—and which are feeling the greatest pressure
  * How supply dynamics are amplifying or easing challenges across markets

Whether you’re tracking leasing progress, adjusting pricing strategies, or planning for the fall, this session delivers critical insight to help you navigate a rapidly shifting student housing landscape.
