# Powerful Insights on Submeter Savings, Mandates and Maintenance

Submeter maintenance is now mandated in 22 areas nationwide—including the entire state of California. And that number will grow dramatically in the coming years.
The good news: Submetering can create substantial benefits, including reducing resident utility consumption by15-29%. The bad news: It opens a new realm of maintenance issues, as well as potential fines and penalties for non-compliance.
As the leading provider of multifamily submeter services in the US, RealPage is the definitive expert on how to get the full benefit of submetering. From initial implementation, to benchmarking and cost recovery, to ongoing maintenance programs that keep you compliant, RealPage brings you unmatched insights in this rapidly-growing area.
Join RealPage experts Jay Mitchell and Donald White as they share best practices for ensuring submeter savings, compliance and maintenance at your properties.
We’ll discuss how you can:
  * Benchmark, track and reduce utility consumption
  * Lower operating expenses and increase NOI
  * Meet or exceed submeter maintenance mandates
  * Devise an easy, effective maintenance plan
