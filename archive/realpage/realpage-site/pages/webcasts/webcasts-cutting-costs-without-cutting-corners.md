In the face of challenging economic conditions reminiscent of the Great Recession, multifamily operators are grappling with the need for optimized expenses to achieve budgeted NOI and meet expected returns.
How are apartment industry leaders strategically managing property-level expenses, especially after significant nationwide tax and insurance increases?
In this Voices for 2024 discussion, operators are taking a deep dive into navigating these fiscal challenges, offering practical solutions to keep site expenditures low while safeguarding the income side of the business.
Join our panel of expert guests to gain a comprehensive understanding of “back to the basics” cost-cutting approaches, hear executive perspectives and learn actionable strategies to ensure financial success in an extremely competitive landscape.
Topics covered include these critical issues:
  * Strategic expense management that keeps the business balanced
  * Navigating tax & insurance challenges
  * Executive insight into real-life, day-to-day experiences with cost control
  * Practical steps for achieving budgeted NOI

### Melissa Miller
Senior Vice President, Allegiant-Carter, Allegiant-Carter
Melissa Miller is a seasoned and accomplished multifamily property management professional with over 26 years in the industry. As the Senior Vice President of Allegiant-Carter, Melissa is focused on the financial operations of the business, using proven strategies to increase revenues and economies of scale for expense control for the firm's 38 assets and over 10k units across a wide variety of asset types, including Class A, Core Plus, Value-Add and New Development.
Her prior experience is well rounded with extensive experience in interior, exterior renovations, asset repositing, acquisitions and dispositions, as well as roles in Senior Management, Multifamily Brokerage and Industry Direct Sales, including serving as Managing Director of American Landmark, as Vice President of Carroll Management Group and as National Account Manager for Valet Living.
She is also an active member of the apartment industry with immediate focus on Legislative Committee for Space Coast Apartment Association, Florida Apartment Association and Apartment and Office Building Association. In addition, Melissa is a major contributor to the Florida Apartment Association APAC, prior President of the Southwest Florida Apartment Association and Space Coast Apartment Association, and also Board Member for the Bay Area Apartment Association.
Her professional and industry certifications include Florida Housing Occupancy Specialist (2006), National Center for Housing Management Tax Credit Specialist (2022), Certified Apartment Manager National Apartment Association (2009) and Certified Apartment Portfolio Supervisor (2012).
