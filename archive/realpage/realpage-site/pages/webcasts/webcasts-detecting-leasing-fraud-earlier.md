# Detect Leasing Fraud Earlier With Prequalification
Aired on Wednesday, April 30, 2025

Rental application fraud is rising, making it a top priority for multifamily owners and operators of all sizes across the country. Driven by increased access to sophisticated technology and sensitive information, this growing threat demands a critical reassessment of industry best practices for reducing financial and security risks for apartment applicants, communities and property managers.
Achieving a seamless verification process means efficiency, transparency and security must work hand-in-hand during the screening phase of _every_ application. That’s why RealPage’s Identity and Income Verification solutions are designed to combat fraud risks and protect your business proactively and consistently as a completely integrated part of your current leasing prequalification process.
Join RealPage® Vice President & GM of Screening, Chad Wittenberg, and Industry Principal, Ed Tonarelli, for an in-depth look at how advanced technology that deepens upfront screening helps your onsite teams stay one step ahead in proactively preventing rental fraud — and promotes a frictionless leasing process for prospective residents.
Topics covered in this interactive, information-packed webcast session include:
  * Fraud reduction strategies and best practices to minimize risk and expense
  * Innovative fraud prevention tools to enhance security, accuracy and efficiency
  * Walkthrough of key capabilities in RealPage Prequalification Solutions
