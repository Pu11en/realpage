Learn how Spend Management in the Financial Suite can allow you to easily enable online sourcing for your business to help you control your indirect spend and ensure that your organization is using the right suppliers.
Tune in to this informative webcast with RealPage® Financial Suite Vice President, Dan Newbern, as he demonstrates how the RealPage Financial Suite allows you to enable vendors and shop online catalogs to reduce inaccuracies and improve visibility into spend activity.
Take away valuable insights that will help you:
  * Learn how to enable online spend management
  * Request online accounts for your properties and PMC
  * Setup approval workflows
  * Shop online and create orders
  * Receive and approve online invoices

Don’t miss this enlightening discussion on how Spend Management in RealPage Financial Suite can bring a truly measurable difference to your bottom line.
### Dan Newbern
Vice President, Financial Suite, RealPage, Inc.
With over 20 years in technology and operations, Dan Newbern joined RealPage in 2016. As the Vice President of the RealPage Financial Suite, Dan brings innovative and customer-focused leadership to the Accounting and Budgeting products for our customers. Prior to RealPage, Dan led multinational ERP teams for one of the largest financial system companies in the United States and EU.
### Bruce Hezlep
Senior Sales Engineer, RealPage, Inc.
With over 30 years of accounting and enterprise sales experience, Bruce brings his technical savvy and customer-focused leadership to the Financial, Commercial and Institutional solutions at RealPage.. Previously, Bruce held various consulting and sales roles at Yardi, Oracle Corporation, PeopleSoft and JD Edwards, as well as serving as Controller/Division CFO for a publicly held corporation. Bruce holds a bachelor’s degree from Grove City College in Business Administration and Financial Planning.
