Florida multifamily markets have faced a series of challenges over the past few years, but the main underlying pain point has been a period where local supply levels have outpaced demand levels. The good news for local operators is that demand has been – and continues to remain – strong as supply is finally starting to ease off its multi-decade peak. This can be seen in local performance readings that are beginning to turn a corner in most of the state’s metro areas, such as normalizing occupancy trends and slow-yet-steady improvement in revenue growth.
Join RealPage® experts as they discuss key apartment market themes across the Sunshine State, along with a discussion focused on Florida’s near-term outlook.
