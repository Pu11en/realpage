# Market Intelligence: 2024 Budgeting SWOT Analysis
Aired on Thursday, September 28, 2023
[ View Webcast Video ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/market-intelligence-2024-budgeting-swot-analysis/#form7)
  * [ Overview ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/market-intelligence-2024-budgeting-swot-analysis/#overview)
  * [ Hosts ](https://www.realpage.com/webcasts/market-intelligence-2024-budgeting-swot-analysis/#hosts)

With the last three years wiping out normal trends in multifamily markets nationwide, budgeting has become a much greater challenge. Our data analysts have key insights that can help you navigate 2024 forecasting.
Join RealPage® multifamily market experts Carl Whitaker and Casey Christian for an overview of national strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats that could impact your 2024 budget planning. You’ll also hear their review of transactional benchmarking data in major multifamily metros, with a Q&A session to follow.
In this webcast, you will learn about:
  * Occupancy, demand and renewal conversions that are driving NOI
  * Transactional performance benchmarking across multiple KPIs that impact your bottom line
  * Key insights for 2024 budgeting

### Carl Whitaker
Vice President & Chief Economist, RealPage, Inc.
Carl Whitaker is RealPage’s Chief Economist, leading the company's team of economists and market analysts. Carl and the Market Intelligence team specialize in analyzing and disseminating economic and market trends for the rental housing industry. He primarily focuses on presenting trends in the rental housing industry to a wide audience, including investors, asset managers, operations professionals and media, among other personas. Carl has been cited in the Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg and GlobeSt and is a frequent keynote speaker for industry events. Carl holds degrees from the University of North Texas, including a Master of Science in Applied Geography.
### Casey Christian
Strategic Performance Advisor, RealPage, Inc.
Casey Christian has been with RealPage® since 2017. He served as a revenue manager working with a diverse portfolio of clients utilizing YieldStar® before joining the Strategic Performance Advisory team in 2021. He has more than 14 years of experience in operations with roles that include leasing, marketing, regional management and director.
