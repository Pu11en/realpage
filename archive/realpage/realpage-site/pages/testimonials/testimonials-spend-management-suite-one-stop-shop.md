# The RealPage Spend Management Suite Is a “One-Stop Shop”
Gavin Greene, Director of Management Systems, Flaherty and Collins Properties, talks about how the Spend Management Suite from RealPage® helps Flaherty and Collins Properties maintain control “from the corporate level all the way down to the site level.” He credits Spend Management with helping Flaherty and Collins Properties ensure best prices, guard against variations in price across all properties, develop better budget forecasts, and accelerate efficiency in the processing and payment of invoices. The Spend Management Suite has significantly reduced invoice processing time at Flaherty and Collins — from 14 minutes per invoice down to 4.5 to 6, according to Greene.
“If you have a property management company that’s doing over 50,000 invoices, that’s a huge savings.” ~ Gavin Greene
