# Wingate Companies Leverages RealPage’s Learning Management System (LMS)
#### RealPage EasyLMS enables self-service employee training
Karoline Lapointe, Regional Vice President of Wingate Companies, talks about how Wingate employees are already knowledgeable about and comfortable with RealPage® products, but when they need to learn something new, [RealPage's EasyLMS](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/learning-management-system/ "RealPage EasyLMS")® (learning management system) is an invaluable instructional resource because it means that Wingate's training department can cut down on the time they spend in one-on-one training with employees. EasyLMS enables employees to learn at their own pace, at the time of their choosing.
"They can learn on their own. It's very user-friendly. I think it's one of the best products I've ever worked with."
Karoline Lapointe
Regional Vice President, Wingate Companies
