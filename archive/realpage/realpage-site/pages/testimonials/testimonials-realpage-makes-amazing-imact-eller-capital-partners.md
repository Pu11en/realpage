# RealPage Makes an Amazing Impact at Eller Capital Partners
#### OneSite saves time for staff and customers
Benjamin Edell, District Operations Manager for Eller Capital Partners, talks about how his company’s "big implementation" in 2022, picking a couple of RealPage® products that have worked particularly well for them by saving time for staff and customers. He praises RealPage for enabling customers to access daily pricing on the Eller Capital Partners websites, input their move-in date and lease length, get screened, and have all their application information go directly into the software. The next day, they’re able to make an onsite visit to make move-in arrangements.
Edell says the impact is "amazing,” as it eliminates the need to send multiple emails and have multiple phone conversations. It also eliminates the need for prospects to travel to see the units, fill out paper applications and sign paper lease agreements. And his staff no longer needs to transfer an applicant's information to an operating system and then use separate systems for screening and lease signing.
"Having those integrated and automated together where you can go end to end without ever having to have an interaction is pretty amazing," says Edell.
######  Benjamin Edell
District Operations Manager, Eller Capital Partners
