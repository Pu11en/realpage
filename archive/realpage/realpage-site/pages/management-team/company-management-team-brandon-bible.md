# Executive Leadership: Brandon Bible

Brandon Bible serves as Realpage’s Chief Growth Officer. His stellar career with RealPage started in 1998 as a core member of the company’s initial startup team. After consistently achieving exponential growth over the following four years, he took the helm as the company’s top Sales executive.

With over 25 years of experience in the multifamily property management and real estate markets, he has successfully scaled sales and customer support organizations from early startup to global organizations with expertise in acquisition and cultural integrations. Brandon has been the strategic lynchpin in helping Realpage consistently multiply earnings year over year, which led to its successful acquisition by parent company Thoma Bravo in 2020.
Recognized as a passionate and dynamic advocate for the multifamily industry, he currently represents Realpage on the Board of Directors for the National Multifamily Housing Council (NMHC).
Brandon received his B.S. in History from Angelo State University and Minor in Finance with Real Estate Specialization.
