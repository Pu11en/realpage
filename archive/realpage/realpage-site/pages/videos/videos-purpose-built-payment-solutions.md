#### Go paperless for security and efficiency
Every time a resident makes a payment, whether it’s rent, application fees or a deposit, there’s an opportunity for mistakes and fraud. Make payments safe and easy for both your residents and staff with RealPage Payment Solutions.
RealPage’s Payment Solutions works seamlessly with our OneSite Leasing and Rents, as well as with most major property management software platforms, like Yardi and MRI.
With RealPage Payments Solutions, you can enjoy all the benefits of a paperless office, including improved efficiency, decreased processing costs, ensured compliance with complex state and federal laws, plus less risk of theft and fraud.
Your residents will still be able to pay with their preferred method (cash, check, money order, ACH payment/bank account, credit or debit card) either online or through a contactless, secure lockbox.
Over 250,000 property management companies enjoy RealPage Payment Solutions. RealPage is the only licensed Money Service Business (MSB) in the multifamily industry and the purpose-built integrated rent payment services complies with all banking regulations, including the Bank Secrecy Act.
For more information, visit <https://www.realpage.com/online-payments/>.
