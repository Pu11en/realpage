### Reduced Risk
eMoney Order, an Electronic Money Order by RealPage takes away the risk of having cash and paper money orders on hand which can be costly and sometimes too hard for some people to resist. Paper money orders can be lost, stolen or altered, which can lead to fraud and lost collections for your property. RealPage eMoney orders can help. RealPage eMoney Order lets your residents purchase an electronic money order at thousands of retail locations. So both you and your residents don’t have to worry about fraud or theft associated with paper items.
### Convenience
It’s incredibly easy; your residents pick up a payment coupon associated with their unit’s account from your leasing office or they can print one from your resident portal. With more than 30,000 eMoney Order supported retail locations to choose from, residents can find the [nearest and most convenient retailer here](https://www.checkfreepay.com/), or by going to the resident portal. Then they just present the coupon with the cash to complete their payment. The payment is electronically sent and immediately posted to the resident’s ledger. Take away the temptation and put an end to lost, stolen or altered eMoney orders.
