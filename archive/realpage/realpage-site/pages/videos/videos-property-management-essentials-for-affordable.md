# RealPage Property Management Essentials for Affordable
#### The end-to-end solution designed to scale as you grow
Gain efficiencies across property management, program compliance and resident management with RealPage® Property Management Essentials for Affordable, the end-to-end solution designed to scale as you grow.
Compliance workflows within OneSite® provide built-in regulation validation and compliance support for HUD, Tax Credit and Rural Development. And OneSite Affordable Waitlist helps you confidently enforce your selection criteria and manage your waitlist in accordance with affordable housing regulations.
The ActiveBuilding® resident app enables communications between staff and residents allowing residents to submit maintenance requests, receive notifications and pay rents. After maintenance requests are received, Facilities and Mobile Facilities raises maintenance service levels and lowers response times.
RealPage Accounting is a feature-rich, web-based property management accounting solution designed for corporate operations of any size and delivers streamlined, automated workflows and dramatic savings in time and labor.
For more information, visit [realpage.com/essentials/affordable/](https://www.realpage.com/essentials/affordable/ "Affordable").
