# RealPage® Platform Securely Organizes Property Operations and Data
Gina Carter of Blue Ridge Companies found herself overwhelmed by the amount of paper stored and cluttered around the office, making it a challenge to easily locate, update or securely organize resident and property information. With RealPage® solutions, all that paper disappeared. RealPage delivers a secure platform for handling and storing important documents and data that keep her properties running smoothly. The Today Page in OneSite® Leasing & Rents allows her staff to sort resident packets and identify which items are missing, eliminating the time-consuming search and guesswork associated with completing packets.
The paperless advantages of the platform also opened the door to other RealPage products for Gina to implement across the Blue Ridge portfolio of properties. With the Mobile Facilities App, residents can easily submit work orders and management can view the full service history for every unit. Electronic invoicing has decreased the frequency of vendor invoices received from daily to monthly. And RealPage solutions also give residents convenient online access to their leasing and community information in a single portal. RealPage® paperless solutions are essential for keeping all the Blue Ridge office operations efficient and organized.
For more information, visit [RealPage Property Management](https://www.realpage.com/property-management-software/).
