#### Part of the Peak Performance Utility Management Suite
It’s important for multifamily property owners and managers to maximize recovery on rising utility costs as well as improve billing efficiency and accuracy. RealPage Resident Billing, part of the Utility Management Suite, simplifies and automates billing to maximize recovery, reduce late fees and increase NOI.
Powered by one of the nation’s largest utility expense databases and supported by expert utility advisors, Resident Billing provides a comprehensive and flexible set of tools and services for online billing and invoice processing so you can launch a successful resident billing and utility cost recovery program.
Customizable so you can give your residents a statement that details their utility use along with line items for rent, amenity fees, balance carryovers or additional charges. Along with the convenience of a single bill, residents will have flexible payment options.
Resident Billing seamlessly integrates with RealPage OneSite and other major property management systems for easier invoicing, billing, final utility bills and accounting. RealPage uses a data verification process to make utility billing fast and accurate for maximum recovery.
The RealPage Utility Management Suite comes with a dedicated utility account advisor who will review your portfolio and make recommendations for added property value. You’ll also be backed by one of the most experienced multifamily legal teams who will advise you on your current legal standing and regulatory compliance, which will mitigate risk.
