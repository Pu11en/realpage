# RealPage® Senior Living Keeps Everything in Order
RealPage Senior Living is a 360º cloud-based platform that integrates and automates every functionality to make the most of your senior living property assets. Whether you have assisted living, memory care, independent living or all three types of properties in your portfolio, this complete solution makes all the right moves for you, so you can boost occupancy, reduce risk and maximize portfolio value.
It begins with engaging, customized websites offering virtual reality tours of your property and stunning property photography. Our dedicated, HIPAA-compliant Senior Contact Center is there to nurture every lead. On-site caregivers can easily manage events, medical alerts, and care assessments. And you can maintain a high-level view of what drives the most leads, brings in the most revenue and provide comprehensive reporting to all stakeholders with [RealPage Business Intelligence](https://www.realpage.com/insights-analytics/business-intelligence/).
From lead to lease to living, [RealPage Senior Living](https://www.realpage.com/senior/) makes all the right moves for you, so you can focus on making the most of your business.
