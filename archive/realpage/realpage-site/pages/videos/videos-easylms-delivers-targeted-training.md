# RealPage Easy LMS Delivers Targeted Training Seamlessly Without the Hassle
Previous learning solutions weren’t strategic or targeted for Christian Virtusio’s team at Woodmont Real Estate Services. But the property management experts at RealPage understood his specific business _and_ training needs and helped simplify the migration of years’ worth of content, information and records. In addition, the preloaded content in the EasyLMS platform already addressed the specific tools and processes used at Woodmont, so they were able to move through implementation to rollout seamlessly—without anyone else even noticing the system switch. Avoiding the extra expense of course customization and backend support, Christian saved time and resources without sacrificing on training strategy. Working closely with his RealPage specialist, he delivered an education program with Easy LMS that not only speaks directly to his employees but also exceeds operational expectations—and he gained a business partnership that “feels almost like a family.”
