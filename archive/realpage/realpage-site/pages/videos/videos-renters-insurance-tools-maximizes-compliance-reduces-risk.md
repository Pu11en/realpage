# Maximizing Compliance with RealPage Renters Insurance
According to a NMHC Apartment Cost of Risk Survey, 30% of third-party renters insurance policies are canceled within the lease term, leaving the property exposed to risk. To help keep apartment communities protected, properties can enroll in our innovative compliance management program.
With this program, RealPage can automatically email residents when their policies lapse or cancel. We can even email residents who show no record of insurance at the time of move in, and those who have third-party policies that are up for renewal. This helps increase compliance by up to 40%, and saves time and administrative costs.
