# RealPage Helps PMI Properties Maintain Vendor Requirements
#### PMI Properties turned to RealPage Vendor Credentialing to help maintain requirements for current and new vendors.
Brian Birdy, CEO PMI Properties, realized that having a qualified crew of vendors was essential in order to be a successful property manager.
Prior to partnering with RealPage® Vendor Credentialing, he found that maintaining requirements for current vendors was a challenging task.
Now, PMI is now able to establish their own standards for each vendor type while RealPage tracks which ones are in compliance and which ones fall short of expectations—all without extra work for the property managers.
With RealPage Vendor Credentialing, Brian and his staff have access to a pre-established vendor base. And, they’re assured that current and new vendors have the proper insurance documents, professional licenses and business requirements—which frees up his team to focus on their residents.
