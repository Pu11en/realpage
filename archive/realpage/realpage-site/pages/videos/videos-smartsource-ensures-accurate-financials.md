#### Lindemann Multifamily Management turned to SmartSource Accounting to help them gain full transparency into their accounting system.
Lindemann Multifamily Management chose RealPage® SmartSource™ Accounting team to help increase three things: scalability, efficiency and savings.
They determined that SmartSource offered the best solution to help them take full control over their data and gain transparency into their accounting system.
As a result, they’ve been able to set up budgets using the budgeting module in OneSite and dispose and acquire properties quickly without having to worry about building out teams.
Plus, they’re more organizationally efficient, with the ability to look for opportunities to be more efficient using their own accounts to find information—as opposed to just having them do transaction entries every day.
And, with SmartSource taking on things like stuffing envelopes with checks and preparing postage, there is substantial labor savings. All in all, scalable, nimble and efficient.
