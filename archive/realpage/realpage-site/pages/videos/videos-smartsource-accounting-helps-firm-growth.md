# SmartSource™ Accounting Helps Firm Grow from 2 to 25 Properties
Sarah Hall of Reliant discovered that growing a small company with only a couple properties to manage was no easy feat with only one accountant. RealPage® SmartSource™ Accounting immediately eased her struggles to find industry-savvy accountants in the competitive and costly San Fransisco market. SmartSource Outsourced Accounting provides her with the knowledge, experience and relationship of an internal accounting department without the cost, time and energy required to hire and train accountants. Working with the RealPage SmartSource team, Sarah grew her business from 2 to 25 properties in just two years. SmartSource always produces books that are up to her standards and helps her achieve her goals while keeping operating costs to a minimum.
