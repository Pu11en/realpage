# RealPage Student Streamlines Housing Operations
Austin-based Campus Advantage, established in 2003, manages and develops over 70 student housing properties that include over 38,000 beds across the United States. Jenn Cassidy, Vice President and COO, knows that Campus Advantage’s partnership with RealPage has helped them grow, develop and maintain properties for more than a decade. RealPage® Student products have become the base of their operations, and the functions provided in each solution allow management to streamline work while saving time. Over the years, as Campus Advantage has implemented more of RealPage’s products—like YieldStar and Ops Technology—Jenn has noticed the continued proficiency and expertise of the RealPage Student team and appreciates their knowledge of the niche student housing market. The RealPage Student team is always looking for new methods of supporting Campus Advantage and recommending products they know will improve efficiencies for the company.
For more information, visit [RealPage Student](https://www.realpage.com/student/).
