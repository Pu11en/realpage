Kevin Wilkinson, Senior Vice President of Accounting for Prometheus reviews the benefits of RealPage vendor management and spend management software, including invoicing, reporting, integration and more.
With spend management software, a portfolio’s complete P.O. and approval process is automated with real-time visibility into spending against budget. Online purchasing, through a custom catalog with approved vendors, offers the benefit of purchase consolidation and standardization for cost savings. Paperless invoice processing software reduces the cost to process invoices and speeds up cycle times for reduced late fees.
Ultimately, the complete procurement solution allows properties to manage renovation projects and track spending against budgets, making it easy for vendors to stay informed and collaborate.
