# RealPage is EPA 2022 ENERGY STAR Partner of the Year for the Second Consecutive Year
From property managers to residents, RealPage helps create better overall living experiences through software and technology. That’s why, for the second consecutive year, RealPage has received the prestigious ENERGY STAR Partner of the Year Award from the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the U.S. Department of Energy.
As the EPA’s highest level of recognition, the Partner of the Year award celebrates RealPage’s commitment to transforming environmental innovation and protection through energy efficiency and ENERGY STAR and acknowledges RealPage’s achievements in driving innovative sustainability and green operations that bring solutions to every aspect of the multifamily housing industry.
Most notable acknowledgments from the EPA this year are focused on RealPage’s Smart Waste Solution, which has enabled RealPage customers to increase recycling by over 14,000 tons.
