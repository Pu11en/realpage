# Integrated Rent Payments Platform Makes Rent Week Easy
[ Contact Us ](https://www.realpage.com/videos/integrated-rent-payments-platform/#form1)
#### RealPage® Payments makes paying and collecting rent faster, easier and more secure.
Streamline and simplify how your residents pay—and you collect—rent payments. RealPage Payments is the complete platform designed to make paying and collecting rent faster, easier and more secure for everyone, both residents and on-site staff.
No matter how residents choose to pay—online with ACH or with credit or debit card, eMoney order, or a check—they’re looking for convenience and safety. And convenience for them means reduced delinquencies and more on-time payments for you. RealPage Payments eliminates the in-office handling and processing of paper payments, supporting a contactless office while allowing your residents to pay rent the way they prefer.
Fully integrated with OneSite® Leasing & Rents, RealPage Payments gives you real-time updates on all rent payments, deposits, fees and balances from one, seamless interface where you can easily see everything all in one place. Ultimately, making rent week easy.
For more information, visit [RealPage Paperless Payments](https://www.realpage.com/online-payments/ "Paperless Payments").
