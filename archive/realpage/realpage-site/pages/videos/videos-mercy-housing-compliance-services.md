# RealPage® Compliance Services Makes a Real Difference for Mercy Housing
The mission of Mercy Housing is to house the most vulnerable parts of the population, so moving fast is extremely important to getting families off the street and into a home of their own. With an in-house compliance team, they just weren’t able to keep up with demand, as well as changing city, state or federal regulations. With RealPage Compliance Services (formerly Windsor Compliance), an average file review times of one to two business days means the world, not only to Mercy’s operational efficiency, but also to its residents—many of whom are in desperate need of a home.
RealPage Compliance Services’ housing compliance professionals are experts at helping to mitigate risk, maximize occupancy and ensure property compliance with federal, state and local government regulations. We offer compliance monitoring for Affordable Housing and asset management for all property types, taking the burden off of your staff, mitigating risk and keeping your properties in line with changing regulations. For more information, visit [RealPage® Compliance Services](https://www.realpage.com/compliance-services/).
