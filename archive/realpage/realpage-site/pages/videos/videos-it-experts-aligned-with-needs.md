# SmartSource Full-Service IT Experts Aligned with Business Needs
BH Management’s Brandy Daniel wanted to separate IT from business intelligence without losing the professional skills and equipment available with a dedicated network technology team. She found that using RealPage® SmartSource™ IT for outsourcing gives her the expert skill set and hardware to run her IT systems to support exactly the way her business needs to operate. The critical difference is that the team at SmartSource IT understands the timing of BH Management’s network needs—remaining responsive and always available for whatever they need, especially during the budget season when the network is running cyclically to support additional data processing and reporting. From scaling up in July and August to scaling down in February, SmartSource works in step with Brandy’s team to anticipate and adjust to their changing network requirements, secure their sensitive data, and support their diverse property tech needs. With SmartSource, she gets what she didn’t have with other vendors: transparency, thorough information, and shared goals. Brandy trusts the knowledgable and honest team at SmartSource IT to support and advise their business through expected and unexpected changes in their real estate operations—and to empower her to understand the reasons behind the technology recommendations that lead to good business decisions.
