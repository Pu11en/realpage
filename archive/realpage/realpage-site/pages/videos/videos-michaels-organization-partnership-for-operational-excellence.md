### Discover how The Michaels Organization transformed their operations and streamlined property management with RealPage OneSite.
#### The Challenge: **Simplify Complex Processes**
For The Michaels Organization, updating their entire portfolio to a new version of their property management system was a significant undertaking. They needed a solution that could not only simplify complex processes but also enhance the user experience for their site teams. They were missing out on the data efficiencies they could gain from using multiple RealPage solutions like using OneSite together with LOFT.
#### The Solution: **A Strategic Transition** with RealPage OneSite
The Michaels Organization partnered with RealPage to adopt new OneSite, a comprehensive property management platform. The partnership was defined by extensive, hands-on support from RealPage’s dedicated team. Kamila Snuffer, Financial Systems Specialist at The Michaels Organization, noted the critical role of this support. "The support we received from RealPage was extensive," she stated. "Having a dedicated team to guide us through every step ensured our teams felt confident and prepared."
#### Key Outcomes for The Michaels Organization
  * **Simplified User Experience**
  * **Centralized Efficiency**
  * **Enhanced Affordable Leasing**
  * **Seamless Integrations with LOFT**

#### The Impact: A Transformative Partnership
By partnering with RealPage, The Michaels Organization successfully modernized its operational framework. The combination of an intuitive platform and dedicated support has empowered their teams, improved property operations workflows, and created a more efficient leasing cycle.
“The support, the new system's ease of use, and the centralized functionalities have been a game-changer for us.”
“I constantly get feedback from my teams about how much easier new OneSite is to use.”
