# How RealPage Benchmarking Positioned ZRS Management for Success

Michael Baily-Tonini, Sr. Manager Operations & Technology at ZRS Management, LLC, shares how RealPage® Benchmarking gives the company the tools and insights they need to best position their assets and portfolio for success.
With real-time visibility into daily leasing transactions, they are able to benchmark their assets against their own portfolio and compare them to the competition. By removing the guesswork and accessing RealPage’s dataset of over 13.5 million units of lease-transaction data, they gain clarity and confidence in making more strategic decisions now and in the future.
RealPage® Benchmarking helps ZRS Management quickly pinpoint their plan of action by supplying reliable data that shows them where they stack up against the market and what opportunities are available.
