# An Online Leasing Platform with More Features That Make You More Money
[ Contact Us ](https://www.realpage.com/videos/online-leasing-solution-overview-presentation/#form1)
RealPage Online Leasing gives renters the online leasing experience they want: convenient, comprehensive and uncomplicated. And makes it easier for staff to capture more qualified leads and close more leases. Accessible on any device—computer, tablet or smart phone—any place, any time and on practically any browser. It also works with any third party property management platform.
Prospects want options and RealPage Online Leasing enables you to provide them. They can choose the right floorplan, the right apartment and the right lease terms that they can customize to ensure they’re getting the right price and you’re getting maximum revenue when you have YieldStar revenue management integrated with the platform.
Margaret Berry with UDR says, “My favorite feature is the shopping cart because I have seen that it does make us money.” Our shopping cart experience lets prospects and residents choose amenities and rentables, select appliances, choose pet accommodations and upgrade packages. They get more control customizing their new home and you get more opportunities for increasing revenue.
