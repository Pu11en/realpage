# RealPage Utility Management Saves Trees, Time and Money
Before switching to RealPage® Utility Management (RUM), Goldrich Kest was cutting, stuffing and mailing over 7,000 checks a month. Chris Nabours knew that this labor-intensive and paper-heavy process was costing the organization valuable time and resources. They chose the industry leader to execute on their electronic bill pay and invoice processing initiative. Now, Goldrich Kest only cuts 1,800 paper checks a month, which saves both the invaluable time of the GK staff and an entire tree’s worth of paper used in their volume prior to the switch. Overall, RealPage Utility Management has created a 67% savings for Goldrich Kest, allowing them to continue their transformation into a more forward-looking company.
