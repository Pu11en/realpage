#### Vacant Cost Recovery lets you recapture utility expenses that should be rightfully paid by the residents who used those utilities
Discover how Vacant Cost Recovery (VCR) can automatically identify utility theft and notify you of residents who’ve not established the correct utilities. Learn how you can pass the costs on to the appropriate residents to help maximize recovery.
With VCR, a key component of the RealPage® Utility Management Suite, you have access to customizable reporting to view trends and metrics that give you transparency into the units where utilities were not correctly transferred to resident accounts at move-in or back to the property at move-out.
In this video, see how you can identify utility theft, pass utility charges on to the appropriate residents, and increase utility cost recovery with the most comprehensive, flexible set of online billing, invoice processing tools, and services in the industry.
