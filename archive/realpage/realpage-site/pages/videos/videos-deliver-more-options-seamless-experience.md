# Deliver More Options & Seamless Experiences
**Marcie Williams, President at** **RKW Residential** , shares her insights:
Remove roadblocks and layovers in your leasing experience that encourage prospects to shop around. Instead, give them convenient, flexible options for virtual and self-guided tours that immediately funnel them into the leasing process — without waiting around for an agent. And give your on-site staff a stake in servicing those online visits for greater leasing success.
