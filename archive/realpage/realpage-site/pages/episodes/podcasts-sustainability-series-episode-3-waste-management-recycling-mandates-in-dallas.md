The Dallas ordinance mandating recycling services for all multi-unit residential buildings with eight or more units went into effect in January 2020. Did you know that Big D property owners are now required to provide at least 11 gallons of capacity per unit under the new ordinance? If you didn’t, you’re not alone. Join our industry experts as they discuss what owners and operators need to know to stay in compliance including:
  * Outlining recycling requirements
  * Details on mandated reporting
  * Insights on the Dallas Zero Waste Plan
