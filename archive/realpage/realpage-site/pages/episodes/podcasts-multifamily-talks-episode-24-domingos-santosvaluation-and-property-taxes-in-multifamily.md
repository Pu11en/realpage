### Arben Skivjani
##### Deputy Chief Economist and Director of Forecasting
##### RealPage, Inc.
Arben Skivjani serves as a Deputy Chief Economist and Data Scientist for RealPage, Inc. His primary focus is on forecasting and econometric modeling, economic impact studies, reporting and analysis of macroeconomic trends that affect the multifamily industry. Prior to joining RealPage, Arben worked for several government and private sector entities, including the District of Columbia Department of Employment Services, Center for Business and Economic Development at the University of Alabama and Camoin Associates in Upstate New York.
Arben is heavily involved in the Dallas/Fort Worth Association for Business Economics where he currently serves as Vice President and is a member the National Association for Business Economics.
Arben holds a master’s degree in economics from The University of Alabama.
### Domingo Santos
##### Real Estate Tax Director & In-house Counsel
##### The Wolff Company
Domingos is responsible for advising The Wolff Company's development, asset management, investment management and preferred equity platforms on state and local tax issues, and providing legal counsel to Revel Communities, a leading luxury senior independent living platform.

Before joining The Wolff Company, Domingos counseled REITs, private equity firms, and real estate developers and operators on state and local tax compliance and tax reduction strategies. He started his career advising the Maricopa County Assessor's Office through the 2008 financial crisis.

Active in policymaking, Domingos has worked with the Arizona Legislature on property tax issues, including leading an effort to curtail the authority of local county assessors to sue taxpayers.

Domingos recently founded Flourish Living, a non-profit organization committed to resident flourishing and housing affordability.
Multifamily Talks is a Feedspot Top 100 Multifamily Real Estate podcast and an iApartments Top 5 Multifamily Operator podcast.
