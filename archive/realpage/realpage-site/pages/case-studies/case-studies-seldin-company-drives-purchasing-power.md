#### Seldin Company turned to RealPage® for a robust procurement solution to manage different spending requirements across a diverse portfolio.
The team at Seldin Company, a leading property management firm with deep roots throughout the Central U.S., was in search of a more robust procurement solution. Their goal was to control spend across a diverse portfolio of properties—from affordable and HUD to conventional multifamily.
And they required a browser-agnostic platform that offered the depth and breadth of accounting knowledge to support their business.
At the beginning of the pandemic, they migrated to RealPage® Spend Management with OpsTechnology. Today, they also leverage RealPage Invoice Processing and RealPage Accounting.
Now, invoice processing takes about half the time it did with their previous provider, and they typically receive invoice processing approvals within one day.
Read more about how Seldin’s migration helped them take charge of spend, purchasing and invoicing across their rental real estate portfolio.
For more information, visit [RealPage Spend Management](https://www.realpage.com/spend-management/).
