## How Dayrise Residential boosted leases, occupancy, and performance with G5 + Knock®.
After migrating more than 40 community websites from legacy platforms to G5 and integrating Knock CRM, Dayrise Residential unlocked dramatic improvements across marketing visibility, renter engagement, and leasing outcomes. The upgraded digital foundation delivered a 57% increase in signed leases, a 24% rise in occupancy, and stronger, more consistent organic performance across Google Search, Maps, and Business Profiles.
With SEO-optimized content, structured website architecture, transparent resident-focused design, and seamless CRM integrations, Dayrise gained full-funnel visibility, from first click to signed lease. This case study explores how G5’s conversion-driven website experience, paired with Knock’s intuitive CRM, helped a lean marketing team work smarter, prioritize high-intent renters, and accelerate leasing velocity.
Learn how a unified marketing + leasing engine created measurable impact and positioned Dayrise Residential for scalable growth in an increasingly AI-driven search landscape.
