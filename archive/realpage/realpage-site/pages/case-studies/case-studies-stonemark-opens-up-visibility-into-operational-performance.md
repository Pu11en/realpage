#### RealPage Knock CRM facilitates more strategic decisions with enhanced reporting and improved prospect and employee communication
Stonemark wanted to ensure it had the right solution to tackle the complex operational challenges of acquiring more properties. Specifically, it sought a customer relation management (CRM) tool that allowed easy access to data aggregation and reporting to help convert prospects into residents along with real-time visibility into the performance of Stonemark properties and employees.
In this case study, see how this multifamily property management company used RealPage® Knock® CRM to:
  * Consolidate the management of 60+ properties throughout the Southeast & Midwest
  * Increase occupancy by 20% for some properties
  * Reduce onboarding time for leasing agents to less than two weeks
