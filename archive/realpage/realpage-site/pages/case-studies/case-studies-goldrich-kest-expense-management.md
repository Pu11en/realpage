#### Goldrich Kest implements comprehensive Expense Management solution for strategic gains in efficiency, control and value.
Multifamily innovator Goldrich Kest Management collaborated with RealPage® to implement a strategy to dramatically improve the long-term value of the company’s properties while also realizing immediate process improvement, efficiency gains and cost savings. The comprehensive initiative involved all three suites of the RealPage Expense Management solution—Utility Management, Procurement and Sustainability—covering operational expense and utility invoice processing, resident billing, sustainability, online procurement and operations.
RealPage’s dedicated Account Managers helped Goldrich Kest exceed goals and institute change throughout the organization with actionable data insights, extensive industry and regulatory experience, and personalized service tailored to address their needs. This successful partnership took the firm from reactive to proactive, improving controls and processes across all aspects of the business—not only substantially improving the resident experience, but ultimately achieving an extraordinary return on Goldrich Kest’s investment.
