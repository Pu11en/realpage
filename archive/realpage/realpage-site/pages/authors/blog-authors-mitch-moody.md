# Mitch Moody: Author Profile | RealPage

RealPage Blog Authors

Senior Director of Product Management, Screening, RealPage, Inc.

Mitch brings more than 15 years of multifamily product leadership to RealPage where he currently serves as the Senior Director of Product Management, spearheading the development of cutting-edge fraud prevention solutions.

Prior to joining RealPage, Mitch co-founded and served as the Chief Technology Officer for Anyone Home, a customer relationship management system focused on the rental housing market. He also played a pivotal role in establishing the first Product Management team and successfully launched multiple flagship products at Entrata. Mitch is a former Army Ranger, having served four combat tours across various theaters.
