# Author Posts: Jennifer Hewett | RealPage Blog

RealPage Blog Authors

Jennifer Hewett is the Industry Principal for Onesite Multifamily Solutions, a product division of RealPage, Inc. As an Industry Principal, Jennifer works closely with the Sales Department and Product Team to guide the direction and continuing development of the RealPage Core product, OneSite Leasing and Rents. She joined RealPage in May 2010, bringing over 20 years experience in sales and customer advocacy. Jennifer received her Bachelor of Arts from the University of California, Berkeley.
