# RealPage Student Living Software

**What it is:** It is a 360-degree platform built for student housing. It is designed to help drive revenue, reduce costs, and meet business goals.
**Who it's for:** Student housing property operators and their staff.
**Also called:** Realpage Student Living, Student Housing Property Management Software

**Parts of it:**
- Student Asset Optimization
- Student Property Operations
- Student Renter Engagement
- Revenue Management
- Business Intelligence
- Market Analytics

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Uses over 1 million student beds of in-depth market data.
- Includes KPIs for nearly 1,000 universities.
- Contains the industry’s most comprehensive historical data set from 2013.
- Offers a 100% paperless payment solution, as the only licensed provider.
