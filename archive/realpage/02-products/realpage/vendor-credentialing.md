# RealPage Realpage® Vendor Credentialing Software

**What it is:** This software automates vendor credentialing, vendor management, insurance tracking, and compliance requirements. It is a full-service solution customizable for unique property management needs.
**Who it's for:** Property management companies and their staff managing vendors for properties.
**Also called:** Compliance Depot

**Parts of it:**
- Vendor Mobile App
- Ability to Contact the Vendor's Insurance Agent
- Insurance Agent Portal
- Credential Key Program
- Automated compliance tracking
- Real-time visibility

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Documents submitted via the Insurance Agent Portal are fast tracked.
- Vendors with the Credential Key distinction are eligible for same-day approval.
- The software processes submitted documents and tracks requirements by property management company.
- It provides custom reporting details on enrolled, exempt, and approved vendors.
