# RealPage Invoice to Investor

**What it is:** This solution manages high-quality utility data across the spectrum from utility expense invoices to sustainability and investor reporting. It controls operating expenses by tracking utility invoices and provides ESG reporting to investors for multifamily properties.
**Who it's for:** Real Estate Property Owners and Operators who manage multifamily properties and portfolios.

**Parts of it:**
- Centralization - Operations and Data
- Management Reporting
- Business Intelligence
- Expense Reduction
- Financial Planning
- Risk Management

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Brings together Realpage Utility Management tools with Sustainability Services.
- Partners with Measurabl to centralize and standardize data.
