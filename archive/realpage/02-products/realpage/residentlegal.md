# RealPage ClickPay Payments

**What it is:** ClickPay Payments enables online electronic payments to the owner or manager of your property. Each payment submitted through the service is made directly to the payee, with ClickPay acting as their authorized agent for processing.
**Who it's for:** Residents making payments to their property owner or manager.
**Also called:** ClickPay
