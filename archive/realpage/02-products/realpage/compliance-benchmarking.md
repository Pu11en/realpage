# RealPage Multifamily Utility Cost Benchmarking

**What it is:** This product helps multifamily building owners track and report their annual energy and water consumption. It allows them to measure and compare utility use against similar buildings, identify areas for improvement, and reduce waste to meet compliance mandates.
**Who it's for:** Multifamily building owners and operators.
**Also called:** Benchmarking Compliance

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Meets Local, State, ESG, and Green Loan Mandates.
- Supports reporting through EPA’s ENERGY STAR® Portfolio Manager®.
- Addresses utility benchmarking ordinances enacted by cities, states, and municipal governments across North America.
