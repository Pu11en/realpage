# RealPage Buildium

**What it is:** Buildium simplifies the complexity of managing mixed portfolios, offering a unified property platform that adapts to every unit type. It helps users stay organized and responsive across every property they manage.
**Who it's for:** Property managers and their teams who manage mixed portfolios across multiple asset types, including residential tenants, community associations, and commercial leases.

**Parts of it:**
- Automated rent and fee collection
- Customizable lease terms
- Maintenance workflows
- Board communications
- Mobile-friendly tools
- Real-time reporting
