# RealPage Realpage Vendor Services

**What it is:** This service provides an end-to-end platform for contractors, service providers, and vendors to connect with property managers. It helps them market their business, get approved for work, and get paid quickly.
**Who it's for:** Contractors, service providers, and vendors (both local and national) that work with multifamily property management companies.

**Parts of it:**
- Vendor Credentialing
- Vendor Marketplace
- Managed Vendor Catalog & eInvoice
- Credential Key
- Insurance Agent Portal

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Access to more than 12,000 potential customers.
- Seamlessly connects with Realpage property management systems.
- Enables faster, even same-day, approvals when requirements are met.
- Submissions through the Insurance Agent Portal are prioritized for quicker processing.
