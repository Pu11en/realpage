# RealPage Knock CRM

**What it is:** Knock CRM is an all-in-one customer relationship management solution for streamlining operations, improving team efficiency, and driving results. It integrates marketing, leasing, analytics, and AI tools into one platform to optimize the entire leasing lifecycle.
**Who it's for:** Multifamily and apartment property managers and property management teams.

**Parts of it:**
- Centralized Leasing Suite
- Automation of repetitive tasks with AI
- AI-enabled omnichannel engagement
- Lead source performance & marketing attribution analytics
- Lumina AI Leasing Agent
- Call Intelligence

**What RealPage says about it:**
- Increases tour conversions by 22% with AI Leasing Agent.
- Covers 2.8 million apartment units.
- Achieves a ~34 day average lead-to-lease conversion time.
- Integrates with major PMS platforms including Realpage, Yardi, and Entrata.
