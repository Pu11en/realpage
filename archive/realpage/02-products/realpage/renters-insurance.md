# RealPage Renter Insurance Solutions for Multifamily

**What it is:** It provides insurance options to cover the assets of multifamily properties and their residents. This includes comprehensive insurance coverage for residents and optional gap coverage for vacant units or uninsured residents.
**Who it's for:** Multifamily property management companies, owners, and residents in apartment communities.
**Also called:** eRenterPlan, eRenter Insurance

**Parts of it:**
- eRenterPlan
- RenterProtection
- Property Coverage
- Liability Coverage
- Additional Living Expenses

- More than 30K properties have eRenterPlan.
- Built into OneSite® Leasing & Rents software.
- Integrated into Online Leasing, On-Site, and the LOFT™ resident portal.
