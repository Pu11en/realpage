# Research: Does RealPage Have Property-Level PMS Competitor Data?

**Source:** SEC EDGAR, RealPage press releases, company product pages, job boards, patent databases
**Fetched:** 2026-09-10
**Method:** WebSearch + WebFetch
**Confidence:** Medium (limited direct employee/insider chatter; strong SEC/product documentation)

---

## VERDICT: NO STRONG SIGN

There is **no public evidence** that RealPage internally possesses or offers property-level data showing which competitor PMS (Yardi, Entrata, AppFolio, etc.) individual apartment communities use, or tracks when properties switch vendors. While RealPage has vast lease transaction data and market analytics assets, these derive from properties actively running RealPage's software—not from monitoring competitors' installed base.

---

## Findings by Source

### 1. SEC Filings (10-K: 2017, 2020)

**Key Quote – Competitive Advantage:**
> "Our competitive advantage primarily results from our large repository of lease transaction data, our expertise in apartment marketing and property management, and our analytical capabilities... our data is our competitive advantage... we have access to a massive repository of real-time lease transaction data" (2017 10-K)

> "The platform provides a single point of access via the Internet with a common repository of prospect, resident and property data" (2020 10-K extract via WebFetch)

**Data Scope – RealPage's Own Units:**
- 2020: ~19.7 million units across ~31,700 clients
- 2017: ~13 million units across ~12,400 clients
- Market coverage: 400+ markets
- **Limitation:** All data originates from properties using RealPage's platform, NOT from monitoring competitor PMS

**Patents/IP Protection:**
> "[RealPage] relies on copyright, trademark, and trade secret laws... currently [has] no issued patents or pending patent applications" (SEC filing)
- **Finding:** No patent portfolio to detect or track competitor software installations

**Competitive Landscape Section (2020 10-K via WebFetch):**
- Lists Yardi, Entrata, AppFolio, Buildium, MRI as competitors
- No mention of internal tools to track which competitor is deployed at each property
- No reference to competitive intelligence on vendor switching or market share by property

### 4. Product Pages & Acquisitions

#### **Axiometrics Acquisition (2016/2017, ~$75M)**
RealPage announced acquisition of Axiometrics, merging it with MPF Research and establishing partnership with Real Capital Analytics (MSCI).

**Key Quote – Strategic Intent:**
> "The acquisition of Axiometrics furthers our goal to become the definitive source for accurate data intelligence regarding the acquisition, operation and disposition of every market-rate apartment in the U.S." – Steve Winn, CEO

**Axiometrics Capabilities:**
- Surveys/researches properties in 500+ apartment markets
- Covers 13+ million units and 400 markets
- Provides market intelligence, benchmarking, competitive analysis
- Features: "monitoring the competition, leveraging pipeline visibility and comparing portfolio performance"
- **Property-level visibility:** Down to floorplan level for "competitive assets"

**Critical Limitation:**
> "helps in identifying development opportunities, monitoring the competition, **leveraging pipeline visibility and comparing portfolio performance**"

These capabilities track **competing apartment properties within markets** (occupancy, rent, concessions, pipeline), NOT which PMS software each property uses.

**URLs:**
- https://www.realpage.com/analytics/axiometrics-to-be-acquired-by-realpage-merge-with-mpf-research/ (announcement via WebFetch)
- https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286225/000128622517000002/exhibit991pressrelease.htm (SEC exhibit via WebFetch)

#### **G5/Demand Marketing & LeaseLabs (Marketing/Website Layer)**
- G5 rebranded as Demand Marketing; manages property websites for 8,300+ multifamily sites
- LeaseLabs (acquired 2018) provides rental market analytics
- **Capability:** Website SEO, paid media, reputation management across directories, lead attribution
- **Search:** Websites, directories, Google Business Profile, reviews—does **not** expose competitor PMS

**Limitation:** Portal/website visibility does not reveal which backend PMS (Yardi vs. Entrata vs. AppFolio) is running the property's leasing/accounting systems

#### **Knock CRM Acquisition (2022)**
- CRM/front-office tool; integrates with PMS systems but is **PMS-agnostic**
- Designed to work with any PMS (Yardi, Entrata, AppFolio, RealPage, etc.)
- Does NOT track or identify competitor PMS installs

**URLs:**
- https://www.realpage.com/apartment-marketing/marketing/ (Demand Marketing product page via WebFetch)
- https://www.geekwire.com/2022/real-estate-property-management-software-giant-realpage-to-acquire-seattle-startup-knock/

#### **Market Analytics Platform (Current Product)**
- Covers property-level rent, occupancy, concessions, floorplan, lease renewals
- Data sourced from:
  - RealPage's own lease transactions (14M+ units)
  - Axiometrics/MPF market surveys
  - MSCI Real Capital Analytics (sales transactions)
  - Historical data (30+ years)
- **Gap:** No mention of PMS software identification in any product documentation

**URL:**

## Conclusion

**RealPage possesses vast data on apartment communities that use its platform (14+ million units), but no public evidence suggests the company has property-level intelligence on competitor PMS software installs or vendor switching.**

Axiometrics provides competitive apartment market analysis (rent, occupancy, pipeline) but this is distinct from PMS identification. G5/LeaseLabs offer website visibility but do not expose backend PMS systems. No job postings, patents, or insider chatter surface a dedicated competitive intelligence effort targeting PMS software detection.

**Confidence:** Medium  
**Alternative Explanation:** RealPage may conduct win/loss analysis via sales teams post-acquisition, but this would be sales-side, customer-reported intelligence, not internally-sourced data on competitor PMS at the property level.
