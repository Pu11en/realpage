## Methodology Note

This research prioritizes **publicly verifiable evidence** only:
- ✅ Court/DOJ settlements (very high confidence—operator explicitly confirmed use)
- ✅ Press releases & announcements from vendors or operators
- ✅ SEC 10-K filings with operational disclosures
- ✅ Published case studies from vendors
- ✅ Job postings requiring specific software (indicates active use)
- ⚠️ Company website technographics (medium confidence; may not reflect entire portfolio)
- ❌ Forum posts, unconfirmed rumors, or secondary speculation
