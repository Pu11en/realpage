## Goal
- **PropertyStack** gives salespeople a weekly, sourced list of **new apartment buildings (20+ units, city-agenda/permit stage through lease-up, 6–18 months before opening) and recently sold buildings in any US metro**. Each lead has the developer/owner, who manages it, which property software it runs (or "not picked yet"), a "why call now" note, and a source link for every fact. It only uses outside-in public data and never guesses.
- **Drew's ultimate goal:** leverage for a **RealPage job interview** (show he can find, research and sell into buildings from public data). Fallback: sell to RealPage's rivals or other buyers.
- **Business goal right now:** get the first **3–5 early-access members** actively using the weekly list and giving feedback. Get the landing page online so outreach has somewhere to point.
- **Guiding framework:** the Minimalist Entrepreneur skills (github.com/slavingia/skills), installed in `business/.claude/skills/`. Sell one by one, processize before productizing, join the community.

## Decisions
- **Business work is separate from the product**: its own folder and git, never touching product files. It reads product lead data read-only only.
- **Any US area, never Plano-centric.** Plano–Richardson is only a labeled sample/test area.
- **Guide = Minimalist Entrepreneur skills.** **Bizkit is rejected**; don't use or recommend it.
- **No pricing at all for now** (Drew: "we not doing price at all").
  - The offer is **free early access**: "your metro's weekly list every Monday, free while we build it, in return for honest feedback (a 15-minute call after a few weeks)."
  - First step: "one free building from your metro."
  - The earlier $150/mo and $50 founding month are parked (`business/pricing/2026-09-14-pricing.md`, `business/marketing/setup/stripe.md`, `business/plans/PLAN-biz-3-payments.md`).
  - This deliberately overrides the book's "charge something."
- **Outreach: LinkedIn is still on, gift first** (a real building in the person's metro, never "want a demo?"), plus gift emails. The book-aligned focus for weeks 1–2 is personal outreach, one platform (LinkedIn) and an email list. Other channels wait until early-access members are using it.
- **Buyers to target:** property-management business development first. Also software sales, resident tech (locks, internet, lockers), suppliers/contractors and lease-up agencies. The landing form's "What do you sell?" answer shows which buyer bites.
- **Booking = Cal.com** (Drew's existing account, username `drew-pullen`), done via the API. **Signups = our own form, not Tally.**
- **Hosting = Railway**, in the existing `propertystack` project as a **4th separate service `propertystack-landing`**. Upload from the landing folder (`railway up`, no GitHub). Signups go on a Railway volume at `/data`, and `ADMIN_KEY` is a Railway variable. The 3 existing services are propertystack (front door), propertystack-chat (Open WebUI) and propertystack-chatbot (Hermes); they must not be touched.
- **No /gowork for business tasks.** Drew wants them done in-session. Subagents may refuse, citing the chunking rule, unless the prompt quotes Drew's approval.
- **Use GLM 5.3 when possible** to save cost (Z.ai via the Claude Code CLI: `set -a; . ~/.config/ccdb/glm.env; set +a; claude -p "..." --model glm-5.3 --dangerously-skip-permissions`). It has a 5-hour usage limit. Fall back to Claude when it's unavailable.
- **Next area of focus, per Drew:** **design** of the landing page, after functionality was confirmed (done).
- **Talking to Drew** (from his global rules): max 5 sentences per reply, plain English, and always end with one multiple-choice question (4–5 strong options, recommendation first). Guide him like a beginner. GitHub, pushing and deploying only after he tries it locally and says OK.
