# Three sample deep dives: what they say

## How these were made
- ✅ The deep-dive recipe ran on **3 real leads**; each brief has the fixed order: bottom line first, then who they are, why now, who to ask for, opener, 3 questions, 2 objections, sources.
- ✅ All three **passed the automatic checker** (every "why now" and "who to ask for" line has a link, no made-up phone numbers or emails, the opener never says "with RealPage").
- Used **17 web searches** out of a budget of 36.
- ⚠️ Yelp, ApartmentRatings and ForRent block automated reading, so resident-review evidence is thin.

## Vantage At Spring Creek (Richardson, 420 units)
- **Call: yes.** Sold on **June 11, 2026** to a new owner (TREA Routh Creek TX Owner LP); runs **Yardi** today.
- Listings still say **Bell Partners** manages it, but Bell's Yelp page is marked closed, so the manager may have changed (not confirmed).
- **Who to ask for:** Nicholas Dyer, Regional Manager at Bell Partners for Dallas–Fort Worth (public LinkedIn). Not confirmed he covers this building.
- ⚠️ The new owner's decision-maker was **not found**.
- Why now also cites a Reddit thread with complaints about Bell's management, and a ForRent review mentioning a payment problem (only the search preview; unconfirmed).

## Legacy Arapaho (Richardson, upcoming, 443 units)
- **Call: yes, early.** A new build by **Legacy Partners**; permit issued June 2026, so **software isn't chosen yet**.
- Legacy Partners manages its own buildings, so one company decision covers this one.
- **Who to ask for:** Brian McNally, VP of Development, named in the Community Impact article on the council vote. Backup: Matt Brendel, Senior Managing Director Southwest (2020 press release, may be out of date).
- ⚠️ Opening date disagrees: our list says **Nov 2028**, the city said **around Oct 2029**. The brief flags it as unconfirmed.

## Creekside At Legacy (Plano, 380 units): the "at risk" case
- **Call: yes.** Already runs **RealPage**. It was sold in **Sept 2024** to Key Creekside LLC and is managed by **Key Real Estate Company**.
- For a RealPage seller it's an account to keep and grow; for a competitor it's a target to win over.
- **Who to ask for:** Caitlin Roniger, Key's **Director of Systems**. Their team page says she vets and rolls out new software. Backup: Gary Soileau, Regional Director.
- Key already adds a separate rent-payment tool called Flex, which is a good question to open with.
- Resident complaints about the portal: **none found** (reviews are mostly positive).
